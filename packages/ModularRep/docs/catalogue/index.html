Source catalogue

Descriptions of all 1857 selected Lean modules, with their mathematical purpose, assumptions, argument, proof-account references and declarations.

Use the mathematical companion and library manual for sustained proofs, and the Start here for navigation and the package scope. This catalogue works offline.

Categories used in descriptions

K: checked deductions
Definitions, constructions and implications checked in Lean under their stated hypotheses.
E1: standard mathematical inputs
Background results supplied as explicit hypotheses.
E2: published implications
Precisely scoped results from the literature, with their hypotheses retained.
E3: computational and completeness inputs
Table data, classifications or numerical information at the stated finite scope.
U: identifications with the mathematical objects
Identifications of groups, representations and blocks, including compatibility of actions, coefficient fields and root choices.

These are categories, not confidence grades. Combined labels indicate that more than one category applies. References to the individually numbered hypotheses E1 through E11 in the generic weight construction use a separate numbering scheme from these categories. The E2 labels in the FLZ 3.18 descriptions denote the category of published implications.

Source descriptions retain their stated hypotheses. An index entry is not an additional proof check.
