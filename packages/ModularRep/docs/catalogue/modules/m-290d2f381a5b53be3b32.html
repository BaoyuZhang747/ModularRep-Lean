Purpose

Applies small-defect equality to the same specified blocks as the Brauer dictionary.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

Specified small-block Brauer counts give weight totals; positive fixed Brauer counts prove block stability, and stable singleton weight fibres are fixed.

Argument or construction

Translate the block labels to the literal operations catalogue and apply the guarded numerical source. A fixed Brauer character forces its block fixed. The two nonsingleton fixed counts remain separate local-row inputs, whereas the singleton fixed counts follow from invariance.

External assumptions and dependencies

Small-defect numerical source, actual defect orders and Brauer signatures; the Klein-four and dihedral weight fixed counts are supplied independently.
