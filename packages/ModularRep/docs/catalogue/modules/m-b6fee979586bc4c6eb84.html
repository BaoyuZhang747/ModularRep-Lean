Purpose

Constructs the forced equal-group candidates for FLZ clause (iii).

Mathematical statement and assumptions

Literal restriction and equality fibres are singletons, centre fibres are full, and the selected identity extension has the requisite induced block equality.

Trivial quotients kill linear characters and centrelessness removes central restrictions. Actual selected-weight block induction supplies the final equality.
