Purpose

Use the supplied constituent orbit, restricted Clifford correspondence and Gallagher twist relation to isolate the correcting linear twist.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

The stabilizer-restricted Clifford and linear-twist hypotheses yield product stabilizer factorization from the specified constituent factorization. Older full-actor declarations require their stronger action hypotheses. Current literal-inertia consumers use characteristicTwoClifford_stabilizerRestricted_factorization. The full-actor characteristicTwoClifford_stabilizer_factorization and characteristicTwoClifford_productStabilizerFactorization remain valid conditional general theorems under their stronger action hypotheses, rather than interchangeable inertia applications.

Argument or construction

Use the supplied constituent orbit, restricted Clifford correspondence and Gallagher twist relation to isolate the correcting linear twist. Odd-order twist data and an exponent-two effective quotient force the correction to be trivial, yielding stabilizer factorization.

External assumptions and dependencies

The stated Clifford constituent orbit and equivariant correspondence, Gallagher twist relation, finite odd-order linear-twist actor, effective exponent-two quotient and its kernel fixedness. The stabilizer-restricted and stronger full-actor formulations have different scopes.
