Purpose

This is the independent type A input to the relative Jordan reduction, not the Type C conclusion.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

For an actual finite SL/SU presentation of positive rank with perfect group and nonabelian simple central quotient, distinct defining and coefficient primes, and InputSemantics, the scoped E2 type-A source gives a Definition 3.5 family on the same specified group and blocks. InputSemantics contains a complete modular system, finite base/actor splitting roots and root-residue calibration.

Argument or construction

The source is scoped to actual finite special linear or unitary groups, a simple central quotient and a distinct defining prime. Apply its literal Definition 3.5 family witness and restrict to the requested block.

External assumptions and dependencies

The independent scoped type-A E2 consequence including the exceptional SL/SU and central-core passages; positive rank, actual finite-field presentation, perfectness and nonabelian simple central quotient. InputSemantics requires ModularSystem, base and block-actor ordinary roots, root_residue equality with the residue map, literal catalogue idempotents, specified local support and root-compatible corresponding weight reductions. No source inhabitant or Type C conclusion is constructed.
