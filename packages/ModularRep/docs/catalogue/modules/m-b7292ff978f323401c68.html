Purpose

Derives central character covering from a literal irreducible restriction.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

An irreducible ambient representation whose restriction is the given irreducible base representation yields CentralCharacterCovers for their actual blocks.

Argument or construction

A central element supported on the subgroup is recovered by mapping its coefficient restriction back upstairs. Consequently its operator action agrees in the ambient representation and the literal restriction. The affording identities translate this operator equality to the catalogue central characters.

External assumptions and dependencies

Actual representations, literal restriction, affording identities and block catalogues; no cyclicity, normality or covering theorem is assumed.
