Purpose

The first coordinate of every label is an actual element of the fixed-form CSp dual.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

A character's second coordinate is an actual ordinary irreducible character of its centralizer, restricted by the source unipotent predicate. The unipotent predicate and the combinatorial core family remain explicit source definitions (U); this file does not reconstruct Deligne--Lusztig theory or the partitions and symbols in FLZ Section 3.4.

Argument or construction

A character's second coordinate is an actual ordinary irreducible character of its centralizer, restricted by the source unipotent predicate. Equation (3.4) and its following paragraph (FLZ, J. Algebra 604 (2022), p. 545) classify all ordinary characters by semisimple/unipotent-character labels. Lean restricts this classification to ell-prime parameters and proves raw label surjectivity.
