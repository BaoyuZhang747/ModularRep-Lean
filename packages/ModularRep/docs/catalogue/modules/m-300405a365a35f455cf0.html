Purpose

Transports the own-quotient induction relation into a stored ambient.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

Independent global and local extension witnesses inherit a base block relation through the actual base/local equivalences and their commuting inclusion square.

Argument or construction

The proof transports roots, characters, primitive decompositions and central character catalogues together. It then applies induction naturality; no relation between the two ambient extension blocks is assumed here.

External assumptions and dependencies

Actual quotient/base equivalences with a commuting local inclusion square, two independent base-extension witnesses, retained roots/decompositions/catalogues and the already derived quotient base induction. No matching of ambient extension blocks is assumed.
