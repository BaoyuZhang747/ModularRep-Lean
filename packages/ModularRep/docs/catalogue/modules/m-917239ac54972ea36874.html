Purpose

Constructs the principal character's own central quotient on the triple cover.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

The quotient kernel is computed from an affording representation, then identified with the fixed projection kernel.

Argument or construction

The representation descends on its original space with restricted roots. Its character equals the accepted deflation and is centrally faithful, yielding the canonical quotient square.

External assumptions and dependencies

Existing principal-support kernel theorem and the actual cover map; full representation faithfulness is not asserted.
