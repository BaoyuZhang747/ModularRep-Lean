Purpose

Provide the perfect central-quotient geometry and identity-extension reduction used in complete collapse.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

A perfect finite group modulo a central subgroup satisfies the centre identity needed for the original central-kernel quotient; identity-extension data reduce the intermediate-group interval to one group.

Argument or construction

Use the centre theorem for perfect central quotients, then identify the quotient by the full centre. With the identity ambient group, every intermediate subgroup above the base is the whole group, so the block clauses reduce to the supplied base induction law.

External assumptions and dependencies

The finite actual group, characteristic prime, compatible coefficient/root data, specified sets of primitive blocks and characters, and the imported transport results are fixed as in the declaration context. Perfectness and centrality for the quotient identity; the identity-extension application separately uses its stated numerical/reduction and Späth interface data.
