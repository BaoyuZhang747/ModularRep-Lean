Purpose

Corrects independently selected automorphism images by an inner conjugator.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

Equality in the actual weight class gives a holomorph element retaining both the specified automorphism and an inner correction.

Argument or construction

The two quotient relations yield equality of whole raw pairs. The computed element then transports raw and Brauer stabilizers.

External assumptions and dependencies

Actual selected representatives and class equality; no equivariant selection or relation covariance is assumed.
