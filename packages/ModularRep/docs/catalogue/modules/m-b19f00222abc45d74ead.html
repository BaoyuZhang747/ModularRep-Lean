Purpose

This module contains only the literal carriers and narrowly stated source interfaces needed for defect-zero reductions and weights at the trivial radical subgroup.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

Actual primitive blocks, Brauer characters, radical classes and weight classes support a literal trivial-radical carrier and injective defect-zero reduction data.

Argument or construction

Construct the quotient at the trivial subgroup and identify its ordinary characters with those of the original group. The source laws supply reduction and trivial-weight membership; no global correspondence is chosen here.

External assumptions and dependencies

The finite actual group, characteristic prime, compatible coefficient/root data, specified sets of primitive blocks and characters, and the imported transport results are fixed as in the declaration context. Numerical AWC, injective defect-zero reduction, trivial-weight and local block compatibility sources remain explicit; full Späth packets also retain the required quotient catalogues and coefficient field source.
