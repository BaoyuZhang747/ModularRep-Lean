Purpose

Applies the historical prime-two stabilizer transfer to the literal constituent inertia under the exact Clifford/Gallagher and extension hypotheses.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

For the fixed prime-two normal chain and compatible Brauer roots, an extension to the full ambient constituent inertia and the stated source formulas are supplied.

Argument or construction

The characteristic-two stabilizer theorem is applied to the literal inertia action, whose actor is restricted to the constituent stabilizer. The resulting factorization concerns the chosen upper character rather than an arbitrary tensor-action model.

External assumptions and dependencies

Modular Clifford theory, Gallagher's theorem, and the existence of the required extensions are not reconstructed here. The ambient and field actions on psi and theta remain explicit inputs, but only their stabilisers of theta act on the fixed carrier IBr(H_theta). Thus the local E_theta action is no longer an independent source input: it follows from preservation of H and naturality of the action on the selected constituent.
