theorem-developmentcurrent

Purpose

The characteristic polynomial of an endomorphism factors as the product of the characteristic polynomials on an invariant subspace and on the quotient.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

A linear endomorphism preserving a finite-dimensional subspace has characteristic polynomial equal to the product of those of its restriction and induced quotient operator.

Argument or construction

Choose a basis adapted to the invariant subspace. The matrix is block upper triangular with the restricted and quotient operators on its diagonal, so its characteristic polynomial factors.

External assumptions and dependencies

A finite dimensional vector space over a field, a linear endomorphism and an invariant subspace.
