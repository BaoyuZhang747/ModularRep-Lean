Purpose

Proves the V3 four-row minor nonzero and constructs its finite rank certificate.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

For a primitive 29th root, the sixteen selected matrix entries and two nontrivial row relations certify rank four of the six raw rows.

Argument or construction

Expand the determinant into 98778939581527603200 times the difference of two complementary power sums. The cyclotomic sum relation rewrites that factor as a nonzero rational polynomial of degree at most 27 evaluated at the primitive root. Its minimal polynomial has degree 28, so the factor cannot vanish. The explicit row reconstruction then supplies the rank upper bound.

External assumptions and dependencies

A primitive 29th root and the selected-entry/row-relation binding to the raw functions. CanonicalSupplementV3Binding separately identifies the developmental V3 transcription and full symmetrizations with the canonical supplement.
