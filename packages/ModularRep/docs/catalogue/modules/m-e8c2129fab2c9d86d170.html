Purpose

Determines the induced defect from a local defect bound.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

A local defect with the radical's cardinality equals its normal copy; with the First Main upper witness the selected induced block has exactly the original radical as defect.

Argument or construction

Normal-p containment and equal cardinality identify the local defect. Nonzero induced support supplies the opposite subconjugacy inequality to the upper witness, forcing equality.

External assumptions and dependencies

Actual primitive decompositions and local catalogues, the stated local defect bound and Navarro417FirstParagraphUpperDefectSource remain explicit; the interval relation and opposite support containment are separately justified.
