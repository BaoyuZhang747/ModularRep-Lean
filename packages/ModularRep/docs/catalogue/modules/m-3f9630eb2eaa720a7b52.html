Purpose

Evaluates the checked integer residuals into a 41-row inverse certificate.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

Vanishing of the seven local moduli together with inverse, constant and reconstruction checks gives RowInverseCertificate of rank 41.

Argument or construction

Evaluate each grouped inverse residual and sum its constant terms to the scaled delta. Divide by the positive integer denominator, which is nonzero in characteristic zero. Evaluate the reconstruction residuals to express every row in the chosen basis.

External assumptions and dependencies

The exact literal checks and root values annihilating their tagged moduli.
