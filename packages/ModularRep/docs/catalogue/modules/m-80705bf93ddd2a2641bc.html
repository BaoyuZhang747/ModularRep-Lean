transport-adapterretained-alternative

Purpose

Constructs the Brauer half of trivial-sector quotient transport on the literal quotient by the center.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

The central p-prime quotient realization and centerlessness yield an equivariant cover-trivial-sector/quotient-Brauer equivalence; six remaining transport fields complete CarrierTransport.

Argument or construction

Identify the cover sector subtype with the block-central character fibre, apply the inverse canonical quotient inflation equivalence, then add the automatic faithful-sector proof on the centerless quotient. Compose the action squares. Retain the separately supplied weight and block equivalences, their squares and pair implication.

External assumptions and dependencies

Invertibility of the cover-center order, root/prime regular realization, automorphism descent square and centerlessness, plus six residual weight/block/pair fields. For 3.Fi24′ this cannot apply in characteristic three.
