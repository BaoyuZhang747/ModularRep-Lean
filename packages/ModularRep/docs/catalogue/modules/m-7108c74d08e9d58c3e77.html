Purpose

Derives finiteness and splitting for Spin from its special-Clifford overgroup.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

A finite special-Clifford overgroup and enough ordinary roots for its order give finiteness of its literal Spin norm kernel and enough roots for the Spin order.

Argument or construction

Subgroup finiteness gives the finite carrier. Divisibility of subgroup order transfers enough ordinary roots from the upper group to Spin.

External assumptions and dependencies

The finite Clifford realization and enough roots for the same upper group.
