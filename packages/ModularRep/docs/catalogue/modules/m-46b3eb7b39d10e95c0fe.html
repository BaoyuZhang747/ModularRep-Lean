Purpose

This file deliberately stops at the representation-level notion RepresentationWeight.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

Group equivalences transport representation weights by their canonical normalizer quotient isomorphisms, preserving radicality, irreducibility, dimension and the defect-zero degree condition.

Argument or construction

Construct normalizer and quotient isomorphisms under group equivalence and pull local representations through them. Dimension and irreducibility are preserved, so the transported radical and representation again form a weight.

External assumptions and dependencies

The actual group equivalence and radical representation weight; local quotient isomorphisms, simple representation pullback and degree preservation are proved dependencies.
