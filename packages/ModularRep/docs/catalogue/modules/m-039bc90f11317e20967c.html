Purpose

Converts the global intermediate interval to Späth's local H interval.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

For base normal in A and base intersection D contained in H contained in D, the law at base join H yields the required H restriction and induction relation.

Argument or construction

Normality proves that the intersection of base join H with D is H. Roots, characters and catalogues are transported through that equality, retaining the same selected restrictions.

External assumptions and dependencies

A normal base B, local subgroup D and the already proved AllIntermediateBlocks output. The local-H conversion requires B intersection D contained in H contained in D; no new character, root or catalogue is chosen independently.
