Purpose

Derives trivial intrinsic defect from modular coefficients.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

The complete specified class/centralizer/coefficient binding implies that the role-two primitive has maximal central Brauer support at the trivial subgroup.

Argument or construction

The checked values vanish modulo three on every element whose centralizer order is divisible by three. Any nontrivial 3-subgroup forces its Brauer-restriction coefficients into those classes, whereas restriction at the trivial subgroup retains the nonzero idempotent.

External assumptions and dependencies

An actual primitive block decomposition, complete class cover, actual centralizer orders and the stated specified modular-idempotent coefficient formula. Trivial maximal Brauer support is the conclusion.
