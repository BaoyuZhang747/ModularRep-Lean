theorem-developmentcurrent

Purpose

The group algebra inclusion induced by a literal subgroup inclusion is injective.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

Under C_G(P) ≤ H ≤ N_G(P), the central Brauer image vanishes in kC_G(P) exactly when its embedded image vanishes in kH.

Argument or construction

Embed the centralizer group algebra into the interval subgroup algebra by an injective map. Apply injectivity to the Brauer image to reflect zero and nonzero support.

External assumptions and dependencies

The established interval Brauer map, characteristic-p coefficients and the injective subgroup algebra embedding C_G(P) ≤ H.
