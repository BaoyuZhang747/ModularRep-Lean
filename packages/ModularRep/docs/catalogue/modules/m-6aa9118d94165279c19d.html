Purpose

Constructs the literal Morita tensor and extension for the retained selected representative.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

From the specified Morita sources and a chosen original Levi character with an affording module and extension over its actual field stabilizer, constructs an extension of the calibrated tensor/Jordan image while retaining those witnesses.

Argument or construction

The representative theorem is invoked once. Diagonal induction over that character's actual field stabilizer constructs the supported Morita square, and the retained Levi extension gives an extension of its tensor Jordan image.

External assumptions and dependencies

The selected representative sources and specified bimodule, finite induction/support, split Marcus and restriction-square inputs; not the excluded full-field representative route.
