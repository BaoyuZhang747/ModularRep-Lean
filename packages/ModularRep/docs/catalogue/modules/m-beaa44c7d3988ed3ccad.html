Purpose

Feng--Li--Zhang, *Advances in Mathematics* 408 (2022), Theorem 3.18, pp. 17--18, recalls the full Brough--Späth criterion.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

Relative compatible raw-weight actions and a normal embedded radical yield the stated cyclic local quotient; the supplied invariant Brauer realization extends under the explicit cyclic extension principle.

Argument or construction

Starting from compatible raw-weight actions, prove cyclic quotients of global and local stabilizers. Apply the extension principle to a supplied invariant local Brauer realization; the quotient and normality hypotheses remain explicit.

External assumptions and dependencies

The displayed relative RawWeightData and compatible actions, a normal embedded radical and an invariant local Brauer realization with fixed roots. Extension existence is the supplied Brauer cyclic extension principle.
