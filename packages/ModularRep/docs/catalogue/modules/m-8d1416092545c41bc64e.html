Purpose

Derives center and selected-cover facts from an actual kernel-three full cover.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

The center has order three; an automorphism inverting it moves some central element; at every prime other than three the full cover is the selected p-prime cover.

Argument or construction

The universal central extension onto a nonabelian simple group has kernel equal to its center. A nonidentity central element cannot satisfy both its order-three relation and equality with its inverse. Distinct prime divisibility gives the p-prime kernel condition for the cover constructor.

External assumptions and dependencies

The actual universal cover, kernel order three, base simplicity and noncommutativity, and the center-inversion equation.
