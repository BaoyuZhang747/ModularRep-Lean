Formalisation.CyclicQuotient

packages/formalisation/Formalisation/CyclicQuotient.lean

theorem-developmentcurrent

Purpose

Restrict the quotient map to a subgroup and calculate its kernel as intersection with the normal subgroup.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

For P normal in A with A/P cyclic, every subgroup K has cyclic quotient by K intersect P. That intersection is a normal p-subgroup when P is a p-group.

Argument or construction

Restrict the quotient map to a subgroup and calculate its kernel as intersection with the normal subgroup. The first isomorphism theorem embeds the resulting quotient in the cyclic ambient quotient, and further quotienting preserves cyclicity.

External assumptions and dependencies

A normal subgroup P with cyclic ambient quotient and the subgroup K; the p-group assertion assumes P is a p-group.
