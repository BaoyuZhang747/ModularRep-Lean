Purpose

Equivariant correspondences for the four faithful block labels, using the canonical routing and the same literal Brauer and weight output maps.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

The complete Brauer and radical-output maps have the canonical block action, the specified sector binding and the two small faithful-block weight counts.

Argument or construction

Canonical routing yields six weights in each large faithful fibre and two in each small one. Cardinality gives their bijections, and the inner-stabilizer deduction gives equivariance for every faithful block.
