Purpose

Restricts a commuting automorphism to a Frobenius fixed-point group.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

An automorphism commuting with the defining endomorphism induces a group automorphism of its fixed points.

Argument or construction

Commutation preserves membership. The inverse also commutes, giving inverse restricted maps and the literal coercion formula.

External assumptions and dependencies
