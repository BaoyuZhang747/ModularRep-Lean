Purpose

The natural action preserves the actual normal subgroup. Every induced automorphism of its order-two quotient is the identity.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

Consequently that left factor normalizes every subgroup containing the embedded base. Actual Brauer inertias therefore agree at all left-factor conjugates, with the existing inverse right-twist convention.

Argument or construction

Every commutator with the actual left factor belongs to the embedded base. The natural action restricts to literal conjugation on the whole left factor.
