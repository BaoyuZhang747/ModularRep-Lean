Purpose

A rank-41 row space with duplicate columns and a descended involutive permutation on 41 quotient columns having 31 fixed points has symmetrized rank 36.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

A rank-41 row space with duplicate columns and a descended involutive permutation on 41 quotient columns having 31 fixed points has symmetrized rank 36.

Argument or construction

Use the quotient-column section to prove injectivity of precomposition and identify the row span with the full quotient coordinate space. The rank of one plus the permutation is (41+31)/2, hence 36.

External assumptions and dependencies

The explicit row space of rank 41, quotient-column map and section, duplicate-column equalities, a descended involution on Fin 41 and its fixed count 31. A specified character interpretation is a separate binding.
