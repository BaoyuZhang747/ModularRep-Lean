Purpose

Transfers one faithful weight total to every nontrivial sector.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

For a centre of order three inverted by the specified automorphism, the corrected Table 8 realization yields 25 weights in either nontrivial sector.

Argument or construction

The first total comes from the exhaustive local ordinary catalogue. The actual automorphism identifies a sector with its inverse, transporting that count to the other sector.

External assumptions and dependencies

The same exhaustive local ordinary catalogue and canonical compatibility as the one-sector Table 8 theorem, its actual count permutation, centre order three and the specified centre-inverting automorphism. The other sector's count is derived.
