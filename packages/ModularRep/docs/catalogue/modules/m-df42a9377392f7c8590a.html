Purpose

This module evaluates the actual ordinary local character on the image of a central element in its normaliser quotient.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

The central restriction and degree of a character weight are independent of its raw representative. A lies-over formula on one representative therefore holds for every representative of the class.

Argument or construction

Evaluate the local character on the canonical image of the ambient centre in the normalizer quotient. Isomorphism and conjugation preserve those values and their degree, so central character formulas survive every raw representative.

External assumptions and dependencies

The set of weights defined by ordinary characters and canonical image of the ambient centre in its local quotient. The specified central character formula for one raw representative is the transported hypothesis.
