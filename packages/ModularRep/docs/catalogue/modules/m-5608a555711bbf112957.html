Purpose

Computes the full automorphism group of a routed principal family.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

A constant-one character in the selected family block proves that block invariant under every automorphism, so its Gamma adapter becomes a full automorphism equivalence.

Argument or construction

Character invariance gives block fixation. Composition with conjugation by the routed Sp equivalence yields actual symplectic coordinates.

External assumptions and dependencies

The actual principal carrier and the existing block-stabilizer adapter, without additional surjectivity or action laws.
