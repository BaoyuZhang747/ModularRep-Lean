Purpose

Specifies a single complete central-kernel witness for each actual matched ordinary row.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

CentralKernelJointPair requires every representative of the matched weight class to carry either a full-center quotient row or a faithful row with trivial central kernel.

Argument or construction

The faithful predicate retains the scalar sector, ordinary and local reduction data, action, and original product-model block comparison. The trivial predicate retains the same downstairs ordinary row, pullback of its canonical Brauer reduction, matching class and reduced pair block witnesses. The disjunction is a precise contract rather than an assertion that either branch has been inhabited.

External assumptions and dependencies

Chosen reductions upstairs and downstairs, central prime-to-p block-image sources, the downstairs equivalence, universal central quotient and equivariance laws.
