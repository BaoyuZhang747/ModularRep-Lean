Purpose

Identifies the actual central kernel from the trivial-sector character equation.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

If the center is p-prime and the Brauer character has its degree value on every central element, its central kernel equals the center.

Argument or construction

The chosen irreducible representation acts centrally by a scalar. Its Brauer value identifies the lift of that scalar with one. The scalar is a characteristic-polynomial root, so injectivity on the relevant root group forces it to be one, giving trivial central action.

External assumptions and dependencies

The actual trivial-sector value equation and the p-prime center; no injectivity of the total field lift is assumed.
