Purpose

These are the group and raw-representative joins needed when a published pair theorem selects a conjugate character.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

Simultaneous conjugation uses one ambient element for the global Brauer character and the raw ordinary weight.

Argument or construction

Conjugation transports both literal inertia subgroups along the corresponding group equivalences. The same pointwise action formulas identify the transported global and local stabilizers.

External assumptions and dependencies

The weight is its actual right twist, with the inverse required by the existing opposite-automorphism action.
