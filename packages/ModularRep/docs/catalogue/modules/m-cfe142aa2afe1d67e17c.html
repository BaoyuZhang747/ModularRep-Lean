Purpose

Defines four typed local rows on an arbitrary elementary-abelian order-nine radical.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

Fixed-Q local operations, radicality and an equivalence Q ≃ C3², four injective defect-zero ordinary characters, their quotient blocks, reductions and common inflated block form a nonexhaustive row adapter.

Argument or construction

The rowwise normalizer reduction is a supplied law. Combining the quotient-block selection and common inflation equalities proves that each reduced row's normalizer block is the inflation of its ordinary block. No ambient block is needed.

External assumptions and dependencies

Actual subgroup and local character/table/coefficient identifications and reduction/block laws. The printed six-number row labels alone provide none of those identifications and no coverage.
