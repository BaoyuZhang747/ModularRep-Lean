Purpose

Extends the faithful representative matrix to all columns.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

A primitive root of order 770385 and full-column checks identify every raw row with its descriptor expansion from the 25-coordinate matrix.

Argument or construction

The residual modulus evaluates to zero. Divisor identities identify the local roots and central cube-root scalars, and the pivot descriptors recover the selected coordinates.

External assumptions and dependencies

A primitive 770385th root in the characteristic-zero field and the complete ColumnChecks for the literal raw/expanded rows. No specified ordinary-character value binding is required for this algebraic expansion theorem.
