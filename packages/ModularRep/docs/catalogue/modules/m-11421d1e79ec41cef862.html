ModularRep.PaperProofs.TypeBPrincipalOrbitOrientation

ModularRep/PaperProofs/TypeBPrincipalOrbitOrientation.lean

theorem-developmentauxiliary

Purpose

Orients an involution-equivariant seed equivalence to satisfy a pointwise relation.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

For an involution-equivariant seed equivalence and a simultaneously covariant relation admitting one of two orientations at every match, constructs a related equivariant equivalence with the same orbit map.

Argument or construction

Switch the source orbit precisely when its original orientation fails. The switch is involutive and equivariant, so composition with the seed preserves its orbit map and achieves the relation.

External assumptions and dependencies

An actual seed equivalence, covariance of the relation and the two-orientation existence statement, to be justified by each application.
