Purpose

This module provides the mechanical axiom check used by the two policy files.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

The command checks permitted axiom dependencies or rejects a theorem with a parameter definitionally equal to its conclusion. These are syntactic necessary checks, not mathematical independence certificates.

Argument or construction

Inspect constant dependencies and compare each parameter type with the conclusion by definitional equality. These commands detect forbidden axioms and direct conclusion assumptions, while logically disguised circularity remains outside their test.
