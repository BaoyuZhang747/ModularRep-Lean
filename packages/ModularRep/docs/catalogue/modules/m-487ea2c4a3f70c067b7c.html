Purpose

Derives total and fixed singleton signatures for a selected actual defect-zero block.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

A nominated trivial-defect block containing a supplied defect-zero reduction has one Brauer character and one weight, both fixed whenever the block is stable.

Argument or construction

Local defect principles force every raw weight in the block to have trivial radical. Defect-zero reduction and block uniqueness identify its sole weight and Brauer character. Stability makes each singleton fibre invariant, hence pointwise fixed.

External assumptions and dependencies

Actual maximal trivial defect, original defect-zero block source, canonical reductions and the three local defect principles.
