Purpose

Proves prime regular lifting through every surjective map from a finite group.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

The map on p-regular elements induced by a surjective group homomorphism with finite domain is surjective, with no kernel restriction.

Argument or construction

Lift the target element arbitrarily and raise the lift to the p-part of its order. This removes the p-part and leaves a p-regular element. Since this exponent is coprime to the target order, a further power recovers the target exactly.

External assumptions and dependencies

Finiteness of the domain, primality of p and surjectivity of the group homomorphism.
