Purpose

Constructs the Clifford, paired-Levi and field actions on the same Spin Brauer characters.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

On the same Spin Brauer characters, perfectness and the literal inclusion/field equations construct Clifford and paired-Levi actions and their compatible semidirect actions by inverse pullback.

Argument or construction

Perfectness identifies the Spin subgroup as characteristic, permitting the field restriction. The inclusion and field equations prove both semidirect compatibility laws.

External assumptions and dependencies

Original Clifford/Levi maps, finite point field action and perfectness of the same Spin group.
