Purpose

Propagates reference-sector block preservation to every raw match of the retained faithful family.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

A block-preserving reference-fibre equivalence and the transported family yield raw block equality and local block induction for every canonical reduction of every matched representative.

Argument or construction

Transport the reference block equation using equivariance of the Brauer and weight block maps. The raw representative's class law gives its block equality. The induced-block definition and canonical local block compatibility give both ordinary and reduced-Brauer induction statements.

External assumptions and dependencies

Reference-fibre block preservation, the faithful sector orbit/family laws and canonical local block compatibility.
