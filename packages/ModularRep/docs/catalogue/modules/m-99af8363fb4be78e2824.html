Purpose

This file turns coherent bijections on block or sector fibres into one global candidate or fully certified datum.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

Coherent equivalences on indexed block or sector fibres combine into a global candidate, and supplied pointwise certification laws on those same maps combine into global certified data.

Argument or construction

Combine coherent fibre equivalences into the global candidate. The supplied pointwise extension, intermediate-block and triple predicates are carried along the same maps to certify the blockwise or sectorwise combined data.

External assumptions and dependencies

Coherent indexed fibre equivalences. Full data additionally require the stated intermediate-block, extension, triple and normalization clauses on those same fibre maps.
