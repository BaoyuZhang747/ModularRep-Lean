Purpose

Constructs intrinsic inverse coordinates for a raw weight on a finite product.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

For a raw weight of a finite product, its radical coordinate projections and the unique tensor factors of its own quotient character give an inverse to raw-weight product.

Argument or construction

Radicality identifies the subgroup product, then quotient transport and ordinary factorization give defect-zero coordinate characters. Their value equations prove both inverse laws and the raw-weight equivalence.

External assumptions and dependencies

The uniform ordinary product family and previously proved radical/normalizer quotient identities.
