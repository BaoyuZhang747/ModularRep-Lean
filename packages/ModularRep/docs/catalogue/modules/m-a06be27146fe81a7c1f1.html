Purpose

Combines local ordinary rows satisfying one retained-root central formula into its actual weight sector.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

A complete radical catalogue and bijective local rows with central restriction degree times the chosen lift of nu yield a sector equivalence and its total-cardinality formula.

Argument or construction

Canonical reduction and local block compatibility identify the ordinary central formula with the actual raw-weight sector. Restrict each local-character/weight-fibre equivalence accordingly and sum over radical classes. Availability on all local defect-zero characters supplies the reverse direction for every original weight.

External assumptions and dependencies

Exhaustive ordinary central-sector rows, canonical reductions on all local characters and literal block compatibility.
