Purpose

Isolates the formula-level Clifford and Gallagher endgame.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

An invariant extension, a trivial quotient action and natural multiplication and induction fix the correspondent and induced weight representative.

Argument or construction

Gallagher factorisation makes the transformed correspondent equal to the original one. Induction naturality and uniqueness then give fixation of the representative.

External assumptions and dependencies

Exact factorisation, induction and uniqueness equations, not a weight-fixation assumption.
