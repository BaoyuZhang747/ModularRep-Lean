Purpose

Constructs the sign twist of an index-two quotient and its supported central trace.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

The sign is one on the subgroup and minus one outside; z plus its sign twist is supported on the subgroup and has nonzero evaluation at a selected idempotent when two is nonzero.

Argument or construction

Index-two multiplication rules prove the sign is a homomorphism. The two coefficients cancel outside the subgroup. The image of an idempotent under an algebra character is zero or one, so adding its selected value one gives either one or two.

External assumptions and dependencies

Index two, a central algebra character selecting the idempotent, and characteristic different from two for nonvanishing.
