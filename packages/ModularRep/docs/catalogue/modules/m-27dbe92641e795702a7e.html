ModularRep.IntegralBasicSetBridge

ModularRep/IntegralBasicSetBridge.lean

theorem-developmentcurrent

Purpose

Evaluate the linear map on permutation basis vectors to extract coefficient equivariance.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

An integral linear map of free modules on A-sets intertwines the permutation actions exactly when its coefficients satisfy the displayed action covariance.

Argument or construction

Evaluate the linear map on permutation basis vectors to extract coefficient equivariance. Conversely those coefficient identities extend by linearity to the intertwining equation and an equivariant permutation-lattice map.

External assumptions and dependencies

Two finite permutation label sets with A-actions and the given integral linear map. This file proves the coefficient/intertwiner equivalence and does not itself assume Conlon's theorem.
