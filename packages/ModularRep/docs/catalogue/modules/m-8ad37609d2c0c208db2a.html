Purpose

Prove that allowing all modules makes the exact Grothendieck group trivial by the countable direct-sum swindle.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

The exact Grothendieck group of the category of all modules is trivial, because countable direct sums give the Eilenberg swindle.

Argument or construction

For any module V, its countable direct sum T satisfies T isomorphic to V plus T. The split exact relation cancels the class of T and gives [V]=0, hence the exact Grothendieck group of all modules is trivial.

External assumptions and dependencies

A ring and the category of all modules, including countable direct sums, together with the exact Grothendieck presentation.
