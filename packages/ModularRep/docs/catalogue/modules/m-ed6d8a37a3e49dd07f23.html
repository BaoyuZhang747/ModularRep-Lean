Purpose

Combine trivial and faithful sector correspondences using fixed and exchanged block orbits.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

For a centre of order three inverted by tau, the five trivial-sector blocks and the two faithful-sector roles combine into an automorphism-equivariant block correspondence from the sector count data.

Argument or construction

Use C2 counts on the fixed trivial-sector blocks and pair the exchanged faithful blocks. The two-sector construction then extends the local equivalences over the full set of blocks.

External assumptions and dependencies

The finite actual group, characteristic prime, compatible coefficient/root data, specified sets of primitive blocks and characters, and the imported transport results are fixed as in the declaration context. The stated finite row/count certificates, regular-class coverage, ordinary decomposition/support and automorphism fusion bindings are required to connect finite calculations with actual sets of Brauer characters or weights.
