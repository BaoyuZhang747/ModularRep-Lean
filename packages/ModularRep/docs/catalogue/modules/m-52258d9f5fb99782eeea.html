Purpose

Identifies the image of one plus an action with the span of symmetrized evaluation rows.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

Spanning generators, injective evaluation and exact evaluation/action equations turn a symmetrized-row rank certificate into the rank of id plus T.

Argument or construction

A linear map sends the span of generators onto the span of their images. Under evaluation these images are precisely each row plus its permuted row. Injectivity preserves dimension, so the finite row certificate gives the desired rank.

External assumptions and dependencies

The spanning and evaluation/action bindings and the symmetrized rank certificate.
