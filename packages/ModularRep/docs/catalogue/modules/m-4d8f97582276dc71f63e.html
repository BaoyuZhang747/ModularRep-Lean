Purpose

Global ordinary tables, two local ordinary catalogues and the guarded small-defect numerical theorem supply all weight-side counts internally.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

Local ordinary tables and the guarded small-defect theorem supply the finite weight counts required for the two-sector equivalence, while the Brauer count bindings remain explicit.

Argument or construction

Establish the small-defect contributions from the theorem at their actual defects. Combine them with the ordinary tables to produce the weight totals and then apply the correspondence theorem.

External assumptions and dependencies

The finite actual group, characteristic prime, compatible coefficient/root data, specified sets of primitive blocks and characters, and the imported transport results are fixed as in the declaration context. The stated finite row/count certificates, regular-class coverage, ordinary decomposition/support and automorphism fusion bindings are required to connect finite calculations with actual sets of Brauer characters or weights.
