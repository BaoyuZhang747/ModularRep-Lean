Purpose

Identifies the block of a defect of order nine.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

In the actual three-role catalogue, a block with maximal defect of order nine must be role one.

Argument or construction

The unique degree-one row identifies role zero as principal, whose Sylow defect has a larger order. Role two has trivial defect, so exhaustion of the three specified roles leaves role one.

External assumptions and dependencies

The actual complete ordinary degree/block allocation, three specified block roles, an order-nine maximal defect and the selected role-two trivial support. Principal/Sylow support and the degree-one identification are supplied by the proved preceding development.
