Purpose

Derives projectivity and nonnegative scalar products for actual principal GGGR projections.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

The actual GGGR induction formula through the correct intermediate odd-order subgroup, projective induction and PIM expansion laws imply that its specified principal projection belongs to the principal projective span.

Argument or construction

Odd-order induction expands as a natural combination of the specified PIMs. Principal projection keeps the supported summands, proving projective membership and nonnegativity.

External assumptions and dependencies

Taylor's actual GGGR induction formula and a uniform odd-subgroup induction/PIM expansion theorem under finite splitting.
