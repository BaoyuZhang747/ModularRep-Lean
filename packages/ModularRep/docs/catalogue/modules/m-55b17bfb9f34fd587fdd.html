Purpose

Constructs the representation on both cosets of an index-two subgroup.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

A conjugation-invariant irreducible representation extends whenever the actual quotient has order at most two.

Argument or construction

The top-subgroup case is immediate. Otherwise the normalized operator defines the representation on the outside coset, and four membership cases verify multiplication using U squared equal to rho of t squared.

External assumptions and dependencies

A finite group, normal subgroup with quotient cardinality at most two, and a finite-dimensional conjugation-invariant irreducible representation over an algebraically closed field. No extension principle is an input.
