Purpose

This module proves the final cyclic-extension deduction in Lemma 3.12 on function-valued irreducible Brauer characters.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

The canonical actual cyclic-field stabilizer of a selected irreducible Brauer character admits an extension of that character under Navarro's explicit cyclic extension principle.

Argument or construction

Instantiate the general cyclic-extension construction with the canonical actual Brauer carrier and inner/field semidirect action. The conclusion extends the selected character over its actual stabilizer, retaining the explicit cyclic extension principle.

External assumptions and dependencies

The actual Brauer root embedding and canonical cyclic-field semidirect action, and Navarro's BrauerCyclicExtensionPrinciple. Inner fixedness and the stabilizer identification are established internally.
