theorem-developmentauxiliary

Purpose

Constructs the nonabelian lower scalar-product permutation from actual upper characters.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

For one Taylor family over a represented geometric class of rank at least four, natural entries and the selected two-fibre conjugation laws give a permutation scalar-product matrix.

Argument or construction

Upper nonnegative sum-one and same-family coverage select the distinguished parent. Frobenius reciprocity, actual restriction constituents and the swapping conjugation action force the lower two-by-two permutation block and the remaining singleton entries.

External assumptions and dependencies

Geck–Hézard/Taylor weighted sums and coverage, distinguished two-constituent parent, actual induction and conjugation, component geometry and restriction laws.
