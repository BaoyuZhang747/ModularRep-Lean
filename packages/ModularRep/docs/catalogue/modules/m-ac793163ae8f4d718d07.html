Purpose

Builds a field rank certificate from scaled integer identities.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

An integer matrix with a nonzero scaled right inverse and integral row reconstruction gives a row-inverse certificate over any characteristic-zero field.

Argument or construction

The proof casts the checked identities and divides by the nonzero denominator. The reconstruction equations survive coefficientwise casting.

External assumptions and dependencies

An integer matrix, basis and pivot choices, nonzero integer denominator, complete scaled inverse checks and row reconstruction checks, over a characteristic-zero target field.
