ModularRep.ModularProjectiveRepresentation

ModularRep/ModularProjectiveRepresentation.lean

carrier-constructioncurrent

Purpose

The operators below are actual linear automorphisms of a nonzero finite dimensional vector space.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

An actual nonzero projective operator family has a normalized unit-valued multiplier satisfying the cocycle equation; an ordinary linear representation embeds with multiplier one.

Argument or construction

Normalize an actual projective operator family and use associativity plus nonzero scalar faithfulness to deduce the multiplier cocycle. Ordinary representations embed with multiplier one; no character-triple relation is inferred.

External assumptions and dependencies

A finite dimensional nonzero coefficient space and actual invertible operators with the specified projective multiplication law. Identification with associated modular characters or a block-triple relation is additional data.
