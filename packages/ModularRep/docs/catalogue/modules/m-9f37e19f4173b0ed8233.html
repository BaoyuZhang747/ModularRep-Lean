Purpose

This file records the elementary subgroup relations used by the block-defect interface.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

Subconjugacy is transitive. For finite groups, mutual subconjugacy gives conjugacy, and inclusion together with reverse subconjugacy gives literal equality.

Argument or construction

Compose conjugating elements to prove transitivity of subconjugacy. Mutual subconjugacy forces equal finite subgroup orders and hence conjugacy; inclusion together with reverse subconjugacy gives literal equality.

External assumptions and dependencies

The group and given subgroup/conjugacy inclusions; finiteness is needed for cardinality-based conjugacy and equality consequences.
