Purpose

Identify defect-zero reduction and quotient inflation for the actual function-valued ordinary and Brauer characters.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

For an ordinary defect-zero character of a normalizer quotient, compatible quotient and normalizer roots identify its inflated Brauer reduction with the concrete local Brauer character.

Argument or construction

Pull the affording representation back along the quotient map. Surjectivity preserves irreducibility, and the supplied defect-zero reduction and inflation laws identify the character values on prime regular elements.
