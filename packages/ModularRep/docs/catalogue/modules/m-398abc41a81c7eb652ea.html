Purpose

Proves field equivariance of the already chosen principal Omega and SO maps with finite splitting.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

Positive twists and inverse-actor left actions are kept distinct on the same fibres.

Argument or construction

GGGR fixes the Omega Brauer fibre and Green naturality fixes the upper SO fibre. FYZ fixes both weight-class fibres, so the existing maps automatically commute with the full field actions.

External assumptions and dependencies

Actual GGGR package, Green naturality and finite-splitting FYZ source; Spin roots are derived from the actual group diagram.
