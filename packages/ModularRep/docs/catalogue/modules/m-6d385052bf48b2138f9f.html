Purpose

Defines literal classical matrix fixed-point models for individual Levi components.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

The unitary form is the fixed subgroup of transpose-inverse composed with entrywise field power on algebraic SL3.

Argument or construction

A specified matrix equation determines each standard Frobenius uniquely. The rational model is its actual fixed subgroup, without installing a field involution on the whole algebraic closure.

External assumptions and dependencies

Standard algebraic-group Frobenius automorphism and the same field cardinality, with Malle–Testerman and Digne–Michel conventions.
