Purpose

Identifies a fixed original-sector radical fibre with the catalogue's corrected fixed rows.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

For an original radical whose quotient image equals a catalogue representative, the fixed sector fibre is equivalent to that representative's FixedLocalRow carrier.

Argument or construction

Change the inner correction without changing weight classes. Descend the fixed local characters, cast along equality of radical subgroups, compare the two corrections, and apply the local-row equivalence.

External assumptions and dependencies

Canonical reductions, quotient compatibility, primitive-image data and the local action square; no fixed cardinality is assumed.
