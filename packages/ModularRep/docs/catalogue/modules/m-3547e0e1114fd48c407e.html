Purpose

The scalar embedding is the actual algebra-map unit embedding in the even vector normalizer.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

An actual nonzero field scalar is embedded as an even Clifford unit.

Argument or construction

The scalar commutes with all Clifford elements, hence normalizes the vector image. Reversal fixes the scalar, so its Clifford norm is its square.

External assumptions and dependencies

The source norm is forced to be the square on the literal scalar map.
