Purpose

Transports automorphisms and Brauer stabilizers to the character's own quotient.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

A universal central cover and full central kernel give the compatible full automorphism equivalence and the selected Brauer-stabilizer equivalence.

Argument or construction

Compose the full-cover automorphism equivalence with the own-quotient/base group equivalence. Pullback commutes with twisting along the resulting square. Canonical inflation and surjectivity on prime regular elements identify fixedness, permitting restriction to stabilizers.

External assumptions and dependencies

Universal cover and actual central-sector equation, p-prime center and canonical Brauer inflation.
