transport-adaptercurrent

Purpose

This source-neutral module isolates the formal diagram used when a correspondence on a central quotient is transported to the trivial central sector of a cover.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

A downstairs matching transported by the supplied Brauer/weight/block equivalences is equivariant and block preserving, and satisfies the upstairs pair predicate when the explicit pair-lifting law holds.

Argument or construction

Compose the supplied cover-to-quotient carrier equivalences with the downstairs matching. Equivariance, block transport and the explicit pair-predicate lifting law prove all declared lifted clauses for that same composite.

External assumptions and dependencies

Explicit equivalences on Brauer characters, weights and blocks with equivariance/block laws, the downstairs matching, and the pair-predicate lifting law. This abstract adapter does not prove those carrier dictionaries.
