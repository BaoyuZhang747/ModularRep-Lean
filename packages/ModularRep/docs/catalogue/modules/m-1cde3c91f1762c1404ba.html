Purpose

Constructs compatible original Levi and Spin semidirect inclusions for the Morita argument.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

Any subgroup of the original field actor may be used on both original groups.

Argument or construction

The finite-point equations prove equivariance of the Levi embedding. Pairing it with the subgroup's field coordinate gives an injective semidirect homomorphism.

External assumptions and dependencies

Existing finite Clifford/Levi realization and the same prescribed field action.
