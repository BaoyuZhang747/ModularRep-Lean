Purpose

Both character roots come from the same modular system and ordinary cyclotomic choice.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

The lower root is definitionally the restriction of the upper root. The existing positive restriction expansion, Navarro (2.2)(d) and (2.3), pp. 18--19, supplies a nonzero coefficient on these functions.

Argument or construction

The lower matching and its effective Brauer stabilizer factorization are derived by the modular Spin theorem. The actual ambient conversion then applies to the chosen constituent.

External assumptions and dependencies

The lower root is definitionally the restriction of the upper root. Every actual upper Brauer character has a literal lower constituent whose ambient inertia is the product of its two actual inertia images. A nonzero coefficient of the same positive expansion selects an actual constituent, with no additional requirement on the ordinary value field.
