Purpose

Constructs the actions and injective character map used to apply the prescribed complete Levi/Jordan packet.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

A LeviSource fixes the complete Jordan bijection, its original packet roots, and the actual paired-Levi embedding into special Clifford.

Argument or construction

Define gammaCharacters by composing the ambient Spin character action with gammaEmbedding, and define fieldCharacters by restricting the original field action to the parameter stabilizer. Define jordanCharacter as the underlying value of source.jordan; injectivity follows from bijectivity of that same packet map and injectivity of subtype values.

External assumptions and dependencies

Consumes the previously specified LeviSource and its actual Jordan bijection, field-action restrictions and paired-Levi embedding. Their geometric, coefficient and published-source interpretation is inherited from that source; the module does not prove representative selection, extension or stabilizer factorization.
