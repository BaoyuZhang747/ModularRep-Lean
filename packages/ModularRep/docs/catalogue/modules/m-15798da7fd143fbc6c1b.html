Purpose

Derives rational-class fibre geometry for the Spin regular embedding.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

Compatible rational-class component parametrizations for the same finite Spin inclusion construct the geometric fibre model, its abelian cases and its unique exceptional double fibre.

Argument or construction

The component quotient yields one exceptional double fibre and singleton fibres elsewhere. The proof constructs relabelling equivalences and the ambient conjugation action, including the swap of the exceptional pair.

External assumptions and dependencies

Taylor component presentation and compatible rational-class parametrisations with their geometric interpretation.
