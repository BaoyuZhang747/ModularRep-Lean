Purpose

Cancels other trivial-sector fibres to obtain the principal numerical equality at two.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

Sector totals 41 on both sides and equal other block fibres imply selected-block equality; fixed totals 31 and equality on the other stable blocks imply the fixed version.

Argument or construction

Positive sector totals imply finiteness. Apply supported fibre cancellation. A moved block contains no fixed character or weight, so only stable other blocks need a supplied fixed-count equality.

External assumptions and dependencies

Actual supported totals, block transport, and equal other fibres or stable fixed fibres.
