Purpose

Compare concrete normalized scalar factor sets for a fixed matched character pair.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

Compatible projective models and trivializations of their normalized scalar two-cocycles yield cohomologous factor sets under the quotient map.

Argument or construction

Divide the two trivializing cochains after pullback to obtain the comparison coboundary. Equivariance identifies the stabilizers; an alternative constructor preserves a source-native factor witness under exact equality of maps.

External assumptions and dependencies

The finite actual group, characteristic prime, compatible coefficient/root data, specified sets of primitive blocks and characters, and the imported transport results are fixed as in the declaration context. The same chosen matching, canonical availability and specified block compatibility are retained. Extension and block selection invoke the cyclic-extension and Navarro principles exactly where they occur in the declaration telescope.
