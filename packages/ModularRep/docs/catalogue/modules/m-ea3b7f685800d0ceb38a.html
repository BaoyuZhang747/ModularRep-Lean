Purpose

This file records, without proving, the exact external block-theoretic input from Navarro (4.11): among p-subgroups, nonzero central Brauer restriction of the supplied literal block idempotent is equivalent to subconjugacy into the nominated subgroup.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

The source states that nonzero central Brauer restriction at any p-subgroup is equivalent to subconjugacy into the fixed nominated defect subgroup, on the literal supplied block.

Argument or construction

Record Navarro Theorem 4.11 as the equivalence of nonzero central Brauer restriction at a p-subgroup with its subconjugacy into the nominated defect subgroup. The record supplies this published characterization; it is not proved here.

External assumptions and dependencies

Navarro Theorem 4.11 in the exact nonzero central Brauer support/subconjugacy form, on the displayed literal blocks and nominated p-subgroup. Defect interpretation comes from this source.
