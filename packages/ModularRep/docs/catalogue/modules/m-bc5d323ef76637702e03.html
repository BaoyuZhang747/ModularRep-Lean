Purpose

Forces the radical to be trivial over a defect-zero block.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

Local defect existence, p-core containment and mapped-defect source laws imply that a normalizer block inducing to an ambient block of trivial maximal support comes from the trivial p-subgroup.

Argument or construction

A local defect contains the normal copy of the radical and maps below an ambient defect. Trivial maximal ambient support forces that mapped defect and hence the original radical to be trivial.

External assumptions and dependencies

Navarro417LocalDefectExistenceSource, Navarro408PCoreDefectSource and Navarro413MappedDefectSource are separate E1 premises on the same actual local/ambient decompositions and catalogues; trivial ambient maximal support is also explicit.
