Purpose

Provides actual PCSp rational-point and Levi geometry for nonprincipal rank-three reduction.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

Coordinate base change and scalar quotient squares identify finite PCSp with Frobenius-fixed algebraic points.

Argument or construction

The supplied connected-centralizer and Levi dimension/intersection laws define quasi-isolation and the strict centralizer product. The least-envelope theorem derives properness and Frobenius stability from noncontainment.

External assumptions and dependencies

Authentic algebraic Levi/identity-component interpretation, point Frobenius and coordinate rational realization.
