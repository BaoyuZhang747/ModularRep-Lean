carrier-constructionauxiliary

Purpose

Realizes the trivial/faithful sector split as literal disjoint unions of characters and weights.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

For a prime-order center, the sums of the trivial and faithful sector carriers are equivalent to the full sets of Brauer characters and weights, preserving underlying elements.

Argument or construction

A nontrivial homomorphism from a prime-order group is injective, so faithfulness is exactly the complement of the trivial sector. Identify central block support with the sector equality using uniqueness of the primitive central-idempotent sector. Compose subtype equivalences with the standard set/complement sum equivalence.

External assumptions and dependencies

Actual central sector definitions and prime cardinality of the center.
