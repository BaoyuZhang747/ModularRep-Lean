theorem-developmentcurrent

Purpose

Choose a square root of the multiplier and divide by the corresponding central scalar.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

If every multiplier value is a square realized by a central scalar, each ambient element is a scalar times a multiplier-kernel element and induces the same conjugation on that kernel.

Argument or construction

Choose a square root of the multiplier and divide by the corresponding central scalar. The residual element lies in the multiplier kernel and induces the same conjugation; odd order provides the required square-root bijection.

External assumptions and dependencies

The multiplier homomorphism and central scalar map with multiplier equal to squaring. The square-root hypothesis, or the finite odd-order hypothesis deriving it, is explicit.
