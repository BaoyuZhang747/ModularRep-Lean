Purpose

Returns the selected Levi character's actions and stabilizer formula to the original group.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

The canonical subgroup copy and original rational Levi use the same root and field data.

Argument or construction

Literal conjugation transports through their equivalence and proves both character-action squares. The copied stabilizer factorization and field-fixer equality then hold for the original character.

External assumptions and dependencies

The previously constructed copy representative and original-extension transport; no new Jordan map or character choice.
