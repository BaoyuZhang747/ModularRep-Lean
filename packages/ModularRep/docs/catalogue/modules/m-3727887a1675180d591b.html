Purpose

Defines the common intrinsic principal carrier and the exact FM source.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

Both FYZ and FM principal weights are the actual local-block-induction fibre of character-weight conjugacy classes in literal Sp.

Argument or construction

The intrinsic bridge is the identity equivalence. The licensed FM certificate supplies the actual fully equivariant principal Brauer-to-weight map, while a separate relation is needed to make a Definition 3.5 seed.

External assumptions and dependencies

Actual coefficients, block operations and FM 6.2; the identity bridge is not an arbitrary-parameter converse.
