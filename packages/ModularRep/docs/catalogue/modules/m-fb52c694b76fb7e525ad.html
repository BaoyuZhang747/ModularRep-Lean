Purpose

Builds the concrete quotient reduction from the family's own chosen reduction.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

The inverse whole-weight map fixes the family class, and a conjugator aligns its selected raw pair with the concrete selected pair.

Argument or construction

The whole-pair equality determines the normalizer quotient equivalence. Transporting the existing reduction through it supplies a compatible concrete table before applying P38.

External assumptions and dependencies

Specified fibre dictionary and the family's stored reduction, not an independently chosen table equality.
