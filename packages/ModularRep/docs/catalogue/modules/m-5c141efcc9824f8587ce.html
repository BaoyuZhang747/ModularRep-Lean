Purpose

The part of the actual CSp order prime to the defining characteristic divides the existing common cyclotomic conductor.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

Choose a primitive root of that order in AlgebraicClosure F and pair it with the corresponding power of the common number-field generator. The same power of Choice.root gives the convention over K, with a pointwise square through Choice.embedding.

Argument or construction

Choose a primitive root of that order in AlgebraicClosure F and pair it with the corresponding power of the common number-field generator. This is the finite root convention used to interpret the semisimple parameter, not the modular ell-residue convention.
