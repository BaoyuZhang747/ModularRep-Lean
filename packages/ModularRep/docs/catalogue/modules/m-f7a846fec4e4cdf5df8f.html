Purpose

Turns fixed central block support into ordinary kernel constancy.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

The local ordinary character is constant at its degree on every element of the kernel of qW.

Argument or construction

Elements of the prime-to-p central subgroup are regular. The canonical reduction identifies their ordinary values with modular Brauer values, which are constant by the local kernel theorem.

External assumptions and dependencies

A canonical local reduction, central prime-to-p subgroup Z, and its local modular kernel inclusion. The block-support specialization supplies that inclusion through FixedCentralBlockKernel and canonical block compatibility; no quotient block-image source occurs.
