Purpose

Identifies catalogue scalars using actual simple modules.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

Every primitive central idempotent supports an irreducible representation, and its catalogue character vanishes on elements with zero Brauer restriction at a normal p-subgroup.

Argument or construction

A maximal submodule of the supported group algebra module gives the simple representation. Its central scalar equals the catalogue character, and the proved normal-p action theorem annihilates the element.

External assumptions and dependencies

Actual primitive decompositions and their central character catalogue over an algebraically closed modular field. The vanishing theorem also uses a normal p-subgroup and zero central Brauer restriction; its supported simple module is constructed.
