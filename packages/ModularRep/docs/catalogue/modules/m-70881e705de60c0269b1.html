Purpose

All stabilizers are comaps of the canonical opposite-automorphism action. The original action is literal conjugation on G normal in A, and the quotient action is the already constructed conjugation action of A/P on G/P.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

It is not required to be a bijection on all Brauer characters or on all weights. The representative adjustment, normalizer intersection, and quotient stabilizer comparisons are deductions, not source fields.

Argument or construction

An injective equivariant map from one supported Brauer fibre to weight classes supplies the elementary joins U <= T and T = G U for a chosen raw representative. The representative adjustment, normalizer intersection, and quotient stabilizer comparisons are deductions, not source fields. The same equality holds for the raw ordinary-character weight inertia; this uses the proved raw transport, not a source-supplied class matching.
