Purpose

Computes quotient actions and graph equivariance for the same strong map.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

Centrelessness and specified block catalogues give the quotient action; equality of descended Brauer characters forces the corresponding weight graph equality.

Argument or construction

A quotient automorphism is pulled back to the original H. Block fixation is proved before invoking the actual block-stabilizer adapter and the witness's equivariance.

External assumptions and dependencies

The original strong witness, centreless equivalence and specified/root dictionaries, with no full-Aut replacement of Gamma.
