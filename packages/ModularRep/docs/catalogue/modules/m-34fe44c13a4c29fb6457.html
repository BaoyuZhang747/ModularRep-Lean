Purpose

Extends one base-sector bijection over the faithful orbit.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

A stabilizer-equivariant equivalence in one sector and orbit coverage give a global equivariant, sector-preserving equivalence with matching stabilizers and radical fibres.

Argument or construction

Choose a transporter from the base sector to each sector and conjugate the reference equivalence by it. Stabilizer equivariance proves independence of transporters and yields equivariance and the fixed-iff laws.

External assumptions and dependencies

Actual faithful carriers and their proved equivariant sector projections, a reference-sector equivalence and stabilizer equivariance, and orbit coverage. The specified specialization derives coverage from the small-centre cases and does not require canonical local reductions.
