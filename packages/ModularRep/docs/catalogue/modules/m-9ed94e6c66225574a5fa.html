Purpose

Retain the initial global and fixed local model characters, then select the global extension with every intermediate block law and its explicit twist.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

Every retained factor-one model pair affording the specified characters admits a compatible selected global twist and intermediate block family under the block-choice inputs.

Argument or construction

Turn the models into genuine extensions, keep the local extension fixed and apply cyclic block selection. Universal quantification over model pairs allows application to previously constructed comparison models.

The same chosen matching, canonical availability and specified block compatibility are retained. Extension and block selection invoke the cyclic-extension and Navarro principles exactly where they occur in the declaration telescope.
