ModularRep.OrdinaryBlockFibre

ModularRep/OrdinaryBlockFibre.lean

theorem-developmentcurrent

Purpose

This neutral module constructs character inflation along a surjective group homomorphism and the literal fibres of any supplied ordinary-block selector.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

A surjective ordinary-block selector has distinct nonempty character fibres, and taking their images under an injective character map preserves injectivity of the block-to-fibre assignment.

Argument or construction

Surjectivity of an ordinary-character block selector makes every fibre nonempty and distinguishes different fibres. An injective map on characters preserves this distinction after taking images.

External assumptions and dependencies

A surjective block selector on the actual set of ordinary irreducible characters; its image-fibre result additionally requires an injective character map.
