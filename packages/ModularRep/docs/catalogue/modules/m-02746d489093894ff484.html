theorem-developmentcurrent

Purpose

This file records the reusable group-theoretic fact that squaring is bijective in a finite group of odd order.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

Squaring is bijective on every finite odd-order group, so every element has a unique square root.

Argument or construction

Use the finite group power bijection for an exponent coprime to the group order. Odd order makes two coprime, giving bijectivity of squaring and a unique square root for every element.

External assumptions and dependencies

A finite group of odd order and the Mathlib coprime power-map bijection.
