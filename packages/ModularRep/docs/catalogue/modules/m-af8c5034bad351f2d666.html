Purpose

Checks reconstruction and duplicate-column identities for original ordinary rows 40–44.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

For original row indices 40–44, every representative-column reconstruction residual is the stated modulus multiple, and each of the 91 full columns equals its routed representative-column polynomial.

Argument or construction

The reconstruction and routing statements are closed finite equalities proved by decide. After the local modulus evaluates to zero, the first identity places the row in the selected span; the second preserves that row on the full class list.

External assumptions and dependencies

The literal row, inverse, reconstruction and conductor arrays; no actual character-table or class identification.
