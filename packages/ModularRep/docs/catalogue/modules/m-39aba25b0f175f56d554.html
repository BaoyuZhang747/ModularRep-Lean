Purpose

This file defines hypotheses only, on arbitrary finite groups G <= M and the actual semidirect product M semidirect E.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

The all-blocks criterion domain consists of actual G inside M, its semidirect product with an abelian E, a specified prime-to-ell cover, all specified blocks and local reductions, structural facts, four extension clauses and the two stabilizer clauses.

Argument or construction

The correspondence is an upstairs equivariant block-preserving map. The final inertia-Hall condition is stated for every possible actual constituent and covered weight, so no downstairs matching or final block witness is an input.

External assumptions and dependencies

Thus all blocks of M occur upstairs and the automorphism group is the full actual group. We specialize the permitted acting group to the abelian alternative and require the whole outer automorphism group to be abelian. Both are stronger than the relevant alternatives of the published theorem.
