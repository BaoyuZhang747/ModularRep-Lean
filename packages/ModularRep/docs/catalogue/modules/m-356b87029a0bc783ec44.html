ModularRep/OrdinaryCharacterKZero.lean

theorem-developmentcurrent

Purpose

Ordinary trace characters are additive on short exact sequences of finite dimensional representations.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

Ordinary character and prime regular restriction define additive homomorphisms on exact K-zero, with the expected formula on each representation class.

Argument or construction

Use trace additivity on exact sequences to define the ordinary-character homomorphism on exact K-zero. Restriction of those functions to prime regular elements gives the ordinary side of the decomposition square.

External assumptions and dependencies

Finite dimensional characteristic-zero representations and short-exact trace additivity. Restriction uses the prime regular element carrier without a modular decomposition source.
