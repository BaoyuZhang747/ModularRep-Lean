Purpose

Proves most of the prime-outside-order branch from semisimplicity.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

When ell does not divide the actual group order, radical subgroups are trivial and each specified block has one simple-module class.

Argument or construction

Maschke and primitive-block arguments give singleton Brauer fibres. A supplied defect-zero ordinary character and uniqueness give the singleton weight fibre, whose unique bijection is equivariant.

External assumptions and dependencies

Coprime ordinary/Brauer block interpretation and the fixed triple relation for the selected pair remain source inputs.
