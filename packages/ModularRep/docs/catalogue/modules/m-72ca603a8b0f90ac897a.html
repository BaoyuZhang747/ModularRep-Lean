Purpose

The four branches are computed from the actual defining characteristic, field order, rank and prime.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

For rank at least three in odd defining characteristic, ell is a prime dividing the order of the same matrix Omega group, and raw source inputs are requested only under their actual numerical guards.

Argument or construction

Classify ell as defining, odd nondefining, generic two or exceptional two. Each branch constructs a complete certificate, with the exceptional natural nine-block presentation kept in its own constructor; quantification over actual prime divisors gives AllPrimeIBAW.

External assumptions and dependencies

Defining characteristic and odd nondefining primes use the complete family proofs on their actual chosen covers. The source package contains each branch's subordinate inputs only under its numerical guard. The simple-group coordinate is the actual matrix group used by the caller.
