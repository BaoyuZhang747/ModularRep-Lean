Purpose

Identifies the retained Q=1 local ambient with the whole ambient group.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

A subgroup equal to top is canonically equivalent to the ambient with inclusion as its homomorphism; the embedded normalizer of a trivial radical is top.

Argument or construction

Transport through subgroup equality and the top equivalence. The trivial subgroup maps to the trivial subgroup, whose normalizer is the whole group.

External assumptions and dependencies

The equality of the radical with bottom or of the local ambient with top.
