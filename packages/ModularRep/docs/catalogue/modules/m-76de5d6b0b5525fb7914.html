Purpose

The finite exceptional-type-B arithmetic and transcript comparisons were originally exposed as independent theorems.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

The listed subset of the exceptional finite certificate is bound to the corresponding shared transcript projections; this bound certificate has exactly the displayed component scope.

Argument or construction

Package only the listed transcript equalities for the exceptional finite certificate. Each component applies a previously checked binding, so the bound certificate authenticates that subset of the numeric model against the shared strings.

External assumptions and dependencies

The stated finite subset of the exceptional certificate and the previously proved transcript bindings of those components. It does not claim to bind every field of the larger certificate.
