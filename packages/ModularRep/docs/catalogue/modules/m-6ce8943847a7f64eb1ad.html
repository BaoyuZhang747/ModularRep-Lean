Purpose

Reassembles conductor-tagged dot products.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

A finite sum whose tagged residuals equal integer constants equals the sum of those constants; a delta budget gives a scaled matrix identity.

Argument or construction

The proof partitions the index set by its tag and replaces each group by its checked constant. It then casts the constant budget and separates diagonal from off-diagonal cases.

External assumptions and dependencies

Finite index/tag sets, their tag function, grouped residual identities and an integer constant budget. The scaled-delta result uses that budget at the specified matrix entry.
