Purpose

Proves the exact stabilizer structure needed by Conlon.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

For J in N semidirect E, the kernel D of its field projection equals J intersect N, and an order bound at most two makes J and all subgroups two-hypoelementary.

Argument or construction

The quotient J/D embeds in the cyclic field group. Its cyclicity and the two-group kernel supply the mark-theorem hypothesis.

External assumptions and dependencies

Li's exact tensor-kernel cardinality bound; normality and the quotient embedding are derived.
