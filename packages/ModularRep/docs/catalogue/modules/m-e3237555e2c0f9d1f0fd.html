Purpose

Derives the matrix-side structural criterion data from the fixed Spin realization.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

The natural matrix action, centre, full cover and root system are the ones used by the principal application.

Argument or construction

Kernel and surjectivity transport along the literal automorphism square. Index two and the cyclic field actor make the outer automorphism group abelian and determine the Hall subgroup.

External assumptions and dependencies

Clifford centre/projection facts, full Spin cover, natural-action kernel and surjectivity, and ambient roots.
