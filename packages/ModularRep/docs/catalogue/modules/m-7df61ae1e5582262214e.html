Purpose

Applies upward MRR inflation to the literal Sp/PSp tuples.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

A downstairs standard block-triple relation implies the upstairs relation for the original weight and its own reduction.

Argument or construction

Computed whole and subgroup equivalences identify the quotient tuple with PSp. The universal interpretation equivalence and MRR 3.14 then supply the upward implication.

External assumptions and dependencies

MRR Lemma 3.14, authenticated standard tuple interpretation and compatible roots.
