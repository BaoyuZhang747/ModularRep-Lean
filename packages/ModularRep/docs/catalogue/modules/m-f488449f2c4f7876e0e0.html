ModularRep.FDRepFiniteLength

ModularRep/FDRepFiniteLength.lean

theorem-developmentcurrent

Purpose

The underlying k[G]-module of a finite-dimensional representation has finite length.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

Finite dimensional representations of a finite group have finite-length group algebra modules and therefore composition series.

Argument or construction

The group algebra of a finite group is finite dimensional. Restriction of scalars makes every finite representation module Noetherian and Artinian, hence of finite length with a composition series.

External assumptions and dependencies

Finite G and finite dimensional coefficient representation. Finite group algebra dimension and module length facts are provided by Mathlib.
