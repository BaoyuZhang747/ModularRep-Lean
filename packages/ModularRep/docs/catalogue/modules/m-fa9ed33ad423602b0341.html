Purpose

Converts class-level field fixation into actual semidirect inertia products.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

The character or weight class has full field stabilizer, but a raw representative need not be fixed by the uncorrected field actor.

Argument or construction

Each field actor is corrected by an inner element fixing the chosen raw representative. This proves the raw normalizer product as well as the simpler class-inertia factorization.

External assumptions and dependencies

Actual semidirect action and field containment proved elsewhere from the principal character and weight sources.
