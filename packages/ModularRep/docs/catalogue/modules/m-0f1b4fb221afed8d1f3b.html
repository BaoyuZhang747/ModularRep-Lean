Purpose

Transports specified block induction through a surjective quotient square.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

Surjective global/local maps, full-preimage subgroup compatibility, specified idempotent images and defined downstairs induction carry an upstairs BlockInducesTo statement downstairs.

Argument or construction

Check the coefficient-restriction square on single group algebra terms and extend linearly. Catalogue exhaustivity and delta values identify the pulled-back central character of a physically mapped block. Evaluate the induced downstairs central character at the mapped global idempotent; its value one forces the desired block index.

External assumptions and dependencies

Actual block catalogues, local and global idempotent-image equalities, surjectivity, saturation and downstairs definedness.
