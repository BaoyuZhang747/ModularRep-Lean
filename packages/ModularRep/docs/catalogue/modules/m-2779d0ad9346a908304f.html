Purpose

This file defines primitive central idempotents by the absence of a nontrivial central idempotent below them.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

A primitive central idempotent has a unique support among a complete orthogonal central family; when it acts as identity on a module, that same index gives the module's support.

Argument or construction

Products of a primitive central idempotent with members of a complete central family are central sub-idempotents. Each is zero or the original primitive element, and orthogonality plus sum one give unique support.

External assumptions and dependencies

A ring, a primitive central idempotent and a finite complete orthogonal central family. The module-support specialization additionally assumes the primitive idempotent acts as identity.
