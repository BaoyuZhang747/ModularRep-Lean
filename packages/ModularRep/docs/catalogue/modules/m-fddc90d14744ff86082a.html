Constructs the PSp triple in the actual PCSp/field ambient group.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

The normal base is the actual PSp image and the local subgroup is the raw-pair stabilizer intersected with the global Brauer stabilizer.

Argument or construction

The base and intersection equivalences are computed from the fixed embeddings. They transport the global character and the own normalizer reduction to the tuple.
