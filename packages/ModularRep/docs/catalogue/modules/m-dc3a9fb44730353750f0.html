Purpose

Turns the Weyl difference calculation into a finite quotient identity.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

For the stable finite normaliser and fixed Levi intersection, a difference lying in the Levi makes the induced quotient endomorphism the identity.

Argument or construction

Commutation places both factors inside fixed points. Quotient equality reduces directly to the supplied difference membership.

External assumptions and dependencies

Normality of the fixed base and the algebraic Weyl difference equation.
