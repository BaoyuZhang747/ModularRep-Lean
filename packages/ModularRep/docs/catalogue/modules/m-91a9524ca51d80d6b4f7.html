Purpose

This additive source interface separates the FULL universal central cover from its quotient by the central p-core, which is the universal prime-to-p cover.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

A full universal cover and its separate central-p-core quotient, with the literal character/block/root dictionaries, allow the specified Spath descent source to produce coherent target bijections; a separate interpretation converts these to Definition 3.5 on the same maps.

Argument or construction

Keep the full universal cover separate from its prime-to-p cover and record the commuting quotient triangle. Apply the specified Spath descent implications and coherent relation conversion to obtain a family on the target carrier.

External assumptions and dependencies

Exact E2 Spath 2013 Proposition 4.6 with r=1 and Spath 2017 Theorem 4.4; a separate full universal cover and prime-to-p quotient diagram, compatible roots and specified block/character dictionaries, and the explicit same-map coherent-relation identification.
