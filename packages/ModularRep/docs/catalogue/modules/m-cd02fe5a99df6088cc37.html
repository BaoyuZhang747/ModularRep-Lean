Purpose

Counts the complement of an invariant singleton.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

A four-element carrier with one distinguished element has a three-element complement; if the singleton is invariant and two elements are fixed, the complement has one fixed element.

Argument or construction

The singleton is fixed because its subtype is subsingleton. Applying finite complement subtraction to the fixed-point carrier gives the second conclusion.

External assumptions and dependencies

A self-map, invariant predicate with cardinality one, and the stated total/fixed-point cardinalities. These are finite set hypotheses and include no specified character data.
