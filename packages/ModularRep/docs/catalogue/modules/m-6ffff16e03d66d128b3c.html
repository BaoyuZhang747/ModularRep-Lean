Purpose

Stores the five faithful small-block rows and rank-two witnesses.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

The complete 74-row block labelling selects five rows; their two basis rows have a scaled inverse and reconstruct all 91 columns.

Argument or construction

Closed fibre, pivot, inverse and polynomial reconstruction checks are proved by decision. The ordinary-block allocation to this printed labelling remains a specified binding.

External assumptions and dependencies

The literal data and closed identities described in this entry. Actual group, ordinary-character, block or coefficient identification is a separate binding and is not proved by this data module.
