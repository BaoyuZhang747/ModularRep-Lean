Purpose

Constructs the literal balanced tensor of a bimodule with a representation.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

For a bimodule represented by G times the opposite of L, the balancing relations define M tensor over kL W and its residual G-action, with the inverse convention on L.

Argument or construction

Coinvariants impose the tensor balancing relation. The commuting residual G action and induced linear maps define the tensor representation and functor before any equivalence hypothesis.

External assumptions and dependencies

The given group actions, tensor product and coinvariant quotient; no Morita source.
