Purpose

Constructs actual character-weight equivalences through the central two-quotient.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

Subgroup image and preimage, with the same normalizer-quotient character transport, give mutually inverse raw and conjugacy class maps.

Argument or construction

The proof establishes raw invertibility before descending through isomorphism and conjugacy. A scoped naturality law gives equivariance under the actual quotient automorphisms.

External assumptions and dependencies

Central quotient and radical correspondence plus the precise actual character-map naturality source.
