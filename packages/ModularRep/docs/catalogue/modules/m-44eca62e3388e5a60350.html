carrier-constructioncurrent

Purpose

Realises the corrected even-multiplicity obstruction in literal matrices.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

A faithful block-monomial wreath representation preserves the repeated tensor form and admits a commuting involution outside the centre.

Argument or construction

A diagonal reflection acts on the multiplicity space while the quaternion factor acts on the other tensor coordinate. Block-sum and conjugation transport preserve the involution obstruction.

External assumptions and dependencies

Odd characteristic, the authenticated corrected source-factor interpretation and the FYZ necessary Sylow-centre clause.
