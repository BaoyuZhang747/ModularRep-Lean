Purpose

Converts integer-polynomial checks into a row-inverse certificate.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

At roots annihilating the six moduli, complete inverse, constant and reconstruction checks yield a 25-dimensional representative-row certificate.

Argument or construction

Polynomial evaluation converts the residual identities to scaled field equalities. The nonzero integer inverse denominator and the factor two are divided out to obtain the inverse and reconstruction equations.

External assumptions and dependencies

Roots annihilating the six specified polynomial moduli and the complete InverseChecks, ConstantChecks and ReconstructionChecks. The finite arrays and nonzero integer denominator are the literal stored data.
