Purpose

Identifies the actual local group at a bottom radical.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

At Q=1 the stored local normalizer is the whole ambient group, and all local/base/own-normalizer maps satisfy the stated naturality squares.

Argument or construction

The image of the trivial subgroup is trivial, whose normalizer is top. The resulting equivalences remove only subtype and quotient wrappers and retain the original maps.

External assumptions and dependencies

The original ambient/local-normalizer component and equality of the raw radical with the trivial subgroup. All local-group and base-map identifications are deduced from these same stored maps.
