Purpose

Extends an invariant actual Brauer character through index at most two.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

A Brauer character fixed by ambient conjugation has an irreducible affording realization admitting an extension when the quotient cardinality is at most two.

Argument or construction

Brauer invariance supplies a conjugation-invariant realization. The explicit index-two representation theorem then constructs its extension.

External assumptions and dependencies

A Brauer root embedding, an actual irreducible Brauer character invariant under all ambient conjugations, an algebraically closed modular field and actual quotient cardinality at most two. The affording extension is constructed.
