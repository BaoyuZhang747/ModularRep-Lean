Purpose

Extracts radical parts and representative local maps from the joined central-sector correspondence.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

The joined equivalence partitions all actual Brauer characters by radical class; its local maps agree exactly with the chosen trivial- and faithful-sector rows.

Argument or construction

Use the sigma fibre equivalence of the radical-class function to obtain a disjoint cover. Injectivity of classAt identifies each local ordinary character with the retained branch value. Nonemptiness of a part is equivalent to existence of a local defect-zero character via the local equivalence.

External assumptions and dependencies

The joined literal equivalence, its branch restriction laws and classAt injectivity.
