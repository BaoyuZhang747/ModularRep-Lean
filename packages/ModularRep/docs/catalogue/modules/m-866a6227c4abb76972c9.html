Purpose

Identifies the ordinary inertia subgroup of an actual faithful match.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

The stabilizer of the raw weight is the intersection of the global Brauer stabilizer and the radical stabilizer.

Argument or construction

Raw isomorphism-class fixedness is converted into equality of the local ordinary character. The same-family fixedness equivalence then proves both inclusions of the stabilizer equality without a reduction premise.

External assumptions and dependencies

The actual faithful family with its FamilyProperties, an original raw match and an automorphism/radical stability condition. No canonical local reduction or local block-compatibility premise is used.
