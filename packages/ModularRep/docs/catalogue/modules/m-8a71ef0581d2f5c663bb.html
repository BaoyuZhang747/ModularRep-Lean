Purpose

Obtains a principal-block outer-equivariant bijection by finite cancellation.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

An unblocked fully automorphism-equivariant global correspondence, the nonprincipal census and normalized defect-zero sources yield an equivalence of principal fibres intertwining the outer involutions.

Argument or construction

Construct the nonprincipal equivalence from total and fixed counts and the defect-zero equivalence from canonical reduction and trivial weights. Cancel these two known finite C2-set fibres from the global equivalence.

External assumptions and dependencies

The unblocked An–Dietrich-style global equivalence and equivariance, centerlessness, three-block source, nonprincipal census and actual defect-zero block/reduction sources.
