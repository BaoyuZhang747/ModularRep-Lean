Purpose

Compute the normalizer-action range in the original extension from surjectivity of its conjugation action.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

An injective original extension with surjective conjugation action identifies the normalizer-action range with automorphisms fixing the centre, subgroup and Brauer character.

Argument or construction

Lift a stabilizing automorphism through the surjective action and use subgroup stability to place the lift in the normalizer. The converse follows directly from conjugation; no splitting or finite ambient is assumed here.

External assumptions and dependencies

The injective original group embedding, surjective conjugation action, faithful central sector and its actual Brauer character. Neither an extension splitting nor a finite ambient is needed for the displayed normalizer-range equality.
