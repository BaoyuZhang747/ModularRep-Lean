Purpose

Attach the actual ordinary and character-triple comparisons to the same transported sector family.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

The accelerated replacement applications preserve a transported ordinary family and its concrete comparisons across the centreless, centre-two, faithful-centre and trivial-outer branches.

Argument or construction

Apply the appropriate replacement theorem to the fixed family, then combine its ordinary, block and intermediate data. The family equality prevents a fresh numerical bijection from silently replacing the map used by the comparisons.

External assumptions and dependencies

The finite actual group, characteristic prime, compatible coefficient/root data, specified sets of primitive blocks and characters, and the imported transport results are fixed as in the declaration context. The initial sector correspondence and its stabilizer, block and pair laws are supplied. Full extension and intermediate-block conclusions additionally use their stated certification or Späth inputs.
