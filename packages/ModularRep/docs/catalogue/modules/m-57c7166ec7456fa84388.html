Purpose

Computes holomorph and global-stabilizer coordinates from a group equivalence.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

The group equivalence determines both the automorphism and holomorph equivalences, with transported character and root.

Argument or construction

Actual opposite-action equivariance proves the global stabilizer image and normal-base kernel image. Raw and local coordinates are handled by a separate consumer of the same map.

External assumptions and dependencies

Only the fixed group equivalence and canonical character transport.
