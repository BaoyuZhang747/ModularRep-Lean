Purpose

Constructs literal Spin Fourier projectors, PIM functions and their dimension formulas.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

Over the supplied splitting field, ordinary orthogonality and specified decomposition support give the PIM scalar-product formula, self-adjoint block projection and its exact principal support behavior.

Argument or construction

Fourier coordinates prove projection idempotence, self-adjointness and naturality. The actual decomposition matrix gives PIM independence, specified projective dimension and the projective–Brauer adapter.

External assumptions and dependencies

Ordinary orthogonality, stable-reduction character compatibility and column independence for the same specified coefficients.
