theorem-developmentauxiliary

Purpose

Constructs the unique odd-order symplectic lift of an odd-order rational PCSp element.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

For an odd-order element in the full rational PCSp carrier, proves it belongs to the projective symplectic image and has a unique odd-order symplectic lift through the central double cover.

Argument or construction

Odd projective order forces the multiplier to be a square, placing the element in that image. The range-restricted projection has kernel ±1, and the central double-cover theorem gives the unique odd lift.

External assumptions and dependencies

Odd characteristic and the literal multiplier/scalar quotient model; no surjectivity onto all PCSp elements.
