Constructs four distinct actual weights from the named local ordinary rows.

The actual radical and four local defect-zero characters give an injective map to weight classes, all lying in the separately specified target block.

Argument or construction

Use the intrinsic local defect-zero-character/weight-fibre equivalence to define the rows and prove injectivity. The interval central character induction law and local inflation/block compatibility identify their actual ambient block.

External assumptions and dependencies

The actual radical, literal row bijection, local defect-zero laws and actual target-induction binding. Exhaustivity and fixed counts require the later local census coverage results.
