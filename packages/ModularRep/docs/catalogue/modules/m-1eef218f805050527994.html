Purpose

Derives the Fischer prime-three cover data from the actual full cover.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

A universal covering map with kernel order three over a nonabelian simple quotient supplies the centreless identity three-prime cover used by the conditional Fischer endpoint.

Argument or construction

The full-cover theorem proves the prime-to-three maximality of the quotient's identity cover. All other Fischer, table, action and coefficient bindings remain explicit in the downstream application.

External assumptions and dependencies

An actual universal central covering map with kernel order three over the specified nonabelian simple quotient. All Fischer correspondence and literal bridge, table/block/radical/action, canonical reduction and compatibility, normalization, ambient coefficient/root and extension/covering hypotheses of the downstream three-prime Definition 4.1 endpoint remain explicit; only the centreless identity-cover data are derived here.
