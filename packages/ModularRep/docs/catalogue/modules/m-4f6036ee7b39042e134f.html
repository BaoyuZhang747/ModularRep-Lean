Completes group-equivalence tuple transport at character level.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

The global transported Brauer character and the two own reduction equations give both tuple character-value laws.

Argument or construction

The ambient root square is automatic for its computed transport. A separate horizontal normalizer-root square completes the tuple isomorphism, then standard transport yields the relation equivalence.

External assumptions and dependencies
