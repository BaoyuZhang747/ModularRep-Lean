Purpose

Serre, Linear Representations of Finite Groups, Section 12.3, Theorem 24 and its corollary (p. 94), with Section 12.1, Propositions 32--33 (pp. 90--91), supplies the narrowly stated splitting-field descent input.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

It concerns every ordinary irreducible character on these actual finite centralizers, not only the later unipotent labels or a desired block fibre. The algebraic closure is a separate ordinary coefficient field.

Argument or construction

There is no map from it into the modular system's fraction field. The chosen descent, its uniqueness, injectivity and conjugation equation are deduced. Finite base and characteristic zero are explicit constructor binders; the root hypothesis is on each exact finite group being descended.
