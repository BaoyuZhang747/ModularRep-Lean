theorem-developmentauxiliary

Purpose

Performs cancellation on sets of characters and weights over the three blocks.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

An unblocked equivariant sector family and equivariant maps on the nonprincipal and defect-zero fibres yield a principal-fibre map, then a block-preserving total map retaining both known maps.

Argument or construction

Pairwise distinctness and exhaustive actual block coverage make the three-part forgetful maps bijective and compatible with the outer involution. Transport the global correspondence to these decompositions and apply finite C2-set cancellation. Reassemble the resulting principal map with the supplied known maps. Their restriction equalities prove block preservation and transport principal signatures.

External assumptions and dependencies

Actual complete three-block census and stability, raw equivariant sector family, known nonprincipal and defect-zero maps, and actual local-operation naturality. RoutineTransportInput is empty; it does not remove the imported operation bindings.
