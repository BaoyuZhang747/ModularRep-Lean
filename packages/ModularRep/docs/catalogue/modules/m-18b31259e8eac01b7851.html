Mathematical statement and assumptions

The B2/B3 constructions have selected the dominated block, deflation, matching and raw representatives before their extension catalogues are supplied.

Argument or construction

Apply the nonprincipal criterion construction with specified sources on those selected pairs. Its resulting witness keeps the original quotient map and matching, including the common intermediate-extension data.

External assumptions and dependencies

Only the specified extension catalogues at those selected pairs remain before the natural quotient witness is returned. Specified inputs only at the representatives selected by the matching.
