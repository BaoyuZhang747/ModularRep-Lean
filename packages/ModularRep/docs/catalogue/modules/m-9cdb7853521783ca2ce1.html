Purpose

Identifies the action realized by the original normalizer.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

For faithful centres of orders 3, 4 or 6 with inversion and outer order two, the normalizer action realizes precisely the centre-fixing radical/Brauer stabilizer.

Argument or construction

Conjugation in the normalizer gives subgroup stability and character fixedness. Conversely the faithful-sector stabilizer is realized by an inner element, and radical stability forces that element into the normalizer.

External assumptions and dependencies

Conjugation and the original radical supply the basic normalizer action. The faithful range theorem additionally uses the actual faithful Brauer character, outer order two, centre order 3, 4 or 6 and a centre-inverting automorphism.
