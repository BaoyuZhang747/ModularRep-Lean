Purpose

Combines the center-three faithful-sector construction with central quotient transport into one complete correspondence.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

A stabilizer-equivariant block-preserving equivalence in one faithful sector, together with the stated quotient, singleton, root, local and extension inputs, yields a fully equivariant block- and sector-preserving global equivalence with retained local and joint witnesses.

Argument or construction

Transport the base faithful-sector equivalence across the center-inverting action and retain its ordinary, block and Q=1 data. Join it to the quotient-transported trivial-sector equivalence on the literal carriers. Extract the radical partition and local maps of this same join and attach the joint central-kernel witnesses.

External assumptions and dependencies

Downstairs correspondence and equivariance, center order three, universal central quotient, canonical reductions and block images, ambient catalogues and roots, defect-zero data, and the explicitly supplied extension/character principles.
