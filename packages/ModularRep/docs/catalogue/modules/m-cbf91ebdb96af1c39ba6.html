Purpose

Separates the uniform principles used by the induced Morita construction.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

Conditional split Marcus and restriction-square principles apply to the fixed balanced tensor and induced opposite-action representation and supply upper Morita and its natural restriction square.

Argument or construction

Distinct principles supply finiteness, support, the restriction square and the degree-zero split Marcus implication. None chooses a simple character or asserts its extension or final block criterion.

External assumptions and dependencies

Standard finite representation/support theory and the scoped Marcus induced-equivalence theorem with coefficient/cohomology interpretation.
