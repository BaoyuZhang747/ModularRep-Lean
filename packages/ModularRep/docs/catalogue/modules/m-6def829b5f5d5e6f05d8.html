Purpose

This file defines the abelian group presented by isomorphism classes of objects of a category, subject to the relation [X₂] = [X₁] + [X₃] for every short exact complex X₁ ⟶ X₂ ⟶ X₃.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

The exact Grothendieck group is the free abelian group on a skeleton modulo short-exact relations. Exact functors induce homomorphisms taking each object class to its image class.

Argument or construction

Form the free abelian group on a skeleton and quotient by short-exact relations. An exact functor preserves these relations and descends to a homomorphism with the stated effect on object classes.

External assumptions and dependencies

The stated essentially small exact/abelian category and short-exact structure; functoriality uses preservation of zero morphisms, finite limits and finite colimits.
