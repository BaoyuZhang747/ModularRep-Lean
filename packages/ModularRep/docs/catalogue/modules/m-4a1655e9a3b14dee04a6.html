Faithful family, AD3, original-model block and same-map Q=1 outputs, together with reduced quotient block output, imply the ordinary partition and CentralKernelJointPair for every match.

Argument or construction

Split a Brauer character by triviality of its central sector. In the trivial branch, classAt injectivity identifies the row supplied by quotient transport.
