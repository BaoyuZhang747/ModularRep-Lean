ModularRep.WeightCharacterBridge

ModularRep/WeightCharacterBridge.lean

transport-adaptercurrent

Purpose

This file identifies weights defined by representations with weights defined by ordinary irreducible characters of defect zero.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

The stated local splitting-character correspondence converts representation weights to actual ordinary character weights. Their isomorphism, conjugacy and right automorphism actions are constructed, with block assignment supplied separately.

Argument or construction

Use the explicit local splitting-character input to replace a simple local representation by its trace character and reflect representation isomorphism. Construct character-weight isomorphism, ambient conjugacy and automorphism actions, with block assignment supplied separately.

External assumptions and dependencies

LocalSplittingCharacterInput stating that simple local traces are ordinary irreducible characters and separate their representation classes. Block-fibre portions separately use the supplied EquivariantBlockAssignment.
