Purpose

Builds the rank-three specified GGGR basis data from raw classwise character sources.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

The raw Chaneb row, wavefront and closure data with original characters, normalized duality and series support instantiate the specified principal GGGR basis source.

Argument or construction

Chaneb selects the classwise rows and proves their diagonal multiplicities. Existing principal membership, projected projectivity and dimension theorems fill the specified basis record, with closure vanishing supplying triangularity.
