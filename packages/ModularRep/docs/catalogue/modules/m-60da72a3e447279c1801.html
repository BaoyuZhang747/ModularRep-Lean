Purpose

Develops the ordinary character-sum projector.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

The scaled inverse-character sum is central, has the explicit product and trace formulas, and is idempotent once it acts as identity in the same representation.

Argument or construction

Coefficient convolution rewrites multiplication in terms of the representation action. Elements annihilated by that action are annihilated by the character sum, so applying this to projector minus one proves idempotence.

External assumptions and dependencies

A finite group and actual characteristic-zero representation. The action-one lemma uses surjectivity of scalars onto its self-intertwining algebra and the trace equation; the idempotence theorem assumes the same projector acts as identity.
