Purpose

Binds paired rank-three root charts to literal Clifford and PCSp point groups.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

Signed root maps, coroots, torus equations and generated subgroups identify the selected Levi and its derived components.

Argument or construction

Generation and support prove properness and component stability. Coordinate Frobenius equations retain the inverse dual root action and the same field cardinality before the rational product is constructed.

External assumptions and dependencies

Authentic algebraic pinnings, paired root datum, full central torus and component product/Frobenius interpretation under E1/U.
