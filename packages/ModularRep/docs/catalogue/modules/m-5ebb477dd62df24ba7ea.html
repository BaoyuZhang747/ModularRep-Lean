Purpose

Factor the specified rank-two order into q-power, q-square-minus-one and q-square-plus-one parts.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

The specified rank-two symplectic factored order, a cyclic subgroup of order q squared plus one and the displayed prime exclusions force all relevant prime subgroups to be cyclic.

Argument or construction

Factor the specified rank-two order into q-power, q-square-minus-one and q-square-plus-one parts. Coprimality places the entire relevant prime part in the supplied cyclic torus, so the cyclic Sylow criterion applies.

External assumptions and dependencies

The concrete order, field-size, centre-size or cyclic-torus equalities explicitly displayed in the theorem. Arithmetic deductions are internal; no concrete group identification, defect theorem or iBAW theorem follows from an order alone.
