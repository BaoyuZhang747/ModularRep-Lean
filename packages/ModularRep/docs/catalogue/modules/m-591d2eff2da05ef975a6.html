Purpose

This file applies the finite argument in CharacteristicTwoClifford to the library's function-valued irreducible Brauer characters.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

The relational characteristic-two Clifford argument specializes to actual Brauer characters with inertia-stabilizer actions and the stated source dictionaries, yielding their product stabilizer factorization. brauerCharacter_stabilizerRestricted_productStabilizerFactorization is the inertia-compatible entry. brauerCharacter_productStabilizerFactorization is retained for cases where its stronger full-actor hypotheses are independently available; it is not classified as an invalid theorem.

Argument or construction

Instantiate the relational Clifford argument on function-valued Brauer carriers and their stabilizer-restricted automorphism actions. The output is the factorization supplied by that argument, conditional on the constituent and twist correspondences.

External assumptions and dependencies

Actual Brauer roots and carrier actions realizing the constituent/correspondence/twist data of CharacteristicTwoClifford. The stabilizer-restricted application uses the proper inertia actions; the full-actor wrapper retains its stronger assumptions.
