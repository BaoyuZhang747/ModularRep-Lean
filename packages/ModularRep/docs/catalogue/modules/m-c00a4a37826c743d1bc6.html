Purpose

Constructs the exact FYZ iterated regular permutation wreath action.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

Elementary abelian two-groups act regularly, and recursion on the source-ordered list gives the prescribed faithful wreath representation.

Argument or construction

Each recursive step adds the outer regular factor. Faithfulness is proved, removing the earlier arbitrary permutation homomorphism.
