Purpose

Joins rank three and rank at least four for the current principal Spin results.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

For every odd-field rank at least three, raw sources produce a rational GGGR basis, full-field Brauer fixation, stabilizer factorization and extension.

Argument or construction

The rank split selects the Chaneb rank-three basis or the higher-rank component/restriction construction. Rational class fixation and specified projection/PIM covariance then feed the common semidirect extension proof.

External assumptions and dependencies

Rank-three Chaneb 2.8 plus the FYZ Corollary 5.17 component-abelian premise, or the higher-rank abelian2.8/nonabelian upper-family route; Taylor support-to-closure and separate Chaneb 2.7/CE 21.14 counts; actual modular system and cyclic extension. No basis or fixation is supplied.
