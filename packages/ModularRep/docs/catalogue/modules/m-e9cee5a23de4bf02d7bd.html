Purpose

The two constructors retain the two complete presentations already proved in the current Type B work: a full Definition 3.5 family witness on its specified universal prime-to-ell cover, or the exceptional q=3 nine-block certificate in its original natural quotient presentations.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

An IBAWCertificate is indexed by the actual finite simple-group coordinate and modular prime.

Argument or construction

One constructor stores a complete FamilyWitness on its specified prime-to-ell cover. The other stores the exceptional nine-block certificate with its original natural quotient presentations; group isomorphism changes only the simple-group coordinate.
