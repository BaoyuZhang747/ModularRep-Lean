Purpose

This file supplies abstract support transport intended for a later formalisation of central character sectors.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

A semilinear module equivalence and ring/index equivalences carrying one complete central family to another carry the unique support index by the same index equivalence.

Argument or construction

A semilinear equivalence carries identity and zero idempotent actions to the reindexed family. Unique support then proves that the supported index is transported by the same index equivalence.

External assumptions and dependencies

The two complete central families, their index equivalence, a ring equivalence and a semilinear module equivalence carrying the families to one another.
