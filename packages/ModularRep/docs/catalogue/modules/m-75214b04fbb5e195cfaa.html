Purpose

The class of relative pairs is the existing nonselective FullHG carrier. The structural input presents each of its finite groups as an actual special linear or unitary group, Sp4, or a Spin group of rank at least three.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

A relative finite group is presented as actual SL/SU, Sp4 or rank-at-least-three Spin, with the same strict predicate and prescribed specified family.

Argument or construction

The Spin dual is the literal PCSp group with a calibrated connected-centralizer/proper-Levi interpretation. The quasi-isolated semisimple order bound together with odd order forces its label to one, and the unipotent two-block statement identifies the selected block with the actual principal block.

External assumptions and dependencies

The source boundaries are the algebraic interpretation of its proper Levis and connected centralisers, Bonnafe's semisimple quasi-isolated order bound, and the unique unipotent two-block theorem. The selected semisimple label, two-regularity and series membership have their usual separate roles. The source class, classifications, algebraic interpretations and character-series dictionary remain E1/E2/U.
