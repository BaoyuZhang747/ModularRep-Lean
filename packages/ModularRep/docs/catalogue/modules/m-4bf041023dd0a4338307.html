Purpose

Restrict the integral decomposition isomorphism to the selected block and stabilizer.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

A block diagonal integral basic set restricted to the actual block stabilizer yields an equivariant basic/Brauer block equivalence under the displayed small-normal-subgroup/cyclic-quotient and Conlon inputs.

Argument or construction

Restrict the integral decomposition isomorphism to the selected block and stabilizer. An embedding of its quotient into a cyclic group and a small normal subgroup supplies the Conlon hypothesis and hence the blockwise Brauer/basic-set equivalence.

External assumptions and dependencies

The actual IBr carrier and primitive block family, restricted integral basic set, block diagonal support and exact stabilizer action, plus a small normal subgroup/cyclic quotient embedding and Conlon/Burnside sources.
