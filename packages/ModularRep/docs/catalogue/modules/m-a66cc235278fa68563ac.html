Purpose

A nonsingleton Brauer block forces modular characteristic to divide the group order.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

A nonsingleton block implies that the prime divides the group order; a defect-zero character then has degree different from one and its reduction is nontrivial.

Argument or construction

Use the semisimple prime-to-order case to rule out a nonsingleton block. The defect-zero degree formula excludes degree one, and reduction preserves degree.
