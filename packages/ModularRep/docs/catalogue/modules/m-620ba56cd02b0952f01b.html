Purpose

Identifies the First Main image of the local block using exact defect and ambient exclusions.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

Local support, Navarro 4.14/4.13 and the upper-defect source, with the two excluded ambient roles, identify the selected image with the nonprincipal block and prove induction.

Argument or construction

The forward half of the split First Main construction gives exact ambient defect Q for the Phase A selector. Three-block uniqueness identifies its value, and the selector characterization gives block induction. The additional central Brauer idempotent formula uses restricted injectivity and therefore separately requires the raw 4.15–4.16 class-sum source.

External assumptions and dependencies

Local support, exhaustive three-block exclusions, Navarro Phase A, mapped defect and upper-defect inputs. Raw class sums are needed only for the final idempotent identity.
