Purpose

Constructs compatible finite Clifford, Spin and original Levi carriers for Jordan transport.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

The original fixed-point equivalence carries the same prescribed field actor to finite points.

Argument or construction

Perfectness identifies Spin with the Clifford commutator subgroup, hence makes it characteristic. The original Levi inclusion and field squares then restrict to Spin without a new norm-action premise.

External assumptions and dependencies

Norm-compatible fixed-point realization, prescribed finite actor and perfectness.
