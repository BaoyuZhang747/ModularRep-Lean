Purpose

Proves Q=1 intermediate self-induction on literal intersection groups.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

When the local ambient equals top, a common character pair and a global restriction to J yield the transported local restriction, equal block labels and BlockInducesTo.

Argument or construction

The intersection of the local ambient with J is canonically J. Pull back the global character and catalogue through this equivalence. Compatibility of restrictions gives the local character, and the block selected after transport has the same index, so induction is self-induction.

External assumptions and dependencies

The common-pair equality and the global character restriction and complete block catalogue at J.
