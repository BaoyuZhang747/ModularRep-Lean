ModularRep.LocalNormalizerBlockNaturality

ModularRep/LocalNormalizerBlockNaturality.lean

transport-adaptercurrent

Purpose

The character of a weight has canonical transport along an automorphism of the ambient group, and that automorphism also gives canonical equivalences of the corresponding normaliser quotient and normaliser.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

Uniform group-equivalence and right-twist naturality of the local block operations gives transport equality of their actual inflated normalizer primitive idempotents.

Argument or construction

Translate the uniform right-twist and group-equivalence laws of local block operations to equality of the actual inflated normalizer idempotents. This preserves the attached local block under the canonical quotient transports.

External assumptions and dependencies

Literal quotient and inflated normalizer primitive blocks, the supplied LocalNormalizerBlockOperations, and the uniform right-twist/group-equivalence naturality laws. These do not include an ambient induced-block selector.
