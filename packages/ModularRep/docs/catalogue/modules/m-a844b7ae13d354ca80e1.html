Purpose

For a 2-block with an actual defect subgroup of order four, the EKK classification consequences and the three published branch principles yield a complete block certificate on the chosen prime-to-two cover.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

For a 2-block with an actual defect subgroup of order four, the EKK classification consequences and the three published branch principles yield a complete block certificate on the chosen prime-to-two cover.

Argument or construction

Order four makes the defect subgroup abelian. Split the classification into principal, Co3, nilpotent-covered and product cases; reject the order-eight case, use Späth or Hu–Zhou where applicable, and apply Zhang–Zhou to the noninertial Klein-four hyperfocal case.

External assumptions and dependencies

At p=2, the actual prime-to-two cover, modular system, subgroup splitting and compatible root data are fixed. ClassificationConsequences supplies the EKK case consequences; PublishedBranchPrinciples supplies Späth Corollary 6.6, Hu–Zhou Theorem 1.1 and the stated Zhang–Zhou result, all with complete block-certificate conclusions.
