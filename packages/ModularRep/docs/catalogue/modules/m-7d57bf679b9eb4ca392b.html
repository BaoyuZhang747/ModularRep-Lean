theorem-developmentcurrent

Purpose

This file constructs O_p(G) as the supremum of the normal p-subgroups of G.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

The supremum of normal p-subgroups is the largest normal p-subgroup. If quotient by that p-core is cyclic, the same holds for every subgroup's quotient by its own p-core.

Argument or construction

Take the supremum of normal p-subgroups and use closure to prove it is itself normal and a p-group. Restriction of the quotient to a subgroup and enlargement of its kernel prove cyclic quotient inheritance.

External assumptions and dependencies

The group and p-group closure facts used to form the normal-subgroup supremum; cyclic inheritance additionally assumes the ambient quotient by that core is cyclic.
