Purpose

Checks the full and representative column involutions and their routing square.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

The full permutation on 91 columns descends to an involution on 41 representative columns, with 31 fixed representatives.

Argument or construction

Finite decision checks prove both involutions and the routing relation. The representative columns form a section of the routing. Enumerating the fixed-point subtype gives 31.

External assumptions and dependencies

The literal fullIndex, columnLabel and pivotColumn arrays.
