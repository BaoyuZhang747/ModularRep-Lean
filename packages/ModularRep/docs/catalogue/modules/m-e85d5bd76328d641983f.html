Purpose

This module verifies two elementary but load-bearing deductions in the proof of the type B GGGR proposition.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

Equal nonnegative integer entries summing to one give the single-term conclusion, while symmetric two-by-two entries summing to one give one of the two permutation matrices and determinant plus or minus one.

Argument or construction

Equal nonnegative integer entries with sum one can have only one nonzero term. A symmetric two-by-two block whose entries sum to one is one of the two permutation matrices, and its determinant is a unit.

External assumptions and dependencies

The displayed finite family of nonnegative integers with equal entries and sum one, or the two entries a,b with a+b=1.
