Purpose

Proves covariance and fixedness for the extracted ordinary maps.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

Automorphisms carry the sector/radical local map to the corresponding twisted map; at a stable radical, global Brauer fixedness is equivalent to local ordinary fixedness.

Argument or construction

Both sides have the same image under the injective representative class map. The class equation and equivariance of the original family therefore determine the actual ordinary character.

External assumptions and dependencies

For covariance, an equivariant sector-preserving faithful family, local canonical availability and compatibility. The stable-radical original fixed-iff theorem uses the family properties and raw-match equation and does not require reductions.
