Purpose

Constructs the selected Spath ambient from the actual automorphism stabilizer.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

For a centerless group and a bijective selected semidirect automorphism presentation, the Brauer stabilizer supplies the required Spath ambient of the character's central quotient.

Argument or construction

Identify the central quotient with the original group and its embedded normal base. Restrict equivariant group and character equivalences to stabilizers. Faithfulness of the automorphism presentation makes the centralizer of the base trivial, hence the ambient center trivial. The semidirect conjugation formula gives the required action square and automorphism quotient equivalence.

External assumptions and dependencies

Centerlessness and bijectivity of the semidirect presentation, or the outer-class and inner-kernel sources that imply it. All extension and block obligations remain downstream.
