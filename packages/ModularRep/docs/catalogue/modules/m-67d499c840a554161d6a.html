Purpose

Proves block induction for literal finite products from factor inductions.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

If each actual subgroup block induces to its factor ambient block, the coefficient-defined product subgroup block induces to the product ambient block via the actual product inclusion.

Argument or construction

Coefficient restriction factors on pure central products. Their spanning property and catalogue evaluation laws then identify the induced central function and hence the product block induction.

External assumptions and dependencies

Uniform centre-product spanning/evaluation sources and the individual factor block-induction equations.
