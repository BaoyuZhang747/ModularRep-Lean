Purpose

Lift prime subgroups uniquely through a finite central prime-to-p quotient.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

A central prime-to-p quotient has a unique lift for each prime subgroup, realized by the prime core of its preimage.

Argument or construction

Centrality and coprime kernel order make the relevant Sylow subgroup unique. This uniqueness proves the inverse equations and the injectivity of the quotient map on each lifted subgroup.

External assumptions and dependencies

Finite groups, a surjective homomorphism with central kernel of prime-to-p order, and the imported finite prime-group/Sylow theory. The subgroup-lifting deduction itself does not require a named-group or table source.
