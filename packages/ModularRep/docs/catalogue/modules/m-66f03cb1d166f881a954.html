Purpose

This file combines the character orthogonality, completeness, and action results.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

Over algebraically closed coefficients and an invertible-order finite central subgroup, the complete central character idempotent family has the irreducible representation's scalar central character as its unique support.

Argument or construction

Combine orthogonality and completeness of central character idempotents. Their abstract unique module support agrees with the previously computed scalar central character.

External assumptions and dependencies

Algebraically closed coefficients, an irreducible representation and the finite invertible-order central subgroup. Completeness and scalar-support calculations are earlier results.
