Purpose

Constructs the two ordinary local extensions for a rank-three raw weight.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

Both target groups are intersections of its original raw inertia with SO or with Omega-by-field.

Argument or construction

The respective quotients are cyclic. Finite roots descend from the same full ambient, and the ordinary cyclic-extension source extends that weight's own normalizer-quotient character.

External assumptions and dependencies

Actual inertia geometry, index two, field cyclicity and finite-splitting ordinary cyclic extension.
