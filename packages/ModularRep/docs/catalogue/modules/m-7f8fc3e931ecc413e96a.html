ModularRep.BrauerCharacter

ModularRep/BrauerCharacter.lean

carrier-constructioncurrent

Purpose

For a finite group G and a prime p, the eigenvalues of a p-regular element are roots of unity whose orders divide primeRegularExponent p G.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

An explicit finite root correspondence defines a Brauer class function by lifting the eigenvalue multiset of every prime regular operator. Isomorphic finite representations have the same resulting function.

Argument or construction

Show that every eigenvalue at a prime regular element is in the chosen finite root group, then lift its characteristic-polynomial multiset and sum. Similarity preserves that multiset and hence the resulting class function.

External assumptions and dependencies

An explicit isomorphism between the finite groups of roots of unity, including p primality. The Brauer-character construction uses characteristic-p k, algebraic closure for eigenvalue splitting, characteristic-zero K and finite dimensionality as stated.
