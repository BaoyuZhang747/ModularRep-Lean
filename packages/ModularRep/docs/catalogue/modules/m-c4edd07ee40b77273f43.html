Purpose

Defines FMZ generic weights over admissible algebraic torus labels.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

A generic witness stores its actual Levi character and the defining e-JGC, series and block conditions; W is the valid-pair conjugacy quotient.

Argument or construction

The finite normalizer is derived as a label stabilizer. Uniform inner coherence makes the generic predicate descend to conjugacy classes.

External assumptions and dependencies

FMZ Section 3.4 semantics, without formal Lusztig induction or an assumed weight correspondence.
