theorem-developmentcurrent

Purpose

For a semisimple ring, every isotypic component of the left regular module is generated by a central idempotent.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

In a semisimple ring, each primitive central block supports exactly one simple-module isomorphism class. The group algebra specialization does not require algebraic closure.

Argument or construction

In a semisimple ring, isotypic components of the regular module are central-idempotent summands. A primitive central idempotent can support only one such simple type, while its nonzero summand supplies existence.

External assumptions and dependencies

A semisimple ring/group algebra and supplied primitive central-idempotent decomposition. The unique supported simple class theorem does not require an algebraically closed coefficient field.
