Purpose

Constructs the selected semidirect Brauer stabilizer and its embedded base.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

The square-one outer carrier gives an action of X ⋊ C2 on irreducible Brauer characters; the original group is canonically equivalent to the embedded base of each character stabilizer.

Argument or construction

Combine the right inner and selected outer actions using their semidirect compatibility. Inner automorphisms fix every irreducible Brauer character, so the entire normal factor lies in the stabilizer and the canonical embedded-base equivalence applies.

External assumptions and dependencies

The actual root convention and selected outer involution. No block or inductive-condition source is involved.
