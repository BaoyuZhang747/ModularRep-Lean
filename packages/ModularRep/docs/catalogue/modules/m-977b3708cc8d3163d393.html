Purpose

Constructs the three actual block roles and their stability from narrow bindings.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

An equivalence between three source roles and a complete block index set, with actual action equal to the parsed role action, gives Fi24ThreeBlockSource.

Argument or construction

The role bijection gives distinction and exhaustivity and composes with the primitive-block equivalence. Closed decisions show the transcript action fixes all three positions. Substitute the action binding to derive actual fixedness and take square-one from the selected involution carrier.

External assumptions and dependencies

The complete literal block decomposition, specified role equivalence and selected-action/table binding. Parsed defects, signatures and positions are numerical metadata until those identifications are supplied.
