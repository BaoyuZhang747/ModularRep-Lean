Purpose

Constructs a complete block witness from one original chosen packet.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

Its map is the original specified block map, and its relative relation, extensions, quotient fibres and graph are computed from that same packet.

Argument or construction

Specified dictionaries and five finite-root guards fill the complete target. The same-extension normalization at the trivial radical is retained.

External assumptions and dependencies

The original witness, actual references and quotient operations, with guards supplied by coherent metadata rather than a new theorem assumption.
