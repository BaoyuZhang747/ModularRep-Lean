Purpose

Computes the selected projector's action on actual irreducibles.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

Norm one makes the projector act as identity on its own representation and makes it idempotent; an irreducible of unequal degree is annihilated.

Argument or construction

The trace and scalar-intertwiner calculation give identity on the selected representation. Orthogonality gives zero trace on a nonisomorphic irreducible, and an idempotent action of trace zero must vanish.

External assumptions and dependencies

The selected representation's norm/scalar-intertwiner data for identity action. Zero action on another irreducible uses the projector's proved idempotence, characteristic zero and unequal dimensions; no modular scalar-descent principle occurs here.
