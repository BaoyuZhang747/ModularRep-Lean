Purpose

Derives radical partitions and representative local maps from one retained global correspondence.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

A normalized fully equivariant block-preserving Omega yields an equivariant radical partition, simultaneous block/radical restrictions and representative-level local defect-zero equivalences.

Argument or construction

Define the partition by the radical of Omega(phi) and use the sigma-of-fibres equivalence. Restrict Omega and its inverse to a block and radical class. Compose the weight fibre with the intrinsic representative defect-zero equivalence. Normalization and Omega injectivity characterize the trivial part exactly as defect-zero reductions.

External assumptions and dependencies

The same Omega, block preservation and Q=1 normalization, with the actual representative-fibre theorem. The noTriple endpoint needs no triple source; the enriched endpoint separately retains the external triple implication.
