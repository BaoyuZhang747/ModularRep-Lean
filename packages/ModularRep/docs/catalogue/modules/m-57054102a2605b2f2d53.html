ModularRep.CentralClassSumExpansion

ModularRep/CentralClassSumExpansion.lean

theorem-developmentcurrent

Purpose

For a finite group, every central group algebra element is the finite sum of its classwise coefficient times the corresponding unnormalised conjugacy class sum.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

A central element of a finite group algebra is the sum of its conjugacy class sums weighted by its constant coefficients on those classes.

Argument or construction

Use conjugacy invariance of coefficients to expand a central group algebra element in class sums. Regrouping the finite support proves the reconstruction formula.

External assumptions and dependencies

A finite group, a central group algebra element and the class-sum basis expansion by conjugacy classes.
