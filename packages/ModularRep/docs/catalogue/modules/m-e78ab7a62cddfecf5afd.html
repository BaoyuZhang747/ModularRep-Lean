Purpose

The subgroups are the criterion's actual raw normalizer inertias inside M semidirect E.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

Each local character is the ordinary quotient character of the prescribed raw weight, on its actual normalizer quotient.

Argument or construction

Embed the radical in the raw inertia and form its quotient. Raw fixedness gives invariance of the transported local character, while subgroup-to-quotient embeddings derive the cyclic quotient needed by extension.
