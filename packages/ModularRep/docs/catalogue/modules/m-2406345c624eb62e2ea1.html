The finite Clifford norm and its square classes are related to the actual Spin projection and special orthogonal commutator subgroup.

Argument or construction

The quotient calculations give Omega index two in SO. A central Omega element maps into the already identified ambient centralizer, and injectivity of the embedding forces it to be the identity.

Surjective preimages and the two unit square classes give the literal index.
