Purpose

Combines component fixation in the finite-subgroup prototype.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

A stable finite subgroup and fixed normalizer character produce fixation of the prototype pair's conjugacy class.

Argument or construction

The normalizer automorphism is computed from subgroup stability. Pair transport and the inverse inner-twist formula give the class equality.

External assumptions and dependencies

This is a prototype and supplies no identification of algebraic generic weights.
