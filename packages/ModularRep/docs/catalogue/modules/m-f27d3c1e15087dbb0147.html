Normalizes one algebraic component after its root and graph form are proved.

The source supplies a classical embedding and a Frobenius equation up to one displayed inner twist, never a factor list.

Lang solves that twist, producing an adjusted embedding commuting with Frobenius. Restriction to fixed points constructs the finite equivalence and its value equations.
