theorem-developmentcurrent

Purpose

This file associates to every function-valued ordinary irreducible character the simple group algebra module class of a chosen realisation.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

Taking the simple-module class of a chosen ordinary irreducible realization gives an injective label on the function-valued Irr carrier.

Argument or construction

Choose a realization of an ordinary irreducible function and take its simple-module isomorphism class. Equality of these labels gives isomorphic representations and hence equal traces, proving label injectivity.

External assumptions and dependencies

The actual ordinary irreducible character realization and the chosen simple-module class of it. Injectivity follows from representation isomorphism preserving trace.
