ModularRep.BrauerCharacterLinearIndependence

ModularRep/BrauerCharacterLinearIndependence.lean

theorem-developmentcurrent

Purpose

The modular trace functions of the simple modules are linearly independent.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

For finite G, algebraically closed characteristic-p k, characteristic-zero K and the explicit finite root correspondence, the actual irreducible Brauer characters are linearly independent over K.

Argument or construction

Choose an invertible evaluation matrix for simple modular traces on prime regular elements. Lift its root-multiset entries by cyclotomic determinant specialization, proving independence of actual irreducible Brauer functions.

External assumptions and dependencies

Algebraically closed characteristic-p k, characteristic-zero K, finite G and the explicit finite root correspondence. Simple trace independence and cyclotomic determinant specialization are proved dependencies.
