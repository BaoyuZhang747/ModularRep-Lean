Purpose

Transport radical conjugacy classes and their automorphism actions through the central quotient.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

The central prime-to-p radical equivalence descends to conjugacy classes and is compatible with the corresponding automorphism actions.

Argument or construction

Transport conjugating elements through the quotient in one direction and use the radical lift in the other. The commuting squares preserve fixed classes and reflect nonconjugacy.

External assumptions and dependencies

The finite central prime-to-p quotient and the already constructed radical equivalence, with the stated commuting automorphism square. The class-level argument adds no numerical census source.
