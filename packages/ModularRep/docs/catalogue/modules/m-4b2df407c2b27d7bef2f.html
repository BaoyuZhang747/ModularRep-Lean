Purpose

Builds the triple-cover family from actual block and fixed counts.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

Away from three, specified block totals and outer-fixed totals, together with the specified cover and lower data, give a complete original Definition 4.1 witness.

Argument or construction

The count theorem first chooses one equivariant block-preserving map. Canonical defect-zero blocks normalize it at Q=1, and the triple-cover family constructs packets for that same map.

External assumptions and dependencies

ActualBlockC2Counts on the specified Brauer/sets of weights, a universal triple cover away from three with the stated quotient outer order and inversion/decomposition data, canonical reduction/compatibility, defect-zero/trivial-weight and singleton ordinary-block sources. The quotient data, trivial-sector ambient data and roots, cyclic extension, coefficient field and Navarro restriction/covering/twist principles needed by OriginalTripleCoverFamily remain explicit.
