Purpose

Recovers the actual local Brauer reduction from a packet.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

The packet's base restriction gives an irreducible Brauer character on the original normalizer, equal to the reduction of the inflated ordinary weight character.

Argument or construction

The local map is identified with a base equivalence followed by inclusion. Pulling back the stored restriction gives the character, and the packet value equation proves its ordinary reduction property.

External assumptions and dependencies

An already constructed ActualWeightPacket indexed by the same raw weight. Its stored intermediate base restriction and ordinary local-value equation suffice; no further root or block compatibility input is introduced.
