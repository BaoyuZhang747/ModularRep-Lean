Purpose

Exclude the principal and defect-zero roles as the block of a supplied elementary abelian defect group of order nine.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

Actual principal and defect-zero blocks cannot carry an elementary abelian defect group of order nine when their defect orders are respectively 3^16 and one.

Argument or construction

Transport defect groups along conjugacy and compare cardinalities. The elementary abelian candidate has cardinality nine, which contradicts either endpoint order; no transcript-to-block identification is deduced.

External assumptions and dependencies

The finite actual group, characteristic prime, compatible coefficient/root data, specified sets of primitive blocks and characters, and the imported transport results are fixed as in the declaration context. The exact specified selector, completeness, support, decomposition or action bindings occurring in the module are supplied; this construction does not authenticate a named character table.
