ModularRep.FDRepGroupAlgebraEquivalence

theorem-developmentcurrent

Purpose

Identify finite dimensional representations of a finite group with finitely generated modules over its group algebra.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

For finite G, finite dimensional representations over k and finitely generated kG-modules are equivalent categories, using restriction of scalars along the finite-dimensional group algebra.

Argument or construction

Restrict scalars from finitely generated group algebra modules and use finite generation of the group algebra over the field. Lift the standard representation/module equivalence to the two finite categories.

External assumptions and dependencies

Finite G, a coefficient field and the established representation/group algebra module equivalence; finite generation passes through the finite field algebra.
