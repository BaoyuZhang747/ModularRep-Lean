Purpose

The same original inner/outer decomposition acts on both Brauer characters and weight classes by either identity or one common involution.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

An inner/tau decomposition of automorphisms makes the tau actions on actual Brauer characters and weight classes involutions even if tau itself does not have order two.

Argument or construction

Apply the decomposition to tau squared. Either it is inner directly, or cancellation shows tau itself is inner, which gives the same conclusion for its square. Inner actions are trivial on both carriers, so the induced actions have square one.

External assumptions and dependencies

The finite actual group, characteristic prime, compatible coefficient/root data, specified sets of primitive blocks and characters, and the imported transport results are fixed as in the declaration context. The supplied inner/tau decomposition and the already established fact that inner automorphisms fix Brauer characters and weight classes. No order-two hypothesis on tau itself is needed.
