Purpose

A field automorphism acts on the fixed coordinate space semilinearly. Conjugation yields the actual linear-group automorphism, and preservation of the displayed split form restricts it to O.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

A field automorphism acts semilinearly on the same fixed coordinate space and its displayed quadratic form.

Argument or construction

Conjugating a linear map by this semilinear equivalence gives the matrix field action. Preservation of the form restricts it to O, determinant naturality restricts it to SO, and characteristicity of the commutator subgroup restricts it to Omega.
