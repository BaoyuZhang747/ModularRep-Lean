ModularRep.CentralIdempotentSupport

ModularRep/CentralIdempotentSupport.lean

carrier-constructioncurrent

Purpose

A complete finite family of pairwise orthogonal central idempotents has a unique support on a simple module: one idempotent acts as the identity and all the others act as zero.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

A simple module has exactly one supporting index in a finite complete orthogonal central-idempotent family: that member acts as identity and all others as zero.

Argument or construction

Each central idempotent acts as an idempotent module endomorphism. Simplicity makes this action zero or identity; sum one ensures existence and orthogonality gives uniqueness of the identity action.

External assumptions and dependencies

A ring, a finite complete orthogonal central-idempotent family and a simple module.
