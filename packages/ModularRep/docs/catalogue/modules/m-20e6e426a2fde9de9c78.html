Purpose

This supporting module instantiates the accepted regular-Levi component and Lang-supported action machinery on the same actual primal Lbar and original rational subgroup copies.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

The actual primal Levi component data and the central Lang source determine the rational product map and its action on the same original subgroup copies.

Argument or construction

Instantiate the supported rational-lift construction on those original component orbits to prove product-image surjectivity. Transport its conjugation formula through the component product equivalence, obtaining the pointwise group square needed by character naturality.

External assumptions and dependencies

Product-image surjectivity and the conjugation square are derived, not fields of a new representative source packet. The lower source boundary is the actual component/Frobenius presentation, the geometric decomposition of the same paired Levi into its derived group and centre, and the central Lang consequence on that same group and F.
