Purpose

Fixes quotient root embeddings by restriction of the original convention.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

Every normal quotient has a canonical root embedding derived from the given ambient embedding, compatible with Brauer-character inflation.

Argument or construction

The quotient cardinal divides the group cardinal, so its prime regular exponent divides the original exponent. Restrict the common root equivalence along this divisibility. The general common-root compatibility theorem yields the inflation equation.

External assumptions and dependencies

The original prime regular root embedding; no additional quotient root choice.
