Purpose

Computes the trivial central sector of the actual exceptional principal block.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

The central character kernel is the kernel of the fixed projection to matrix Omega(7,3).

Argument or construction

Principal support makes the central odd kernel act trivially on every supported simple representation. The resulting constant-one central character identifies the dominated central quotient independently of the matching.

External assumptions and dependencies

Specified principal support and the established kernel theorem for the actual triple-cover projection.
