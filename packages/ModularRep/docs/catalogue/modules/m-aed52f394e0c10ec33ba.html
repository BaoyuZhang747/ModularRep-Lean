theorem-developmentcurrent

Purpose

This module transports composition factors through the arrows of a short exact sequence, proves additivity of the formal Jordan--Hölder factor sum, and descends that sum to the exact Grothendieck group of FDRep.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

Composition-factor sums of finite representations are independent of a chosen composition series and additive on short exact sequences, so each Jordan-Holder coordinate defines a homomorphism on exact K-zero.

Argument or construction

Map and pull back composition series through injective and surjective linear maps. Their factor isomorphisms prove short-exact additivity of the formal factor sum, which descends to Jordan-Holder coordinates on exact K-zero.

External assumptions and dependencies

Finite dimensional representations of finite G, the chosen finite-length composition series and exact sequences. Jordan-Holder equivalence and quotient isomorphisms are the relevant prior results.
