Purpose

Separates the ordinary local row input from fixed-count consequences of the full table.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

The record supplies one- and four-element ordinary row bijections, canonical reductions, primitive images, induction intervals and the two target-allocation histograms.

Argument or construction

The fields retain the actual rows and their supporting blocks. Fixed radical classes, distinctness and the four-row fixed count are deliberately obtained from the full table by the next constructor, rather than asserted by this narrower interface.

External assumptions and dependencies

The actual local ordinary rows, quotient block images and interval allocation laws; the record is not constructed unconditionally.
