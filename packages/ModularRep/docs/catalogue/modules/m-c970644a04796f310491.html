Purpose

Proves exact ranks and full-column invariance of the remaining trivial-sector literal rows.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

The dihedral rows have rank three and are fixed by the column permutation; each zero-row family has rank one and is fixed.

Argument or construction

Evaluate the checked constant polynomial identities to identify the dihedral rows with integer rows. Use their scaled row inverse for rank three. For singleton rows, the checked unit column gives a nonzero minor, while polynomial and root-tag invariance proves fixedness for any chosen root.

External assumptions and dependencies

The closed integer and polynomial checks from RemainingTrivialBlockData; specified row identifications are separate.
