Purpose

A sectorwise candidate is a family of bijections with equivariance and pairwise compatibility; the stronger certification additionally supplies extensions, intermediate blocks and the character-triple condition.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

A sectorwise candidate is a family of bijections with equivariance and pairwise compatibility; the stronger certification additionally supplies extensions, intermediate blocks and the character-triple condition.

Argument or construction

Combine disjoint central sectors and restrict the resulting map back to each fibre. The one-point countermodel separates this construction from the stronger certification, so the missing clauses cannot be inferred from a candidate alone.

External assumptions and dependencies

The abstract sector action and supplied candidate/certification laws. The countermodel has its own one-point carriers and does not require any named group or published classification.
