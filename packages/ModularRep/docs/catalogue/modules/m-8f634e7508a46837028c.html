Purpose

Computes product p-cores and the coordinate radical criterion.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

For finite factor groups and the original product coordinates, characterizes radical product subgroups factorwise and constructs the natural equivalence between the product normalizer quotient and the factor quotient product.

Argument or construction

Maximality of p-cores identifies the product p-core. Applying this to product normalizers proves radicality iff each factor is radical and constructs the normalizer quotient equivalence.

External assumptions and dependencies

Finite group theory and previously proved normalizer/product coordinates.
