Purpose

Identifies original local maps with a retained centreless family.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

When the centreless faithful-family image equals a global correspondence, their radical fibres and actual ordinary local maps coincide.

Argument or construction

The fibre equivalence preserves the underlying Brauer character in both directions. Injectivity of the representative class map upgrades equality of weight classes to equality of the retained ordinary characters.

External assumptions and dependencies

A centreless group, the actual root/carrier data, an existing faithful family, a global Brauer/weight equivalence and an equation identifying their underlying images. The local-map identification additionally uses the retained ordinary map's representative-class equation. No new canonical reduction or block source is assumed for the fibre comparison.
