Purpose

Proves all three clauses of current principal-series Lemma 4.8 at rank at least two.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

For odd q and rank at least two, scoped CE 21.14, Bonnafe5.3(a) and the odd-label dictionary imply the full ordinary principal two-series equality, quasi-isolated-character inclusion, and strict/principal equivalence on one specified Spin block selector and the same PCSp labels.

Argument or construction

The published full equality and Bonnafé fourth-power bound imply quasi-isolated support. Odd order forces a strict label to one; specified fibre nonemptiness and series disjointness prove both block directions, with identity strictness proved geometrically.

External assumptions and dependencies

CE Theorems 21.14 and 9.12, guarded Bonnafé 5.3(a), explicit ordinary roots and algebraic dual/Levi dictionaries; no rational symplectic lift.
