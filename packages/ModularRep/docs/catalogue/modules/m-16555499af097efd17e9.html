Purpose

For a finite group G and a subgroup Q, this file uses the quotient N_G(Q) / Q.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

Package a radical subgroup and a simple finite ordinary representation of its normalizer quotient satisfying the degree p-part condition.

Argument or construction

Package a radical subgroup and a simple finite ordinary representation of its normalizer quotient satisfying the degree p-part condition. Radicality gives its p-group property; no character or block identification is built into the record.

External assumptions and dependencies
