Purpose

Defect-zero total and fixed counts before blockwise cancellation.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

A block of maximal defect zero has singleton Brauer and weight fibres under canonical raw support and defect-zero reduction data.

Argument or construction

The support law forces every weight subgroup to be trivial. The unique defect-zero reduction then identifies every weight class, giving both cardinality one and, for an invariant block, fixed-point count one.
