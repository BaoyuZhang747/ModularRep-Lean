Purpose

States the one-way modular Jordan map on complete literal packets.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

One character map and one primitive-block map come from the same reduced cohomology assignment and preserve whole-packet actions.

Argument or construction

Supporting-block compatibility puts each image into the ambient packet. This yields a packet-valued map without assuming surjectivity or a stabilizer conclusion.

External assumptions and dependencies

FLZ Jordan Theorem 4.2 and the proof of Proposition 5.2 with same-label idempotents, roots, points and field actor.
