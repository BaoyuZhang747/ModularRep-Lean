Purpose

This is the one-way composite E1 specialization of Navarro, Corollary 8.20, p. 176: restrict an actual extension to an intermediate group and apply modular Gallagher there.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

Irreducible modular characters of the abelian quotient are linear (Navarro, Theorem 1.20 and the following paragraph, p. 14; their linear character interpretation is Problem 2.7, p. 46). The source conclusion is the literal product formula on prime regular elements.
