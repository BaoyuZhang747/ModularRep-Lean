applicationcurrent

Purpose

Joins complete FYZ products to the intrinsic FM principal carrier.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

Joint source data are supplied for each existing principal weight, and the constructed principal FM class is proved equal to that original class.

Argument or construction

Corrected factors are excluded, the product subgroup is matched, and inverse local-character enumeration determines the height assignments. The resulting equivalence is the intrinsic identity on actual principal classes.

External assumptions and dependencies

Joint FYZ decomposition, normalized bases, full normalizer and An/FM character sources; no arbitrary-parameter principal converse is supplied.
