Purpose

This source-facing module records the smallest ordinary-to-modular block boundary required by literal ordinary-character fibres.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

The source supplies a surjective literal primitive-block selector on ordinary irreducible characters and nonempty block-sound decomposition support; neither selector interpretation nor support is constructed from arbitrary labels.

Argument or construction

Store an actual primitive-block selector on ordinary irreducible functions and its surjectivity. The nonempty, block-sound decomposition-support law supplies its intended interpretation; no such support relation is constructed here.

External assumptions and dependencies

LiteralOrdinaryPBlockSource with actual primitive blocks, a genuine nonempty sound decomposition-support relation and selector surjectivity. The reduction adapter separately assumes every displayed coefficient-one Brauer reduction belongs to that support relation.
