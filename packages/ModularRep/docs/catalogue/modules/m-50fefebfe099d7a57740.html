Purpose

Applies the odd-field FLZ reduction while retaining only its block maps.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

The sole ambient family is tied to a literal Sp problem, and all strict blocks of the unfiltered full-HG class supply the map consequence of FLZ 5.2/5.7.

Argument or construction

The stronger hypothesis for the full field group uses actual conformal and field actions and representation extensions. The external composite includes the choice of the permissible automorphism group for each semisimple parameter and restriction to it. Returned block maps are combined and passed to the Feng–Malle criterion.

External assumptions and dependencies

FLZ Remark 5.4, FM Corollary 4.6 and the FLZ map consequence, including the choice and restriction to the permissible groups inside the field group. Actual algebraic, root and block interpretations remain explicit. The choice and restriction are not separately implemented.
