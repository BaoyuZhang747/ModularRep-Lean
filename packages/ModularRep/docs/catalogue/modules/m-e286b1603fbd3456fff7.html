ModularRep.KZeroTwist

ModularRep/KZeroTwist.lean

transport-adaptercurrent

Purpose

Twisting a finite dimensional representation by a group automorphism is an exact autoequivalence.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

Automorphism twisting induces an exact K-zero action that commutes with both the ordinary and Brauer character homomorphisms.

Argument or construction

Automorphism pullback is an exact autoequivalence of finite representations. Its induced map acts on K-zero, and the generator formulas prove commutation with ordinary and Brauer character homomorphisms.

External assumptions and dependencies

A group automorphism and the established exact finite-representation categories. Brauer compatibility uses the same fixed finite root embedding.
