Purpose

Identify the original canonical local Brauer character as a pullback from the reduced quotient.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

The original canonical local Brauer character is the pullback of the reduced local character through the actual quotient map.

Argument or construction

Use canonical root compatibility to compare Brauer values on prime regular elements. The affording-representation transport then realizes this pullback on the actual local carrier.
