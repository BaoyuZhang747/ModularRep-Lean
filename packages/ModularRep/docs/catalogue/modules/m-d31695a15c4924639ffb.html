Purpose

Constructs the stronger principal-block criterion and same-pair Späth witnesses from raw higher-rank GGGR sources.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

For rank at least four and the stronger explicit criterion inputs, constructs the principal complete criterion on the actual matrix Omega block with its prescribed roots and original Spin cover.

Argument or construction

The GGGR selector supplies Spin fixation, transported to Omega. Counts construct the upper correspondence, the one-block criterion chooses a normalized downstairs matching, and the forward Späth passage yields witnesses for each of its own raw matches.

External assumptions and dependencies

Specified block/weight sources, rational class counts, cyclic extensions, Brough–Späth fixed-block source and the forward Späth theorem with same-pair joins.
