ModularRep.PrimeRegularRootEmbeddingPQuotient

ModularRep/PrimeRegularRootEmbeddingPQuotient.lean

transport-adaptercurrent

Purpose

A normal p-subgroup does not change the prime-to-p part of the group order.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

Quotient by a normal p-subgroup leaves the prime regular exponent unchanged, so a quotient root embedding transports to one upstairs with the identical root lift.

Argument or construction

A p-group kernel changes only the p-part of a finite group order. The prime regular exponents of group and quotient are equal, so transport of the root embedding has literally the same root lift.

External assumptions and dependencies

A normal p-subgroup, the finite quotient group and its given prime regular root embedding. Equality of prime-to-p exponents is proved from the kernel order.
