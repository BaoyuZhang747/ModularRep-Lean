carrier-constructioncurrent

Purpose

Constructs a faithful two-dimensional symplectic quaternion group.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

Matrices i and j with squares minus identity and ij=-ji realise Q8 over odd characteristic whenever a squared plus b squared equals minus one.

Argument or construction

Finite-characteristic field algebra supplies the parameters. Direct matrix calculations prove the relations, form preservation and faithfulness.

External assumptions and dependencies

No source conjugacy class label is derived; the FYZ alpha=0 right-factor identification remains separate.
