Purpose

Checks prime-three cancellation purely at C2 orbit-data level.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

Transcript Brauer signatures (25,25),(4,2),(1,1), the two known weight signatures and equality of total orbit data force principal weight signature (25,25).

Argument or construction

Signature injectivity identifies each known part's orbit data. Cancel their sum from the complete sector orbit-data equality. The principal Brauer row then transfers to the principal weight row.

External assumptions and dependencies

Semantic identification of the three rows, known weight signatures and total orbit-data equality. This module represents no actual group or set of characters or weights.
