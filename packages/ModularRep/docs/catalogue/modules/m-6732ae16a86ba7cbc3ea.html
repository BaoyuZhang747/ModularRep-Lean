Mathematical statement and assumptions

The lower Q=1 and positive nonprincipal sources classify every matched pair into a realized Q=1 case, a realized positive nonprincipal case, or an explicitly retained principal positive-radical residual.

Argument or construction

Choose both positive extensions from the same cyclic extension theorem and retain their equalities in the resulting block data. Derive nonprincipal coverage from the local census, then split on the radical and the three block roles. The singleton defect-zero fibre excludes that role for a positive radical. The principal positive case remains a residual in this window, not a completed packet.

External assumptions and dependencies

Normalized block-preserving Omega, canonical reduction, local census and radical support, Q=1 realization, and positive extension/induction sources. Later full family constructors use stronger inputs to close the complete application.
