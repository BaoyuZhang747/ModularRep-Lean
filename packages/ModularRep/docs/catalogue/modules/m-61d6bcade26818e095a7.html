Purpose

The faithful-centre character-triple deduction for centres of orders three, four and six.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

In the faithful-centre cases of orders three, four and six, an equivariant matched pair admits identity-ambient gamma-product models with equal trivial factor sets.

Argument or construction

The faithful central scalar excludes the outer stabilizer branch, so both characters extend in the original-group ambient itself. Use their common scalar to realize the gamma products with the same chosen representations, without a cyclic-extension input.

External assumptions and dependencies

The finite actual group, characteristic prime, compatible coefficient/root data, specified sets of primitive blocks and characters, and the imported transport results are fixed as in the declaration context. The faithful central-scalar, centre-order, inner/tau and specified support bindings are supplied. Their actual representation and root identifications are required, rather than merely a cardinality for the centre.This identity-ambient branch does not use a cyclic representation-extension principle.
