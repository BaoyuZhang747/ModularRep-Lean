Purpose

Identifies actual block central sectors with scalar actions on the chosen global and local representations.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

The chosen representation of a Brauer character has scalar central action given by its block sector; a canonically reduced raw weight in sector nu has scalar action nu on the embedded center.

Argument or construction

Primitive central-idempotent support identifies the global sector with the representation's central character. The local raw-weight block compatibility gives the corresponding central character inside the normalizer. Applying each central character scalar law produces the operator identities without assuming a matched-pair scalar equality.

External assumptions and dependencies

Literal block idempotents, invertibility of the center order, the chosen canonical local reduction and its block compatibility.
