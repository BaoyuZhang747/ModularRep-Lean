Purpose

Any intermediate invariant subspace is invariant under the irreducibly acting smaller group, proving irreducibility of restriction.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

Irreducibility on a smaller subgroup implies irreducibility of restriction to every intermediate subgroup. Ambient representation operators intertwine the conjugation twists on a normal intermediate subgroup.

Argument or construction

Any intermediate invariant subspace is invariant under the irreducibly acting smaller group, proving irreducibility of restriction. Operators for ambient conjugation give an explicit intertwiner and equality of the restricted trace functions.

External assumptions and dependencies

An ambient representation whose restriction to the specified smaller group is irreducible. Intermediate normality is needed for the conjugation action and trace-fixedness statements.
