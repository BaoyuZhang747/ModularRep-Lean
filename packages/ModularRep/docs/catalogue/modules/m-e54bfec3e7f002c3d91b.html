carrier-constructioncurrent

Purpose

Constructs conformal quotient-linear tensor and field actions.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

Linear Brauer characters trivial on the normal subgroup are identified with quotient characters, and compatible ordinary lifts define the combined action.

Argument or construction

The quotient equivalence intertwines field actions. Separate series and block stability laws restrict the action to the literal block stabilizer.

External assumptions and dependencies

Ordinary lift existence and root-compatible field reduction, together with source series and block stability.
