Mathematical statement and assumptions

An equivariant basic/Brauer equivalence obtained from the stated Conlon inputs, composed with independent generic and Alperin weight correspondences, gives an equivariant Brauer/Alperin equivalence.

Argument or construction

Apply Conlon to the equivariant integral basic-set map, invert the resulting basic/Brauer equivalence and compose with the supplied generic-to-Alperin equivalences. Each component's covariance proves equivariance of the composite.

External assumptions and dependencies

The supplied basic-set linear equivalence and equivariance, two-hypoelementarity and Conlon/Burnside sources, plus the independent generic-weight and Alperin-weight equivariant equivalences.
