transport-adapterauxiliary

Purpose

Transports the complete raw pair to any representative of its weight class.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

Equal weight classes supply an inner conjugator of the subgroup and ordinary character together; precomposing an ambient embedding preserves its image and the embedded radical.

Argument or construction

Extract the conjugator successively from the conjugacy and isomorphism quotients. The induced normalizer equivalence gives the ordinary character value equation. Functoriality of subgroup images preserves the embedded radical, and inner invariance preserves global Brauer values.

External assumptions and dependencies

Equality of the actual weight classes and the chosen ambient embedding.
