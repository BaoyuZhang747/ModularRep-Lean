Propagates Li's raw stabilizer formula by actual whole-pair conjugation.

G-conjugacy coverage of standard radicals and the formula for every own defect-zero character give the formula for all actual weights.

Argument or construction

Conjugation by G preserves M and GE. Transport of the own character therefore removes the conjugated-field-group notation and supplies the fixed-factor statement.
