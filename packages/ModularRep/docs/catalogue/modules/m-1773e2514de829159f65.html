Purpose

Records degrees for the order-three normalizer quotient.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

The listed O8+(3).3.2 degrees have no degree divisible by the full quotient 3-part, namely 3 to the thirteenth power.

Argument or construction

The file computes the quotient order's 3-part and the degree nondivisibility. Actual quotient identification and complete degree coverage are supplied separately.

External assumptions and dependencies

The literal data and closed identities described in this entry. Actual group, ordinary-character, block or coefficient identification is a separate binding and is not proved by this data module.
