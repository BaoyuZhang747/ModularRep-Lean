Purpose

Constructs a common Q=1 extension from one retained global projective model.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

A factor-one model affording the global base character, compatible roots and the Q=1 base identity yield global and local extensions with literal restriction equality.

Argument or construction

Build the global extension from the model. Transport it through the identification of the local ambient with the whole ambient; the commuting base square gives its required restriction. A root seed can first be adjusted to agree on the finite subgroup roots needed by the representation.

External assumptions and dependencies

The retained factor-one global model, its irreducibility and affording identity, the Q=1 base equation and an ambient root seed or compatible root.
