ModularRep.PrimitiveBlockAutomorphism

ModularRep/PrimitiveBlockAutomorphism.lean

transport-adaptercurrent

Purpose

An automorphism of a finite group acts on its modular group algebra by transporting the group basis.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

Group automorphisms act on literal primitive central block idempotents, and inner automorphisms act trivially.

Argument or construction

Map a primitive central idempotent through the group algebra automorphism. Primitivity and centrality are preserved; conjugation invariance of its coefficients makes the inner action trivial.

External assumptions and dependencies

An actual primitive central idempotent and group automorphism. Inner fixedness follows from coefficient conjugacy invariance.
