Purpose

Derives containment of the normal p-core in a supplied local defect representative.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

For the actual local block-idempotent decomposition, every Navarro defect representative satisfying its support characterization contains the p-core.

Argument or construction

The central Brauer restriction of a nonzero primitive idempotent at a normal p-subgroup is nonzero. The supplied support characterization makes the p-core subconjugate to the defect representative. Normality removes the conjugation and yields containment.

External assumptions and dependencies

Navarro 4.11 support characterization on the supplied defect representative; the preceding normal-p central-support theorem.
