ModularRep.SubgroupIntervalTwo

ModularRep/SubgroupIntervalTwo.lean

theorem-developmentcurrent

Purpose

If a normal subgroup has quotient of order at most two, there is no proper intermediate subgroup.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

If a finite quotient by a normal subgroup has order at most two, every intermediate subgroup is either the normal subgroup or the whole group.

Argument or construction

Use the normal-subgroup quotient correspondence. A finite quotient of order at most two has only trivial and whole subgroups, so an intermediate subgroup equals the base or the whole group.

External assumptions and dependencies

A finite group, a normal subgroup with quotient cardinality at most two and an intermediate subgroup containing that normal subgroup.
