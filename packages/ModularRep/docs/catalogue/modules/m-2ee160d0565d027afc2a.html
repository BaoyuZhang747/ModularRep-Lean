ModularRep.PaperProofs.CharacterWeightRepresentativeFibre

ModularRep/PaperProofs/CharacterWeightRepresentativeFibre.lean

transport-adaptercurrent

Purpose

This module identifies the representative-level defect-zero character set at a fixed radical subgroup with the corresponding block and radical fibre of global character-weight conjugacy classes.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

Ordinary defect-zero local characters at a fixed radical Q are equivalent to the global character-weight conjugacy classes whose radical class is that of Q.

Argument or construction

Identify local defect-zero characters with raw weight isomorphism classes at a fixed radical. The orbit-fibre equivalence then identifies them with global weight classes whose radical lies in that conjugacy class.

External assumptions and dependencies

p prime, an actual radical subgroup and the function-valued set of local ordinary characters of defect zero; the established weight isomorphism/conjugacy and orbit-fibre constructions are prior results.
