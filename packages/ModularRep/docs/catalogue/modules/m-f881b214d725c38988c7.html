Purpose

Constructs the literal raw product weight from factor weights.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

Given a tuple of raw weights, constructs a raw weight with the actual product radical and product ordinary character on its normalizer quotient and proves the required defect-zero condition.

Argument or construction

Radical and defect-zero facts transfer through the quotient-coordinate equivalence. Enough roots descend from the ambient product by order divisibility, giving exact values on normalizer lifts.

External assumptions and dependencies
