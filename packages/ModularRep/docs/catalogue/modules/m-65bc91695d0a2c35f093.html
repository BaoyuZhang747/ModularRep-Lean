theorem-developmentauxiliary

Purpose

Constructs the upper principal correspondence by matching one- and two-element cover fibres.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

Independent specified character and weight counts determine compatible strata in the actual upper fibres.

Argument or construction

Double fibres are matched first and their complements second. The constructed equivalence is then shown compatible with field and quotient-linear actions, with equal matched inertias.

External assumptions and dependencies

Principal covering laws, actual character/weight counts, field covariance and quotient-linear source laws; no downstairs matching.
