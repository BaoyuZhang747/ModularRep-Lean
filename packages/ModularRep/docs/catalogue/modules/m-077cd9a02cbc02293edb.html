Purpose

The one-way transport in BrauerCharacterEquivTransport is an equivalence.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

Transport of IBr along a group equivalence is itself an equivalence and commutes with the corresponding conjugated opposite-automorphism actions.

Argument or construction

Construct inverse character transports along a group equivalence and its inverse. The root transport and group automorphism conjugation square prove that this equivalence intertwines the opposite automorphism actions.

External assumptions and dependencies

A finite group equivalence and the prescribed root embedding transported along it. The automorphism actions are conjugated by that same equivalence.
