Purpose

Identifies representative block/radical fibres across a central p-prime quotient.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

For a trivial-sector block, ordinary defect-zero rows and weight fibres at Q are equivalent to those at its literal quotient image and quotient block.

Argument or construction

Compute the quotient block from the specified image of its primitive idempotent. Derive constancy on the local quotient kernel and both directions of block membership. Restrict ordinary descent/inflation to this block fibre and compose with the local-character/weight-fibre equivalences.

External assumptions and dependencies

Canonical reductions and compatibility upstairs/downstairs, actual quotient block operations, and global/local central prime-to-p primitive-image sources.
