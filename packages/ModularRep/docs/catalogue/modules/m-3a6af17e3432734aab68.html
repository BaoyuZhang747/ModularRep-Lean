theorem-developmentcurrent

Purpose

This file reflects a zero determinant from characteristic zero to characteristic p.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

For a finite matrix whose entries are sums of matched finite roots, a nonzero determinant in k implies a nonzero determinant after characteristic-zero lifting of the roots.

Argument or construction

Represent a root-sum matrix over the integral cyclotomic quotient. Injectivity of characteristic-zero evaluation shows that zero lifted determinant would imply zero modular determinant; contraposition transfers nonvanishing.

External assumptions and dependencies

Fields k and K, characteristic zero for K, positive root order, a finite multiplicative root equivalence, primitive root and the nominated nonzero modular determinant.
