Purpose

The quotient character is the reduction of the given ordinary weight's own local character.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

The original pair, its embedded semidirect carriers, and the prescribed splitting-root data are fixed before any triple witness is transported.

Argument or construction

The pair representative construction applies the existing splitting implication on these carriers. Its coordinate equations keep the selected Brauer character and raw weight tied to their original representatives.
