Purpose

Separates the torus and local-character deductions in an abstract pair model.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

A characteristic torus attached to a fixed Levi and a fixed local character yield fixation of the pair's orbit.

Argument or construction

The torus equality is derived from naturality. The Clifford--Gallagher endgame supplies the second equality before the inner-orbit argument is applied.

External assumptions and dependencies

Characteristic-torus and local character source data; the actual algebraic dependent-pair route is developed separately.
