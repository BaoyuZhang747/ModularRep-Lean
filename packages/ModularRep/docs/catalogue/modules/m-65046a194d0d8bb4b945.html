Purpose

The normalizer and normalizer-quotient automorphisms are induced by the same ambient conjugation.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

The normalizer inertia is defined by actual radical normalization and invariance of its ordinary quotient character.

Argument or construction

The canonical reduction of a defect-zero character identifies this with the local Brauer inertia. The identification uses the same normalizer quotient and compatible root convention.

External assumptions and dependencies

The only external inputs used are the previously stated Navarro 3.18 certificate and prime regular quotient lifts. The transported character is invariant under the actual raw inertia's conjugation on its local base, as required for literal triple carriers.
