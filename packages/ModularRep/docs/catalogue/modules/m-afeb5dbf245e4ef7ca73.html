Purpose

Supplies the degree-twelve cyclotomic relation for a primitive thirteenth root.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

The sum 1+w+…+w^12 vanishes when w is primitive of order 13.

Argument or construction

Specialize the geometric-polynomial evaluation theorem at N=q=13. Expand its finite sum to the displayed polynomial.

External assumptions and dependencies

Primitivity of the selected thirteenth root.
