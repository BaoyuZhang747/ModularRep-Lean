carrier-constructionauxiliary

Purpose

Rows are actual finite subsets of the natural numbers. A symbol is the quotient by row interchange and simultaneous beta-set shift.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

A symbol is the quotient of two finite rows of natural numbers by row interchange and simultaneous beta-set shift; rank and absolute defect are functions on this quotient.

Argument or construction

The integer rank formula and row-cardinality defect are invariant under both generating relations. Literal hook/cohook removal preserves odd defect, and transitive closure therefore cannot reach an equal-row symbol.

External assumptions and dependencies

Quot takes their equivalence closure, so all simultaneous iterated shifts and unordered-row identifications hold. Existence and uniqueness of a reachable terminal symbol are not assumed here.
