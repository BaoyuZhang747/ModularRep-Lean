Purpose

Cancel the already understood block fibres from an unblocked global correspondence.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

A global equivalence and total/fixed-point counts for its known complement determine the corresponding counts and a C2-equivariant equivalence on the remaining actual block.

Argument or construction

Subtract the known cardinalities from the global cardinalities, and repeat for fixed points. The finite involution classification converts the two remaining equalities into the required equivalence.
