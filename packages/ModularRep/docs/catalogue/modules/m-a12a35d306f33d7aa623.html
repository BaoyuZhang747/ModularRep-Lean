Purpose

Extract a moved Brauer character from the nonprincipal total and fixed-point census.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

An outer quotient of order two and a nonprincipal Brauer fibre with four elements but two fixed points provide a nontrivial outer action.

Argument or construction

If every character in the fibre were fixed, its fixed-point cardinality would be four. The census therefore gives a moved character, to which the Brauer-witness reduction applies.

External assumptions and dependencies

The finite actual group, characteristic prime, compatible coefficient/root data, specified sets of primitive blocks and characters, and the imported transport results are fixed as in the declaration context. Outer-quotient cardinality, kernel/class-map laws or the stated specified Brauer census are input facts; the named sporadic identification is not derived here.
