Purpose

The subgroup is mathlib's literal subgroup of squares in Fˣ. Its index is computed by the existing power-map index theorem for finite cyclic groups and Nat.card_units.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

The field is finite of odd cardinality, and the quotient is its actual unit group by the subgroup of squares.

Argument or construction

The power-map index formula for a finite cyclic group gives index two. An equivalence with the fixed two-element cyclic group supplies the square-class map and its exact kernel criterion.

External assumptions and dependencies

This formulation uses only [Finite F] and does not select an external enumeration of the field.
