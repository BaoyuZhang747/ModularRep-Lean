Purpose

Constructs compatible positive-radical extensions and all intermediate block equalities by cyclic choice.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

A fixed local extension, initial global extension, base induction and cyclic covering/twist/interval data give new global/local extensions and an IntermediateBlockSource.

Argument or construction

Use the cyclic block-choice theorem to twist the initial global extension into one with the required top block. Retain the local extension and transport the base relation to the new choice. Quotient cardinality at most two makes every intermediate subgroup base or top, so these two relations suffice.

External assumptions and dependencies

Navarro restriction-covering, covering and cyclic-twist principles, exact block catalogues and interval data, base and intersection induction, quotient bound and initial individual extensions. The An–Dietrich character-triple clause is separate.
