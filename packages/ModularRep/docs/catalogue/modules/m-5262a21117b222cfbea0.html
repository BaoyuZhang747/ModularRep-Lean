Purpose

Describe a kernel element of the local quotient map by a central element and the radical subgroup.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

The own local ordinary character of a weight is constant at its degree on the local quotient kernel determined by the kernel of its given global central character.

Argument or construction

Describe a kernel element of the local quotient map by a central element and the radical subgroup. The global central character formula is trivial on the specified central kernel, so the local character equals its degree there.

External assumptions and dependencies

The given global central character, its central-kernel subgroup identity and the local character's lies-over formula. The local quotient kernel calculation is a prior internal result.
