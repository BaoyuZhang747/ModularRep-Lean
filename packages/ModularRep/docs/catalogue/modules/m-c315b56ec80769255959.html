Purpose

This file isolates the deduction in condition (ii) of the Brough--Späth criterion used in Proposition 3.14.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

Representative stabilizer-equivariant Brauer/label maps combine over all block orbits; composition with the supplied global label/weight equivalence yields the equivariant block-preserving condition-II matching.

Argument or construction

Transport each stabilizer-equivariant Brauer/label equivalence along its block orbit, then combine the orbits. Compose the resulting same-label map with the supplied equivariant label/weight equivalence to obtain the global condition-II bijection.

External assumptions and dependencies

Stabilizer-equivariant equivalences at representative block fibres and a supplied equivariant block-preserving label/weight equivalence. The construction from orbit representatives itself needs no extra Conlon theorem once those local maps exist.
