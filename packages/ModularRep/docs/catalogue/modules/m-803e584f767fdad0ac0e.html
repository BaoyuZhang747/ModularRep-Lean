Purpose

Builds the character and weight extension selectors used by the fixed-block criterion.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

For fixed principal character and weight classes with the supplied index-two and inertia data, constructs extensions for every required conjugate constituent and each selected raw weight in its own inertia group.

Argument or construction

Index two makes the overgroup quotient cyclic and field fixedness factors the character stabilizer. Cyclic extension is applied separately to every conjugate and to the actual raw inertia quotient; fixation of a weight class is not promoted to raw-representative fixation.

External assumptions and dependencies

Uniform modular cyclic-extension theorem, ordinary cyclic-extension theorem with sufficient roots, actual covering and field fixedness.
