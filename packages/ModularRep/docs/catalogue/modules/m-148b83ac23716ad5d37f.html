Purpose

This file instantiates the actual tensor and field actions occurring in Lemma 4.2 for an ambient finite group M with a normal subgroup G0.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

In the manuscript these are the finite special Clifford group and its Spin derived subgroup. The tensor factor is the literal group of modular linear characters of M trivial on G0; the field factor acts by pullback.

Argument or construction

In the manuscript these are the finite special Clifford group and its Spin derived subgroup. The tensor factor is the literal group of modular linear characters of M trivial on G0; the field factor acts by pullback. Lean constructs the combined actions on function-valued Irr, function-valued IBr, and both exact Grothendieck groups.

External assumptions and dependencies

The cited basic-set equivalence is used only to select the labels; equivariance is not an input. The tensor/field groups, character actions and exact K0 constructions remain useful with their displayed field hypotheses. The ordinarySeriesBasicSet/labelledKZeroActionData modular-system composition is superseded by TypeBSpecialCliffordActionSplitting. The superseded applications combine ordinary algebraic closure with the same nontrivial DVR fraction field; retaining their formal conditional declarations does not supply a usable modular system.
