ModularRep.EvaluationMatrix

ModularRep/EvaluationMatrix.lean

theorem-developmentcurrent

Purpose

A finite linearly independent family of scalar-valued functions admits a choice of evaluation points for which the square evaluation matrix has nonzero determinant.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

A finite linearly independent family of field-valued functions admits a tuple of evaluation points giving a square matrix with nonzero determinant.

Argument or construction

Independent functions span enough evaluation vectors to select a square invertible evaluation matrix. Column independence is equivalent to nonzero determinant over the field.

External assumptions and dependencies

A field and a finite linearly independent family of functions on an arbitrary evaluation set.
