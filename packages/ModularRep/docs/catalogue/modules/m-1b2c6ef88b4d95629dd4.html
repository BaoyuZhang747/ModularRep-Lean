carrier-constructionauxiliary

Purpose

Isolates the square-one automorphism carrier without block data.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

A chosen opposite automorphism whose square is one defines a cyclic subgroup of cardinality at most two and the corresponding semidirect product.

Argument or construction

Convert the opposite subgroup to ordinary automorphisms by inverse-unop, and check that inverseOpHom recovers its inclusion. The order bound follows from the square equation and the cardinality of the generated cyclic subgroup.

External assumptions and dependencies

Only the chosen automorphism and its square relation. The identity is permitted, so nontrivial outer class and full automorphism coverage require separate inputs.
