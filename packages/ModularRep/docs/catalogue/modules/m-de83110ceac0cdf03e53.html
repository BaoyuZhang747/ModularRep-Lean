Purpose

Passes the specified GGGR basis from characteristic-zero linear algebra to the rational PIM span.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

Given the specified K-basis, natural PIM expansions and the class/dimension equality, the original projected GGGR functions form a Q-basis of the rational PIM span.

Argument or construction

Natural PIM expansions prove membership and the specified basis proves independence. Equality of class cardinality and rational dimension then constructs a basis whose vectors are the original projections.

External assumptions and dependencies

The specified GGGR source, literal projection identification and the two PIM dimension formulas.
