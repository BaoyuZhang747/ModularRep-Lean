Purpose

Proves intermediate block conditions for retained factor-one models by explicit sign choice.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

At quotient order at most two, a matched base induction and unselected top/interval catalogues give the complete model-pair block output with a quotient-character twist.

Argument or construction

Keep the original local model and derive its covering law from literal restriction. Choose the global extension in the induced top block using the concrete signed-Brauer theorem. Combine base and top conditions over the whole intermediate interval.

External assumptions and dependencies

Actual model restrictions, base induction, root seed and block/interval catalogues; no separate covering-extension or Navarro 8.20 source is needed.
