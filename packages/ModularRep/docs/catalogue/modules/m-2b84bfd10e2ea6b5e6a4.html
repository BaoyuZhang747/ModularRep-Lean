Purpose

Reflects local ordinary fixedness across a quotient square.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

A commuting ambient automorphism square gives stable image radicals and a commuting qW square; a surjective factorization reflects ordinary character fixedness.

Argument or construction

The proof checks the normalizer quotient formula on representatives. Pullback of character equality proves one direction, and surjectivity proves the converse.

External assumptions and dependencies

A normal quotient subgroup, commuting ambient automorphism square and stable radical subgroup. Fixedness reflection uses only a surjective group homomorphism, a commuting local automorphism square, and the ordinary character factorization over a characteristic-zero field.
