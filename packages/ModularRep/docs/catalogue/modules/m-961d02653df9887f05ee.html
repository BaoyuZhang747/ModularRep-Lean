Purpose

Derives Brauer support from actual normalizer induction.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

A local Brauer block inducing from the normalizer of a p-subgroup gives nonzero Brauer support of the induced ambient block at that subgroup.

Argument or construction

The induced central element acts as identity on an actual simple local representation. If its Brauer restriction vanished, the normal-p kernel calculation would make that action zero, a contradiction.

External assumptions and dependencies

Actual local/ambient block decompositions and central character catalogues, a p-subgroup, an actual local Brauer character and the specified block-induction relation. The normal-p action theorem used for nonzero support is proved internally in the imported development.
