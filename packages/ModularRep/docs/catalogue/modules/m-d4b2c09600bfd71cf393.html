Purpose

Constructs the fixed-point product for the original monomial Frobenius cycles, with the explicit inverse Frobenius-diagonal formula.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

The geometric factors, their original monomial maps, and the actual point automorphism are prescribed. Successor normalization and the full-return identity are the checked Lemma 4.4 group constructions.

Argument or construction

Successor normalization and the full-return identity are the checked Lemma 4.4 group constructions. The first coordinate is fixed by the literal full return.

External assumptions and dependencies

The inverse fixed-product equivalence therefore uses toBase.symm at each position. The return used in the factor carrier is the actual full power of the same geometric point automorphism, not a separately supplied map.
