Purpose

Proves that nontrivial central characters of a prime-order group are faithful.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

A homomorphism from a group of prime cardinality is trivial or injective, and injectivity is equivalent to being nontrivial; the trivial sector is fixed by every automorphism.

Argument or construction

Its kernel is bottom or top by the prime-order subgroup dichotomy. An injective trivial homomorphism would make the domain a singleton, contradicting prime cardinality. The action on the trivial character is checked pointwise.

External assumptions and dependencies

Elementary prime-order subgroup facts; no character classification input.
