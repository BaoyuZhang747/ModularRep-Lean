Purpose

Relates raw normalizer inertia to the inertia of its corresponding weight class.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

The statements hold for every raw representative in the actual semidirect ambient.

Argument or construction

Raw stabilization implies stabilization of its class. Conversely, an inner correction of a class stabilizer produces raw stabilization, so multiplication by the embedded base recovers the whole class inertia.

External assumptions and dependencies

The actual character-weight class action and its inner-conjugacy interpretation.
