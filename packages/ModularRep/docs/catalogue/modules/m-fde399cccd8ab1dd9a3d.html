Purpose

Derives the seven local modulus equations from one retained primitive conductor root.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

The powers of a primitive 10015005-th root associated with conductors 1,5,13,21,23,29,33 annihilate the literal tagged moduli.

Argument or construction

Split the seven conductor tags. Each positive conductor divides 10015005, so the selected power is primitive of that conductor. Apply the corresponding short cyclotomic relation and normalize the polynomial evaluation.

External assumptions and dependencies

Primitivity of the conductor root and the proved local cyclotomic relations.
