Purpose

This file contains only the visible wrapper pairing the published Navarro (4.11) support criterion with separately supplied p-group evidence for a nominated literal defect representative, together with its local and ambient normalizer-carrier abbreviations.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

A defect representative consists of a p-subgroup and the Navarro 4.11 nonzero-support characterization for the nominated literal block; this yields the local and ambient predicates used by the adapters.

Argument or construction

Bundle a p-subgroup and the Navarro support characterization as a defect representative. Define the corresponding local and ambient defect predicates for later adapters using literal primitive decompositions.

External assumptions and dependencies

The Navarro 4.11 support source and p-group condition bundled for the nominated subgroup and literal block; this defines the exact defect predicates for the later adapters.
