Purpose

Specified inputs for the B2 and B3 branches of the nine-block construction.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

B2 and B3 source records are indexed first by their specified upstairs blocks and later by the already constructed dominated downstairs blocks.

Argument or construction

The first records supply inputs needed for central descent. The later records retain its actual primitive image and order-two or dihedral defect data, so no arbitrary downstairs block can be substituted.

External assumptions and dependencies

The first records contain the inputs used before central descent. The specified B3 inputs before its dominated block is constructed. The B2 sources on the constructed block and the displayed specified system.
