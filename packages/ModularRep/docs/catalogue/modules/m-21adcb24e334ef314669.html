Purpose

Controls subgroup orders inside an order-nine defect.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

A p-subgroup subconjugate to an order-nine 3-group has order one, three or nine; equal-cardinality subconjugacy gives equality of radical conjugacy classes.

Argument or construction

Subgroup cardinal divisibility gives the three possible powers. A contained conjugate with equal finite order is the whole subgroup, so the quotient class equality follows.

External assumptions and dependencies

Finite p-subgroups with the specified order-nine/subconjugacy or equal-cardinality hypotheses. No block role, ordinary character, root embedding or weight-count source is used.
