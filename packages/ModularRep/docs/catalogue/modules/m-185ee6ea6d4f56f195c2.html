Purpose

Produces the ordinary local lift over the same full-centre character.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

The inflated ordinary normalizer character lies over every ordinary central character over which the matched global Brauer character lies.

Argument or construction

The modular Schur scalars coincide by induction. Compatibility of retained root lifts on the actual central eigenvalues converts this into the ordinary scalar identity; ordinary inflation supplies the required lift.

External assumptions and dependencies

The same problem, original Brauer character, raw weight, canonical local reduction, block compatibility, common block equation and prime-to-p centre order. The supplied ordinary central character is tested by its actual global lying-over equation.
