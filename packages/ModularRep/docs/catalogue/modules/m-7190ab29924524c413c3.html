carrier-constructioncurrent

Purpose

Constructs the two actual Brough extension lanes.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

The field lane and conformal lane have PSp as outer kernel; their raw inertia quotients have the own normalizer quotient as kernel and cyclic outer image.

Argument or construction

The actual raw action proves normality of the selected radical. Kernel and quotient calculations provide the groups to which extension theorems apply.

External assumptions and dependencies

The fixed conformal/projective group structure and its cyclic outer quotients.
