Moves the original local reduction and chosen extensions to reference coordinates.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

The stored own reduction and exact quotient reduction have equal inflated values, while the original global and local extensions pass through computed ambient maps.

Argument or construction

The two ordinary reduction equations prove the local value identity. Group-coordinate squares transport extension restrictions, without inferring independent-root compatibility.
