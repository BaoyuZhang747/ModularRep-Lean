Purpose

Constructs the own-quotient packet while deriving specified block images and induction.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

For a matched raw weight whose selected character stabilizer is inner, quotient catalogues and generic interval/image laws give OriginalActualWeightPacket; faithful-center variants derive the innerness premise.

Argument or construction

Use the identity own-quotient ambient. Transport the original block induction through the two specified quotient images and build the top block data. Faithfulness together with an outer automorphism moving the center excludes the outer stabilizer branch.

External assumptions and dependencies

Canonical reduction and compatibility, raw block match, quotient catalogues and primitive-image/interval laws, normalized correspondence and coefficient field.
