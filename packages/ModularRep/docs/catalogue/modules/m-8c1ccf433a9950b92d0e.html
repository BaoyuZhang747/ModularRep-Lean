Purpose

Defines neutral actual three-block fibres and their restricted involutions.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

A role-labelled complete index census and actual stable blocks give Fi24ThreeBlockSource; block transport restricts the selected outer action to each Brauer and weight fibre.

Argument or construction

The complete decomposition's primitive-block equivalence transports index distinction and coverage. Restrict the global actions to stable fibres and apply the square-one relation to prove involutivity. Fi24ThreeKnownEquivalences records only the two independently known nonprincipal and defect-zero maps.

External assumptions and dependencies

Complete actual block decomposition, role census, selected stability and raw-operation transport. This interface does not construct either known map or any principal correspondence.
