Purpose

Proves the centreless comparison from quotient-order bounds.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

For the same actual matched pair with outer order two, global and local associated models have trivial factor sets matching under the natural quotient equivalence.

Argument or construction

The actual quotient cardinalities bound both extension problems by two. Extend the invariant global and local realizations explicitly, then use their factor-one models and the constant-one coboundary.

External assumptions and dependencies

The centreless original group, actual root and equivariant raw match, its canonical local reduction and outer quotient cardinality two. Global/local quotient bounds and representation extensions are derived, without a cyclic-extension source premise.
