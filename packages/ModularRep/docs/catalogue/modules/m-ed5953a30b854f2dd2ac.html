ModularRep.ExactCharacterBridge

ModularRep/ExactCharacterBridge.lean

transport-adaptercurrent

Purpose

Suppose ordinary and modular exact Grothendieck groups map to one additive character group, the modular character map is injective, and stable-lattice reduction preserves the relevant character.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

An ExactCharacterBridge with injective modular character homomorphism and uniform stable-lattice compatibility gives the unique exact decomposition homomorphism satisfying its character square.

Argument or construction

Use injectivity of the modular character homomorphism to identify all stable reduction classes and reflect short-exact additivity. Descend the chosen classes to a unique exact decomposition map satisfying the character square.

External assumptions and dependencies

ExactCharacterBridge with an injective modular character homomorphism, ordinary character homomorphism and compatibility for every stable lattice. The concrete Brauer application obtains these from earlier results and the stable-reduction source.
