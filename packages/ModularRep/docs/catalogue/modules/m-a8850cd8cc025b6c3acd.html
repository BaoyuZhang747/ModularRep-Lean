ModularRep.KZeroLinearCharacterTensor

ModularRep/KZeroLinearCharacterTensor.lean

theorem-developmentcurrent

Purpose

This file constructs the operation induced on exact K₀ by tensoring a finite dimensional representation with a one dimensional representation.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

Tensoring by a linear character is an exact autoequivalence and induces an action on exact K-zero, with inverse tensoring by the inverse character.

Argument or construction

Tensoring by a linear character is an exact autoequivalence, inverse to tensoring by its inverse. Its induced K-zero homomorphism gives a genuine group action and the expected ordinary trace formula.

External assumptions and dependencies

A unit-valued linear character and finite representations; exactness of tensoring by this one-dimensional character is proved by its inverse autoequivalence.
