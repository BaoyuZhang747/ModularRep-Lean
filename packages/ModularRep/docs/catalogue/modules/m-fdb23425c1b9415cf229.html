Purpose

Derives a rational diagonal product using one Levi Lang correction.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

For a Frobenius-fixed product of geometric central and Levi factors, the common defect and Lang correction produce rational factors and the required rational product equality.

Argument or construction

Their common Frobenius defect lies in the original Levi. A single Lang solution corrects that defect and yields rational original-group and paired-Levi factors, including the intersection identity.

External assumptions and dependencies

Geometric central decomposition, original Levi intersection and Lang surjectivity on the same connected Levi.
