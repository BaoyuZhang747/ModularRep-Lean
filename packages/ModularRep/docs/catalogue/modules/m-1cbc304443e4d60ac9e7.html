source-interfaceauxiliary

Purpose

Fixed points are equalizers of the specified Frobenius and the identity. The geometric derived subgroup is the actual subgroup commutator, and the paired Levi is the actual product with the geometric ambient centre.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

Exact E1/U descent data for the actual split Clifford polynomial model. Sources: FLZ type B, §2.5 pp. 539--540 and §3.1 p. 541; Geck--Malle, Definition 1.4.1 pp. 40--41, Example 1.4.2 p. 41 and §1.4.5 p. 42.

Argument or construction

The identification of these point subgroups with a chosen algebraic Levi, its geometric derived group and its simple components is an explicit E1/U boundary (FLZ, Jordan decomposition, proof of Proposition 5.6, p. 31; Geck--Malle Definition 1.7.1, pp. 80--81). The fixed-range assertion remains a finite-field descent input; it is not claimed to follow merely from those generator equations. Identifying levi with an algebraic Levi remains U until that construction is attached.
