Purpose

Models algebraic torus labels with characters of their finite normalisers.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

A genuine automorphism action on labels induces transport of dependent ordinary-character pairs and their valid conjugacy classes.

Argument or construction

Normaliser equivalences are derived from the label action. Factoring the inverse inner twist proves that fixation of a pair implies fixation of its class by the original field automorphism.

External assumptions and dependencies

The inner-stable generic-pair predicate and actual sets of characters.
