Purpose

The generic criterion domain is bound to the already constructed Clifford norm kernel, Frobenius semidirect product, natural automorphism map, and literal inertia subgroups.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

The generic criterion is specialized to the prescribed Spin norm kernel inside the special Clifford/field semidirect product.

Argument or construction

Both the Brauer and weight inertias contain the Spin-centre join. The odd-prime index calculation makes their products with the same Hall preimage equal to the full special Clifford group.

External assumptions and dependencies

Both sides of the published J_G compatibility clause equal the whole special Clifford group. The criterion's actual constituent and covered-weight predicates remain visible in its type; the stronger index deduction makes the equality independent of those choices. The stronger, choice-independent form used at the published criterion boundary.
