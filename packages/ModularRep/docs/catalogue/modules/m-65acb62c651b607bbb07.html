ModularRep.CentralCharacterIdempotent

ModularRep/CentralCharacterIdempotent.lean

theorem-developmentcurrent

Purpose

For a finite subgroup Z ≤ G and a multiplicative character nu : Z →* kˣ, this file defines (1 / |Z|) ∑ z : Z, nu(z)⁻¹ z ∈ k[G].

Mathematical statement and assumptions

The normalized linear character weighted sum on a finite invertible-order subgroup is an idempotent; it is central when the subgroup is central.

Argument or construction

Construct the normalized weighted sum of central subgroup elements. Multiplicativity of the linear character gives idempotence, and centrality of the subgroup makes the sum central in the group algebra.

External assumptions and dependencies

A finite subgroup and a unit-valued linear character, with subgroup order invertible in the coefficient ring. Centrality conclusions assume containment in the ambient centre.
