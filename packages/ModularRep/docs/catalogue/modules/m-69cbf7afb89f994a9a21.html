ModularRep.BrauerCharacterExtensionBridge

ModularRep/BrauerCharacterExtensionBridge.lean

transport-adaptercurrent

Purpose

Recover equality of modular traces from invariant Brauer functions and use simple trace separation to obtain representation equivalences.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

A fixed irreducible Brauer character yields conjugation invariance of its affording modular representation. Given the Brauer cyclic extension principle and a cyclic quotient, that representation has an actual extension.

Argument or construction

Recover equality of modular traces from invariant Brauer functions and use simple trace separation to obtain representation equivalences. Apply the explicitly supplied cyclic extension principle to this genuine invariance.

External assumptions and dependencies

The ordinary Isaacs Theorem 11.22 or modular Navarro Theorem 8.12 cyclic extension principle, as selected by the declaration. Actual character realizations, finite compatible roots, invariance and cyclic quotient are required; local applications additionally require their raw quotient and selected-reduction dictionaries.
