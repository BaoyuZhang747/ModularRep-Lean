Purpose

Proves global clauses for blocks whose maps equal the original odd-two map.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

The complete target's existential radical fibres are identified with the original radical-class parts, preserving local equivalences and central values.

Argument or construction

The original map gives global bijectivity and equivariance. The fibre identification transfers radical covariance and ordinary trivial-radical reduction.

External assumptions and dependencies

Block maps already identified with the original map; actual block witnesses and same-extension normalization are constructed elsewhere.
