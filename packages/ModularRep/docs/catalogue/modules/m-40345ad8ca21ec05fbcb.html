Purpose

Constructs the actual descended normalizer Brauer character.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

The same canonical local reduction descends to the quotient normalizer and reduces the constructed quotient ordinary character.

Argument or construction

The local kernel theorem permits Brauer deflation along the surjective normalizer map. The commuting normalizer-quotient square and regular-element lifting establish the new reduction equation.

External assumptions and dependencies

The original raw match, canonical reduction, local block compatibility, common block equation, prime-to-p own central kernel and its fixed-central-quotient normalizer source. The local kernel, descended character and reduction are outputs.
