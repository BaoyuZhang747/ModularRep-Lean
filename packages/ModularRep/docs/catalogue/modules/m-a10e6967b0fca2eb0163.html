Purpose

The Fi24 input constructor at a prime other than three in the cyclic-defect branch supplies a specified case model, an equivalence of its simple quotient with the named base, and all source data needed by the original-cover cyclic application.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

The retained inputs and deductions provide the raw sporadic certificate stated by this module. They do not establish the strengthened complete certificate without the additional compatible-root or packet assumptions used by the current application.

Argument or construction

Preserve the specified groups, correspondence, block operations and source hypotheses in the raw conditional deduction. Complete current application certificates are constructed in ManuscriptIBAW with the additional compatibility assumptions.

External assumptions and dependencies

The finite actual group, characteristic prime, compatible coefficient/root data, specified sets of primitive blocks and characters, and the imported transport results are fixed as in the declaration context. The raw input constructor retains the actual full-cover, named-base equivalence, prime, source theorem, numerical and representation hypotheses of its application; no inhabitant is manufactured for this telescope. The raw conclusion alone is not the full current inductive condition.
