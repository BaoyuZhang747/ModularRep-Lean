Purpose

Keeps principal stabilizers unchanged under conformal translation.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

The actual PCSp commutator lies in the inner PSp image, and principal field fixation makes all ambient actions commute on the fibre.

Argument or construction

Inner actions fix characters, so conformal commutators act trivially. Commuting actions imply equal stabilizers for every translate and preserve containment for the original weight.

External assumptions and dependencies

The fixed group structure and principal field-fixation theorem.
