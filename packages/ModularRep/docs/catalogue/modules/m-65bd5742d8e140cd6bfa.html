Purpose

Bounds actual principal-character inertia quotients by two.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

The SO action factors through the literal SO/Omega quotient because Omega acts by the same inner automorphisms.

Argument or construction

Surjectivity onto the outer quotient gives its cardinal bound. Every character-stabilizer quotient embeds in it, hence also has order at most two and is cyclic.

External assumptions and dependencies

Matrix automorphism realization and the actual index-two inclusion.
