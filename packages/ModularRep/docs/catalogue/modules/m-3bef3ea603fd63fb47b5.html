Purpose

Builds global FM hypotheses by construction from block orbit representatives.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

Complete representative block components combine globally, and equivariance transfers the diagonal/field fixedness condition in Remark 3.5.

Argument or construction

The source-facing carriers are literal Sp matrices, central quotient, primitive blocks and character-weight classes. The construction keeps the coefficient fields and actual automorphisms fixed.
