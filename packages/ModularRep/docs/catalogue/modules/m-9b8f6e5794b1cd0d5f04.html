Purpose

Restricts the same faithful equivalence to scalar carriers or all centerless Brauer characters.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

The restricted maps retain the original matched image and inherit equivariance from the faithful-family map.

Argument or construction

Uniqueness of the central scalar character puts a scalar Brauer character in its correct faithful sector. When the center is trivial every character lifts to the faithful carrier. Injectivity of the underlying-value maps proves the action and original-image identities.

External assumptions and dependencies

The existing faithful equivalence and its equivariance, plus faithful scalar or centerless hypotheses.
