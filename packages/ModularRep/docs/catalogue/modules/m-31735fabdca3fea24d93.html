ModularRep.BrauerCharacterExact

ModularRep/BrauerCharacterExact.lean

theorem-developmentcurrent

Purpose

Brauer characters defined from an explicit prime regular root embedding are additive on invariant subspaces and quotients.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

For a fixed finite root embedding, Brauer characters are additive on an invariant subspace and its quotient, hence on every short exact sequence of finite representations.

Argument or construction

Factor a characteristic polynomial over an invariant subspace and quotient using a block triangular matrix. Identify the kernel and quotient of a short exact sequence, take the union of root multisets and sum their lifts.

External assumptions and dependencies

A finite group, a fixed prime regular root embedding and the characteristic/splitting hypotheses of Brauer values. Exactness of the displayed short complex and characteristic-polynomial factorization supply additivity.
