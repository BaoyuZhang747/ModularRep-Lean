Purpose

Matches the original own local character through an exhaustive enumeration.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

The inverse enumeration selects the defect-zero character of the conjugated original weight, proving equality of the whole raw pair and its principal class.

Argument or construction

Character transport preserves defect zero. Enumeration invertibility gives the character equality, hence the pair and induced block equalities.

External assumptions and dependencies

An actual character enumeration, subgroup conjugacy and ordinary splitting interpretation; numerical counts alone are insufficient.
