Purpose

Checks the printed dihedral and singleton row fibres and their integer witnesses.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

The five designated dihedral rows exhaust printed role two, the two singleton rows exhaust roles three and four, and the displayed rank/action identities hold.

Argument or construction

Finite decision checks prove the row allocations, constant polynomial values and full-permutation invariance. The explicit three-by-three inverse and reconstruction coefficients certify the dihedral row span. A unit entry certifies each singleton row is nonzero.

External assumptions and dependencies

The literal raw row and printed-block arrays; no actual character-table identification.
