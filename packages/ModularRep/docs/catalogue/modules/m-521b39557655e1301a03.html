theorem-developmentcurrent

Purpose

Reduce an invariant sum under a p-group action to the sum over its fixed points in characteristic p.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

For a p-group acting on a finite set and an invariant characteristic-p-valued function, the full sum equals the sum over fixed points.

Argument or construction

Partition the finite set into p-group orbits. Invariance makes each orbit sum its size times one value; all nontrivial orbit sizes vanish in characteristic p, leaving exactly the fixed points.

External assumptions and dependencies

A p-group acting on a finite set, a characteristic-p commutative coefficient semiring and a function invariant under the action.
