Purpose

Prove preservation of ordinary defect zero under inflation along a surjection with coprime kernel order.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

Inflation along a surjection whose kernel has prime-to-p order preserves irreducibility, degree and the defect-zero condition.

Argument or construction

Surjectivity preserves simplicity of the pulled-back representation. The quotient-order formula gives equality of the prime parts of the group orders, so the ordinary degree criterion for defect zero is unchanged.

External assumptions and dependencies

A surjective group homomorphism of finite groups with prime-to-p kernel order and the actual ordinary representation. No additional centrality hypothesis is needed for this inflation result.
