Purpose

Proves the literal full-field stabilizer and honest Spin extension from principal fixation.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

Given full principal Brauer field fixation, proves the actual Clifford-by-field stabilizer is the product of Clifford inertia and the full field group and constructs extension over Spin-by-field.

Argument or construction

The fixed field factor splits the Clifford stabilizer. The Spin kernel of the right projection is identified with the original group, and its invariant irreducible is extended across the cyclic quotient.

External assumptions and dependencies

Actual Frobenius/norm action and Navarro Theorem 8.12 on the algebraically closed modular field.
