theorem-developmentcurrent

Purpose

A normal two-subgroup lies in the two-core, so its cyclic quotient maps onto the quotient by that core.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

A finite group with a normal two-subgroup and cyclic quotient has cyclic quotient by its two-core, as does every subgroup; normal subgroup order at most two suffices.

Argument or construction

A normal two-subgroup lies in the two-core, so its cyclic quotient maps onto the quotient by that core. Apply subgroup inheritance; a normal subgroup of order at most two is a two-group by its order.

External assumptions and dependencies

A finite group with a normal two-subgroup and cyclic quotient; the cardinality-at-most-two specialization proves its two-group condition.
