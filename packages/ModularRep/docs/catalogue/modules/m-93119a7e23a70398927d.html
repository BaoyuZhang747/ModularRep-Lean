ModularRep.PaperProofs.SporadicFi24P3LiteralSpanBindingConstruction

ModularRep/PaperProofs/SporadicFi24P3LiteralSpanBindingConstruction.lean

carrier-constructionauxiliary

Purpose

Transports the actual nonprincipal Brauer basis and outer action through a literal span equality.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

Equality between the six literal regular-function row span and the actual nonprincipal Brauer-function span gives a basis and action on the ordinary row span with the exact Brauer permutation.

Argument or construction

Restrict linear independence of all Brauer functions to the selected block and construct the basis of its span. The actual outer pullback preserves this span and permutes that basis. Transport both structures through the one supplied equality of submodules, preserving evaluations and the basis-action equation.

External assumptions and dependencies

The literal/actual span equality, block fixedness and actual Brauer functions. OrdinarySpanFromDecomposition subsequently derives the span equality from decomposition, complete rows and actual values.
