Purpose

Records the five specified Monster block roles at two.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

The source binds the exact five defect exponents, complete block roles and whole-group Brauer/weight totals, without assuming a principal-fibre count.

Argument or construction

Finite roles give the actual block decomposition and the total supplies finiteness of weights. Both carrier totals are expressed as sums over the same specified block map.

External assumptions and dependencies

Actual characteristic-two Brauer/sets of weights, primitive block operations and the supplied MonsterTwoSource record. Its fields identify exactly five specified block roles, their specified defect exponents and defect subgroups, and the whole-group Brauer/weight totals; no principal-fibre cardinality is assumed.
