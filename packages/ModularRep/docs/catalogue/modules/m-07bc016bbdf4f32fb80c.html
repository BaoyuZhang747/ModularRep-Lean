carrier-constructionauxiliary

Purpose

Carries a cyclotomic conductor root through the original prime regular root embedding.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

A primitive source n-th root with n dividing the prime regular exponent gives a primitive target root equal to the original iota lift of that source root.

Argument or construction

Include the source root into the ambient root group and restrict the ambient equivalence to n-th roots. Preservation of order gives primitivity. The lift definition gives the exact target-root identity.

External assumptions and dependencies

The source primitive root and conductor divisibility; agreement with a printed character-table root remains a specified binding.
