Purpose

This file defines the ordinary induced-character formula for a subgroup of a finite group and proves its naturality under an automorphism stabilising that subgroup.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

Finite character induction commutes with an ambient automorphism preserving the subgroup, and induction of a character fixed by its restricted automorphism is fixed as well.

Argument or construction

Reindex the finite sum defining induction along the ambient automorphism. Stability of the subgroup identifies summation conditions and the restricted automorphism, giving induction covariance and its fixed-character consequence.

External assumptions and dependencies

The finite induction sum, an ambient automorphism preserving the subgroup and the specified restricted-character invariance for the fixedness conclusion.
