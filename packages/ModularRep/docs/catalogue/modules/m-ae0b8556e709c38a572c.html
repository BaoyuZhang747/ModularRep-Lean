Formalisation.IndexedAssembly

packages/formalisation/Formalisation/IndexedAssembly.lean

theorem-developmentcurrent

Purpose

This file formalises the general mechanism for combining bijections indexed by blocks, central character sectors, or any other A-set.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

Equivalences between corresponding fibres of equivariant projections combine to inverse total-space maps; explicit reindexing compatibility makes these maps equivariant and index preserving.

Argument or construction

An equivalence on every fibre and its inverse combine through dependent pairs to inverse maps of total spaces. The supplied reindexing compatibility gives equivariance and exact preservation of the index.

External assumptions and dependencies

The equivariant index projections and a fibre equivalence for each index, with explicit reindexing compatibility under the acting group.
