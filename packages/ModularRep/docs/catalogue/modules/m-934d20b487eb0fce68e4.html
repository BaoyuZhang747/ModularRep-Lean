Purpose

Kernels of actual irreducible representations are determined by their Brauer characters for the same root convention.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

Equality of irreducible Brauer characters computed with the same roots identifies the kernels of their chosen representations.

Argument or construction

Pass from the Brauer values to modular traces and use irreducible-character injectivity to obtain an isomorphism of representations. An intertwining isomorphism preserves the kernel.

External assumptions and dependencies

The same root embedding for both irreducible Brauer characters and the imported irreducible representation/character injectivity result. Equality with values computed at unrelated embeddings is not sufficient.
