Purpose

This module supplies only the group carrier needed in the central-quotient step of the sporadic argument.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

The central quotient induces an actual map on normalizer quotients with the calculated local kernel. The fixed Navarro-Tiep normalizer-image source makes it surjective, and its prime-to-p kernel preserves the p-part of the order.

Argument or construction

Construct the normalizer-quotient map and calculate its actual kernel using the local intersection with the central kernel times Q. The fixed Navarro-Tiep normalizer-image source gives surjectivity, and kernel order gives p-part preservation.

External assumptions and dependencies

A fixed finite central prime-to-p quotient and radical subgroup, together with Navarro-Tiep Lemma 2.3(c) giving radical image and exact normalizer image. The local kernel and quotient-order p-part equality are derived.
