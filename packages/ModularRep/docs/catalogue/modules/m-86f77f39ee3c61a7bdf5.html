Purpose

This file specialises the abstract support theorem to the group algebra module attached to an irreducible representation.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

An irreducible representation has a unique supporting index in a supplied complete central idempotent family, with identity action there and zero action elsewhere.

Argument or construction

Apply unique support of a complete central idempotent family to the group algebra module attached to an irreducible representation. The supported idempotent acts as identity and the others as zero.

External assumptions and dependencies

A complete central idempotent family and an irreducible representation, viewed as a simple group algebra module.
