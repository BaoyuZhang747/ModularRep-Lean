Purpose

Proves Q=1 normalization of the representative local bijection.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

If the global map sends a defect-zero reduction to its trivial weight, the local map at Q=1 returns its ordinary character, which transports back to the original global character.

Argument or construction

Use injectivity of the local-character/weight-fibre equivalence and the global normalization to identify the local image. The normalizer quotient equivalence and its inverse then cancel.

External assumptions and dependencies

Defect-zero reduction, trivial-weight source and normalization of this same global map.
