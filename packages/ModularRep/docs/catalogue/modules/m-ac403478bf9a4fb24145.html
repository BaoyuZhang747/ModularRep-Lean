Purpose

Späth, *A reduction theorem for the blockwise Alperin weight conjecture*, J. Group Theory 16 (2013), 159--220, Theorem C and its proof on pp. 215--217, gives the complete inductive condition in defining characteristic.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

The literal Spin projection to matrix Omega is supplied with universal prime-to-p maximality, structural simplicity and specified coefficient/block data in defining characteristic.

Argument or construction

The centre order two is prime to the odd defining prime. Apply Spath Theorem C on that actual cover to construct the fixed complete primitive-block family; the source scope includes rank-three field-three at p=3 without asserting Spin is the full sixfold cover.

External assumptions and dependencies

The proof specifies the universal prime-to-p cover and observes that the exceptional Schur multiplier part is a p-group. Unlike GenericSpinCoverSource, this contract includes the exceptional (n,q)=(3,3) in characteristic three. Its maximality is the prime-to-p universal property, not the full-cover property that fails for Spin7(3).
