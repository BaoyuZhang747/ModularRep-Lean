Purpose

Certifies sparse cyclotomic identities using torsion and short geometric sums.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

A rational polynomial expressed as a combination of X^N−1 and geometric sums for divisors q>1 of N vanishes at every primitive N-th root.

Argument or construction

Evaluate through the rational polynomial homomorphism. The torsion term vanishes by the root equation, and each geometric sum vanishes at the induced primitive q-th root. Apply this to differences to obtain equalities of sparse scalar evaluations.

External assumptions and dependencies

The explicit polynomial combination identity and divisor conditions; the witness coefficients alone do not prove the identity.
