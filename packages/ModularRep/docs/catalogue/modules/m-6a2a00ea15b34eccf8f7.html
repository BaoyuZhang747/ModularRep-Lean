Purpose

This file supplies the two scalar-change constructions needed for integral lattices and modular reduction: restriction along an algebra map and tensor extension along an algebra map.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

Restriction and tensor extension of scalars construct representations over the new coefficient rings, with the tensor action operating on the original representation factor.

Argument or construction

Restrict scalars along an algebra map and extend scalars by tensor product. The group action on a pure tensor acts only on the representation factor, giving the displayed formula.

External assumptions and dependencies

The stated commutative coefficient rings, algebra structures and scalar-tower/module instances for restriction and tensor extension.
