Purpose

Specializes the historical prime-two Clifford stabilizer proof to the literal rational Levi chain and its prescribed field actor.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

Lean constructs the conjugation actions on the actual function-valued Brauer characters and restricts the supplied field automorphisms to all three relevant groups. The literal group of Gallagher twists is the group of modular linear characters of the inertia group that are trivial on the copy of N inside it.

Argument or construction

External assumptions and dependencies
