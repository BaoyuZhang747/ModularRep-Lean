Purpose

Identifies the full original normalizer action when the outer quotient is trivial.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

The normalizer realizes precisely the radical-stabilizing, Brauer-fixing and centre-fixing automorphisms, without a faithful-sector or centre-size assumption.

Argument or construction

Every automorphism in the relevant stabilizer is inner by the outer-order-one hypothesis. The subgroup-stability equation then puts its conjugating element in the actual normalizer.

External assumptions and dependencies

The actual Brauer character, raw subgroup, and cardinality one of the original group's literal outer quotient. No centre-size, faithful-sector, canonical reduction or block-catalogue assumption is required.
