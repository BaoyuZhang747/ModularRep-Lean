Purpose

Retains the historical odd-prime complete criterion wrapper.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

It constructs the criterion's five clauses on actual matrices, but its coefficient conjunction is superseded by the current finite-splitting application.

Argument or construction

The original constituent, correspondence, extension, Hall and normalizer constructions are combined before invoking the certificate. This wrapper is not presented as an inhabited alternative.

External assumptions and dependencies

Historical modular-system inputs requiring an algebraically closed ordinary fraction field; use TypeCCurrentOddPrimeApplication.
