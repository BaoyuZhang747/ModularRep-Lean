Purpose

Derives a local kernel from ambient trivial-central block support.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

A normalizer block inducing to a block in the trivial sector of a prime-to-p central subgroup has that subgroup in every affording local representation kernel.

Argument or construction

Coefficient restriction preserves the central-idempotent support through induction. The local primitive sector must therefore be trivial, giving the kernel statement for the canonical reduction without choosing a global character.

External assumptions and dependencies

For the general theorem: actual local/ambient primitive decompositions and central character catalogues, a central subgroup of invertible order contained in the local group, an inducing local block, an irreducible local representation supported on it, and trivial-sector support upstairs. The weight specialization derives the inducing block from canonical local compatibility.
