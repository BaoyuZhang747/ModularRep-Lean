Purpose

Derives complete support on the retained order-nine radical.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

For a role-one raw weight, order-nine defect support, a restricted order-three subgroup classification and actual quotient degree coverage force its radical class to be the retained one.

Argument or construction

The subgroup order is one, three or nine. Bottom weights lie in role two, degree coverage excludes the order-three class, and equal-cardinality subconjugacy identifies the remaining class.

External assumptions and dependencies

Role-one support below the order-nine defect, canonical bottom-weight allocation, a classification only of the indicated order-three subgroups, and complete actual order-three normalizer-quotient degree coverage. No final correspondence or full radical support is input.
