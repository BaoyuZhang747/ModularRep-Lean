Purpose

The canonical copy of the normal factor in a semidirect-product stabiliser is preserved by conjugation.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

Under the canonical embedded normal-factor equivalence, conjugation in the actual semidirect stabilizer is exactly the corresponding ambient automorphism.

Argument or construction

Conjugate the canonical embedded normal-factor element inside the semidirect stabilizer. The multiplication formula identifies this with the actual induced ambient automorphism, proving the commutative conjugation square.

External assumptions and dependencies

The compatible semidirect action, a point fixed by the normal factor and the canonical embedded normal-factor equivalence in its actual stabilizer.
