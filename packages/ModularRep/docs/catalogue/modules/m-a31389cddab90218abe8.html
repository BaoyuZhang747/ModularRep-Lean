ModularRep.BlockCentralCharacters

ModularRep/BlockCentralCharacters.lean

source-interfacecurrent

Purpose

For a finite group over an algebraically closed field, Navarro's Theorem (3.11) identifies the blocks with the algebra homomorphisms from the centre of the group algebra to the coefficient field.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

A supplied Navarro central character catalogue on a finite primitive block family has injective block characters and gives a unique block for every algebra homomorphism from the group algebra centre to k.

Argument or construction

Evaluate equal block central characters on a block idempotent. The delta laws force equality of block indices, and the supplied exhaustivity law then gives a unique block for any central algebra homomorphism.

External assumptions and dependencies

A fixed complete primitive block decomposition over an algebraically closed field and the Navarro Theorem 3.11 central character catalogue (delta values and exhaustivity). Block induction additionally needs multiplicativity of its induced central function.
