Purpose

Full AD(3) on the same ordinary family, enriched without new witnesses.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

The centre-two ordinary family on its actual extension can be completed with all An–Dietrich geometric clauses while keeping the same comparison models.

Argument or construction

Combine the extension's normalizer-action range and factorization equations with the retained ordinary output. The construction applies to the original possibly nonsplit extension.

External assumptions and dependencies

The finite actual group, characteristic prime, compatible coefficient/root data, specified sets of primitive blocks and characters, and the imported transport results are fixed as in the declaration context. The initial sector correspondence and its stabilizer, block and pair laws are supplied. Full extension and intermediate-block conclusions additionally use their stated certification or Späth inputs.
