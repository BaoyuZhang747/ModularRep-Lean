Purpose

This file formalises the pairwise trace-separation argument underlying Navarro, *Characters and Blocks of Finite Groups*, Theorem (1.19), printed pp. 12--13.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

Over an algebraically closed field, equality of trace characters of irreducible finite representations of a finite group implies representation equivalence.

Argument or construction

Use Schur's lemma and density to separate nonisomorphic simple modules by an algebra element with rank-one trace on one module and zero on the other. Equality of trace functions therefore gives an intertwining isomorphism.

External assumptions and dependencies

Algebraically closed coefficient field and irreducible finite dimensional representations of a finite group. The Schur and density facts used from Mathlib are explicit proof dependencies.
