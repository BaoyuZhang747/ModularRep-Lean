ModularRep.CyclotomicRootSumReflection

ModularRep/CyclotomicRootSumReflection.lean

theorem-developmentcurrent

Purpose

Reflect equality of characteristic-zero sums of finite roots of unity to equality of their modular sums.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

For coprime finite root order and the specified multiplicative root equivalence, equality of two lifted root-multiset sums in characteristic zero implies equality of the original sums in characteristic p.

Argument or construction

Express finite root multisets as polynomials in a primitive root. Equality in characteristic zero forces their difference into the cyclotomic ideal, whose modular evaluation vanishes as well.

External assumptions and dependencies

Fields k and K with characteristic p and zero respectively, a coprime finite root order and its multiplicative root correspondence. The algebraically closed specialization supplies a primitive modular root.
