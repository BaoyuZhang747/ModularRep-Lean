Purpose

This module formalises the representation-free deductions used after the algebraic-group input in the paired-Levi reduction.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

Coordinatewise product orbits are products of coordinate orbits. The supplied diagonal/action identification and local factorization give correcting elements and the return-fixed component construction.

Argument or construction

For coordinatewise product actions, choose a transporter independently in each coordinate to identify the product orbit. A correcting diagonal element and local factorization force the cyclic return to fix the representative.

External assumptions and dependencies

Product-coordinate actions and the supplied comparison with the relevant diagonal action, together with orbit membership and local stabilizer factorization for the return correction.
