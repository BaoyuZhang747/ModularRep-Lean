theorem-developmentauxiliary

Purpose

Prove independence and equivariance of sector transport from one stabilizer-equivariant fibre map.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

A transitive sector action and a stabilizer-equivariant initial bijection determine a global equivariant bijection; a character-triple comparison additionally needs its own factor-set source and identification.

Argument or construction

The transporter formula cancels changes of choice in the initial stabilizer. Factor-set trivializations and source identification are separate inputs to the later pairwise comparison, rather than consequences of cardinality or transitivity.

External assumptions and dependencies

The finite actual group, characteristic prime, compatible coefficient/root data, specified sets of primitive blocks and characters, and the imported transport results are fixed as in the declaration context. The initial sector correspondence and its stabilizer, block and pair laws are supplied. Full extension and intermediate-block conclusions additionally use their stated certification or Späth inputs.
