Purpose

Fixes the actual SO, derived Omega and Spin carriers for the higher-rank principal criterion.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

Omega is the matrix commutator subgroup and the identity prime-to-two cover is derived from the given full Spin projection.

Argument or construction

The two-kernel and full universal property produce the cover. Simplicity gives centrelessness and central faithfulness, while projection squares transfer the field action and principal fixation.

External assumptions and dependencies

Rank at least four, Clifford-to-matrix realization, centre, simple/nonabelian quotient, full-cover source and calibrated roots.
