carrier-constructioncurrent

Purpose

Constructs literal CSp and PCSp on the same symplectic matrix coordinates.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

CSp consists of matrices preserving the form up to a unit, and PCSp is its whole-centre quotient with the induced PSp inclusion and field action.

Argument or construction

Matrix multiplication proves closure and entrywise Frobenius preserves the multiplier equation. Centre intersection gives inclusion injectivity and quotient action squares.

External assumptions and dependencies

Rank and odd-field scope, centre intersection, derived subgroup, index two and full natural automorphism structure are group-only source inputs.
