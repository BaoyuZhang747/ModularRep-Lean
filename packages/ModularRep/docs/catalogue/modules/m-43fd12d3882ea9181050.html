ModularRep.CentralCharacterBlockSector

ModularRep/CentralCharacterBlockSector.lean

theorem-developmentcurrent

Purpose

A block idempotent of a finite group algebra is a primitive central idempotent.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

Every primitive central idempotent has a unique sector in the complete central character idempotent family. Any supported irreducible representation has that same scalar central character.

Argument or construction

Apply primitive central-idempotent support to the complete family of central character idempotents. Its unique sector agrees with the scalar central character of every simple module supported by that block.

External assumptions and dependencies

Algebraically closed coefficients, a finite central subgroup of invertible order and the given primitive central idempotent; identification with a representation uses its irreducibility and support.
