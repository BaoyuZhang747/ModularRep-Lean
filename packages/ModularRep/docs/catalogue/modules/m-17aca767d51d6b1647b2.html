Purpose

This file isolates the elementary group-theoretic content of a defect-group interface.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

For a finite group, a p-subgroup D and a support predicate with nonzero Q equivalent to Q subconjugate to D, D is maximal among supported p-subgroups by inclusion.

Argument or construction

Use the supplied equivalence between nonzero support and subconjugacy. If the nominated subgroup lies in another supported p-subgroup, the reverse subconjugacy bounds its order and finite cardinality forces equality.

External assumptions and dependencies

A finite group, nominated p-subgroup and a nonzero-support predicate with the explicitly assumed subconjugacy characterization.
