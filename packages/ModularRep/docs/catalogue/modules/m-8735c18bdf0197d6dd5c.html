Purpose

Completes the forward quotient descent to an equivalence of whole block fibres.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

Common quotient roots and the reverse block-membership source make the injective descent map surjective onto the fixed quotient block.

Argument or construction

Root transport puts all descents in one set of characters. The membership equivalence gives existence and uniqueness of preimages and hence the fibre equivalence.

External assumptions and dependencies

The original relative witness, fixed-root agreement and reverse membership, separately justified even in centreless applications.
