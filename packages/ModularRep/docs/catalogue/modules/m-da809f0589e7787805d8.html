Purpose

This package contains the subordinate geometric, representation theoretic and published source data. The Brauer hypothesis and the complete strict relative-block family are constructed by their separate Lean proofs before the localized reduction is applied.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

The generic prime-two application fixes the actual modular system, matrix Omega cover, specified primitive blocks, complete relative class and subordinate geometric and representation sources.

Argument or construction

Construct the Brauer hypothesis through the principal/nonprincipal representative proof, and independently construct the complete strict relative-block family. Apply the localized reduction to these two derived hypotheses; the ambient exceptional pair is excluded only from this generic endpoint.

External assumptions and dependencies

Apply the localized theorem only after both of its hypotheses have been proved from their construction inputs.
