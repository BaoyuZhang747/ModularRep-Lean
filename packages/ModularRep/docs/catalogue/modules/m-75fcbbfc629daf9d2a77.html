carrier-constructioncurrent

Purpose

This file contains only the literal sets of blocks and the three operations needed at one fixed subgroup Q: selection of the quotient block of a defect zero ordinary character, inflation to the normaliser, and a complete literal normaliser block decomposition.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

LocalNormalizerBlockOperations stores quotient-block selection for defect-zero ordinary characters, inflation to literal normalizer blocks and a complete normalizer decomposition, without ambient block induction.

Argument or construction

Package selection of the literal quotient block of a defect-zero local character, its inflation to the normalizer, and a complete normalizer primitive decomposition. Ambient block induction is deliberately a later operation.

External assumptions and dependencies

Literal quotient and inflated normalizer primitive blocks, the supplied LocalNormalizerBlockOperations, and the uniform right-twist/group-equivalence naturality laws. These do not include an ambient induced-block selector.
