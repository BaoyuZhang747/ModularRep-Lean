Purpose

Proves the normal-p-subgroup kernel theorem from fixed vectors.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

A normal p-subgroup acts trivially in an irreducible representation over characteristic p.

Argument or construction

A finite additive orbit span supplies a nonzero fixed vector by p-group counting. Normality makes the invariant subspace ambient-stable, and irreducibility forces it to be the whole space.

External assumptions and dependencies

A p-group acting in characteristic p with p prime. The kernel theorem additionally uses a normal subgroup and irreducibility; the final Navarro232-shaped principle is derived from the fixed-vector argument.
