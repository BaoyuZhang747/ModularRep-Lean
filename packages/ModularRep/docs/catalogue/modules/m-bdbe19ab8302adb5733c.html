Purpose

Relates arbitrary prescribed raw representatives to a retained principal packet.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

Equality of weight classes is realized by one inner conjugation, used throughout the inflation graph and base embedding.

Argument or construction

The conjugated embedding sends the new radical and normalizer to the old ones. Its action and projection squares preserve the original extension values, roots and catalogues.

External assumptions and dependencies

Actual weight-class conjugacy, fixed projection/central quotient and the original extension packet.
