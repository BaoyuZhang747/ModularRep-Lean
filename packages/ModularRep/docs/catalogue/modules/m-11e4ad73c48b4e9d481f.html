ModularRep.CentralAction

theorem-developmentcurrent

Purpose

This file packages the scalar by which a central element acts on a finite dimensional irreducible representation over an algebraically closed field.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

An irreducible finite representation over an algebraically closed field has a unique unit-valued scalar character on each central subgroup.

Argument or construction

A central element acts by a scalar on an irreducible module over an algebraically closed field. Uniqueness of that scalar proves multiplicativity, unit-valuedness and uniqueness of the central character.

External assumptions and dependencies

An irreducible finite dimensional representation over an algebraically closed field and a subgroup contained in the centre. Schur's lemma supplies scalar action.
