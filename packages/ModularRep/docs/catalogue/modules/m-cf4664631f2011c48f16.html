The original specified selector guard is assumed only for compatible roots and reductions of the actual local character.

Argument or construction

Pull a target own-normalizer reduction back to the original group. Its primitive block is identified by the original guard, and the backward operation formula transports that equality to the target.

Character transport and the prescribed backward operation equation give the target guard. The target guard follows from the original guard and the exact backward primitive formula of the transported operations.
