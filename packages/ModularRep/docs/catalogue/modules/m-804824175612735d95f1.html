Purpose

Packages one ordinary local family with the original global correspondence.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

A base-sector bijection and sector orbit coverage produce one faithful family; canonical local availability realizes its local ordinary equivalences with covariance and stable-radical fixedness.

Argument or construction

The family is chosen first from orbit transport. Local realization is added afterward through the class map, so neither the global bijection nor its stabilizer information is reselected.

External assumptions and dependencies

The reference-sector equivalence with stabilizer equivariance and the faithful-sector orbit cases. Canonical local availability and compatibility are required only when realizing the ordinary local output after the family is chosen.
