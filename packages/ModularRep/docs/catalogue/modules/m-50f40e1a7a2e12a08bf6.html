Purpose

Proves invariance of the specified principal block under every group automorphism.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

Principality is the action of a primitive central idempotent on the trivial representation.

Argument or construction

Orthogonality of the specified decomposition proves uniqueness of the principal idempotent. Twisting preserves the trivial representation, hence fixes that idempotent and its supported Brauer fibre.

External assumptions and dependencies

Specified block decomposition and the existing Brauer-block transport laws.
