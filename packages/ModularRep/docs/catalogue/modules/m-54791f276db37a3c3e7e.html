Purpose

Constructs a unique least stable Levi overgroup by dimension minimization.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

For a fixed subgroup contained in an eligible Levi, natural-valued dimension, strict monotonicity and intersection laws give a unique least Frobenius-stable Levi envelope, retaining properness when applicable.

Argument or construction

Choose an overgroup of least dimension. Intersecting it with any other proves it is least; uniqueness forces automorphism stability, and any proper overgroup makes it proper.

External assumptions and dependencies

Only the explicit Levi predicate, dimension/intersection laws and preservation by the given automorphism.
