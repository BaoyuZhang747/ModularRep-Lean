Purpose

The same complete modular system supplies every prime-to-ell root from the algebraically closed residue field.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

The coefficient field must contain the ell-part roots for the actual Clifford/field ambient, while its prime-to-ell roots come from the same complete modular system.

Argument or construction

The ambient order is the Clifford order times the field degree. If the latter is coprime to ell, its contribution does not enlarge the ell-part, so the existing ordinary primitive root suffices.

External assumptions and dependencies

A literal primitive root for this ell-part supplies the exact guard required by the checked four-extension construction. When the field degree is prime to ell, the existing choice already contains the required ell-part; all other roots are constructed by Hensel.
