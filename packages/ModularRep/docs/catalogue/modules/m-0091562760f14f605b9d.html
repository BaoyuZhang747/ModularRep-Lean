Purpose

Transfers the selected Levi factorization to the ambient Clifford action on its Jordan image.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

Given a character in the complete original Levi packet and its equivariant injective Jordan map, transfers the selected stabilizer assertions to its actual ambient image.

Argument or construction

Packet injectivity and equivariance first transfer the Levi factorization and field fixer. The rational Spin-times-Levi product then enlarges the acting group to Clifford while preserving the image's packet and block.

External assumptions and dependencies

The authenticated Jordan injection, prior Levi factorization and derived rational diagonal product.
