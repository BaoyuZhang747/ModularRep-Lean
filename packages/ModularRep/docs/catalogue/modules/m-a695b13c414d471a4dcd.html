carrier-constructioncurrent

Purpose

Constructs the algebraic-closure symplectic fixed-point group and its cyclic field action.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

For positive a, the fixed points of the a-th entrywise Frobenius form a finite group with an action of the cyclic group of order a.

Argument or construction

Finite matrix entries satisfy a finite polynomial equation. Frobenius powers descend to fixed points, while ambient lifts are retained only as a pointwise family.

External assumptions and dependencies

No character or block theory is used in the carrier construction.
