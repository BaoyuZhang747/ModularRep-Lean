Purpose

This file contains only group-theoretic carrier facts for a subgroup P and its normalizer.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

The actual normalizer subtype contains a canonical copy of P. Its injective inclusion reflects equality of local subgroups, and for p-subgroup P supplies the canonical upper central Brauer interval.

Argument or construction

Realize the normalizer and the embedded copy of the nominated subgroup as actual subgroup types. Injectivity of the normalizer inclusion reflects subgroup equality and supplies the canonical central Brauer interval.

External assumptions and dependencies

A group and subgroup P; the canonical interval construction additionally assumes P is a p-group.
