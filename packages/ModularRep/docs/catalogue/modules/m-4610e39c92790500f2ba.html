Constructs the SO matching induced by a fixed equivariant Omega seed.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

The seed is composed with the literal weight covering and the actual constituent-orbit equivalence.

Argument or construction

Constancy on orbits gives a bijective orbit-to-weight map. Its representative formula proves that restriction occurrence is equivalent to DGN covering for precisely the resulting upper correspondence.
