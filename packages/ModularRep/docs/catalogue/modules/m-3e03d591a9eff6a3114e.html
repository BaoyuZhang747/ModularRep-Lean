Purpose

Constructs the actual global and raw-pair stabilizer tuple.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

The normal base is the right-projection kernel and its local intersection is the normalizer of the same raw weight's subgroup.

Argument or construction

Computed group equivalences transport roots and both characters. The own reduction equation identifies the local character with the selected ordinary quotient character.

External assumptions and dependencies

Actual semidirect action, Brauer character and own-normalizer reduction; full raw containment is not assumed.
