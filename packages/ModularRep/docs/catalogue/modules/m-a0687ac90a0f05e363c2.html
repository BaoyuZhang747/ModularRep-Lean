Purpose

Transfers stabilizer factorization through an injective equivariant packet map.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

An injective packet map equivariant for the same Levi and field actions transfers stabilizer product factorization and identifies the source and image field stabilizers; surjectivity onto ambient Brauer characters is not assumed.

Argument or construction

Injectivity reflects equality after either Levi or field action. This preserves the product stabilizer and proves equality of the field stabilizers of the source character and its image.

External assumptions and dependencies

An actual injective map with both equivariance squares and the preceding Levi factorization.
