Purpose

Derives the four nonprincipal weight signatures from actual ordinary local block data.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

Actual one-row and four-row local catalogues with their target allocation and corrected fixed count, together with small-defect data, determine the four nonprincipal signatures.

Argument or construction

The one-row and four-row supports split between the K4 and D8 blocks according to the supplied histograms. Their fixed contributions give one and three, while the small-defect and defect-zero sources supply the remaining totals and fixed counts.

External assumptions and dependencies

Actual radical/reduction/ordinary catalogues and induction intervals, allocations [true] and [false,false,false,true], distinct fixed radical classes, corrected local fixed count two, and the specified small-defect and Brauer signatures.
