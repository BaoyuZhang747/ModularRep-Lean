Purpose

Identifies the selected raw-pair stabilizer with the Spath ambient local normalizer.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

A fully equivariant matched bijection and centerless selected ambient yield group and base equivalences between pair stabilizers and the required ambient local carriers, with commuting inclusion squares.

Argument or construction

Equivariance and injectivity identify the matched global weight and Brauer stabilizers. The raw pair stabilizer is their intersection with the radical normalizer. The central quotient embedding identifies the raw and ambient radicals, giving the local group equivalence. Restrict to right-coordinate kernels to obtain the base equivalence and its squares.

External assumptions and dependencies

The same matched Omega and full equivariance, selected raw-weight support uniqueness, centerlessness and the full semidirect automorphism presentation. No extension or block-induction relation is assumed.
