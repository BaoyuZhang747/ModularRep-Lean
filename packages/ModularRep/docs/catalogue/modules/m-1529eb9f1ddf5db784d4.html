Purpose

Use block-supported decomposition columns and global spanning to compare ordinary and Brauer spans one block at a time.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

Global decomposition and spanning data with block-supported columns and independent Brauer vectors imply equality of the ordinary and Brauer spans in each block; an exhaustive selected ordinary family has that same span.

Argument or construction

Use block-supported decomposition columns and global spanning to compare ordinary and Brauer spans one block at a time. Independence eliminates contributions outside the selected block; an exhaustive list of ordinary labels gives the identical span.

External assumptions and dependencies

Global ordinary/Brauer decomposition and spanning, block-supported columns, and Brauer linear independence are explicit hypotheses. Exhaustiveness of selected ordinary labels is required to identify a particular row span.
