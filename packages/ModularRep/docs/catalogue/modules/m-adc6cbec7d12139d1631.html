Purpose

Identifies conjugacy classes of product weights with products of factor classes.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

The product raw-weight construction commutes with coordinatewise conjugation and induces an equivalence between tuples of factor weight classes and product weight classes.

Argument or construction

Raw inverse laws identify the product conjugacy relation with coordinatewise conjugacy. Descending the raw product map then gives a bijection with exact class formulas.

External assumptions and dependencies

The proved raw product coordinates and the same ordinary quotient product source.
