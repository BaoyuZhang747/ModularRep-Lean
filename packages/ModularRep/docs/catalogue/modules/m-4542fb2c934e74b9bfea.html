Purpose

Tracks eigenvalues under negation for quotient-sign twisting.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

Negating a finite-dimensional operator sends its characteristic roots, with multiplicity, to their negatives.

Argument or construction

The determinant formula proves the characteristic-polynomial substitution identity. The polynomial root theorem then yields the multiset equality used by the sign-character calculation.

External assumptions and dependencies

A finite-dimensional endomorphism over an infinite field for the polynomial identity. The multiset-of-roots theorem retains the same field and endomorphism and uses the proved negation identity.
