theorem-developmentcurrent

Purpose

Derives the complete Q=1 normalization of an unchanged global map.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

A block-preserving Brauer/weight equivalence with canonical local compatibility and defect-zero singleton blocks maps every global defect-zero reduction to its trivial-radical weight, with a unique reverse preimage.

Argument or construction

The canonical block theorem first proves the forward normalization. Injectivity of the global equivalence and of defect-zero reduction gives the unique reverse fibre, and injectivity of the representative local class map identifies its actual ordinary character.

External assumptions and dependencies

The same global Brauer/weight equivalence, its specified block-preservation equation, canonical raw reductions and local block compatibility, DefectZeroReductionSource, TrivialWeightSource and DefectZeroOrdinaryBlockSource.
