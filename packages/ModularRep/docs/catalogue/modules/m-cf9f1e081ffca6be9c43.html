Purpose

States standard block-triple invariance under simultaneous group coordinates.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

One whole-group equivalence and exact subgroup images compute both character-domain maps; compatible roots and pullbacks give an iff of the fixed standard relations.

Argument or construction

The source is universal and independent of principal weights or FM maps. Its consumers may transport or reflect a relation without asserting either premise.

External assumptions and dependencies

MRR Definitions 3.2/3.4 and automorphism Lemma 3.6(ii), with the general different-group form licensed by the definitions.
