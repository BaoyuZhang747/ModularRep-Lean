ModularRep.IBrBlockEquivTransport

ModularRep/IBrBlockEquivTransport.lean

transport-adaptercurrent

Purpose

This file proves that the block index of an irreducible Brauer character is unchanged when the character, its affording representation, and a complete primitive block-idempotent decomposition are transported along a group equivalence.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

Transport along one group equivalence, its corresponding roots and primitive family preserves the block index and gives an equivalence of the actual IBr block fibres.

Argument or construction

Transport the primitive family and the affording module along one group isomorphism. The supporting index is unchanged, proving that character transport restricts to an equivalence of corresponding literal block fibres.

External assumptions and dependencies

One actual group equivalence, its transported root embedding and primitive block family, and simple/Brauer injectivity for the two coefficient carriers.
