Purpose

Selects global ordinary rows from the abelian and nonabelian component alternatives.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

The actual upper selection and lower constituent data produce classwise ordinary rows and diagonal cases; rational-series support is checked before normalized duality.

Argument or construction

The abelian Chaneb identity supplies its rows. In the nonabelian case the previously constructed restriction matrix supplies the rows and diagonal permutation, so no completed lower family is assumed.

External assumptions and dependencies

Chaneb Proposition2.8 on abelian component fibres; actual component presentation and upper same-family Taylor/Geck-Hezard data on nonabelian fibres; uniform pre-duality constituent series/support; natural multiplicities supplied by odd-induction PIM expansion.
