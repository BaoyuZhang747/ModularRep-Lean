Formalisation.SporadicPrimeArithmetic

packages/formalisation/Formalisation/SporadicPrimeArithmetic.lean

theorem-developmentcurrent

Purpose

Checks the prime divisors of four explicitly factored integers used for the boundary census.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

For prime p, divisibility of each defined J4, Fi24, Baby or Monster order integer is equivalent to membership in its displayed prime set.

Argument or construction

Expand divisibility across the product and its positive powers. A prime divisor of a displayed prime factor equals that factor, and conversely each listed factor divides the product. Exact integer normalization also verifies the decimal values of the four factorizations.

External assumptions and dependencies

The identification of these integers with the orders of the actual named groups is an Atlas input, separate from the proved arithmetic.
