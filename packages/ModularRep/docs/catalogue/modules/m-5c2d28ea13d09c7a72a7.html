Purpose

Builds actual PSp-to-PCSp covering objects for the principal argument.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

Scoped Clifford and DGN laws give covering characters and weight classes; trivial modular linear characters imply uniqueness and field fixation.

Argument or construction

The quotient has order two in characteristic two, killing its LinBr group. Covering support and primitive block laws then show that covering a principal pair stays in the common principal block.

External assumptions and dependencies

Brough--Spath covering laws, actual DGN semantics, cyclic extensions and compatible root/block sources.
