Purpose

Constructs field actions and principal-above naturality on four literal principal fibres.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

Given the specified four principal fibres and compatible group automorphisms, constructs their field actions and proves that the unique principal character above a constituent commutes with field twisting.

Argument or construction

Restriction occurrence commutes with the group square. Green uniqueness identifies the twisted upper character with the upper character of the twisted constituent, proving naturality without assuming fixation.

External assumptions and dependencies

Actual automorphism square, principal stability and Green/principal restriction sources.
