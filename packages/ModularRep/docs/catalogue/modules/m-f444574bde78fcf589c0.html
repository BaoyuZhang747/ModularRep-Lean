Purpose

Extends principal SO/Omega weight-class fixation from the defining-prime Frobenius generator to the full field group.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

The source is block-scoped on actual ordinary weight classes with one modular system and calibrated roots.

Argument or construction

The two FYZ generator laws are propagated through positive and inverse powers using the coordinate power equations. The result concerns classes and does not assert that a chosen raw representative is fixed.

External assumptions and dependencies

FYZ Corollary 3.63 in the source version used by the retained proof, sufficient ordinary roots and specified principal weight calibration.
