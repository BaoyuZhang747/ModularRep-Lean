Purpose

Construct reduced extension models with trivial factor sets over the cyclic quotient.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

Under the cyclic extension principle, the reduced centreless matched pair admits genuine extension models with trivial factor sets.

Argument or construction

Use the outer quotient of order at most two to obtain cyclicity. Extend the chosen invariant representations and apply the associated-model construction, preserving the characters being compared.
