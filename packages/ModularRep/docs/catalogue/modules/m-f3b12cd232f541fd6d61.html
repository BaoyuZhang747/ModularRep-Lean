Purpose

Proves that a radical subgroup of a finite product equals the product of its coordinate images.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

For a radical p-subgroup of a finite product, proves equality with the product of its actual coordinate images, then derives coordinatewise membership and the normalizer product formula.

Argument or construction

The coordinate hull is a p-group normalized by the original normalizer. The finite p-group normalizer condition and radicality force equality, yielding coordinatewise membership and normalizer formulas.

External assumptions and dependencies

Finite group hypotheses and the actual prime; no character or block source.
