The supplied source identifies the normalizer Brauer block of an actual inflated local reduction with the literal inflation of its own ordinary weight's selected quotient block.

Argument or construction

Record the specified block compatibility of a local ordinary Brauer reduction after normalizer inflation. Applying that exact law identifies the actual normalizer Brauer block with the selected inflated local character block.

The uniform Navarro local reduction/inflation block-compatibility source on the same ordinary weight, Brauer reduction, roots and literal normalizer primitive decomposition.
