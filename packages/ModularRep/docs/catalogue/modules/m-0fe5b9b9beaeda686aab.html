Purpose

Conjugate a stabilizer-equivariant base-fibre map by transporters.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

A stabilizer-equivariant base-block or base-sector matching extends over its orbit, preserving equivariant labels and the supplied pointwise compatibility predicates and normalization.

Argument or construction

Conjugate a stabilizer-equivariant base-fibre map by transporters. Equivariant labels and supplied pointwise pair properties survive this conjugation, including normalization when it holds on the base block.

External assumptions and dependencies

The base-fibre equivalence, stabilizer equivariance and equivariant block/sector labels. Full transport requires the pointwise pair predicates and the stated base normalization.
