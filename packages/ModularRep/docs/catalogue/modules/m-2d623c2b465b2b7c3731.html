Purpose

Embeds actual upstairs character stabilizers in the downstairs automorphism ambient.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

The fixed triple-cover projection supplies the exact action square and compatible principal Brauer values.

Argument or construction

The universal property of the full cover proves automorphism descent injective. Restriction to opposite stabilizers preserves the inner base and constructs the required image subgroup.

External assumptions and dependencies

Existing full-cover universal property, quotient surjectivity and principal deflation value equality; no automorphism-surjectivity premise.
