ModularRep.PrimeOutsideOrder

ModularRep/PrimeOutsideOrder.lean

theorem-developmentcurrent

Purpose

A p-subgroup's order divides |G| and is a p-power.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

If p does not divide the finite group order, every p-subgroup is trivial and the only p-radical subgroup is the trivial subgroup.

Argument or construction

A p-subgroup's order divides |G| and is a p-power. If p does not divide |G| it is trivial, so the only radical is the trivial subgroup; no block conclusion is added.

External assumptions and dependencies

A finite group, p prime and nondivisibility of the group order by p.
