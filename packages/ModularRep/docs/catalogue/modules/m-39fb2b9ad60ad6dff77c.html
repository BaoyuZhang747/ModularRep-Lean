Purpose

For an actual radical subgroup Q, define the Brauer subset by the radical class of its image under the global correspondence.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

The same global equivariant block-preserving Brauer/weight matching determines a radical partition and canonical representative local maps; they preserve the block and satisfy automorphism covariance without new matching choices.

Argument or construction

Define the Brauer radical fibre as the inverse image under the same global matching of a weight radical fibre. Compose with its canonical local-character inverse to obtain representative maps, whose block law and covariance follow from that original matching.

External assumptions and dependencies

The same actual global equivariant Brauer/weight bijection and its block preservation, plus the canonical radical and representative-fibre constructions. Extension and Q=1 clauses are not consequences of these local maps.
