Purpose

The complete-group argument in the sporadic section has two logically different parts.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

Blockwise finite cardinality equality and trivial actions produce an equivariant block-preserving candidate, without Q=1 normalization or the full extension/triple clauses.

Argument or construction

Choose a finite equivalence in each block from equal fibre cardinalities and combine them. Trivial actions make the combined matching equivariant; its radical partition is induced from weight labels, with no Q=1 or triple certification.

External assumptions and dependencies

Equal finite block-fibre cardinalities and trivial actions. It produces only a candidate and supplies no Q=1 normalization or extension/triple certification.
