Purpose

Lists unselected lower data on each own quotient.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

For each original Brauer character and raw weight, the record supplies normalizer laws, quotient primitive decompositions and catalogues, interval laws and guarded primitive-image principles.

Argument or construction

The record fixes the specified quotient and local normalizer as indices. It supplies no selected quotient character, matched block, extension, induction equality or correspondence; constructors derive those later.

External assumptions and dependencies

Unselected quotient primitive decompositions and central character catalogues, the fixed-central-quotient normalizer law, the actual quotient normalizer interval law and guarded global/local primitive-image laws. The descriptor is indexed by the original character and raw weight and prime-to-p centre.
