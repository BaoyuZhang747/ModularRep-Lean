Purpose

Analyzes the selected outer C2 semidirect carrier and its full automorphism presentation.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

Square-one gives selected cyclic order at most two and stabilizer quotients at most two; the inner-kernel match and centerlessness give bijectivity of the semidirect map to Aut(X).

Argument or construction

The order divides two. Every stabilizer quotient embeds into the selected cyclic group, so each intermediate subgroup is base or top. A nontrivial selected outer class represents the unique nonidentity class, giving an inner-times-selected factorization of every automorphism. Faithfulness on the selected subgroup and centerlessness kill the two coordinates of the kernel.

External assumptions and dependencies

Selected square relation; for the full presentation, the actual C2 outer-class map, nontriviality, identification of its kernel with inner automorphisms and centerlessness.
