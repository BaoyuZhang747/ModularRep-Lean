Purpose

Builds the identity own-quotient ambient when only the selected character stabilizer is inner.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

Innerness of every automorphism fixing the chosen Brauer character gives a surjective original action and the required OriginalSpathAmbient on its central character quotient.

Argument or construction

Represent stabilizer elements by inner automorphisms, producing surjectivity. Divide by the proved central kernel of this action to obtain the quotient-stabilizer isomorphism. Use the whole own quotient as the ambient base.

External assumptions and dependencies

Selected-stabilizer innerness, perfectness and the p-prime center hypothesis.
