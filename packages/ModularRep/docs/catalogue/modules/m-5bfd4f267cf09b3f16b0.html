Purpose

The multiplicative group of a finite characteristic-two field has odd order, so its squaring map is onto.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

For a finite characteristic-two field and a central scalar realization of multiplier squares, the conformal action fixes every kernel prime regular class function and irreducible Brauer character.

Argument or construction

The multiplicative group of a finite characteristic-two field has odd order, so its squaring map is onto. The conformal scalar factorization makes conjugation inner on the kernel and hence fixes its Brauer characters.

External assumptions and dependencies

A finite field of characteristic two, a multiplier map and central scalar square realization. Brauer-function specializations additionally use their explicit root embedding.
