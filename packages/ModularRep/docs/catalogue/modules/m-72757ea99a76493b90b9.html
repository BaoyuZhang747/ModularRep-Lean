ModularRep.BlockIdempotentDecomposition

ModularRep/BlockIdempotentDecomposition.lean

theorem-developmentcurrent

Purpose

A finite block decomposition is represented here by a complete orthogonal family of primitive central idempotents.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

A supplied complete primitive central-idempotent decomposition assigns a unique literal block to each simple module. Its representation specialization identifies the scalar central character sector of every supported simple representation.

Argument or construction

Use primitive support in a complete central decomposition to assign a unique block to each simple module. Combine that support with central character idempotents to prove equal central sectors for modules in the same block.

External assumptions and dependencies

A ring and a supplied complete primitive central-idempotent family. Its representation/central character specializations additionally assume an algebraically closed coefficient field and an invertible-order finite central subgroup.
