Purpose

Proves the actual inertia-Hall products with characteristic-zero coefficients.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

Both own M-inertias contain Sp times the conformal centre; its index divides two, while the Hall preimage has odd ell-power index.

Argument or construction

Inner and central actions fix the Brauer character or weight class. Normality and coprime indices force both products to be all of CSp.

External assumptions and dependencies

The actual multiplier/scalar-square index bound and cyclic CSp/Sp structure, without an inertia-product source.
