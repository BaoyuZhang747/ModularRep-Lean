Purpose

This source interface contains only a presentation of the original geometric point subgroup and its original Frobenius.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

Each component is an actual subgroup of that same H. It is not an input about rational factors, supported lifts, product action images, or character orbits.

Argument or construction

E1/U: the subgroups must be the actual simple components of the chosen connected simply connected derived algebraic Levi, in a finite presentation of the Frobenius cycles (including singleton cycles). Sources: Malle--Testerman Proposition 12.14, p. 103; Geck--Malle Lemma 1.5.15, pp. 63--64 and Corollary 1.5.16, p. 64; the geometric opening of the proof of FLZ, Jordan decomposition for weights, Proposition 5.6, p. 31. Only the ORIGINAL geometric automorphism has a source square; its rational restriction and all coordinate conjugation squares are deductions.

External assumptions and dependencies
