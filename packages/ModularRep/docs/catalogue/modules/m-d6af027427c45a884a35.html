ModularRep.LinearCharacterCompleteness

ModularRep/LinearCharacterCompleteness.lean

theorem-developmentcurrent

Purpose

Use enough roots of unity to identify the finite character dual of the central abelian subgroup.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

A finite central subgroup of invertible order with enough coefficient roots has a complete family of central character idempotents summing to one; algebraic closure gives the stated enough-roots specialization.

Argument or construction

Use enough roots of unity to identify the finite character dual of the central abelian subgroup. Dual separation and orthogonality show the central character idempotents sum to one; algebraic closure supplies enough roots when its order is invertible.

External assumptions and dependencies

A finite central subgroup of order invertible in k and enough roots of unity for its exponent; the algebraically closed specialization constructs those roots internally.
