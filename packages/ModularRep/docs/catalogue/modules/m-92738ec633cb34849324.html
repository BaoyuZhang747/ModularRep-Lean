Purpose

Realizes the faithful ordinary family on the original group.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

For faithful centres of order 3, 4 or 6 with the stated inversion and outer-order-two data, the identity ambient embedding supplies AD(3a) and the original model comparison.

Argument or construction

Inner conjugation fixes the relevant characters, and raw inertia inside the original group is its normalizer. The proof attaches these actions and the gamma-product comparison to each row of the already chosen ordinary family.

External assumptions and dependencies

Outer order two, faithful centre order 3, 4 or 6 and its inverting automorphism, one reference-sector equivalence with stabilizer equivariance, and canonical local availability/compatibility. The original-group identity action and comparison models are derived.
