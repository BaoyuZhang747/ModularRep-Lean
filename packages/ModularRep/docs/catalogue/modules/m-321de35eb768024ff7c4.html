Purpose

Derives the Q=1 base-character identity from the normalized selected weight itself.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

A normalized equivariant match with trivial selected radical identifies the raw ordinary reduction with the matched Brauer character and gives QOneLocalTransportData in the centerless quotient presentation.

Argument or construction

Transport the raw local ordinary character through the trivial normalizer quotient to obtain a global defect-zero character. Injectivity of the same normalized correspondence identifies its reduction with the match. Combine ordinary descent and Brauer inflation pointwise to compare the quotient base characters, without equating independently chosen root lifts.

External assumptions and dependencies

Defect-zero reduction and trivial-weight sources, the same-map normalization and the actual ordinary descent/Brauer inflation equations.
