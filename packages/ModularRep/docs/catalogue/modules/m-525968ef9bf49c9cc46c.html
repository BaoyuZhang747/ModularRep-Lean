Purpose

Proves rank three of the explicit symmetrized six-by-thirty matrix and defines the raw replay interface.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

The displayed six rows have rank three over every characteristic-zero field; a raw-row minor/reconstruction packet and the pointwise symmetrization identity give raw rank four and basis-plus rank three.

Argument or construction

The selected three-by-three minor is 1580463033304441651200. The other rows repeat the third row or are half the sum of the three selected rows, giving both rank bounds. Linear-map images preserve equality of spans, so symmetrizing the selected raw basis gives the same span as symmetrizing all rows.

External assumptions and dependencies

Characteristic zero and, for attachment to raw data, the explicit finite certificate and permutation/symmetrization equations. This module does not instantiate the canonical table or actual character binding.
