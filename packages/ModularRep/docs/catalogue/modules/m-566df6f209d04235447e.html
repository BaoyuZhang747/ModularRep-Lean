Purpose

Derives the nonprincipal Brauer signature from literal values.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

Six actual ordinary rows on thirty regular classes and their fusion binding yield four Brauer characters and two outer-fixed characters in the selected block.

Argument or construction

The raw rank certificate identifies its Brauer dimension. The symmetrized rank, actual basis action and involution trace formula give the fixed count.

External assumptions and dependencies

A complete actual six-row ordinary block catalogue, actual regular representative cover, primitive 29th-root encoding and value binding. The fixed-count result additionally uses the block's stability, actual outer decomposition and fusion dictionary.
