Purpose

Navarro 7.6 (pp. 137--138) and 9.10 (p. 201), restricted to a central normal p-subgroup, supply the primitive-idempotent theorem for the actual quotient algebra map.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

The representation square, principal-block test and supported Brauer-fibre equivalence are deductions. A supported character has an actual affording irreducible representation on which the specified idempotent acts as one.

Argument or construction

The representation square, principal-block test and supported Brauer-fibre equivalence are deductions. The equivalence is restricted only AFTER support compatibility has been proved on literal idempotents.
