Purpose

Extracts prime-to-p cover data from an actual universal extension.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

A universal central extension of a nonabelian simple group is perfect, has kernel equal to its centre, and gives an ell-prime cover whenever that kernel has prime-to-p order.

Argument or construction

Universality rules out a nontrivial abelianization. Its lift to any perfect central extension is surjective by central commutator cancellation, proving the required maximality.

External assumptions and dependencies

An actual universal central extension onto a nonabelian simple group, with the stated finite-carrier and prime-to-p kernel-order hypotheses for the ell-prime-cover specialization. No character, coefficient-root or block source occurs.
