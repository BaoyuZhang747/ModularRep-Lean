Purpose

FLZ Section 2.5, p. 540 identifies the centre of its even-unit vector normalizer with the scalar units when the orthogonal dimension is odd.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

Section 3.1, p. 541 restates this for the actual finite groups over F_q, with q odd and n positive. Here the dimension and nondegeneracy of the literal split form are checked; the remaining one-way centre-to-scalar statement is an exact E1 certificate on this carrier and source scope.

Argument or construction

Scalar centrality is already proved. Centre equality is deduced from the one-way certificate and checked scalar centrality; it is not a diagonal-kernel or index input.

External assumptions and dependencies

Here the dimension and nondegeneracy of the literal split form are checked; the remaining one-way centre-to-scalar statement is an exact E1 certificate on this carrier and source scope. In particular, the separate generated Spin/Lipschitz carrier is not substituted for the normalizer used in the source. E1, FLZ Section 2.5 p. 540 and finite-point specialization Section 3.1 p. 541.
