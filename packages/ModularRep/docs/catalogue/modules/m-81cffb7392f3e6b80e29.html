Purpose

Actual primitive-block totals and fixed-point totals construct a fully automorphism-equivariant correspondence.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

Actual blockwise total and fixed-point counts, together with an inner/tau decomposition of automorphisms, yield a full automorphism-equivariant block correspondence.

Argument or construction

Choose C2 orbit bijections on block representatives and extend them over the block action. Inner automorphisms fix both carriers, so the tau equations imply full equivariance.
