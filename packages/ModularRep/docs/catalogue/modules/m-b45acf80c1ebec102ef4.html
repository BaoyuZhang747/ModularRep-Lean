Formalisation.PCore

packages/formalisation/Formalisation/PCore.lean

theorem-developmentcurrent

Purpose

This file constructs the p-core of a group as the supremum of its normal p-subgroups.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

The largest normal p-subgroup exists by p-group closure, and cyclicity of quotient by it passes to every subgroup's quotient by its own p-core.

Argument or construction

Construct the largest normal p-subgroup using the established p-group closure lemmas. The quotient by its intersection with a subgroup is cyclic, and the quotient by the larger subgroup p-core is cyclic too.

External assumptions and dependencies

The group and the prior normal p-subgroup closure results; cyclic inheritance assumes cyclicity of the ambient quotient by its p-core.
