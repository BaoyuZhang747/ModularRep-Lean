Purpose

Handles the remaining rank-two odd-field primes by cyclicity.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

If odd nondefining ell divides the simple-group order but not q squared minus one, actual Sp4 order and a cyclic torus of order q squared plus one force every ell-subgroup cyclic.

Argument or construction

Arithmetic isolates the torus factor and the Sylow argument proves cyclicity. The general cyclic-defect source gives the same complete family and actual cover.

External assumptions and dependencies

Actual Sp4 order and torus existence, exact cover facts and the general cyclic-defect theorem, including q=3, ell=5.
