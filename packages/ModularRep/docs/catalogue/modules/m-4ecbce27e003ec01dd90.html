theorem-developmentcurrent

Purpose

Constructs a numerical blockwise partition of actual Brauer characters by radical classes.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

Equal cardinalities of literal Brauer and weight fibres in every actual block give a global block-preserving equivalence, its radical partition and local fibre equivalences.

Argument or construction

Choose a finite equivalence separately on each block and combine it over the block index. Assign each Brauer character the radical class of its image weight. Sigma decomposition and restriction to each block/radical fibre yield the partition equivalences; numerical equality alone supplies no equivariance or inductive extension data.

External assumptions and dependencies

LiteralCarrierAdapter and LiteralBlockwiseCardinality on actual block fibres.
