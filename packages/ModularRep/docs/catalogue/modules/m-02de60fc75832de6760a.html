Purpose

Implements a linear character twist on the actual group algebra.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

Coefficient multiplication by an involutive linear character gives an algebra automorphism and corresponding centre automorphism; the twisted representation action is its pullback.

Argument or construction

The proof checks the algebra map on basis elements and the involution coefficientwise. Linear induction over group algebra elements proves the representation-action equation, without claiming a whole block acts by one scalar.

External assumptions and dependencies

A coefficient field, group, linear character and the stated square-one condition for involutivity. The action comparison also has an arbitrary representation. No assertion that a primitive block acts by one scalar is assumed.
