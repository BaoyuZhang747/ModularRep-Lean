Purpose

Proves the exhaustive central-kernel dichotomy when the center has prime order.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

The central character kernel of any Brauer character is either trivial or the full center; in the latter case its values on prime regular central elements equal its value at one.

Argument or construction

View the kernel as a subgroup of the prime-order center. Such a subgroup is bottom or top, and mapping it back gives the dichotomy. Membership in the full kernel makes the chosen representation act trivially, giving the character equality.

External assumptions and dependencies

Prime cardinality of the actual center and the chosen affording representation.
