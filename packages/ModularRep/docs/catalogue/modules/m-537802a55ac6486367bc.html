transport-adapterauxiliary

Purpose

Glues the quotient-transported trivial sector and constructed faithful sectors.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

A trivial-sector quotient source, faithful-if-and-only-if-nontrivial central-sector coverage and a one-faithful-sector source produce the all-sector AnDietrichSectorInput.

Argument or construction

Identify the complement of the trivial sector with faithful characters and weights. Sector transport constructs the faithful equivalence. Take its disjoint union with the trivial-sector map. Automorphisms preserve the two components, so equivariance, block induction and the pair relation are checked within the unique component. Restrict the result back to central-sector fibres.

External assumptions and dependencies

Actual center-three sector coverage, quotient transport including its pair implication, one-faithful-sector data and equivariance of the pair relation. No all-sector map or Spath input is assumed.
