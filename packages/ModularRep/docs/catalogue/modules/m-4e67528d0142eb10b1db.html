Purpose

Derives inertia stability from invariance of the selected Levi character.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

The character inertia group is stable under the normaliser automorphism, and the normaliser difference equation restricts to it.

Argument or construction

The proof transports conjugation of the base character through the automorphism. It then supplies the derived inertia and quotient premises to the pair-character bridge.

External assumptions and dependencies

A fixed base character and the universal normaliser difference equation.
