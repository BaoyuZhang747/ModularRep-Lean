Purpose

Corrects an automorphism by an inner automorphism simultaneously upstairs and downstairs.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

A homomorphism intertwining two automorphisms and a radical conjugacy class fixed upstairs admit compatible corrections stabilising the chosen radical and its image.

Argument or construction

Choose the conjugating element supplied by fixedness of the radical class. The homomorphism sends conjugation by this element to conjugation by its image, so the corrected square still commutes. Stability of the image follows from this square.

External assumptions and dependencies

The fixed-radical inner-correction lemma and the supplied intertwining square.
