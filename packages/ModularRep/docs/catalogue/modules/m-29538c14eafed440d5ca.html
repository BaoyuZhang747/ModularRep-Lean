Purpose

Classifies proper rank-three root subsets and their admissible actions.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

The B3 Cartan matrix and squared root lengths are explicit, and an actor preserves them only on the selected subset.

Argument or construction

Finite case analysis gives the proper-subset alternatives and component actions. It permits A2 reversal while ruling out exchange of the isolated unequal-length roots.

External assumptions and dependencies

The eventual specified root/pinning/Frobenius interpretation is external; this file proves finite diagram combinatorics.
