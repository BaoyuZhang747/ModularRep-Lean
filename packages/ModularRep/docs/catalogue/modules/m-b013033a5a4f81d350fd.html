transport-adapterauxiliary

Purpose

Specializes central quotient transport to the Fischer trivial sector.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

A centerless quotient's one-sector source, automorphism descent and exact Brauer/weight/block/pair transport diagrams construct the cover's TrivialSectorQuotientSource.

Argument or construction

Centerlessness makes the faithful central-sector label unique, so replace its transporter by the identity. Use sector transport to construct the quotient correspondence and restrict its action along descent. Conjugate the correspondence through the actual carrier equivalences; the supplied diagrams transport equivariance, block preservation and the pair relation.

External assumptions and dependencies

The independent quotient one-sector source and pair equivariance, centerlessness, descent homomorphism and actual carrier, action, block and pair bridges. No cover correspondence is assumed.
