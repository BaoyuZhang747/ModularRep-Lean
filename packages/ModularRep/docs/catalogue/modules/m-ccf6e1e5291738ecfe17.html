Purpose

Transports both finite replay ranks into the actual restriction space.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

An injective evaluation with the exact raw-row image and outer-action square yields restriction rank four and rank(id+T) three, constructing the earlier computation alignment.

Argument or construction

Evaluation carries id+T to row symmetrization. Its mapped range is the replayed plus-row span. Injectivity preserves dimension, and the raw image equality transports the rank-four certificate in the same way.

External assumptions and dependencies

The finite replay source and the actual evaluation's injectivity, image equality and action compatibility.
