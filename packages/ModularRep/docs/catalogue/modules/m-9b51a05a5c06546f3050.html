Purpose

For a faithful scalar sector, an outer automorphism inverting a centre of order greater than two cannot stabilize its Brauer character.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

For a faithful central character on a centre of order greater than two inverted by the outer involution, the Brauer stabilizer contains only inner automorphisms.

Argument or construction

A Brauer stabilizer fixes its faithful central scalar pointwise, whereas the outer branch inverts a nontrivial central element. Exclude that branch using the inner/tau decomposition and realize the ambient by the original group.

External assumptions and dependencies

The finite actual group, characteristic prime, compatible coefficient/root data, specified sets of primitive blocks and characters, and the imported transport results are fixed as in the declaration context. The faithful central-scalar, centre-order, inner/tau and specified support bindings are supplied. Their actual representation and root identifications are required, rather than merely a cardinality for the centre.
