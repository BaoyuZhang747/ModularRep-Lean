ModularRep.OrdinaryCharacterLiesOverKernel

ModularRep/OrdinaryCharacterLiesOverKernel.lean

theorem-developmentcurrent

Purpose

This neutral module proves that a finite subgroup on which a representation character is constantly its degree acts trivially.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

If a finite subgroup has constant ordinary trace equal to the degree on a characteristic-zero representation, it is contained in the representation kernel.

Argument or construction

Average the restricted finite-subgroup action to project onto invariants. Constant trace equal to the degree makes the projector have full rank in characteristic zero, so all subgroup elements act identically.

External assumptions and dependencies

A characteristic-zero representation, finite dimensionality, a finite subgroup and equality of its traces with the degree at every subgroup element.
