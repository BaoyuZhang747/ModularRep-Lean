Purpose

Defines neutral universal-cover predicates and an elementary splitting lemma.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

A section of a central extension with perfect source is surjective, while the cover predicates express the exact universal property.

Argument or construction

Every element differs centrally from a section value. Commutators therefore lie in the section range, and perfectness makes that range the whole group.

External assumptions and dependencies

Group-theoretic centrality and perfectness; no BAW or FLZ theorem is encoded here.
