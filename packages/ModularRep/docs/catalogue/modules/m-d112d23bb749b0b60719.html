Purpose

Construct the embedded normal-factor copy in a semidirect stabilizer and embed its quotient into the outer cyclic group.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

The embedded normal-factor copy in a semidirect stabilizer has quotient embeddable in the cyclic outer actor. For a fixed irreducible Brauer character the cyclic extension principle yields an extension over that stabilizer.

Argument or construction

Construct the embedded normal-factor copy in a semidirect stabilizer and embed its quotient into the outer cyclic group. Brauer fixedness supplies representation invariance, to which the explicit cyclic extension principle applies.

External assumptions and dependencies

Compatible semidirect actions, inner fixedness and a cyclic outer factor; the extension endpoint additionally requires its actual Brauer root/realization and BrauerCyclicExtensionPrinciple.
