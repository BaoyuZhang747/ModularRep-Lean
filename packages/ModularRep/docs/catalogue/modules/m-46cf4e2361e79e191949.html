ModularRep.FDRepEquiv

ModularRep/FDRepEquiv.lean

transport-adaptercurrent

Purpose

This small bridge allows representation-level equivalences to be used in the categorical exact K₀ presentation, whose generators are isomorphism classes of objects of FDRep.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

An intertwining linear equivalence yields an isomorphism in the finite-representation category and equality of the corresponding exact Grothendieck classes.

Argument or construction

Convert an intertwining linear equivalence into a categorical isomorphism of finite dimensional representations. Isomorphic objects have the same class in the exact Grothendieck quotient.

External assumptions and dependencies

An actual intertwining linear equivalence of the two finite representations and the exact Grothendieck class construction.
