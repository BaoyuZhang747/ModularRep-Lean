Purpose

Constructs the nonprincipal rank-three Spin label and its least proper rational Levi.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

For a nonprincipal actual Omega block in rank three, constructs its dominating Spin block, nonidentity odd rational PCSp label and least proper Frobenius-stable dual Levi under the scoped series and geometry sources.

Argument or construction

The odd symplectic lift and guarded Bonnafé bound contradict nonprincipality if the label were quasi-isolated. Noncontainment, dimension minimization and intersections then produce the least proper Frobenius-stable Levi.

External assumptions and dependencies

Specified ordinary union, central block transport, algebraic Bonnafé input and actual rational-point/Levi geometry.
