Purpose

Extracts the larger fibre in a two-block support of total 25.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

For an exhaustive two-role support of total 25, a second fibre of size two forces the first fibre to have size 23.

Argument or construction

Positive total gives finiteness. The sigma-of-fibres decomposition yields 25 = card(first) + 2, and natural-number arithmetic gives 23.

External assumptions and dependencies

The actual support and role bijection, total 25 and second-fibre count two.
