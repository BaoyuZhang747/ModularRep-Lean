Purpose

Compares a specified block-stabilizer tuple with the full holomorph.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

The faithful map (id,gamma) is surjective on the actual stabilizer of a character in the selected block, and on the needed raw stabilizer for a weight in that block.

Argument or construction

Character or weight fixation implies specified block fixation, which supplies the missing actor coordinate. Restricted maps preserve the same roots, characters and own reduction.

External assumptions and dependencies

The exact block-stabilizer adapter and specified block covariance, without replacing Gamma globally by full Aut.
