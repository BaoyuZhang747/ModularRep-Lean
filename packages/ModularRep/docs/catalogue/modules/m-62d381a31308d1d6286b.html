Purpose

Constructs a fixed-label specified Levi block packet.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

The ordinary union uses commuting two-elements multiplied by the prescribed rational semisimple label.

Argument or construction

Closure on specified ordinary blocks defines a finite primitive-block sum. Orthogonality proves central idempotence and exact support for the same union.

External assumptions and dependencies

A one-way Broué–Michel ordinary block-closure source and the authentic paired-Levi/rational-family coefficient interpretation.
