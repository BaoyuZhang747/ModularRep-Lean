Purpose

Retains the original actual inertia-Hall subgroup argument.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

Both own inertias contain Sp times the centre, and the Hall preimage has coprime index, forcing their products to equal CSp.

Argument or construction

Inner and central fixation gives containment. The index-dividing-two and odd ell-power index calculations prove the product statements.

External assumptions and dependencies

Actual multiplier structure and cyclic quotient; the current copy retains this argument with reduced coefficient hypotheses.
