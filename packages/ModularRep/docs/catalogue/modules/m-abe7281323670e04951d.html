Purpose

The vector map is coordinatewise prime Frobenius on the same split space.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

The prescribed field action acts on the actual matrix orthogonal and special orthogonal groups, through the fixed Clifford vector-action map.

Argument or construction

The coordinate Frobenius equation gives the vector-action square. Restriction to determinant one and then to the commutator subgroup preserves the same matrices.

External assumptions and dependencies

The square follows from the existing algebra-Frobenius source equations and the constructed Clifford conjugation action. The generator square uses the actual Clifford action and actual coordinate Frobenius.
