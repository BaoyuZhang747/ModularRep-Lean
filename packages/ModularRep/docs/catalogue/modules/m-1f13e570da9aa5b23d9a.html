Purpose

For a = (lambda,e), the group algebra automorphism sends the basis element [h] to lambda(field e h) [field e h].

Mathematical statement and assumptions

This is the inverse of the algebra pullback occurring in the existing inverse-tensor/inverse-field character action. Primitive transport and Brauer-block equivariance are deductions from that cancellation and the actual supporting irreducible module.

Argument or construction

Primitive transport and Brauer-block equivariance are deductions from that cancellation and the actual supporting irreducible module. Ordinary decomposition-row and ordinary-block transport are separate. Transport actual supporting modules, using the existing tensor formula only to identify their actual function-valued Brauer characters.
