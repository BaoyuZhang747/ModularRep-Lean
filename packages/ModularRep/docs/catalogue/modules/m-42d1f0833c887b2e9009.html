Purpose

Constructs a character's own central quotient and Brauer deflation.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

For Z equal to the centre intersected with the chosen representation kernel, the quotient representation is irreducible and its Brauer character inflates to the original; perfectness gives central faithfulness.

Argument or construction

The representation factors through the quotient by its actual kernel inclusion. Surjective pullback preserves irreducibility and compatible root lifts preserve character values; kernel comparison transfers central faithfulness to the selected affording realization.

External assumptions and dependencies

A Definition35Problem and an actual Brauer character with its chosen affording representation. Perfectness is required for the central-faithfulness theorem. No quotient-character existence, local reduction, normalizer law or block-image source is assumed.
