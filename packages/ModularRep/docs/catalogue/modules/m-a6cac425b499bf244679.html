Purpose

A supplied equivariant dual indexing transfers fixedness from projective labels to Brauer labels.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

An equivariant projective/Brauer dual indexing transports projective fixedness to Brauer fixedness. An injective equivariant projective realization reflects fixedness from its ambient vector space.

Argument or construction

A supplied equivariant dual indexing transfers fixedness from projective labels to Brauer labels. Injectivity of the projective character realization reflects triviality of the action from an ambient character space.

External assumptions and dependencies

An explicitly supplied equivariant projective/Brauer dual indexing and an injective equivariant realization of the projective labels. The duality and specified character identification are not proved here.
