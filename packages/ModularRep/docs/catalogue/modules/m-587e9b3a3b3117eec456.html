Mathematical statement and assumptions

Finite blockwise weight cardinalities and compatible injective defect-zero data give a block-preserving matching extending the prescribed Q=1 normalization. Trivial actions give equivariance; IdentityCertification supplies the remaining full data.

Argument or construction

Fix the prescribed defect-zero normalization first, compare the remaining finite block complements, and extend to blockwise equivalences. Trivial actions give equivariance; the separate identity certification supplies extension, triple and intermediate-block clauses.

External assumptions and dependencies

Finite blockwise AWC cardinalities, injective prescribed defect-zero normalization and trivial actions. Its full data constructor additionally requires IdentityCertification for intermediate blocks, extensions and triples.
