Purpose

Derives the trivial-weight block law from actual reduction and self-induction.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

The literal operations, defect-zero reduction and canonical local block compatibility yield TrivialWeightBlockCompatibility without a separate block-matching premise.

Argument or construction

Two block decompositions select the same primitive idempotent on a simple module, hence the same central character. At Q=1 the local normalizer is the whole group and induction is self-induction. Compare the reduction's supporting idempotent to obtain the weight-block identity.

External assumptions and dependencies
