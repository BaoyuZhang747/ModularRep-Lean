Purpose

This source-neutral module proves the automorphism square for the literal equivalence between IBr (G / Z) and the trivial central character fibre in IBr G.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

The actual quotient/cover Brauer inflation equivalence on the trivial central character fibre is equivariant for the commuting automorphism actions, as is its inverse.

Argument or construction

Use the quotient/cover automorphism square and root agreement to commute Brauer inflation with the two actions. Restrict to the actual trivial-central character fibre and transfer equivariance to the inverse.

External assumptions and dependencies

The actual central quotient and literal trivial-central character fibre, compatible cover/quotient roots and prime regular surjectivity, and the commuting automorphism square for equivariance. Scalar central action uses algebraically closed k.
