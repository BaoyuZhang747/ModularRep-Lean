theorem-developmentauxiliary

Purpose

Cancels all other fibres inside a supported fixed-point carrier.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

For finite block and supported fixed carriers, equal supported totals and equal other fibres imply equality at a selected supported block.

Argument or construction

Flatten sector, fixedness and block subtypes into one fibre. A block outside the support has empty fibre, so the generic finite fibre-cancellation theorem applies to arbitrary fixed predicates without requiring an action on sectors.

External assumptions and dependencies

Finiteness, equality of supported totals and equality of the other supported fibres.
