ModularRep.LinearBlockStabilizer

ModularRep/LinearBlockStabilizer.lean

theorem-developmentcurrent

Purpose

The central-sector argument, prime-primary cancellation, quotient descent and cardinality estimate in the general linear-block-stabiliser lemma.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

For finite G and algebraically closed k of characteristic p, faithfully parameterized modular linear characters trivial on N and acting through SimpleBlockTensorSource have block stabilizers factoring through G/NZ(G), of order at most [G:NZ(G)].

Argument or construction

A block-stabilizing linear character is trivial on central prime regular elements by equal central sectors, and on central primary elements by its coefficient/order constraint. It factors through G/NZ(G), whose linear character count bounds the stabilizer.

External assumptions and dependencies

Algebraically closed field k of characteristic p, p prime, finite G, N normal, faithful linear character parameterization trivial on N, and SimpleBlockTensorSource giving supported simple modules and tensor support. The ordinary version restricts to prime-to-p-order linear characters.
