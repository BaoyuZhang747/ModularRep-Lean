Purpose

Preserves literal central-idempotent support under restriction and representation isomorphism.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

Restriction along a fixed group homomorphism transports support along its group algebra map, and representation isomorphisms preserve support by a central idempotent.

Argument or construction

Their algebra actions agree by functoriality. For an isomorphism, conjugation carries the idempotent action to the new representation and preserves the identity operator.

External assumptions and dependencies

Only the given finite representations, group map or representation isomorphism.
