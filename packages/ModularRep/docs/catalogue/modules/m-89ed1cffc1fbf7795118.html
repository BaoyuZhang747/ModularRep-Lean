Purpose

Adds original-pair comparisons to one faithful-sector correspondence.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

For a centre of order 3, 4 or 6 inverted by an outer representative, a stabilizer-equivariant base-sector bijection produces a transporter-independent family with comparisons at every actual match.

Argument or construction

The sector transport chooses the family before any reductions. Specified global and local scalar equations then supply the original-pair comparison for every available canonical reduction; availability yields its nonempty realization.

External assumptions and dependencies

The specified faithful carriers, one stabilizer-equivariant base-sector equivalence, outer order two, centre order 3, 4 or 6 with its inverting automorphism, and canonical local block compatibility. Comparisons hold for every canonical reduction at the actual match; availability is used only for the existence consequence.
