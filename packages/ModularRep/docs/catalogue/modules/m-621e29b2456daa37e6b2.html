Purpose

Derive radical-subgroup transport from the actual finite central prime-to-p quotient.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

For a finite central prime-to-p quotient, normalizer images and prime cores commute sufficiently to identify radical subgroups on the two sides.

Argument or construction

Compute the normalizer image and the prime core of the preimage, then prove radicality in both directions. These deductions instantiate the fixed central-quotient radical source in this finite setting.

External assumptions and dependencies

Finite central prime-to-p quotient data and the imported finite prime-core and normalizer results. The module proves the radical-source laws in this setting rather than assuming their instance.
