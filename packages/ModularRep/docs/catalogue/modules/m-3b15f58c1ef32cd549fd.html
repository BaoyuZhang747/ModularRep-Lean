Purpose

The actual Brauer central kernel and its literal quotient when the original group is centreless.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

For a centreless group the intersection of its centre with any Brauer kernel is trivial, so the own central-kernel quotient is canonically the original group.

Argument or construction

Use centrelessness to compute the intersection, then construct the quotient equivalence and transport radical subgroups. This concerns the central intersection and does not assert that the full Brauer kernel is trivial.

External assumptions and dependencies

The finite actual group, characteristic prime, compatible coefficient/root data, specified sets of primitive blocks and characters, and the imported transport results are fixed as in the declaration context. The quotient map has the stated central prime-to-p kernel and actual root/catalogue compatibility. Ordinary descent, nonzero primitive-image or cover-lifting sources remain conditional wherever required by the declaration.
