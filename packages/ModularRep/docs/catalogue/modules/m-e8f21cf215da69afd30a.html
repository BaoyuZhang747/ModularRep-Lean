Purpose

Convert a structural outer-class kernel equality into the action-fixation laws used downstream.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

A supplied nontrivial two-class map with kernel equal to the inner automorphisms yields the action laws used by the Brauer and weight cancellation arguments.

Argument or construction

Translate the kernel equality into the assertion that a kernel element acts trivially on both class carriers. The construction uses the supplied map and kernel law and does not identify a sporadic group from its name.
