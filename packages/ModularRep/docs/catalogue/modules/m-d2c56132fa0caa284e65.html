ModularRep.ModularTraceFunction

ModularRep/ModularTraceFunction.lean

theorem-developmentcurrent

Purpose

Navarro's trace-separation theorem is stated for the trace functions of representations of a finite-dimensional algebra, whereas the project uses the character of a group representation as a function on group elements.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

The linear extension of a representation trace to the group algebra is determined by its values on group elements, so equality of these trace functionals is equivalent to equality of trace characters.

Argument or construction

Extend the representation trace linearly to the group algebra. Equality of algebra trace functionals is equivalent to equality on the group basis and therefore to equality of trace characters.

External assumptions and dependencies

A finite dimensional representation over a coefficient field and linear extension from the group basis of its group algebra.
