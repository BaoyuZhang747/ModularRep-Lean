Purpose

Defines coherent finite scalar root tables and their restrictions.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

A convention supplies multiplicative prime-to-p root tables with restriction coherence; each finite group's root is computed at its prime regular exponent.

Argument or construction

Exponent divisibility proves subgroup and quotient restriction, group-equivalence naturality and representation compatibility. Agreement outside the relevant finite domain is never asserted.

External assumptions and dependencies

Existence and admissibility of one standard modular-system convention remain E1 source choices.
