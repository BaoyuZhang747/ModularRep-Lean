Purpose

Presents the same faithful family as Späth's indexed local partition.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

The ordinary local equivalences give coverage, disjointness, covariance and equivalence between nonempty partition parts and nonempty actual local sectors.

Argument or construction

The proof consumes the already constructed ordinary output once. It retains its maps and derives support from their bijectivity, avoiding an equality-iff assertion for empty parts.

External assumptions and dependencies

The same faithful family and retained OrdinaryLocalOutput. The source-facing family theorem additionally uses the base-sector equivalence, orbit cases, canonical local availability and compatibility; support is deduced from the actual local equivalences.
