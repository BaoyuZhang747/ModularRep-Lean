Purpose

This module applies the neutral surjective-descent API to the literal qW.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

The descended local ordinary character under the constructed central prime-to-p quotient remains irreducible and defect zero, using its proven kernel constancy.

Argument or construction

Apply ordinary character descent to the constructed local quotient map using its proven kernel constancy. The central prime-to-p kernel preserves the group-order p-part and therefore defect zero of the descended character.

External assumptions and dependencies

The constructed surjective local quotient with its central prime-to-p kernel, Navarro-Tiep Lemma 2.3(c) at that fixed radical, and the given kernel-constant ordinary defect-zero local character.
