Purpose

The carrier is the actual finite-row quotient from the symbol module. Odd defect excludes the associated-copy ambiguity of degenerate symbols.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

The source concerns terminal removal on the actual odd-defect symbol carrier, at positive length and a specified hook/cohook mode.

Argument or construction

Choose the unique reachable terminal symbol. Uniqueness gives idempotence and invariance after any initial removal sequence, while odd-defect preservation excludes degeneracy.

External assumptions and dependencies

Only the standard terminal-removal existence/uniqueness theorem is a source input; the core operator, its specification and idempotence are deductions. E1: exact terminal-removal existence/uniqueness on the literal quotient and odd-defect carrier. The intended published scope is Fong-Srinivasan/Olsson with the explicit hook/cohook convention.
