Purpose

The ambient and base groups are the literal normal subgroups P ≤ G ≤ A.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

The quotient base is G / (P.subgroupOf G) and its embedding into A/P is constructed from the original inclusion. All quotient actions are actual conjugation actions through this embedding.

Argument or construction

The quotient base is G / (P.subgroupOf G) and its embedding into A/P is constructed from the original inclusion. The final group-only helpers specialize the checked radical and normalizer quotient transport to this same kernel. The ell-core is used only to prove that an arbitrary normal ell-subgroup lies in every radical subgroup.

External assumptions and dependencies

The ell-core is used only to prove that an arbitrary normal ell-subgroup lies in every radical subgroup. The same radical image gives the canonical local normalizer quotient equivalence used by the later weight and triple transports.
