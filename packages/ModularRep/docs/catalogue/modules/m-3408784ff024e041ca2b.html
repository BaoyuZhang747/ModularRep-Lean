Purpose

Records Li's standard radical subgroups and weight stabiliser factorisation over the specified coefficient fields.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

The certificate assumes a family of standard radical subgroups representing every radical subgroup up to conjugacy, together with the weight stabiliser factorisation, over every coefficient field of characteristic zero containing the roots required by the full semidirect product.

Argument or construction

Conjugating both components of a weight gives the stabiliser factorisation for every weight. Separate cyclic extension assumptions give the four extension clauses on the specified inertia quotients.

External assumptions and dependencies

Li, Section 2.C and Lemma 5.5(1), interpreted as the stated uniform family and stabiliser factorisation assumption. The extension clauses use separate modular and ordinary cyclic extension assumptions.
