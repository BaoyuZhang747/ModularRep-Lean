ModularRep.Twist

ModularRep/Twist.lean

theorem-developmentcurrent

Purpose

This file defines pullback of a representation along a monoid homomorphism and twisting along a monoid automorphism.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

Pullback of a representation along a surjection preserves and reflects irreducibility. Automorphism twists are inverse equivalences and have trace given by function pullback.

Argument or construction

Pull back operators along a homomorphism. Surjectivity identifies the subrepresentation lattices, and inverse automorphisms give inverse twists; trace formulas follow by evaluating the same operator.

External assumptions and dependencies

A representation and a group homomorphism; irreducibility reflection requires surjectivity. Automorphism twists use the inverse group equivalence.
