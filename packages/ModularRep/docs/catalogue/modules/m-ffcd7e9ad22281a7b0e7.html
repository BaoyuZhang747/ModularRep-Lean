ModularRep.CentralBrauerMapToBlockExpansion

ModularRep/CentralBrauerMapToBlockExpansion.lean

theorem-developmentcurrent

Purpose

This file expands the interval central Brauer image of one raw ambient primitive central idempotent in a separately supplied local primitive idempotent family.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

The interval Brauer image of a primitive ambient block idempotent is a sum of its supported local primitive block idempotents and is nonzero exactly when that local support is nonempty.

Argument or construction

Map an ambient primitive idempotent through the interval Brauer homomorphism. Expand the resulting central idempotent in the supplied local primitive family; its nonvanishing is equivalent to nonempty local support.

External assumptions and dependencies

A p-subgroup, the interval inclusions C_G(P) ≤ H ≤ N_G(P), characteristic-p coefficients and a supplied complete primitive block family for H.
