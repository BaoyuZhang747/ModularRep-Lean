Purpose

FLZ, J. Algebra 604 (2022), Section 3.2, p. 542, defines the xi-dual by the roots xi / alpha.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

Here it is the actual normalized, reversed and root-scaled polynomial. The multiplicity below is polynomial-divisor multiplicity in the same profile characteristic polynomial.

Argument or construction

Finiteness is proved before the guarded natural-number version is exposed. The three component predicates use this formula; in particular an F2 component is the polynomial product, not a choice of one of its irreducible factors.

External assumptions and dependencies

The three component predicates use this formula; in particular an F2 component is the polynomial product, not a choice of one of its irreducible factors. The F0 predicate uses monic irreducible divisors of X^2 - C xi. Its identification with the printed square/nonsquare cases, the even-degree claims, primary-decomposition multiplicity interpretation and all partition/symbol/core model identifications are separate obligations.
