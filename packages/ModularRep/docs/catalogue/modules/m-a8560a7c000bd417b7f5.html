Purpose

Aligns local and ambient exact-defect predicates on one operations catalogue.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

Two Navarro 4.11 support criteria for the common local block and a bare ambient target imply their exact-defect representative packages.

Argument or construction

The local table's radicality gives the p-group conditions, including the copy of Q in its normalizer. Pair these with the support criteria. The older named-nonprincipal facade converts to the bare-target core without using other blocks or weight operations.

External assumptions and dependencies

Actual fixed-Q local operations and two central Brauer support criteria. No First Main image, block induction, coverage or concrete Fischer/table identification is supplied.
