Purpose

Proves the wreath reflection obstruction at group level.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

A noncentral commuting involution on the coefficient space gives a constant-coordinate involution outside the permutation wreath subgroup.

Argument or construction

Wreath multiplication verifies centralization and square one. Faithful maps and independent product factors preserve the obstruction and identify the embedded centre.

External assumptions and dependencies

The reflection and actual source realisation are separate geometric and classification inputs.
