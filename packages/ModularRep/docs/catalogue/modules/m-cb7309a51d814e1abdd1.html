Purpose

This module contains the generic deductions used in both the rank-three and higher-rank arguments at the prime 2: the odd-order lift and centraliser calculations for a central double cover, the Bonnafé order-four deduction, and the promotion of a constituent-orbit factorisation to the full field group.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

The shared arguments use central double-cover maps, odd-order lifts, and an actual constituent orbit with compatible actions.

Argument or construction

An odd-order element has a unique odd-order lift across the central two-kernel, controlling its centralizer. Separately, a combined character fixer forces preservation of the constituent orbit, promoting factorization from its setwise field stabilizer.

External assumptions and dependencies

The exact finite group data used from the simply connected symplectic double cover of the adjoint dual group. The concrete algebraic-group model and the fact that its kernel has order two are external structural inputs.
