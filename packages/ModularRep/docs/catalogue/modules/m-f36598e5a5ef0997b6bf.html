Purpose

Restricts the original root convention along a finite surjective group map.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

A surjection gives exponent divisibility, a derived root embedding on the target and compatibility with Brauer-character pullback.

Argument or construction

Target cardinality divides source cardinality, and taking the p-prime part preserves divisibility. Restrict the original root equivalence along that divisor and apply common-root compatibility.

External assumptions and dependencies

The actual surjective homomorphism and the original root embedding.
