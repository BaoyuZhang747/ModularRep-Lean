Purpose

Derives the two automorphism cosets from the literal outer quotient.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

If the actual outer quotient has order two and the selected automorphism is non-inner, every automorphism is inner or inner times the selected one.

Argument or construction

The quotient has exactly one nonidentity element. Equality of nontrivial quotient classes gives the second coset, with inverse conventions tracked through the opposite group.

External assumptions and dependencies

Cardinality two of the actual opposite outer quotient and a specified automorphism whose quotient class is not the identity. No chosen square relation or table action is assumed.
