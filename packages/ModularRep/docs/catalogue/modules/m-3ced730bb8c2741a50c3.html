Purpose

Connects the corrected quaternion tensor factor to the reflection obstruction.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

The left multiplicity reflection commutes with the right quaternion tensor action and remains a noncentral involution in the wreath ambient group.

Argument or construction

Kronecker multiplication proves commutation. The faithful ambient map transfers the involution, contradicting the necessary Sylow-centre property of a principal weight.

The actual FYZ factor/image interpretation and the sourced orthogonal reflection geometry.
