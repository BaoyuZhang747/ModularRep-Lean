Purpose

Compatibility of the two actions defines the semidirect product action by applying the right factor then the left.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

Compatible factor actions define a semidirect action, with the stated stabilizer factorization when the right actor fixes the point; trivial left actions also extend right-equivariance to the semidirect product.

Argument or construction

Compatibility of the two actions defines the semidirect product action by applying the right factor then the left. If the right factor fixes the chosen point, the stabilizer condition reduces to the left one; trivial left actions also transport equivariance.

External assumptions and dependencies

Two group actions and the displayed semidirect compatibility. Fixedness/triviality hypotheses are assumed only in the corresponding stabilizer and equivariance specializations.
