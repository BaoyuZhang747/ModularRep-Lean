Purpose

Forces Q=1 normalization on the same faithful block-preserving map.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

Canonical local availability, block compatibility and defect-zero ordinary-block uniqueness imply SameMapQOneOutput.

Argument or construction

The canonical operations place the trivial weight in the block of the defect-zero reduction. Apply the inverse faithful map to that weight; block preservation and singleton Brauer-block uniqueness identify its preimage. Injectivity of classAt then identifies every retained Q=1 ordinary row and its global transport.

External assumptions and dependencies

Defect-zero and trivial-weight sources, original defect-zero block uniqueness, canonical reductions and compatibility.
