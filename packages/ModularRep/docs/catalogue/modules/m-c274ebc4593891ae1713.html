transport-adapterauxiliary

Purpose

Identifies fixed ordinary local characters with fixed ambient weight classes.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

For an automorphism stabilizing Q, classAt is injective and intertwines local twisting with the weight action, including fixed block fibres.

Argument or construction

Construct the local twist by transporting the entire raw weight. Injectivity of the representative fibre map converts fixedness of the weight class into equality of the ordinary local character. Restrict to the block fibre using automorphism transport of raw blocks.

External assumptions and dependencies

The representative local-character equivalence and literal local block transport.
