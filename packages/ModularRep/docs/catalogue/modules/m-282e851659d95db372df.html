Purpose

This file packages the full subgroup interval used by Navarro's Theorem (4.14).

Mathematical statement and assumptions

CentralBrauerInterval records a p-subgroup P and an intermediate subgroup H with P C_G(P) ≤ H ≤ N_G(P), supplying the inclusions required by the interval Brauer map.

Argument or construction

Package the inclusions P C_G(P) ≤ H ≤ N_G(P) and the p-group hypothesis. Their projections give the centralizer inclusion required by the constructed interval Brauer map.

External assumptions and dependencies

A p-subgroup P and the two interval inclusions P C_G(P) ≤ H and H ≤ N_G(P).
