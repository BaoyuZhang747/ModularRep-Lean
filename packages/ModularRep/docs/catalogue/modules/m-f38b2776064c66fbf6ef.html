Purpose

Constructs the identity p-prime cover from a universal cover with p-group kernel.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

A nonabelian simple group admitting the supplied full cover with p-group kernel is its own maximal p-prime cover; kernel order three gives the Fi24 specialization.

Argument or construction

A map from the p-group kernel into a p-prime kernel is trivial by coprime element orders. Factor the universal lift through the base to obtain a section of any competing central p-prime extension. Perfectness makes this section surjective, giving the required maximality.

External assumptions and dependencies

The actual universal covering map and its kernel hypothesis, plus simplicity and noncommutativity of the base.
