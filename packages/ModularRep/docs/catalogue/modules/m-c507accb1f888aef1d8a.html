Purpose

The actual Brauer stabilizer modulo its embedded inner subgroup embeds in the literal outer automorphism quotient.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

The actual Brauer stabilizer modulo its inner base embeds in the outer quotient, so an outer quotient of order two gives a cyclic quotient of order at most two.

Argument or construction

Construct the quotient map and prove injectivity from the inner-kernel calculation. The resulting cardinal bound excludes proper intermediate subgroups above the base without requiring a splitting.
