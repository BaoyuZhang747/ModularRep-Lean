Purpose

Constructs actual Brauer inflation and principal fibres through Sp's centre.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

The normal two-core equals the centre, and compatible inflation gives an equivariant Brauer equivalence preserving the principal block.

Argument or construction

The simple projective quotient kills the two-core downstairs. Representation descent constructs the character map, and primitive block projection identifies the principal fibres.

External assumptions and dependencies

Navarro kernel-triviality, regular lifts and Theorem 9.10 on the actual central two-kernel.
