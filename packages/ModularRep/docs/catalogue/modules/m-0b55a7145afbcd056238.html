Purpose

Constructs raw normalizer reductions from quotient reduction sources while retaining their characters.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

A quotient Brauer reduction for each raw weight gives its inflated normalizer reduction; selected and raw families can be converted, and normalized Q=1 matches satisfy the base-character identity.

Argument or construction

Inflate the quotient representation through the normal p-subgroup with the derived normalizer root convention. Transport a selected reduction to an arbitrary representative by an inner conjugator of the entire raw pair. At Q=1, injectivity of the normalized correspondence identifies the global defect-zero reduction and hence the local character values.

External assumptions and dependencies

Quotient Navarro reduction data, the selected reduction family where used, and defect-zero/trivial-weight sources plus normalization for Q=1.
