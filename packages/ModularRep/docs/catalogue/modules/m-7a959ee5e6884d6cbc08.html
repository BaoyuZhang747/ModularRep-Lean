Purpose

Hensel lifting for X^m - 1 makes the actual residue map bijective on integral roots of positive order prime to the residue characteristic.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

The actual modular system is complete and the finite root orders are positive and coprime to the residue characteristic.

Argument or construction

Hensel lifting and uniqueness identify integral roots with residue roots. Composing with the fraction-field inclusion gives coherent cross-characteristic root equivalences under divisibility.

External assumptions and dependencies

Their composition gives the cross-characteristic root equivalence, with uniqueness and coherence under order divisibility.
