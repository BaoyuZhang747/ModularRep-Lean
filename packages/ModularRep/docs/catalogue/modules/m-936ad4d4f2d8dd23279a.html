Purpose

Proves vanishing of the conductor moduli at retained roots.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

The six local and seven full conductor roots obtained as powers of a primitive 770385th root annihilate their assigned moduli.

Argument or construction

Each conductor is handled by its primitive-root relation. The proof translates the explicit polynomial arrays into the corresponding cyclotomic equations.

External assumptions and dependencies

A primitive 770385th root and the stored conductor/modulus arrays. The needed small-conductor root relations are imported proved identities.
