Purpose

Proves the group hypotheses of the Brough butterfly step.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

The natural map from the PSp holomorph to the PCSp/field ambient group has the required global image and local inverse-image laws.

Argument or construction

Both groups act through the same automorphism subgroup. Explicit action squares prove stabilizer preimages and preserve the own raw pair.

External assumptions and dependencies

The fixed natural full-automorphism realisations; no butterfly relation theorem is applied here.
