theorem-developmentauxiliary

Purpose

Constructs original-group gamma-product comparisons.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

A faithful small-centre pair with inversion data, or any same-scalar pair when the actual outer quotient is trivial, admits the concrete original model comparison.

Argument or construction

The original group surjects onto the relevant stabilizer ambient in the indicated cases. Top-base models have trivial factor sets, and their scalar product formulas give the gamma and cocycle comparison under the natural local quotient map.

External assumptions and dependencies

The original same-scalar Brauer/raw-weight pair and the local scalar equation. The faithful branch uses faithfulness, centre order 3, 4 or 6, centre inversion and outer order two; the outer-trivial branch uses only outer order one instead of those extra centre conditions.
