Purpose

Proves the ordinary-character half using rational-series labels.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

The prime regular part of a unipotent block equals its identity-series part and is pointwise fixed by field automorphisms.

Argument or construction

A character's prime-power and prime regular labels are conjugate, hence both trivial. Universal unipotent fixation then applies.

External assumptions and dependencies

Block-series membership, disjoint rational series and unipotent-field invariance.
