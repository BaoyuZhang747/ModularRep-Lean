Purpose

Constructs a bare global Brauer-character extension for any cyclic outer carrier.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

For finite H and cyclic E acting on H, the selected character extends to its semidirect stabilizer when the supplied ambient/base roots agree and the base character is correctly identified.

Argument or construction

Inner character invariance identifies the whole H with the embedded base. The generic cyclic representation-extension result gives an irreducible extension; root agreement establishes the compatibility needed to turn its Brauer character into the stated extension witness.

External assumptions and dependencies

The cyclic Brauer extension principle, exact base-character transport and ambient/base root agreement. No block or weight hypothesis is used.
