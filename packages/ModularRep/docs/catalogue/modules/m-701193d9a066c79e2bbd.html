Purpose

The norm is followed by the actual unit square-class quotient. Surjectivity comes from explicit Clifford norm preimages.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

The norm is the actual surjective Clifford norm and the narrow centre certificate identifies scalars with the special Clifford centre.

Argument or construction

The norm followed by the finite-field square-class map is surjective. The preimage-of-squares calculation identifies its kernel with the Spin-centre join, constructing the full diagonal source.

External assumptions and dependencies

The exact kernel is the proved Spin-scalar join, with the centre substituted using the narrow literal E1 centre certificate. The former diagonal source is constructed, retaining only the matched one-way external centre certificate on the same finite odd Clifford carrier.
