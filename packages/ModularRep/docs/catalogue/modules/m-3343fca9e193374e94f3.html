Purpose

Computes local tuple coordinates for the literal image of a raw weight.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

The holomorph map induced by e sends raw stabilizers and local intersections to those of the whole image pair, including its own character.

Argument or construction

The raw action equation proves stabilizer membership equivalence. Restriction of the same ambient map gives the canonical normalizer coordinates and full-containment equivalence.

External assumptions and dependencies

A fixed group equivalence and actual image pair; separately selected representatives need later correction.
