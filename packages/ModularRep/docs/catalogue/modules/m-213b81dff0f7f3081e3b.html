Purpose

Separates the elementary kernel deductions behind even-field fixation.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

A prime-power element conjugate to a prime regular element is trivial, so a character in the corresponding intersecting series is unipotent.

Argument or construction

Coprimality kills the semisimple label. A separately fixed inner twist then fixes the original conjugacy class.

External assumptions and dependencies

Series-disjointness and unipotent character fixation are hypotheses, not proved character theory.
