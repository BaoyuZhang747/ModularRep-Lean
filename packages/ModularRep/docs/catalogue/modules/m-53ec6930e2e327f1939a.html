Purpose

Constructs global Brauer extensions in both Brough lanes.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

A character of the actual PSp base extends to its inertia group with a root compatible along the actual base inclusion.

Argument or construction

Inertia membership and the conjugation square prove base-character invariance. Navarro's cyclic extension principle gives a representation and the compatible root identifies its character restriction.

External assumptions and dependencies

The scoped cyclic Brauer extension principle and compatible ambient roots.
