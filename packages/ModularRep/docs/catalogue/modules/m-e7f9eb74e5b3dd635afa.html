transport-adaptercurrent

Purpose

Computes weight-block transport from individual primitive dictionaries.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

Ambient and own-normalizer idempotent equations determine raw block equality and an equivalence of the existing block fibres.

Argument or construction

Central characters are transported through the actual group algebra maps. Uniqueness of induced blocks proves the block equality before the target action source is constructed.

External assumptions and dependencies

Individual primitive equations, without a supplied whole-weight map, block equality or relation.
