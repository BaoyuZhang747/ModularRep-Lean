Formalisation.IBAWBlockWitness

packages/formalisation/Formalisation/IBAWBlockWitness.lean

transport-adaptercurrent

Purpose

A BlockWitness is the set-theoretic and compatibility datum attached to one block.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

Global certified data restrict to a block witness at every block, and an explicitly equivariant family of block witnesses combines back into global data.

Argument or construction

Restrict global certified data to the fibre of one block. Conversely an equivariant family of block witnesses gives coherent fibre maps, whose construction supplies the global certified datum.

External assumptions and dependencies

An existing global certified datum, or a block witness for every block with explicit family equivariance. The actual representation theoretic predicates are those of the supplied Context.
