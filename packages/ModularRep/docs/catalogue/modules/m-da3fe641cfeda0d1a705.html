Purpose

Spin is the full universal covering group in this application. It is not the universal prime-to-two cover.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

The original Spin group is a full universal cover in the nonexceptional scope, and its central two-kernel determines the prime-to-two cover on matrix Omega.

Argument or construction

Apply the composite Jordan reduction source after proving the stronger hypothesis for the full field group and the complete strict-block hypothesis. Choose dominating blocks through their primitive idempotents and use the published passage to a family of complete block witnesses.

External assumptions and dependencies

The E2 composite combines Feng–Li–Zhang Theorem 5.7 with the choice of its groups inside the full field group, restriction of the stabiliser and extension property, the central O2 quotient and Späth Theorem 4.4. It takes the full-field and complete strict-block hypotheses, which are constructed before the source is applied. The group choice and restriction are not separately implemented.
