Purpose

Discharges the principal Spin field antecedent before applying the published principal Omega theorem.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

The result supplies an equivariant bijection with coherent own-match extension/block data, followed by authenticated BAW-good or Definition 3.5 interpretations.

Argument or construction

Raw GGGR sources prove field fixation. FYZ Theorem 5.16 and the Späth block formulation then produce the matching, and same-pair normalization preserves its characters and roots.

External assumptions and dependencies
