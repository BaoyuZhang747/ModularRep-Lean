Purpose

The two inertia groups here are literal stabilizers of a Spin Brauer character and a Spin character-weight CONJUGACY CLASS under special Clifford conjugation.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

The Hall input is a subgroup of the literal quotient special Clifford/Spin, with prime-to-ell order and ell-power index. Its preimage is defined by the actual quotient map.

Argument or construction

Inner Spin conjugation and central special Clifford conjugation fix these carriers, so both inertias contain Spin sup center by checked deductions. Its preimage is defined by the actual quotient map. The checked Type B index argument proves that the join of either actual downstairs inertia with this preimage is the whole special Clifford group.

External assumptions and dependencies

The Hall input is a subgroup of the literal quotient special Clifford/Spin, with prime-to-ell order and ell-power index. This routine cyclic-quotient source is the one used in Brough--Spaeth Section 2, Lemmas 2.14--2.15, and FLZ Remark 2.2, p. 537. The published definitions of J_G, and the precise constituent or covered weight chosen there, remain separate source joins.
