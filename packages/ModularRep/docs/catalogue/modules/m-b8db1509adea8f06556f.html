Purpose

This file isolates the remaining classical representation theoretic input for the decomposition map.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

A modular system, finite root embedding and uniform stable-reduction character compatibility determine the exact decomposition homomorphism, realize every lattice reduction and give its automorphism-twist naturality.

Argument or construction

Choose stable lattice reductions and identify their images under the injective Brauer K-zero map with ordinary restriction. Injectivity proves choice independence, exact additivity, the decomposition square and twist naturality.

External assumptions and dependencies

A complete modular system, explicit finite root correspondence and uniform StableReductionBrauerCharacterCompatibility. Tensor conclusions also require BrauerLinearTensorProductFormula and matching ordinary/modular linear character roots; label/field parameterizations are the displayed ones.
