Purpose

Combines the simple Type B census through actual rank-one, symplectic, exceptional B2(2) derived-group and odd high-rank identifications.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

The latter covers simple PSL_n(q), PSU_n(q) for n <= 7 at all primes, including the exceptional PSL2(9). Its source application below uses the actual universal prime-to-ell cover, not an assumed SL2 full cover.

Argument or construction

The low/even structural inputs are actual finite group equivalences from the same matrix Omega carrier. The exceptional rank-two field-two case uses its derived convention and maps to PSL2(9), not to nonsimple Sp4(2). The complete witness is obtained only by the application below.
