ModularRep.CentralCharacterTwist

ModularRep/CentralCharacterTwist.lean

transport-adaptercurrent

Purpose

For the convention rho.twist alpha = rho.comp alpha, the central character of the twisted representation is the pullback of the original central character along the automorphism induced by alpha on the centre.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

The scalar central character of an automorphism twist is the corresponding pullback of the original central character, whenever the displayed central subgroup is preserved.

Argument or construction

Pull the scalar central action through an automorphism and use uniqueness of the central character. The resulting character is exactly the pullback along the induced automorphism of the central subgroup.

External assumptions and dependencies

An irreducible representation over algebraically closed k and an automorphism preserving the displayed central subgroup.
