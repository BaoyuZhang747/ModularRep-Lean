Purpose

Joins two already chosen sector equivalences without choosing a new family.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

Trivial-sector and faithful-sector equivalences on a prime-order center produce a global equivalence; their action and block laws imply the global laws.

Argument or construction

Conjugate the sum of the two equivalences by the literal disjoint-union carrier equivalences. Compute its value on each summand. A case split on whether the Brauer sector equals one proves equivariance and block preservation; applying the block-sector function gives sector preservation.

External assumptions and dependencies

The two given equivalences and their respective equivariance and block-preservation hypotheses.
