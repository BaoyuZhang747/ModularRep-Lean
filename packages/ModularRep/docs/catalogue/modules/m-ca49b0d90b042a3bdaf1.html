Purpose

Checks the scaled polynomial inverse identities for selected basis rows 15–19.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

For basis indices 15–19, every one of the 41 target columns and seven conductor groups satisfies its exact inverse residual identity, and the constants sum to the scaled Kronecker delta.

Argument or construction

Each statement is a closed decidable equality of the literal integer-polynomial data and is proved by decide. Summing the seven conductor contributions is what turns the grouped residuals into the common-denominator inverse identity used by the rank certificate.

External assumptions and dependencies

The literal row, inverse, reconstruction and conductor arrays; no actual character-table or class identification.
