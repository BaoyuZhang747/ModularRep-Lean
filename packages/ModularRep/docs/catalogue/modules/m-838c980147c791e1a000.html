Purpose

Counts the actual faithful small block from its five ordinary rows.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

Complete specified allocation of the 74 faithful rows to two blocks and the literal value dictionary imply that the second block has two Brauer characters.

Argument or construction

The finite printed-label fibre proves that the five selected ordinary rows exhaust that specified block. The rank-two certificate then supplies its Brauer count.

External assumptions and dependencies

The same faithful ordinary decomposition/catalogue, complete allocation to the two specified sector blocks, actual regular representatives, same-iota conductor root and full value dictionary. The five-row rank-two certificate is imported as proved.
