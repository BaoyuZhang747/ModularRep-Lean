Purpose

Partitions fixed sector weights by their fixed radical class.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

The sigma of sector and fixed weight fibres over fixed radical classes is equivalent to the full fixed sector carrier.

Argument or construction

Equivariance of the radical map makes the radical class of a fixed weight fixed. Reorder the three subtype predicates and use the standard sigma-of-fibres equivalence.

External assumptions and dependencies

A complete radical index and equivariance of its class map.
