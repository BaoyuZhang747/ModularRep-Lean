Purpose

Proves operator-valued conjugacy-orbit cancellation for a p-subgroup in the representation kernel.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

A central group algebra element with zero central Brauer restriction acts by zero in any representation whose kernel contains the p-subgroup; normal p-subgroups give the irreducible specialization.

Argument or construction

Apply all linear functionals to extend the scalar fixed-point sum formula to module-valued sums. In the operator sum, central coefficients and trivial action of the p-subgroup make every conjugacy orbit contribution invariant. Nonfixed p-orbits vanish in characteristic p, leaving coefficients on the centralizer, all zero by the restriction hypothesis.

External assumptions and dependencies

The p-group fixed-point sum theorem and the preceding theorem that a normal p-subgroup lies in the kernel of an irreducible representation.
