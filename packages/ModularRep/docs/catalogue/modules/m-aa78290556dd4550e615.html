Purpose

Enlarges a Levi stabilizer factorization to the regular overgroup.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

The ambient group is the product of the inner base and the literal Levi acting group.

Argument or construction

Every ambient action can be replaced by its Levi factor because the inner base acts trivially on characters. The Levi product factorization therefore implies the ambient semidirect factorization.

External assumptions and dependencies

Actual inclusion/product equations, inner triviality and compatible actions supplied by the specified Jordan consumer.
