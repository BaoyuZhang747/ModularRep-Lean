Purpose

Define prime regularity by coprimality of element order with p.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

Prime regularity means coprimality of element order with p; it is preserved by homomorphisms and holds for every element of a group of order prime to p.

Argument or construction

Define prime regularity by coprimality of element order with p. Order divisibility proves preservation under homomorphisms and the automatic regularity of elements in a group of prime-to-p order.

External assumptions and dependencies

The stated group and element-order hypotheses; finite group order divisibility is used in its prime-to-order specialization.
