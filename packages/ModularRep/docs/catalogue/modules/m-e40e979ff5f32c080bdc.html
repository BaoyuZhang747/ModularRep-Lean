Purpose

Instantiates the component-cycle representative construction after binding the original monomial action, its full return and the prescribed Brauer-character product.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

That presentation does not cover an arbitrary permutation of factors: a return automorphism need not have a root of the cycle length. Here successors are normalised to the identity and the wrap retains the actual return automorphism.

Argument or construction

We reuse its product-orbit and cyclic-fixedness deductions and the checked local semidirect stabiliser lemma. A presentation of arbitrary original factors by these normalised cycle coordinates remains a separate group-carrier obligation. The global orbit stabiliser and each return subgroup are computed from the actual character orbit and the selected generator. generator_first and generator_succ describe group automorphisms, not character fixedness. return_coordinate and return_diagonal bind the local return homomorphisms to restrictions of the same actual ambient actions.

External assumptions and dependencies

The finite-product Brauer theorem is a narrow E1 input: Navarro, *Characters and Blocks of Finite Groups*, product definition p. 176 and Theorem (8.21), p. 177, iterated over a finite index. A presentation of arbitrary original factors by these normalised cycle coordinates remains a separate group-carrier obligation. The only stabiliser factorisation premise is the local hypothesis of the lemma.
