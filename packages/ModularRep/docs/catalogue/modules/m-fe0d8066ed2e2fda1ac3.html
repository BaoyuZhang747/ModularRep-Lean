Purpose

Proves the full relative fixation theorem with one rational-Levi choice per pair.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

Uniform field-automorphism compatibility and the source character packages imply both components of FixationConclusion.

Argument or construction

The ordinary half uses series disjointness. The generic half applies the unified pair proof to each representative and each field element.

External assumptions and dependencies

Pointwise algebraic lifts are allowed; no generally impossible homomorphic lift of the finite cyclic field action is required.
