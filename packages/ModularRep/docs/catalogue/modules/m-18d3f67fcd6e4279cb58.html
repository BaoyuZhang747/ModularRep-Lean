carrier-constructioncurrent

Purpose

Constructs tensor and field actions on actual character-weight classes.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

An ordinary lift trivial on each radical descends to its normalizer quotient and twists the own defect-zero character without changing its radical.

Argument or construction

Character transport proves defect preservation, group laws and descent through isomorphism and conjugacy. The right-action formulas give the combined semidirect action.

External assumptions and dependencies

The exact radical-kernel property of the chosen ordinary lifts, without any weight correspondence or block assignment.
