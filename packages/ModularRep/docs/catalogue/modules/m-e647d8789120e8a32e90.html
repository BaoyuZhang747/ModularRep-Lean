Purpose

Identifies whole ordinary root-lift functions across the principal Spin and orthogonal diagram.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

The functions include their zero values outside the finite prime-to-two root domain.

Argument or construction

The central two-quotient and index-two inclusion preserve the prime-to-two part of group order. Equal residue conventions and equal exponents therefore identify the entire lift functions.

External assumptions and dependencies

One modular system, actual central quotient and matrix equivalence, index two and compatible root conventions.
