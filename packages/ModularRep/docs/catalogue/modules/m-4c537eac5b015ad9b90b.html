Purpose

Constructs the natural diagonal quotient isomorphism from the actual Levi inclusion.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

The rational product decomposition and derived-intersection kernel construct a natural quotient equivalence onto finite Clifford modulo its literal Spin norm kernel.

Argument or construction

The geometric intersection computes its kernel as the original rational Levi. The derived rational product proves surjectivity, so the first isomorphism theorem gives the quotient equivalence and its point formula.

External assumptions and dependencies

The fixed-point inclusion, original intersection and proved finite product; no abstract quotient isomorphism source.
