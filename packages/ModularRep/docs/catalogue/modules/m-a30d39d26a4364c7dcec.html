Purpose

Derives commutation and addition laws for Frobenius powers.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

Powers of one endomorphism commute and act on its iterated fixed points with exponents reduced modulo the defining exponent.

Argument or construction

Function-iterate identities supply commutation and addition. Fixed-point membership proves the defining-exponent reduction.
