Purpose

Converts a selected minor right inverse and row reconstruction into an exact rank theorem.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

A RowInverseCertificate for n selected rows implies their full row span has dimension n.

Argument or construction

Taking determinants in the right-inverse identity proves the selected minor determinant is nonzero. Insert that fact and the stored reconstruction coefficients into FiniteRowRankCertificate and apply its rank theorem.

External assumptions and dependencies

The exact right-inverse and reconstruction identities stored in the certificate.
