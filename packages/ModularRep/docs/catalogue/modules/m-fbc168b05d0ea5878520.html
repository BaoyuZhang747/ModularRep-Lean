Purpose

Transports outer quotients through literal group equivalences.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

A group equivalence induces an equivalence of opposite outer quotients; an own quotient with full central kernel inherits the simple quotient's outer order.

Argument or construction

Conjugation naturality identifies the inner-inverse ranges. The universal covering map supplies the own-quotient equivalence used in the cardinal-two specialization.

External assumptions and dependencies

A group equivalence for the general transport. The own-quotient specialization uses the actual universal cover, simple nonabelian quotient, full-central-kernel equation and the quotient outer-order-two fact.
