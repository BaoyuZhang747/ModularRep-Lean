Purpose

Extracts ordinary local bijections from the faithful correspondence.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

For a central sector and radical representative, the Brauer fibre is equivalent to the retained-root local defect-zero sector, and the resulting weight class is the original image.

Argument or construction

The construction composes the global fibre equivalence with the inverse local-to-weight equivalence. Injectivity of the representative class map shows that changing available canonical reductions leaves the local map unchanged.

External assumptions and dependencies

The same faithful family and its sector-preservation law, canonical reductions of all actual local ordinary rows at the selected radical, and canonical local block compatibility. The output local map is proved independent of those reductions.
