ModularRep.OrdinaryIrreducibleCharacterSurjectiveDescent

transport-adaptercurrent

Purpose

This neutral module descends a function-valued ordinary irreducible character when its value is constant at the degree on the kernel.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

A kernel-constant ordinary irreducible character descends uniquely along a surjection, inversely to inflation. A prime-to-p kernel also preserves its defect-zero degree condition.

Argument or construction

Kernel constancy implies representation kernel containment by averaging, so the representation descends along the surjection. Its trace gives inverse inflation/descent; a prime-to-p kernel preserves the full p-part needed for defect zero.

External assumptions and dependencies

A surjective homomorphism from a finite group, a field of characteristic zero, and an ordinary irreducible character constant at its degree on the kernel. Descent of defect zero additionally assumes that p does not divide the kernel order. Finite averaging proves the required kernel containment.
