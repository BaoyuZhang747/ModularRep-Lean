Purpose

Glue the quotient construction of the trivial central sector to separately supplied faithful-sector data.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

A quotient factory for the trivial central sector and separate faithful-sector coverage produce the original-cover sector family under compatible pair laws.

Argument or construction

Construct the trivial-sector data through the quotient factory, retain its roots and descent carriers, and glue with the faithful-sector data. The argument continues to require the supplied An–Dietrich base source and pairwise equivariance.

External assumptions and dependencies

The finite actual group, characteristic prime, compatible coefficient/root data, specified sets of primitive blocks and characters, and the imported transport results are fixed as in the declaration context. The initial sector correspondence and its stabilizer, block and pair laws are supplied. Full extension and intermediate-block conclusions additionally use their stated certification or Späth inputs.
