ModularRep.OrdinaryIrreducibleCharacter

ModularRep/OrdinaryIrreducibleCharacter.lean

carrier-constructioncurrent

Purpose

Define ordinary irreducible characters as functions with an irreducible finite-coordinate realization.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

The carrier of ordinary irreducible characters consists of functions with an irreducible finite-coordinate realization; automorphism pullback defines the corresponding twist on this carrier.

Argument or construction

Define ordinary irreducible characters as functions with an irreducible finite-coordinate realization. Pullback along automorphisms twists that realization and supplies the exact function-valued action.

External assumptions and dependencies

The ordinary coefficient field and existence of the displayed finite irreducible realization of each character function. The carrier definition itself asserts no splitting theorem.
