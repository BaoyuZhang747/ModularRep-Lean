carrier-constructioncurrent

Purpose

Constructs the central quotient of the full Sp and PSp holomorphs.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

The canonical projection is surjective with embedded Sp centre as kernel, and actual global and raw-pair stabilizers have exact preimages.

Argument or construction

The projective automorphism map is fixed before the lifting input is used. Weight injectivity and compatible Brauer inflation prove the two stabilizer calculations.

External assumptions and dependencies

Full-cover automorphism lifting and literal quotient naturality, not supplied stabilizer equalities.
