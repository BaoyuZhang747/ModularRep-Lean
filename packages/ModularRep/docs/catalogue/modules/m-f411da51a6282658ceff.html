Purpose

Constructs the intrinsic selected reduction from the fixed family table.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

The inverse computed weight fibre map chooses the family weight, and equality of classes gives an inner correction of its whole own-character pair.

Argument or construction

The corrected normalizer and quotient equivalences transport exactly the stored family reduction. No equivariant representative selection is required.

External assumptions and dependencies

Actual class equality and the fixed family localReduction, without independent-root equality or new reduction existence.
