theorem-developmentauxiliary

Purpose

Transport a faithful central-sector correspondence to the global sets of characters and weights.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

An equivariant bijection on one faithful central sector extends to all sectors in its automorphism orbit, preserving block and radical-local data when the corresponding input laws hold.

Argument or construction

Transport both sides by a chosen automorphism and compare two choices through the stabilizer of the initial sector. Stabilizer equivariance proves independence, after which covariance and the supplied pair condition descend to the transported family.

External assumptions and dependencies

The finite actual group, characteristic prime, compatible coefficient/root data, specified sets of primitive blocks and characters, and the imported transport results are fixed as in the declaration context. The initial sector correspondence and its stabilizer, block and pair laws are supplied. Full extension and intermediate-block conclusions additionally use their stated certification or Späth inputs.
