Purpose

Iterate each component transport from its chosen representative.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

Positive-length component cycles with a specified return-fixed representative admit a tuple satisfying every forward transport equation and the last-to-first wrap equation.

Argument or construction

Iterate each component transport from its chosen representative. The supplied full-return equality proves the wrap at the end of each cycle and gives a coherent tuple of all coordinates.

External assumptions and dependencies

Positive component cycle lengths and the full-return equality of each iterated transport at its chosen representative.
