Formalisation.FibreTransport

packages/formalisation/Formalisation/FibreTransport.lean

theorem-developmentcurrent

Purpose

This file formalises the abstract orbit argument used when local bijections are combined over a group orbit.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

A stabilizer-equivariant equivalence on one fibre over a transitive A-set extends by transport to an equivariant equivalence of total spaces, independently of transporter choices and preserving the projection.

Argument or construction

Choose a transporter from the base index to every point of its orbit and conjugate the base fibre equivalence by it. Stabilizer equivariance cancels the difference between choices and proves global equivariance and projection preservation.

External assumptions and dependencies

Equivariant projections to one transitive index orbit, a base fibre equivalence and its stabilizer equivariance; transporters exist by transitivity.
