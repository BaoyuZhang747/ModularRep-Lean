Purpose

Proves the linear-algebra step from fixed GGGR basis to fixed Brauer labels.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

Given an equivariant injective PIM labeling and a fixed basis of the corresponding projective space, proves all principal Brauer labels fixed by every actor.

Argument or construction

Equality on basis vectors makes every acting linear map the identity. Equivariance and injectivity of PIM indexing transfer that fixation to the Brauer labels.

External assumptions and dependencies

The previously constructed basis and standard projective–Brauer indexing; this file does not construct GGGRs or PIMs.
