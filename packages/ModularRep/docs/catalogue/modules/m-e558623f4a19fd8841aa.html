Purpose

The accepted component-return deduction is applied once, then its actual character is transported back to the prescribed root on N <= Gamma.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

The diagonal quotient need not be faithful: surjectivity and a point-level conjugation square identify the orbits. The same field action identifies their setwise stabilizers, and fixation lifts the factorization to Gamma.

Argument or construction

All global orbit/fixation/factorization conclusions are derived; only the per-cycle selector is supplied here. The product presentation, surjective diagonal map and its conjugation square are intermediate outputs of the regular-Levi application.
