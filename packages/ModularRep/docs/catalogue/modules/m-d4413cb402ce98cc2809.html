Purpose

This file formalises the abstract linear-algebra step used in eq:restriction-rank in the computational appendix.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

A surjective coefficient map followed by injective evaluation has range dimension equal to the evaluation index count. A full spanning row family gives the equivalent restriction-matrix formulation.

Argument or construction

Surjectivity of the coefficient map makes its composite with evaluation have the same range as evaluation. Independence of the Brauer-model vectors identifies that range dimension with their index count.

External assumptions and dependencies

A surjective coefficient map or full spanning row family and an injective evaluation map/independent character-model vectors. Establishing these hypotheses for a computed block requires its separate decomposition and table inputs.
