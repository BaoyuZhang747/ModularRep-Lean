Purpose

Specializes the prime-centre family to the actual triple cover.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

Away from three, a universal cover with kernel order three and centre inversion supplies the prime-to-p cover and all original packets for the given normalized map.

Argument or construction

The same covering map identifies its centre and proves the prime-centre and moved-centre facts. The general prime-centre family is then applied without changing the correspondence.

External assumptions and dependencies

A prime different from three, an actual universal central cover with kernel order three over a nonabelian simple quotient, quotient outer order two, and the specified two-coset automorphism decomposition and centre inversion. The normalized equivariant block-preserving map, canonical reduction/compatibility, quotient data, trivial-sector ambient data and roots, cyclic extension, coefficient field and Navarro restriction/covering/twist principles are still supplied.
