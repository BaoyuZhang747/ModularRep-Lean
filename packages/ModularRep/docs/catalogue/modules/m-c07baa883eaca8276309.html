Purpose

Constructs a positive-radical trivial-sector actual weight packet.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

For a nontrivial radical, the full-cover quotient data, compatible match and ambient block sources yield OriginalActualWeightPacket.

Argument or construction

Construct the individual extensions, derive base induction, choose a compatible cyclic top extension and reduce each intermediate subgroup to base or top. Transport the original restrictions. The Q=1 implication is vacuous because the radical is nontrivial.

External assumptions and dependencies

Universal cover and simple base, p-prime center and sector values, canonical reductions and block compatibility, quotient and ambient data, same Omega, outer order two, extension and Navarro 9.2/9.5, 9.4/9.5 and 8.20 principles.
