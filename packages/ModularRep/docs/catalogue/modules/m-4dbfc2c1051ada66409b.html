Purpose

Constructs the rank-three principal Omega seed and its SO correspondence from derived orbit counts.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

The independent counts and specified weight covering are the numerical sources, not a supplied seed or orbit-count conclusion.

Argument or construction

Green/Clifford constituent orbits derive the required orbit arithmetic. Finite involution matching builds Omega and orbit descent builds SO, preserving occurrence/covering and matched Hall products.

External assumptions and dependencies

Independent principal counts, calibrated covering, Green/Clifford/principal sources and actual index-two actions.
