ModularRep.RadicalSubgroup

ModularRep/RadicalSubgroup.lean

carrier-constructioncurrent

Purpose

For a subgroup Q ≤ G, its normaliser is regarded as a group in its own right.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

A radical subgroup is exactly the image of the p-core of its own normalizer and consequently is a p-group.

Argument or construction

Define radicality by equality with the image of the normalizer p-core. The image is a p-group, so the definition provides the p-subgroup property needed by weights.

External assumptions and dependencies

The constructed p-core of the actual normalizer and its embedding into the ambient group; radicality is the displayed equality.
