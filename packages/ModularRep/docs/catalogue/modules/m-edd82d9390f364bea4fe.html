Purpose

Constructs the all-orbit Brauer representative hypothesis on the actual Spin and special Clifford groups from the principal supplier and the prescribed nonprincipal Levi/Jordan packets.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

At prime two and odd defining characteristic, rank is at least three and every nonprincipal primitive block has its same-parameter proper-Levi/Jordan source on the original groups.

Argument or construction

The prime-field case is immediate from trivial field action. Principal characters use the separate specified principal fixation theorem; otherwise invert the complete Jordan packet, construct the same-y Levi representative, and transport its factorization and cyclic extension back to the original Spin orbit.

External assumptions and dependencies

Requires the actual principal GGGR/fixation sources, the universal prime-two cyclic extension principle, and a same-parameter LeviSource for every nonprincipal primitive block. Each such source retains the minimal-Levi geometry, component data, specified block interpretation, common roots, multiplicity-free/Clifford principles and published equivariant modular Jordan bijection.
