Purpose

Binds the fixed family's selected quotient roots to one ambient table.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

Each stored family quotient root is a restriction of the transported ambient table, and its actual transport gives the intrinsic convention.

Argument or construction

The selected quotient equivalence transfers the binding in the kernel. The downstairs root is chosen as the corresponding restriction, making that part reflexive.

External assumptions and dependencies

The source table-identification record; arbitrary own-root packet transport is a separate theorem.
