carrier-constructioncurrent

Purpose

Defines specified Spin decomposition numbers from stable-lattice reductions.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

For the same modular system and ordinary/Brauer roots, stable-lattice Jordan-Hoelder coordinates define nonnegative decomposition numbers and prove the character expansion, twist formula and specified ordinary-block support dictionary.

Argument or construction

The Grothendieck-group coordinate expansion gives the decomposition row. Stable-reduction compatibility proves the actual character formula and automorphism naturality, while the ordinary block selector is tied to this support.

External assumptions and dependencies

One modular system, sufficient roots, stable-reduction/Brauer compatibility and Navarro column independence on these exact coefficients.
