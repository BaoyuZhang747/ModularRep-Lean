computation-bindingauxiliary

Purpose

Stores the representative trivial-sector matrix and scaled inverse arithmetic.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

The finite data contain 108 polynomial rows on 41 columns, 41 chosen basis rows, seven conductor tags, inverse numerator entries, reconstruction coefficients and a positive common denominator.

Argument or construction

The inverse is grouped by conductor tag so its residuals can be evaluated using the corresponding cyclotomic modulus. Reconstruction residuals subtract the explicit integer combination of selected rows. Their required identities are checked in separate modules.

External assumptions and dependencies

The preserved literal arithmetic export.
