ModularRep.CharacterWeightRadicalOrderFixedness

ModularRep/CharacterWeightRadicalOrderFixedness.lean

theorem-developmentcurrent

Purpose

The canonical radical projection assigns a subgroup order to every character-weight class.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

An equivariant weight-block fibre consisting of two classes with radical subgroup orders eight and four is pointwise fixed by its block-stabilizer action.

Argument or construction

Radical subgroup order is invariant under weight-class automorphisms. In a fibre exhausted by two classes of orders eight and four, an automorphism cannot exchange them and must fix both.

External assumptions and dependencies

A given equivariant block assignment on actual character-weight classes and an exhaustive two-element fibre whose radical orders are eight and four.
