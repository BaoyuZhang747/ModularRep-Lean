Purpose

Constructs the weight on the matched character's quotient.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

The image radical and the descended ordinary character form a defect-zero weight whose pullback along qW is the original local character.

Argument or construction

The proved kernel constancy permits ordinary character descent along the local quotient. The central prime-to-p normalizer result preserves radicality and defect zero.

External assumptions and dependencies

The raw match and canonical reduction/compatibility, common block equation, prime-to-p central kernel and NavarroTiep23cFixedCentralQuotientSource on that kernel and radical. The ordinary descent and defect-zero character are constructed from these inputs.
