Purpose

Extracts the representative local maps of the same global Fischer correspondence.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

A global equivalence gives a radical partition and local defect-zero bijections; global equivariance and block preservation give local covariance and block induction.

Argument or construction

Restrict the global equivalence to a radical fibre and compose with the intrinsic local-character equivalence. Its class equation retains the original global image. Apply the global block law and the actual induced-block definition to obtain the local block statement.

External assumptions and dependencies

The chosen global correspondence and, for the additional laws, its equivariance and block preservation. This supplies the representative-map clauses, not the extension clauses.
