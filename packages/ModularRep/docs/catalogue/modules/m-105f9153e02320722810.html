Purpose

Averaging supplies nonvanishing before a block or character is chosen downstairs.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

Nonzero images of primitive blocks in the trivial central sector determine an injective quotient-block map under the averaging and primitive-image laws.

Argument or construction

Averaging proves the relevant images nonzero. Orthogonality of distinct primitive idempotents prevents two nonzero images from becoming the same primitive quotient block.
