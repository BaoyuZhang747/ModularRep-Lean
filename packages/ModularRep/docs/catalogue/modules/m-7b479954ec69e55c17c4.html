Purpose

Adds intermediate block laws to each retained original model pair.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

Every factor-one pair affording the same global/local characters inherits the complete original-group intermediate block law from the specified base induction.

Argument or construction

The top-model character theorem identifies the models' extension characters with the retained originals. The universal output can then be specialized to the unchanged representation carriers.

External assumptions and dependencies

The actual original global/local characters and canonical local reduction, their catalogue induction relation, and the two retained irreducible affording models with trivial factor sets. The universal theorem applies to every such original model pair.
