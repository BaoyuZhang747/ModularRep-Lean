Purpose

Combines the 26 named sporadic cases in a source-relative finite theorem.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

For any predicate Goal, the published result on 22 names, the result on every listed boundary pair, and exact relevant-prime support on the four remaining names imply Goal for every relevant sporadic pair.

Argument or construction

Split the 26-name inventory into the 22 published cases and the four boundary names. In the latter branch choose the corresponding boundary enum, use exact prime support to enter the 45-pair list, and apply Proposition 5.7's supplied result. This generic construction is distinct from the later fixed Definition 4.1 certificate construction.

External assumptions and dependencies

Three separate premises: the published 22-group result, the boundary-pair result and Atlas boundary-prime support.
