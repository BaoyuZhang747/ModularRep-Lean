Purpose

Derives all-inner automorphisms of the universal cover.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

If the nonabelian simple quotient has trivial actual outer quotient, every automorphism of its universal cover is inner.

Argument or construction

Descend an automorphism through the characteristic central kernel and realize the quotient action by conjugation. The original automorphism and the corresponding lifted conjugation are the same by uniqueness in the universal property.

External assumptions and dependencies

An actual universal central extension onto a nonabelian simple quotient whose literal opposite outer quotient has cardinality one. No automorphism assertion about arbitrary central quotients is assumed.
