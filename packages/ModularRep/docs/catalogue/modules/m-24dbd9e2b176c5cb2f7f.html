ModularRep.OrbitFibreEquiv

ModularRep/OrbitFibreEquiv.lean

theorem-developmentcurrent

Purpose

Quotient the equivariant support map by group orbits.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

An equivariant support map whose support stabilizers fix the supported objects identifies the fibre at one support with the corresponding fibre on orbit spaces.

Argument or construction

Quotient the equivariant support map by group orbits. A representative whose support is conjugate to the fixed support can be transported into its fibre; the support stabilizer fixing the object makes that representative unique.

External assumptions and dependencies

An equivariant support map for which the stabilizer of an object's support fixes that object. This stronger fibre hypothesis is essential for uniqueness on the orbit fibre.
