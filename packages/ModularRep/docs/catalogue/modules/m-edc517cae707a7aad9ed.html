Purpose

Stores the four selected Klein-four ordinary rows.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

A complete printed allocation, row action, rank-three inverse and rank-two symmetrized inverse are checked on the literal integer matrix.

Argument or construction

The checks establish selected-row completeness as a finite label identity and certify reconstruction of raw and symmetrized rows. Their specified meaning still needs the ordinary-value and block-allocation dictionaries.

External assumptions and dependencies

The literal data and closed identities described in this entry. Actual group, ordinary-character, block or coefficient identification is a separate binding and is not proved by this data module.
