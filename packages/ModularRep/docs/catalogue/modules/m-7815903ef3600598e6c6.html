Purpose

Applies the forward Späth passage to one normalized principal match.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

The full Spin cover, character-central quotient, hatted raw weight and its own ordinary local character are retained.

Argument or construction

Literal pullbacks construct the hatted global and local characters and their quotients. A normalized Clause III packet supplies both extensions and all intermediate block equations for the canonical pair witness.

External assumptions and dependencies

Späth Theorem 4.4 forward implication, specified hatted-block induction, exact inertia/normalizer joins and coefficient normalization.
