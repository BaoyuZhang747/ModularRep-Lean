computation-bindingauxiliary

Purpose

Turns sparse annihilator identities into exact row-inverse and rank certificates at the retained root.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

Proved inverse and reconstruction residual identities for the sparse arrays yield a rank-n certificate after evaluation at the target root of the original iota.

Argument or construction

Each annihilator combination evaluates to zero. This gives the matrix right-inverse and row reconstruction equations. Package them as RowInverseCertificate and use the conductor-root primitivity theorem for the same-iota specialization.

External assumptions and dependencies

Literal sparse arrays with their proved polynomial identities and SameIotaConductorRoot.
