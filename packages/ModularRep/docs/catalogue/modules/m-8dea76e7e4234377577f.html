Purpose

Raw-character descent is independent of conjugate representatives.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

Raw ordinary descent is independent of the representative of a weight class under the central-quotient factorization law.

Argument or construction

Conjugate representatives descend by the image of the conjugating element. Kernel constancy follows from factorization, so both descended characters lie in the same quotient weight class.

The finite actual group, characteristic prime, compatible coefficient/root data, specified sets of primitive blocks and characters, and the imported transport results are fixed as in the declaration context.
