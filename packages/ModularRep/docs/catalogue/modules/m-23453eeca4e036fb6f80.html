Purpose

Builds the induced diagonal module from the same honest field action.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

Given an honest base-pair-by-field action and a selected subgroup of the field actor, restricts to the associated split diagonal and constructs the induced finite representation on the two upper groups.

Argument or construction

The honest representation is pulled back to its literal split diagonal on the same space. Installed representation induction along the diagonal inclusion defines the output and its generator equation.

External assumptions and dependencies

The original honest action and the two field actions; no Morita or simple-extension source.
