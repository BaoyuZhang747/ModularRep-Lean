Purpose

Derives stability of all three actual 3-blocks from literal ordinary values.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

Complete ordinary decomposition and the bound six-row value matrix give a nonprincipal block fixed by the outer involution; together with a distinct fixed trivial-character block this fixes all three blocks.

Argument or construction

A block whose complete ordinary rows vanish at one regular element cannot contain the trivial Brauer character, because their span vanishes there and the trivial character has value one. Rank four gives a nonempty selected Brauer fibre and closure of the literal rows under the outer fusion fixes its block. An injective permutation of three points fixing two distinct points is the identity.

External assumptions and dependencies

Actual complete ordinary decomposition, block labels, literal values and outer fusion; no block-action identity is assumed.
