Purpose

Derives local invariance from an original equivariant match.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

A global Brauer stabilizer element whose quotient action stabilizes the image radical fixes the actual descended local Brauer character.

Argument or construction

Normalizer saturation and radicality recover stability of the original subgroup. Raw-weight uniqueness yields ordinary fixedness, and the canonical reduction/pullback square transfers it to the descended character.

External assumptions and dependencies

The original equivariant correspondence and raw match, canonical reduction/compatibility, prime-to-p own quotient and its normalizer source, and a commuting automorphism square with global Brauer fixedness and quotient-radical stability.
