Purpose

The given Clifford field action restricts to the same norm-one Spin group.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

Inverse pullback gives its actions on exact K0, primitive blocks, Brauer characters and ordinary characters. One literal automorphism square proves the covariance with actual special Clifford conjugation for every carrier.

Argument or construction

One literal automorphism square proves the covariance with actual special Clifford conjugation for every carrier. A literal restriction square transports conjugation on a normal subgroup.

External assumptions and dependencies

The only field source is the existing FieldActionSource on the prescribed Clifford algebra and norm.
