Purpose

Derives principal Spin fixation, the Clifford-by-field stabilizer product and extension in rank at least four.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

For rank at least four, the specified GGGR basis and field-naturality inputs imply principal Brauer fixation, the regular-overgroup stabilizer factorization and extension over the whole field group.

Argument or construction

The higher-rank GGGR theorem constructs the rational basis internally. Fixed GGGR columns fix the PIM span and Brauer labels, after which the semidirect stabilizer and cyclic-extension arguments apply.

External assumptions and dependencies

Raw GGGR, rational-class and Taylor sources, specified PIM formulas and Navarro's cyclic modular extension principle.
