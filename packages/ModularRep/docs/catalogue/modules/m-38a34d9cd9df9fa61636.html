Purpose

Proves cancellation of one unknown finite fibre.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

Equal total cardinalities and equality of every other fibre imply equality at a distinguished block.

Argument or construction

The sigma-fibre equivalence expresses each total as a sum over blocks. Separating the distinguished summand and cancelling the remaining sum proves the assertion.

External assumptions and dependencies

Finite carriers, the two actual maps to a finite block set, equality of their total cardinalities and equality of all nondistinguished fibres. No character or block-theory source is assumed.
