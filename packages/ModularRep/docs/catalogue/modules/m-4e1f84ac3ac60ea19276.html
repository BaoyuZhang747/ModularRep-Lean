Purpose

Shows that the effective kernel H C_Gamma(H) acts on the constituent inertia by inner conjugation, supporting the historical prime-two stabilizer proof.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

Consequently, on H it acts by the same conjugation as the H-factor. Source-shaped kernel theorem on the correct fixed carrier.

Argument or construction

Consequently, on H it acts by the same conjugation as the H-factor. No Clifford correspondence, Gallagher theorem, Brauer-character extension, or stabiliser factorisation is assumed or proved here. The manuscript-specific kernel-to-inertia deduction.

External assumptions and dependencies

The inverse orientation is the one used when the manuscript's right action is encoded as a Lean left action. Unlike the discarded global adapter, this theorem does not postulate an action of elements that move theta on the fixed group H_theta. The only action-specific input says that elements centralising H act trivially on the selected constituent.
