Purpose

Identifies the Frobenius-fixed coefficient field and standard finite matrix model.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

The fixed coefficient field has 2^a elements for positive a, and its symplectic group is equivalent to the literal fixed-point group.

Argument or construction

Finite-field uniqueness identifies the coefficient fields. Entrywise ring transport and fixed-point matrix reconstruction give inverse group maps.

External assumptions and dependencies

Finite-field algebra and the standard alternating symplectic form.
