Purpose

Lifts a modular central scalar to an ordinary linear character.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

The same prime regular root embedding sends the finite central scalar homomorphism to an ordinary linear character, preserving its degree, faithfulness and local/global lying-over equations.

Argument or construction

The scalar values lie in the required root group, so the root embedding lifts them multiplicatively. The associated one-dimensional representation supplies the ordinary irreducible character and its explicit values.

External assumptions and dependencies

A finite group, modular field of characteristic p, ordinary field of characteristic zero, the retained prime regular root embedding and a given modular central homomorphism. The faithful conclusion requires injectivity of that homomorphism; the Brauer-sector formula additionally uses an algebraically closed modular field and actual scalar-sector membership. No block or local reduction source is required.
