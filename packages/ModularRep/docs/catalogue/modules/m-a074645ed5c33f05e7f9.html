Purpose

Derives selected numerical consequences from the parsed sporadic transcript records.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

Parsed Baby, Monster and Fi24 records together with explicitly stated alignment and subtraction inputs imply the selected principal residuals, orbit signatures and p=5 comparisons.

Argument or construction

Read the exact restriction ranks, defect-zero labels and finite rows through the transcript bindings. Cancel known nonprincipal contributions and use the involution parity argument for the three-element Fi24 fibre. The p=3 and p=5 endpoints retain label-neutral numerical conclusions; they do not identify printed rows with actual blocks or prove radical completeness.

External assumptions and dependencies

Parsed computation transcripts plus BabySubtractionInputs, MonsterCancellationInputs and Fi24TwoCancellationInputs where required; semantic table and character identifications remain separate.
