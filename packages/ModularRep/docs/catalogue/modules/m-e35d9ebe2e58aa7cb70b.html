ModularRep.BrauerCharacterKZero

ModularRep/BrauerCharacterKZero.lean

theorem-developmentcurrent

Purpose

Brauer-character formation is additive on short exact sequences, so it descends to the exact Grothendieck group of finite dimensional modular representations.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

Short-exact Brauer additivity defines an injective character homomorphism from the exact Grothendieck group of finite modular representations to prime regular class functions.

Argument or construction

Descend short-exact Brauer additivity to exact K-zero. Express a class in simple coordinates and use linear independence of the corresponding Brauer functions to prove the character homomorphism injective.

External assumptions and dependencies

The exact simple-coordinate equivalence, short-exact Brauer additivity and previously proved Brauer-function independence for the same finite root embedding.
