ModularRep.PaperProofs.SporadicFi24P3Definition44NamedCarrierTrivialIntegerWitnesses

ModularRep/PaperProofs/SporadicFi24P3Definition44NamedCarrierTrivialIntegerWitnesses.lean

computation-bindingauxiliary

Purpose

Stores the polynomial quotient and constant witnesses for the trivial-sector residual checks.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

The payload specifies inverse residual quotients and constants for 41 by 41 entries in seven conductor groups, and reconstruction quotients for 108 by 41 entries.

Argument or construction

These are explicit candidate witnesses for divisibility by each local modulus. They acquire proof force only when the check modules verify the residual identities and constant sums; the arrays alone assert no rank.

External assumptions and dependencies

The preserved arithmetic witness export.
