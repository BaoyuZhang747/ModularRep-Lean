Purpose

States the guarded numerical input for actual two-blocks with defect group order at most sixteen.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

SmallDefectNumericalSource gives only equality of actual Brauer and weight cardinalities under an actual Navarro defect representative and the order bound.

Argument or construction

Specialize the guarded equality to a supplied exact defect order. Finite arithmetic verifies the six orders 4,8,1,1,8,8 meet the bound. Supplied Brauer ranks then give weight counts 3,3,1,1,2,2.

External assumptions and dependencies

The external small-defect numerical theorem, actual defect representatives and specified Brauer counts; no equivariant bijection or extension data is included.
