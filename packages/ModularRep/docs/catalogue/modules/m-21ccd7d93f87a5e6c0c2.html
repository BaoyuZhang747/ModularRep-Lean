Purpose

Normalizes the operator for a possible nonsplit index-two extension.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

An invariant finite-dimensional irreducible representation over an algebraically closed field has an intertwiner U satisfying U squared equals the action of the square of the outside element.

Argument or construction

The original intertwiner squared differs from that group action by a scalar, by Schur's lemma. The scalar is nonzero, and an algebraic square root rescales the intertwiner to the required identity.

External assumptions and dependencies

A finite-dimensional irreducible representation over an algebraically closed field, conjugation invariance, and an ambient element whose square lies in the normal subgroup. The normalization is proved from these representation hypotheses.
