Purpose

Equates global and local modular scalars on the full centre.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

For a matched raw weight and a prime-to-p centre, actual block induction gives equality of the two affording representations' full-centre scalar characters.

Argument or construction

The local reduction compatibility derives the selected normalizer block. The common ambient block then lets the central character induction theorem compare the scalar actions on the original representation carriers.

External assumptions and dependencies

The Definition35Problem, its original Brauer character and the same raw weight, CanonicalRawReduction, canonical local block compatibility, their common actual block equation, and prime-to-p order of the full centre. No quotient normalizer or primitive-image source is required.
