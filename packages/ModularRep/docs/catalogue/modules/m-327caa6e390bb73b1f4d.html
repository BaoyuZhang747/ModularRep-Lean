Purpose

Shows that two fixed points determine an injective permutation of a three-point carrier.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

An injective self-map of a carrier equivalent to Fin 3, fixing two distinct points, fixes every point.

Argument or construction

For the remaining point, injectivity excludes either fixed point as its image. Exhausting Fin 3 leaves only the original point.

External assumptions and dependencies

The three-point equivalence, injectivity and two distinct fixed points.
