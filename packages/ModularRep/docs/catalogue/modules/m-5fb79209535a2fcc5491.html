Purpose

Binds rank-three principal counting to literal SO and Omega fibres.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

The chosen SO element need only lie outside Omega; its action squares to the identity because its square lies inside Omega.

Argument or construction

Inner fixation and principal stability give involutions on characters and weight classes. Narrow numerical and covering data then feed finite involution classification to construct a correspondence.

External assumptions and dependencies

Published principal counts and covering arithmetic with actual roots, idempotents and local block assignments.
