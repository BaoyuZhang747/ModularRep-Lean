Purpose

Proves nonzero Brauer restriction on a normal p-subgroup.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

Every nonzero central idempotent has nonzero central Brauer restriction at a normal p-subgroup.

Argument or construction

The idempotent image in the regular representation contains a nonzero p-fixed vector. Cancellation of nontrivial conjugation orbits in characteristic p shows that a zero Brauer restriction would annihilate this vector, contradicting its idempotent support.

External assumptions and dependencies

A normal p-subgroup of a finite group in characteristic p, and a nonzero central idempotent. The proof requires no ordinary-character table, root embedding or external defect principle.
