ModularRep.CentralCharacterBlockInductionRestriction

ModularRep/CentralCharacterBlockInductionRestriction.lean

transport-adaptercurrent

Purpose

The first part proves the literal coefficient-restriction formula for the standard central character idempotent.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

If a subgroup containing an invertible-order central subgroup induces a local block to an ambient block, irreducible representations supported by those blocks have corresponding scalar central characters on that central subgroup.

Argument or construction

Restrict a central character idempotent to a subgroup containing the central subgroup. Induced-block equality transports its value one, and uniqueness of local support identifies the two central characters.

External assumptions and dependencies

The stated subgroup containing the finite central subgroup of invertible order, algebraically closed coefficients, actual local/ambient central character catalogues and induced-block relation, and supported irreducible modules.
