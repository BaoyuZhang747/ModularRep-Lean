Purpose

Derives the literal Brauer restriction-span binding from ordinary decomposition.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

Global ordinary/Brauer decomposition and support, complete enumeration of the six ordinary characters in the selected block, and their literal restrictions construct BrauerRestrictionSpaceBinding.

Argument or construction

Completeness identifies the literal row span with the ordinary block span. The decomposition theorem and Brauer linear independence identify that span with the Brauer block span. Compose these equalities; no additional span, rank or action assertion is assumed.

External assumptions and dependencies

Actual ordinary decomposition and block allocation, including the universal Brauer-to-ordinary spanning law from Navarro Corollary 2.16, complete six-character coverage and literal row values.
