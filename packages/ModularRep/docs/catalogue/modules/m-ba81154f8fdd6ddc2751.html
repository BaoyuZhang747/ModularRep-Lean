Purpose

Proves extension liftability is preserved by a commuting category square.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

In a naturally commuting square with fully faithful base functor and essentially surjective upper functor, an object lies in the essential image of restriction if and only if its base-functor image does.

Argument or construction

One direction sends an upper preimage through the square. The other chooses an upper preimage and reflects the resulting base isomorphism by full faithfulness.

External assumptions and dependencies

The displayed natural isomorphism and functor properties, abstracting the category argument used in Ruhstorfer's extension passage.
