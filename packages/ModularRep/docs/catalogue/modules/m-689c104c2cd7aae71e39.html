Purpose

Makes the external character-triple retention implication explicit.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

Block preservation gives equal central sectors and full equivariance gives equal stabilizers; a separately supplied implication from these equalities yields the character-triple relation for the combined pairs.

Argument or construction

Sector equality follows by applying the block-sector map to the block identity. Injectivity and equivariance identify the two stabilizers. Apply the supplied CharacterTripleRetentionSource pointwise while retaining the previously known fibre maps.

External assumptions and dependencies

Centerlessness and the exact character-triple implication, together with the raw sector, known-fibre and outer-action sources. Sector and stabilizer equalities alone are not asserted to prove the published character-triple theorem.
