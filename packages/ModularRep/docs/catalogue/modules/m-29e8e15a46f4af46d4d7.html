ModularRep.OrdinaryBlockFibreCommonWitness

transport-adaptercurrent

Purpose

This source-neutral K adapter turns a supplied one-way inclusion of inflated quotient ordinary-character fibres into a common character witness.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

Surjectivity of the quotient ordinary-block selector and containment of the inflated fibre in the cover fibre yield a common inflated ordinary-character witness.

Argument or construction

Choose an ordinary character in the nonempty quotient block fibre. The given inclusion of its inflated fibre places the same inflated character in the designated cover fibre, producing a common witness.

External assumptions and dependencies

A surjective ordinary block selector and the stated inclusion of its inflated fibre in the cover fibre, giving a concrete common ordinary-character witness.
