Purpose

Derives principal Brauer orbit counts from the actual order-two quotient action.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

SO acts through SO/Omega on the same specified character representatives.

Argument or construction

The quotient and original action have equivalent orbit quotients. Burnside's formula and the involution cycle identity express their cardinality as fixed points plus two-cycles, then the constituent-orbit equivalence identifies the upper count.

External assumptions and dependencies

Actual index two and the established Green/Clifford orbit equivalence; no numerical orbit count source.
