Purpose

Joins gamma-product models to all intermediate block laws.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

An original product comparison, block laws for every original model pair, and the Q=1 restriction equation give a product comparison with the same models and source-H block data.

Argument or construction

The proof destructures the existing comparison instead of choosing new models. Its universal model-block law is specialized to those operators, and the common Q=1 relation is carried into the resulting source-H output.

External assumptions and dependencies

A concrete OriginalProductComparison for the retained raw pair and models, EveryOriginalModelPairBlocks, and the Q=1 global/local restriction equation. No replacement model or independent extension family is supplied.
