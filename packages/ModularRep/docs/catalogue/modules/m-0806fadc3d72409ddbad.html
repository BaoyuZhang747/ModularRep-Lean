Purpose

Connects ambient extension restriction to Gallagher factorisation.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

An irreducible base restriction gives an irreducible intermediate restriction, and quotient triviality fixes the corresponding Gallagher character.

Argument or construction

Trace conjugacy invariance fixes the extension character. The quotient factor is unchanged, so the factorisation proves invariance of the correspondent.

External assumptions and dependencies

A genuine ambient representation and the exact Gallagher factorisation.
