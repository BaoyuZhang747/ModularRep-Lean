Purpose

Identifies the local Schur scalar of an induced block.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

The canonical local Brauer representation has the central character of its own specified ambient weight block.

Argument or construction

Central-idempotent support passes through the actual coefficient-restriction induction relation. The local irreducible block scalar is therefore the transported ambient sector.

External assumptions and dependencies

The actual local induction operations, raw weight, canonical normalizer Brauer reduction, local block compatibility, and invertible order of the specified central subgroup. Its ambient central-idempotent support is the input; no quotient catalogue is needed.
