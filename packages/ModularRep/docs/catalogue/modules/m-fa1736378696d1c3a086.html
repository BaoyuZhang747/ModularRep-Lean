Purpose

This file proves that the exact decomposition homomorphism commutes with tensoring by compatible ordinary and modular linear characters.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

With matching ordinary/modular linear roots, stable-reduction compatibility and the Brauer tensor formula, the exact decomposition map commutes with linear character tensoring, including its composite with an automorphism twist.

Argument or construction

Apply the injective Brauer K-zero map to ordinary and modular tensor twists. Compatible linear roots and the Brauer product formula make their character images equal; injectivity proves decomposition compatibility, also after automorphism twisting.

External assumptions and dependencies

Complete modular system, stable-reduction character compatibility, the Brauer tensor formula and ordinary/modular linear characters agreeing on the chosen finite roots.
