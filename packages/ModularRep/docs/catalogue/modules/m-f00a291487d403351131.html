Purpose

Appends a derived consequence to an existential conjunction without changing its witnesses.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

From two witnesses satisfying seven predicates and a deduction of an eighth predicate from the first and third, produces the eightfold existential conjunction using exactly the same witnesses.

Argument or construction

Existential elimination exposes the witnesses once. Reassembling the same seven proofs and the derived eighth clause preserves every earlier choice.

Pure logical hypotheses; no representation theoretic source or mathematical matching is introduced.
