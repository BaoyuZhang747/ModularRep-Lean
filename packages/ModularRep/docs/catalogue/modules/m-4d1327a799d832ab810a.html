ModularRep.PrimeRegularEigenvalues

ModularRep/PrimeRegularEigenvalues.lean

theorem-developmentcurrent

Purpose

For a finite group G, primeRegularExponent p G is the part of |G| prime to p.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

Every characteristic-polynomial root of a prime regular representation operator has order dividing the prime-to-p part of the finite group order.

Argument or construction

The operator at a prime regular element has order dividing the prime-to-p part of |G|. Each characteristic-polynomial root therefore has a power equal to one and belongs to the finite root group used in Brauer values.

External assumptions and dependencies

The specified finite root correspondence and coefficient hypotheses. Brauer separation and independence use algebraically closed characteristic-p k and characteristic-zero K; homomorphism transports require the displayed agreement of the root lifts. No splitting-system or independent table-root equality is inferred.
