Purpose

Defines actual rational unipotent conjugacy classes and their field action on Spin.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

On actual rational unipotent conjugacy classes, the geometric parameter and GGGR sources identify the algebraic meaning and give full-field GGGR fixation through the proved class action.

Argument or construction

The actual Spin automorphisms induce the class and geometric-fibre actions. Rational component parametrisation and Taylor covariance give GGGR fixation, and an exhaustive enumeration constructs the older indexed adapter.

External assumptions and dependencies

Authentic unipotent/geometric/component/Frobenius interpretation and the same rational GGGR source.
