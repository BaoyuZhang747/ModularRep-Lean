Purpose

Defines the disjoint faithful-sector radical partition.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

All central-sector and radical-conjugacy indices are retained, with coverage, covariance and disjointness; injectivity of parts is asserted on supported indices.

Argument or construction

The label is the pair consisting of the global sector and the radical of its weight image. Fibre identities give the partition laws, while equality of two empty fibres is explicitly allowed.

External assumptions and dependencies

The already specified faithful equivalence for partition identities and its FamilyProperties for covariance. These are actual set fibres; no local reduction, table or extension source is used.
