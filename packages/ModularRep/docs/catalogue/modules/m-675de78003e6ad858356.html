Purpose

Provides actual carriers for faithful central sectors.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

Faithful central homomorphisms, function-valued irreducible Brauer characters and literal weight conjugacy classes inherit the opposite-automorphism action.

Argument or construction

The definitions restrict existing actions to invariant faithfulness predicates. Forgetful fixedness equivalences and sector/radical projections preserve the underlying values and weights.

External assumptions and dependencies

The actual ambient root embedding, Brauer injectivity and primitive decomposition, invertible centre order, literal weight block operations and their RoutineTransportInput. The source and transport data index the faithful subcarriers; local canonical reductions are not used in defining them.
