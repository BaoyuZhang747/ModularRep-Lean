Purpose

Identifies original and quotient Brauer stabilizers by their actual character values.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

For a centerless group, the canonical central character quotient induces an isomorphism from the original character stabilizer to the quotient stabilizer.

Argument or construction

The quotient map is a group equivalence. Its inflation equation identifies the two class functions, and conjugation of automorphisms through this equivalence preserves fixedness. Restrict the induced automorphism equivalence to the corresponding stabilizer subgroups.

External assumptions and dependencies

Centerlessness and the actual quotient character inflation identity; whole-field root equality is unnecessary.
