Purpose

Keeps the original Brauer stabilizer as the automorphism target.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

Under the stated all-inner hypothesis, the own central quotient acts on the original character stabilizer with kernel its centre and the indicated quotient equivalence.

Argument or construction

The map factors the original inner action through the central character kernel. Perfectness identifies the quotient centre and preserves its prime-to-p order; no full automorphism claim about an arbitrary quotient is assumed.

External assumptions and dependencies

The original Definition35Problem and Brauer character; perfectness for the kernel/centre identification, all-inner automorphisms of the original group for surjectivity, and prime-to-p original centre for the final order claim. No full automorphism group of the own quotient is supplied.
