Purpose

Transports principal Brauer fibres and their field action from Spin to matrix Omega.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

The central two-kernel quotient and matrix equivalence use roots from the same modular system.

Argument or construction

Inflation gives an equivalence of supported principal fibres and principal uniqueness identifies the target block. Cardinality and field fixation then transfer through this equivalence without choosing a character matching.

External assumptions and dependencies

Central-two inflation, root compatibility, specified quotient/matrix block dictionaries and the independent rational Spin class count.
