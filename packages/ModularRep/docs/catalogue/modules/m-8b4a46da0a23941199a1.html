Purpose

Certifies the raw and symmetrized Klein-four ranks.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

The four selected ordinary rows have rank three and their outer-symmetrized rows have rank two.

Argument or construction

Constant polynomial checks identify these rows with the integer matrix. Scaled inverse/reconstruction certificates then prove both dimensions and transfer them back to the original literal rows.

External assumptions and dependencies

The literal four-row integer and polynomial identities, finite row action and scaled inverse/reconstruction checks over a characteristic-zero field. Actual ordinary characters and fusion are bound separately.
