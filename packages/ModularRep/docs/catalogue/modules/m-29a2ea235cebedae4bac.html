Purpose

Derives ambient induced-block images from local images.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

For factorizing local ordinary characters on a central prime-to-p quotient, the two actual raw weight blocks correspond under the group algebra quotient map.

Argument or construction

The local specified image comes from the two canonical reductions. Saturation gives the coefficient-restriction square, and the two defined induction relations determine the ambient image.

External assumptions and dependencies

The same two canonical reductions, ordinary factorization, central prime-to-p quotient, both actual block operations/compatibility records, and the global and local guarded primitive-image laws. Normalizer saturation and the induced ambient block image are derived.
