Purpose

Lifts and descends automorphisms along the same universal cover.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

When the covering kernel equals the centre, the supplied map induces an automorphism-group equivalence with explicit naturality and inner-conjugation equations.

Argument or construction

Universality constructs the unique lift of a quotient automorphism. Uniqueness proves multiplicativity and inverse laws, while surjectivity of the covering map checks the descent square.

External assumptions and dependencies

The actual universal central extension and the equality of its kernel with its centre. Uniqueness and naturality of automorphism lifts are derived from that universal property.
