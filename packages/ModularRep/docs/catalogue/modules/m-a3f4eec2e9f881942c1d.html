ModularRep.BrauerCharacterHomPullback

ModularRep/BrauerCharacterHomPullback.lean

transport-adaptercurrent

Purpose

Brauer characters in this project are defined relative to a chosen lift of prime regular roots of unity.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

Agreement of the two finite root lifts on the eigenvalues of a pulled-back representation makes its Brauer character equal to pullback of the original Brauer function. Existing representation extensions consequently give character extensions.

Argument or construction

Compare the same operator through pullback and use agreement of the two root lifts on its eigenvalues. This proves the Brauer-character pullback formula and converts representation extensions into actual character extensions.

External assumptions and dependencies

A group homomorphism and actual agreement of the finite root lifts on the affording representation's eigenvalues. Extension conclusions start with an existing representation extension and its irreducibility.
