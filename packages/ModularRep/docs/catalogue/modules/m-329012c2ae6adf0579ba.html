theorem-developmentcurrent

Purpose

This file computes the characteristic polynomial and its multiset of roots for a scalar endomorphism.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

A scalar operator has characteristic polynomial (X-a) to the dimension and its repeated-root multiset. Any root lift therefore has root sum equal to dimension times the lifted scalar, including central scalar actions.

Argument or construction

The characteristic polynomial of a scalar operator is the corresponding linear factor raised to the dimension. Its root multiset is constant with that multiplicity, so lifting roots before summing gives degree times the lifted scalar.

External assumptions and dependencies

A finite dimensional scalar operator over a field; the central character specialization additionally uses an irreducible representation over an algebraically closed field.
