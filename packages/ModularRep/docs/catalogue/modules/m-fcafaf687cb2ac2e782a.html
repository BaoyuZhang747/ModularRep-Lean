Purpose

Determines an ambient primitive image from local induction.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

A surjective saturated subgroup square, local primitive image and two induction relations force the corresponding ambient primitive image, assuming guarded preservation of primitivity.

Argument or construction

Central character evaluations commute with coefficient restriction. Evaluation on the source block first proves its image is nonzero, then primitivity and the target catalogue isolate the unique target block.

External assumptions and dependencies

A surjective saturated subgroup square, four actual primitive decompositions and central character catalogues, the local primitive-image equality, both induction relations, and guarded preservation of ambient primitivity. Nonzeroness of the ambient image is proved.
