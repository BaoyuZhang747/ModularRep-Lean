theorem-developmentcurrent

Purpose

This file packages the roots of a characteristic polynomial at a prime regular element as roots of unity.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

Equality of characteristic-zero lifted root sums reflects to equality of the corresponding modular sums. Applied to eigenvalue multisets, equal Brauer values imply equal modular traces.

Argument or construction

Represent eigenvalue sums in a common cyclotomic ring. Equality after characteristic-zero specialization implies equality in that ring, hence after modular specialization; the result recovers trace equality.

External assumptions and dependencies

Characteristic-zero and modular fields with the specified finite multiplicative root correspondence and coprime root order; uses the proved cyclotomic root-sum reflection.
