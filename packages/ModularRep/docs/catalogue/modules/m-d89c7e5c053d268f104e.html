Purpose

Construct a block-preserving correspondence from the total An–Dietrich map by cancelling the two known block fibres.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

A supplied global An–Dietrich equivalence and two known block correspondences yield an automorphism-equivariant equivalence between all irreducible Brauer characters and weights, preserving each of the three blocks.

Argument or construction

Cancel the known C2 summands to obtain the principal equivalence, reassemble the three fibres, and promote C2 equivariance using the inner/outer decomposition. Triple relations and trivial-radical normalization require additional arguments.

External assumptions and dependencies

The finite actual group, characteristic prime, compatible coefficient/root data, specified sets of primitive blocks and characters, and the imported transport results are fixed as in the declaration context. The stated finite row/count certificates, regular-class coverage, ordinary decomposition/support and automorphism fusion bindings are required to connect finite calculations with actual sets of Brauer characters or weights.
