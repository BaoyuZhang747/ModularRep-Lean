Purpose

Supply compatible packets for every pair of one specified correspondence.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

Replacing packets preserves that same correspondence, equivariance, block correspondence and trivial-subgroup normalisation.

Argument or construction

Choose each supplied compatible packet and construct the full certificate without altering the matched characters.

External assumptions and dependencies

Existence of compatible extension and intermediate block packets for the specified correspondence. This existence is not deduced from numerical counts.
