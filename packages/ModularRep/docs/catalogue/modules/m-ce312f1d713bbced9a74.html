Purpose

Constructs all Q=1 intermediate global and local block data from base and top.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

For the selected ambient quotient of order at most two, the base catalogue and one top catalogue give IntermediateBlockSource for the shared extension packet.

Argument or construction

Construct the global restricted character and catalogue at the base and at top. Every intermediate subgroup equals one of these, so case analysis supplies the whole global family. Apply the Q=1 block-transport construction to derive the local family and induction equalities.

The selected ambient, shared global extension, Q=1 local transport, top catalogue and Spath coefficient field data.
