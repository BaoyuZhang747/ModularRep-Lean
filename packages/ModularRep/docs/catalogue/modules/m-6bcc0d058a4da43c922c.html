Purpose

Handles the quaternion case with three local defect-zero characters.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

At a=2 and gamma=0, the actual normalizer quotient has a supplied three-character enumeration for every admissible wreath list.

Argument or construction

The inverse enumeration selects the transported original own character. The resulting whole-pair match proves principal membership without uniqueness.

External assumptions and dependencies

The exact exceptional subgroup coverage, An's character enumeration and compatible specified support for block interpretation.
