Purpose

Transfers the rational diagonal product to the original finite Clifford group.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

The paired-Levi rationality and geometric product inputs imply every finite special-Clifford element is a product of an element of its Spin norm kernel and an element of the same finite Levi.

Argument or construction

The geometric Lang-corrected product is pulled back through the finite-point equivalence. Coordinate equations retain both original finite factors and their geometric images.

External assumptions and dependencies

Geometric central decomposition/intersection, Levi Lang source and norm-compatible finite points.
