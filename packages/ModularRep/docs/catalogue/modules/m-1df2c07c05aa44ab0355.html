Purpose

Builds the actual nonexceptional quaternion-wreath pair.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

Constrained matrix coordinates, radicality and local defect-zero existence and uniqueness give the model pair; genuine subgroup conjugacy identifies it with the original principal weight.

Argument or construction

The uniqueness bridge proves equality of the own local characters. Principal membership and induced block equality follow from that whole-pair match.

External assumptions and dependencies

An's scoped a>=3 local theorem and actual source subgroup conjugacy, without arbitrary alpha-class identification.
