Purpose

Constructs associated projective models for the same selected global and local characters.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

Cyclic Brauer extension data give global and local models with trivial factor sets, retaining affording and ordinary/Brauer reduction identities.

Argument or construction

Turn actual extension representations into associated projective models. Transport the local extension through the pair-normalizer and pair-base square, and inflate through the surjective local base map. Surjectivity preserves irreducibility and the selected ordinary reduction is retained.

External assumptions and dependencies

The same EquivariantMatch, selected outer source, local reduction and cyclic Brauer extension principle.
