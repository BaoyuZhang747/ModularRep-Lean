Purpose

Transports actual Brauer characters between inverse sectors.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

A centre-inverting automorphism induces an equivalence between a Brauer sector and its inverse and transports the count of one nontrivial sector to both when the centre has order three.

Argument or construction

The restricted twist action supplies the equivalence and its inverse. The two nontrivial homomorphisms are a character and its inverse, giving complete sector coverage.

External assumptions and dependencies

The actual Brauer root/decomposition and centre-inverting automorphism. The all-nontrivial-sector count specialization additionally requires centre order three and one actual nontrivial-sector count.
