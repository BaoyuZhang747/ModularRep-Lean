Purpose

This source-neutral module isolates the character-carrier part of transport through a central quotient.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

With actual central quotient and compatible roots, modular inflation is an equivalence from quotient IBr to the literal trivial-central character fibre, inverse to descent of the affording representations.

Argument or construction

Trivial scalar action of the central kernel permits descent of an affording modular representation. Compatible roots identify inflation and deflation as inverse functions, yielding the actual trivial-central character fibre equivalence.

External assumptions and dependencies

The actual central quotient and literal trivial-central character fibre, compatible cover/quotient roots and prime regular surjectivity, and the commuting automorphism square for equivariance. Scalar central action uses algebraically closed k.
