Purpose

Transports the pair-level local extension into the selected Spath ambient normalizer.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

The selected pair extension, actual ambient equivalences and three root compatibility conditions give a local Brauer-character extension in the ambient used by the matched pair.

Argument or construction

Show the direct pair-base/quotient-normalizer equivalence equals the route through the ambient local base. This equality makes the base-character restrictions agree, so transport the pair extension along the ambient-local equivalence.

External assumptions and dependencies

Centerlessness, same fully equivariant matched Omega, semidirect automorphism bijectivity, cyclic extension principle and quotient/local/pair root compatibility. No block relation follows here.
