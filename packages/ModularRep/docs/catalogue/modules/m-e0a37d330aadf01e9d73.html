Purpose

The scalar norm formula identifies the image of the literal scalar subgroup.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

The quotient is by the Spin subgroup joined with the scalar image, and the norm is the specified Clifford norm.

Argument or construction

The inverse image of the square subgroup is computed using norm surjectivity and the scalar identity norm(z)=z squared. Quotienting gives the actual norm-square-class identification.

External assumptions and dependencies

This step uses neither a centre identification nor a finite-field, rank or norm-surjectivity input. The image of the actual scalar subgroup is precisely the unit squares.
