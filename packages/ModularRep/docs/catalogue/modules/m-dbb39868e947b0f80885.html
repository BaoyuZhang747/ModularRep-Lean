ModularRep.PrimeRegularPart

ModularRep/PrimeRegularPart.lean

theorem-developmentcurrent

Purpose

For an element g of a finite group and a prime p, this file constructs the canonical commuting decomposition of g into its p-primary and p-regular parts.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

Every finite-order element has commuting primary and prime regular parts with product the original element and the specified exact orders; these constructions commute with group equivalences.

Argument or construction

Use coprime primary and prime-to-p exponents to construct commuting powers whose product is the original finite-order element. Their exact orders and uniqueness properties give naturality under group isomorphisms.

External assumptions and dependencies

A finite group and p prime; coprime factorization of the element order supplies the primary and regular powers.
