Purpose

This file contains only finite group cardinality and subgroup-containment arguments.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

If a normalizer subgroup D contains the canonical P and its ambient image lies in a conjugate of P, finiteness forces D to equal that canonical P.

Argument or construction

Map the local subgroup into the ambient group. The two containments sandwich it between the nominated subgroup and a conjugate of equal finite order, forcing equality; injectivity of the normalizer inclusion reflects it locally.

External assumptions and dependencies

A finite group, P in its normalizer, the local containment P ≤ D and the ambient containment of D in a subgroup conjugate to P.
