Mathematical statement and assumptions

Argument or construction

The finite odd-field source gives only determinant one, surjectivity of that map to SO, its scalar kernel, and the norm-square criterion for the actual derived subgroup of SO. Scalar adjustment proves that Spin maps onto that derived subgroup. The kernel is proved to be the two scalar signs.
