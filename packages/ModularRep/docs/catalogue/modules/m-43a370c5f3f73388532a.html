ModularRep.SimpleModuleClassFinite

ModularRep/SimpleModuleClassFinite.lean

theorem-developmentcurrent

Purpose

Every simple module over a ring of finite length occurs among the factors of one fixed composition series of the regular module.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

A finite-length regular module has only finitely many simple quotient types: every simple module class occurs among its composition factors. Hence finite group algebras have finitely many simple classes.

Argument or construction

A simple module is a quotient of the regular module and must occur in a composition series of that module. The finite list of factors therefore surjects onto the simple isomorphism classes.

External assumptions and dependencies

A Noetherian Artinian ring or one fixed finite composition series of its regular module. The group algebra specialization uses finite G.
