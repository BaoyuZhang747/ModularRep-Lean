ModularRep.PaperProofs.TypeBRankThreeMoritaDiagonal

ModularRep/PaperProofs/TypeBRankThreeMoritaDiagonal.lean

carrier-constructionauxiliary

Purpose

Defines the split diagonal for two actions of the same field group.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

For two groups acted on by one group, constructs the split-diagonal subgroup and the homomorphism from the base-pair semidirect product, using inverse field coordinate in the opposite factor.

Argument or construction

Base and field embeddings satisfy the split action law. Their product defines the explicit homomorphism from the base-pair semidirect product, without requiring an abelian actor.

External assumptions and dependencies

Only the two group actions and the opposite-group convention.
