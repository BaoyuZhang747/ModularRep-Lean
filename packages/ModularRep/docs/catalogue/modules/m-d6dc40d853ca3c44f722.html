Purpose

Descends exactly the local ordinary characters belonging to the trivial central sector.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

A raw weight is in the trivial sector exactly when its ordinary character is degree-valued on the quotient kernel; these representatives correspond to all downstairs defect-zero characters, including fixed variants.

Argument or construction

Use canonical reduction and central restriction for the forward implication. Descend a kernel-trivial character for the converse and use the specified nonzero block image. Ordinary inflation is the inverse, and the quotient action square restricts the equivalence to fixed points.

External assumptions and dependencies

Canonical local reduction, block compatibility, primitive block images, surjectivity and the local automorphism square. No selected ambient block is imposed.
