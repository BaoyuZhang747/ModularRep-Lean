Purpose

Characterizes primitive blocks surviving a central quotient by the trivial central sector.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

With invertible central subgroup order, a primitive central idempotent has nonzero quotient image exactly when its sector is trivial.

Argument or construction

The averaged trivial central character idempotent maps to one. A primitive idempotent in a different sector annihilates this average, so its quotient image must vanish. Combine the contrapositive with the established nonvanishing theorem for the trivial sector.

External assumptions and dependencies

Primitivity, centrality and invertibility of the subgroup order.
