Purpose

Applies the low-rank even-field theorem on the actual full automorphism family.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

Ranks two and three over fields of size at least four, at odd ell, receive the complete fixed family on the actual cover.

Argument or construction

The finite field determines the source exponent. The own universal-cover property gives the prime-to-ell cover, retaining the exceptional graph automorphism in rank two.

External assumptions and dependencies

Schaeffer Fry Theorem 5.5 and actual structural/specified data; Sp6(2)'s odd-prime double cover is handled separately.
