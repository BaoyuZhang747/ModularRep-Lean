carrier-constructioncurrent

Purpose

A complete family of primitive central idempotents assigns a unique block index to every simple group algebra module.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

A supplied primitive block decomposition assigns a unique block to each actual irreducible Brauer character through the simple-class equivalence, which restricts to corresponding block fibres.

Argument or construction

Use the simple-class/IBr equivalence to pull the unique primitive-idempotent support onto actual Brauer functions. The same equivalence restricts to each block fibre.

External assumptions and dependencies

A fixed prime regular root embedding and its simple-character injectivity, plus a supplied complete primitive block decomposition.
