Purpose

Commutes evaluation with finite polynomial sums.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

List sums and finite indexed sums of integer polynomials evaluate to the corresponding sums of field values.

Argument or construction

List induction proves the basic result. The finite-index version rewrites the list-of-function representation and applies it.

External assumptions and dependencies

Finite lists or finite indexed families of integer polynomials and the coefficient-ring evaluator. No root, table, block or character hypothesis is present.
