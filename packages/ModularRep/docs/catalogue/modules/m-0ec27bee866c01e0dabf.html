Purpose

Compute that the unit group has odd cardinality in a finite characteristic-two field.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

The unit group of a finite field of characteristic two has odd order, and every unit has a unique square root.

Argument or construction

Compute that the unit group has odd cardinality in a finite characteristic-two field. Apply the odd-order power bijection to obtain the unique square root of each unit.

External assumptions and dependencies

A finite field of characteristic two and the finite odd-group squaring bijection.
