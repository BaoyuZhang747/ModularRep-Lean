ModularRep.StableLattice

ModularRep/StableLattice.lean

theorem-developmentcurrent

Purpose

This file defines a stable lattice in a representation, constructs one from a finite basis when the acting group is finite, and defines its reduction by tensor extension.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

A finite ordinary representation has a stable integral lattice obtained from a finite basis; its tensor reduction carries the stated modular group action.

Argument or construction

Sum translates of a basis lattice over the finite group to obtain a finitely generated stable lattice spanning the same vector space. Restrict the action integrally and tensor with the residue field to define reduction.

External assumptions and dependencies

Finite G, a finite basis over the fraction field and the valuation-ring lattice hypotheses. The reduction requires the residue algebra; lattice independence is a later result.
