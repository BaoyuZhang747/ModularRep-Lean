Purpose

Computes the local four-character action from a separating probe.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

Four exhaustive actual local characters, eight specified values and one conjugacy fusion equation determine their permutation and exactly two fixed characters.

Argument or construction

The first probe values separate the four rows, proving injectivity. The second probe and fusion identify each twisted row, and restriction of the resulting row equivalence transports the finite fixed count.

External assumptions and dependencies

Four exhaustive actual local defect-zero characters, two actual quotient probe elements, eight specified character values at those elements, the root parameter and its separation condition, and the actual conjugacy fusion under the local automorphism.
