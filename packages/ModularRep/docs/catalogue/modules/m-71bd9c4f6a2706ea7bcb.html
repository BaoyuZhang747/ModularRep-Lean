Purpose

Records the Type C dependency graph and its conditional realizations.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

The graph tracks source and derived nodes and separately exposes reflection, even-field transport and principal inputs.

Argument or construction

Rank decrease proves acyclicity and graph evaluation propagates supplied interpretations. The supplementary Sp6 numerical audit is explicitly not a dependency of the headline node.

External assumptions and dependencies

Bookkeeping interpretations and derived-rule realizations only; the graph is not an independent mathematical proof of Type C.
