Purpose

Combines fixed trivial-sector weights from fixed downstairs radical classes and rows.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

A complete downstairs radical catalogue gives an equivalence between its sigma of fixed local rows and actual fixed original-sector weights, hence a sum formula.

Argument or construction

Lift the radical representatives and identify fixed indices using the compatible automorphisms. Apply the corrected fixed local-row equivalence at each index, then the fixed sector radical-union equivalence.

External assumptions and dependencies

Canonical reductions, local compatibility and primitive block images, and the global and local quotient-action squares.
