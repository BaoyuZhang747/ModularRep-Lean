ModularRep.BlockInduction

ModularRep/BlockInduction.lean

theorem-developmentcurrent

Purpose

For a subgroup H ≤ G, the central function induced from a block central character of H is the linear map constructed by coefficient restriction in GroupAlgebraCentralFunctions.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

For H ≤ G, multiplicativity of the local central character composed with coefficient restriction defines a unique induced ambient block, relative to the supplied local and ambient Navarro catalogues.

Argument or construction

Compose coefficient restriction with the local central character. Once multiplicativity is proved, preservation of one makes an algebra homomorphism; exhaustivity selects an ambient block and delta separation proves uniqueness.

External assumptions and dependencies

A fixed complete primitive block decomposition over an algebraically closed field and the Navarro Theorem 3.11 central character catalogue (delta values and exhaustivity). Block induction additionally needs multiplicativity of its induced central function.
