Purpose

The full block construction supplies two complete fibres and an invariant matching graph.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

This module turns that graph into equivariance for the actual stabilizer of the quotient block. Every quotient character is centrally faithful, and both sides of the correspondence retain the same original preimage and its stored extension and intermediate block data.

Argument or construction

No family of other blocks or trivial-radical normalization is required for this deduction. Equality of root records transports central faithfulness of the same irreducible character, including its chosen affording representation. Decode the internally constructed full witness into the independent single-block condition.

External assumptions and dependencies

Every character of the full quotient block is centrally faithful in the one fixed root convention used for that block.
