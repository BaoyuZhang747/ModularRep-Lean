Purpose

Determines the two signatures on a five-block trivial sector.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

An exhaustive Fin 5 block labelling, totals 41 and fixed totals 31 on both sides, and the four nonprincipal signatures yield signature (33,25) at role zero and equal signatures on every block.

Argument or construction

Apply principalSignature_of_five separately to Brauer characters and weights. Subtraction removes (3,1),(3,3),(1,1),(1,1). Exhaust the five roles and transport along the supplied bijection.

External assumptions and dependencies

The actual role bijection, actual supported totals and the four nonprincipal signatures. Role zero's identification with the mathematical principal block is a separate dictionary.
