Purpose

Extracts defect-zero information from a complete actual degree table.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

The explicit ordinary table realization makes row 93 the unique global ordinary defect-zero character and gives injectivity of its regular restriction.

Argument or construction

The full 3-part of the degree singles out exactly the selected row in the exhaustive table. Uniqueness gives the subsingleton and therefore the restricted-character injectivity statement.

External assumptions and dependencies

FullOrdinaryDegreeTable supplies actual irreducible characters, completeness, degree values and group order. It supplies no defect-zero character, block singleton, vanishing or regular-restriction injectivity assertion.
