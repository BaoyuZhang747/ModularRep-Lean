Purpose

Exposes role-one defect and the bottom-radical allocation.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

The actual local defect computation supplies role-one Brauer support, while canonical bottom weights belong to role two and every role-one weight subgroup is subconjugate to the chosen defect.

Argument or construction

Local induction and its support dictionary identify the defect. The canonical bottom character is ordinary defect zero, and general nonzero normalizer support gives the subconjugacy statement before choosing a correspondence.

External assumptions and dependencies

The actual local defect/induction data for role one, the full ordinary degree/block allocation, canonical local availability/compatibility, and global defect-zero/trivial-weight data. Subconjugacy uses the explicit Navarro411 support dictionary for the selected block and defect.
