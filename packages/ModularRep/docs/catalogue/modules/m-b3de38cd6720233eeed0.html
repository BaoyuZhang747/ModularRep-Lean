Purpose

For a finite group in characteristic zero, the character averaging formula computes the dimension of the actual intertwining-map space.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

The finite group acts on actual irreducible finite-dimensional representations over a characteristic-zero field.

Argument or construction

The character averaging formula identifies the dimension of the intertwiner space. Equal traces make this space nonzero; irreducibility turns a nonzero intertwiner into an equivalence, giving injectivity of simple-character labels.
