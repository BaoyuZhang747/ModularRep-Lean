applicationauxiliary

Purpose

Compute prime support and the full five and seven parts of the supplied doubled order.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

The specified order of the exceptional double cover of Sp6(2) has the computed prime support and prime-order Sylow groups at five and seven, so every subgroup at either prime is cyclic.

Argument or construction

Compute prime support and the full five and seven parts of the supplied doubled order. Sylow subgroups at those primes have prime order and are cyclic, so their subgroups are cyclic as well.

External assumptions and dependencies

The concrete order, field-size, centre-size or cyclic-torus equalities explicitly displayed in the theorem. Arithmetic deductions are internal; no concrete group identification, defect theorem or iBAW theorem follows from an order alone.
