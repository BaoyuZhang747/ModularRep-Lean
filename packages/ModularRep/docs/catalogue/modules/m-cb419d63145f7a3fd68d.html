Purpose

The quotient root is the restriction already used by actual Brauer descent.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

The quotient root on matrix G3 is the restriction of the original root under the fixed surjection from X.

Argument or construction

Surjectivity gives divisibility of the prime regular exponent and of the ordinary group order. Restrict the sufficient-root instance and use that exponent divisibility to prove the same residue calibration downstairs.

External assumptions and dependencies

Its residue calibration and sufficient ordinary roots follow from the given upstairs data and the surjectivity of the same projection. The actual residue map of the same modular system remains calibrated.
