Mathematical statement and assumptions

A given downstairs block matching lifts through the central p-core quotient to an equivariant block matching on actual character/weight fibres. Its graph yields full local stabilizer containment, and the corresponding weight quotient square supports the specified upward relation passage.

Argument or construction

Construct actual Brauer and weight fibre equivalences through a central p-core quotient and compose the downstairs matching. Specified block transport and graph equivariance give block preservation and full stabilizer containment; the actual quotient square lifts the relation for every matched raw weight.

External assumptions and dependencies
