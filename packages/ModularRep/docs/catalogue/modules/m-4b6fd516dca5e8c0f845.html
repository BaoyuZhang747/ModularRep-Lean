Purpose

For n ≥ 1, the displayed split form represents every nonzero scalar on its first hyperbolic plane.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

A product of the corresponding Clifford vector with the zeroth coordinate vector is an actual even unit normalizing the literal vector image, and its reversal norm is the prescribed scalar. The construction works over every field; the finite odd-field application, including q=3, is a specialization.

Argument or construction

All membership and norm statements are deductions from Clifford identities. The construction works over every field; the finite odd-field application, including q=3, is a specialization. The reversal product computes the norm on the actual constructed pair.
