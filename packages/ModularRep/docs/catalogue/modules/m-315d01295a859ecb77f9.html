Purpose

Converts the supported Morita restriction square into honest-extension equivalence.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

For the supported finite-representation tensor square, establishes extension if and only if the tensor image honestly extends, retaining the actual object maps and vector-space transport.

Argument or construction

The full square restricts to supported subcategories. Essential-image transport gives extension existence, and conjugation by the resulting isomorphism realizes the extension on the original vector space.

External assumptions and dependencies

Constructed base/upper equivalences and restriction square, with specified support maps.
