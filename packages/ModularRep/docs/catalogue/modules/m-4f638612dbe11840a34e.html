Purpose

Identifies the nonprincipal block as the sole possible exact-Q ambient defect block.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

Two independent exclusions for the principal and defect-zero blocks imply that any block with exact defect representative Q is nonprincipal; separate existence gives uniqueness.

Argument or construction

Exhaust the three actual block roles. The two excluded roles contradict their defect exclusions, leaving the nonprincipal role. Obtain existence only from the separately supplied support alignment when constructing the unique-existence form.

External assumptions and dependencies

An exhaustive list of three blocks and the exclusions of Q as a Navarro (4.11) defect representative for the principal and defect zero blocks. Existence for the nonprincipal block remains an additional hypothesis.
