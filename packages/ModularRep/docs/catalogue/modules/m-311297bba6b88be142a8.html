Purpose

Reconstructs canonical raw reduction on the own quotient.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

The descended normalizer character gives the canonical quotient-local reduction and pulls back to the original local Brauer character.

Argument or construction

The normal radical lies in every irreducible modular kernel by the proved fixed-vector theorem. Deflation and prime regular lifting then recover the quotient reduction, retaining the normalizer character exactly.

External assumptions and dependencies

The own-quotient weight and normalizer construction inputs, including the fixed-central-quotient normalizer law. The normal-p kernel principle and regular-element lifting used to recover raw reduction are imported proved results, not additional external principles here.
