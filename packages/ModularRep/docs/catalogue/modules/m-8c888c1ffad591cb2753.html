carrier-constructioncurrent

Purpose

This file packages the coefficient-ring data of a complete p-modular system.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

ModularSystem records the complete DVR, fraction field, residue field and surjective residue map with maximal-ideal kernel; residue vanishing is exactly membership in that ideal.

Argument or construction

Package the complete discrete valuation ring, characteristic-zero fraction field, characteristic-p residue field and residue-kernel equality. The last equality rewrites vanishing of a residue as membership in the maximal ideal.

External assumptions and dependencies

The fields, complete discrete valuation ring, fraction-field relation and surjective residue map with maximal-ideal kernel stored in ModularSystem. No splitting-root construction is asserted here.
