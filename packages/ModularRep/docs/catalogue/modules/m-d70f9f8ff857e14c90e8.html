applicationauxiliary

Purpose

Attaches centerless and center-two comparisons to the same transported faithful family.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

A stabilizer-equivariant reference-sector bijection yields a choice-independent family whose every matched raw row and canonical reduction carries the corresponding small-center comparison.

Argument or construction

Construct the family from the faithful sector orbit. Restrict it to the original centerless or scalar carrier and apply the pair comparison to the same class match. In the center-two branch local scalar action follows from canonical block compatibility; reduction availability is used only for the separate existential consequence.

External assumptions and dependencies

Branch-specific center/outer data, cyclic extension principle, reference-fibre equivariance and, for center two, local block compatibility.
