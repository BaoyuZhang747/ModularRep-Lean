Purpose

Constructs a selector in a prescribed type-A diagonal orbit from the literal FLZ constituent theorem on actual SL/SU inside GL/GU.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

At prime two with odd defining prime, the prescribed standard pair is actual SL/SU inside GL/GU in degree at least two, with literal matrix Frobenius and graph formulas.

Argument or construction

Choose an upper character lying above the prescribed lower base. FLZ Theorem 8.1(1) selects a factorizing constituent below it, and Clifford conjugacy puts it in the base orbit. In degree two, graph innerness enlarges the published field actor to the full field-plus-graph subgroup before return restriction.

External assumptions and dependencies

Requires Sources.above, the literal Clifford85_87Source, and published:FLZ2021Theorem81 on the same actual matrix pair and compatible roots, together with SpathCoefficientField at two. MatrixModel supplies degree at least two, positive field exponent, prime p distinct from two, and the actual GL/GU presentation. FieldGraphData supplies both matrix-value formulas and the degree-two innerness witness.
