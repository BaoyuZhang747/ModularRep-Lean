Purpose

Construct the two-point outer action from a quotient of order two and a noninner automorphism.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

A finite automorphism quotient of cardinality two, together with a noninner element, admits a two-point action whose kernel is exactly the inner subgroup.

Argument or construction

Use the regular action of the quotient and an equivalence with the two-element carrier. Faithfulness of the quotient action computes the kernel of the pulled-back action.

External assumptions and dependencies

The finite actual group, characteristic prime, compatible coefficient/root data, specified sets of primitive blocks and characters, and the imported transport results are fixed as in the declaration context. Outer-quotient cardinality, kernel/class-map laws or the stated specified Brauer census are input facts; the named sporadic identification is not derived here.
