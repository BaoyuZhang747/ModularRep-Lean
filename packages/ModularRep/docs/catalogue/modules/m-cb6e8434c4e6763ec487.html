ModularRep.PaperProofs.TypeBPrimitiveRootEquivalence

ModularRep/PaperProofs/TypeBPrimitiveRootEquivalence.lean

carrier-constructionauxiliary

Purpose

Two specified primitive m-th roots determine the unique multiplicative equivalence of the full m-th-root groups carrying the first root to the second.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

Both parametrizations use the same exponent in ZMod m. It assumes no map between the rings, no characteristic or splitting-field certificate, and no supplied root equivalence.

Argument or construction

This is a K construction over commutative domains. A homomorphism from the root group is determined by this one specified root.

External assumptions and dependencies

It assumes no map between the rings, no characteristic or splitting-field certificate, and no supplied root equivalence. A pointwise value equation suffices; no equality of chosen unit presentations is needed. Uniqueness is among all group equivalences with the specified generator value.
