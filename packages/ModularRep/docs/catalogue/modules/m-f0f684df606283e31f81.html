Purpose

Defines the same-parameter proper-Levi geometry and complete modular Jordan packet needed by the current Brauer representative proof.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

Each semisimple parameter fixes the original minimal proper Levi, its rational subgroups, complete literal idempotent packet and original coefficient roots.

Argument or construction

The geometric record supplies component presentations and their specified lower-block interpretation. Its extension adds the published modular Jordan bijection, block correspondence and exact diagonal/field formulas on the same full packets; no selected representative or stabilizer conclusion is a field.

External assumptions and dependencies

The interpretation of the minimal Levi uses Malle--Testerman. Ruhstorfer, Lemma 4.5(a)--(b), supplies a conjugate Frobenius stabilising the Levi and its idempotent. The manuscript then conjugates the Levi and the corresponding dual pair. The modular Jordan correspondence uses the FLZ source. The component and block-support interpretation is E1/E2/U on those exact groups. Common roots, restriction sources, and the complete Jordan/block equivariance equations must be supplied; this module constructs no source inhabitant.
