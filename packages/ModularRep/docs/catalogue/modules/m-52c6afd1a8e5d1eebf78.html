carrier-constructionauxiliary

Purpose

Provides a finite-subgroup prototype for dependent normaliser-character pairs.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

Group equivalences transport pairs and their conjugacy classes; an inner twist fixing a pair fixes its class under the original automorphism.

Argument or construction

Normalizer equivalences and character pullback supply the dependent action laws. The inverse-action factorisation proves the right-action orbit statement.

External assumptions and dependencies

This prototype uses finite subgroups, not the algebraic e-tori required by the actual set of generic weights.
