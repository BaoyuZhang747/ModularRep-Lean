Purpose

Constructs an original packet by the exhaustive central-kernel split.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

For a universal central extension of a nonabelian simple group with prime-order p-prime center and the supplied outer and character data, one original matched row has an original packet.

Argument or construction

Split its actual central kernel into bottom and the full center. Use the faithful original-packet construction in the first case. In the second, derive central triviality and the required outer quotient size, then apply the trivial-sector original-packet construction to the same matched row.

External assumptions and dependencies

Original quotient and ambient block data, canonical reduction and raw block match, equivariant correspondence and Q=1 law, root seeds and extension principles.
