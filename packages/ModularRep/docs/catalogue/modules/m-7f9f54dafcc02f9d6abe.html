Purpose

Chooses the global covering-block extension while retaining the local one.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

Given literal base restriction, a cyclic quotient and uniform covering/twist principles, the global extension can be chosen so its block is induced from the stored local extension block.

Argument or construction

Covering is proved from the local model operators and their restriction. The subgroup-intersection catalogue is transported through its actual equivalence, after which the covering-block extension theorem selects the global character.

External assumptions and dependencies

Navarro9495 and Navarro820 principles, an actual cyclic quotient, literal roots and global base-root agreement, actual primitive decompositions/catalogues, the derived base induction and local representation restriction, plus the stated coefficient field record.
