Purpose

Extends same-map Q=1 normalization to arbitrary raw representatives.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

Every raw Q=1 row representing the image of a faithful character has an original defect-zero character with that reduction, and its canonical local Brauer character equals the global restriction.

Argument or construction

Extract the global ordinary character from the raw Q=1 weight. Block compatibility makes its reduction faithful and SameMapQOneOutput identifies its image. Injectivity of the faithful map identifies the original character; compare the two reduction equations pointwise.

External assumptions and dependencies

The same faithful map's Q=1 output, canonical availability and compatibility, and defect-zero/trivial-weight sources.
