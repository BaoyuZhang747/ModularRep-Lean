Purpose

Adds an honest ambient extension to the selected rank-three Jordan representative.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

For a selected original Levi character with extension and the authenticated Jordan packet data, transports its representative, stabilizer factorization and extension to that same Jordan image.

Argument or construction

The representative theorem supplies stabilizer factorization and equality of field fixers. Cyclic extension applied at that equality gives an extension of the same ambient character.

External assumptions and dependencies

The complete representative/Jordan source package and the existing cyclic-extension principle; no Morita functor is constructed here.
