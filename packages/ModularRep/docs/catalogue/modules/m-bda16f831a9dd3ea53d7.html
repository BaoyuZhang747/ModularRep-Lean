Purpose

Eliminates the central character kernel in the centreless case.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

The intersection of the centre with any affording kernel is trivial, making the quotient canonically isomorphic to the original group.

Argument or construction

The quotient map is bijective and transports the fixed cover. Equation-(3.17) relation semantics and reverse block-fibre data are not eliminated by this argument.

External assumptions and dependencies

Centrelessness and the fixed cover and character packets.
