Mathematical statement and assumptions

On the actual full central character quotient of a full universal cover, the supplied Spath Theorem 4.4 source equates the four extension/block clauses with the actual modular triple condition including the full raw-pair stabilizer containment.

Argument or construction

Package the actual universal cover, full central character quotient, matched local weight and full stabilizer containment. Apply the specified two directions of Spath Theorem 4.4 to convert clause III and the actual triple condition.

External assumptions and dependencies

Exact E2 Spath 2017 Theorem 4.4; full universal cover, full central character kernel quotient, actual matched weight and own reduction, full local stabilizer containment and E1/U specified dictionaries. The ordinary coefficient field is algebraically closed in this criterion.
