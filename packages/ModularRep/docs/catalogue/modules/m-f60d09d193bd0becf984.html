Purpose

Allocates local ordinary rows by actual interval evaluation.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

A complete injective local ordinary catalogue and Boolean interval evaluations on a target primitive identify the hit-row subtype with that block's representative defect-zero fibre.

Argument or construction

The interval formula converts evaluation one into equality of the induced ambient block. Restricting the row equivalence then yields the actual fibre without assuming a block assignment or count.

External assumptions and dependencies

Actual local induction operations and NormalizerIntervalSource; for the row equivalence, a complete injective local ordinary catalogue, its canonical reductions/compatibility and the Boolean interval evaluations on the selected primitive. Neither induced block labels nor fibre counts are assumed.
