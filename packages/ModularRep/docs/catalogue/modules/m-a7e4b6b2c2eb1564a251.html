ModularRep.BrauerCharacterEquivTransport

ModularRep/BrauerCharacterEquivTransport.lean

transport-adaptercurrent

Purpose

This file transports the explicit prime regular root embedding and the function-valued irreducible Brauer characters used by the project along a group equivalence.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

A group equivalence transports the fixed root embedding and an irreducible Brauer function to the pullback function on the target, realized by the transported representation.

Argument or construction

Transport the finite root group and affording representation along a group isomorphism. Its characteristic polynomial at corresponding elements is unchanged, yielding the transported irreducible Brauer function.

External assumptions and dependencies

A finite group equivalence and the given prime regular root embedding, transported along that same equivalence, with the coefficient hypotheses of the Brauer carrier.
