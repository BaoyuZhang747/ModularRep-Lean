Purpose

Builds independent products of the four surviving basic matrix models.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

The product subgroup has a literal independent block-diagonal image, with source multiplicities unconstrained by a triangular-number condition.

Argument or construction

Each copy varies independently under the product embedding. Character core sizes are assigned per local colour later, rather than imposed on subgroup multiplicity.

External assumptions and dependencies

The actual basic models and coordinate embedding; arbitrary products are not asserted radical or principal.
