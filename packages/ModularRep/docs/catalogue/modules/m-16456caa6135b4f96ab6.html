Purpose

Extends conformal translates on the original inertia group.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

Equality of the two inertia subgroups permits the target character to extend using the original base, ambient group and roots.

Argument or construction

The stabilizer equality proves invariance on that fixed base. The same cyclic extension principle supplies the extension without substituting a new inertia group.

External assumptions and dependencies

The actual inertia equality, Navarro cyclic extension and compatible roots.
