Purpose

The Clifford and matrix field actions use the same coordinate Frobenius. The field squares follow from their proved vector formulas and faithful linear actions; they are not additional structural source fields.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

The matrix automorphism and the Clifford field automorphism must act on the same finite coordinate field and norm kernel.

Argument or construction

The established pointwise vector square identifies their restrictions on every element of Spin. This yields the equivariance of the fixed Spin-to-matrix projection.

External assumptions and dependencies

The quotient field square uses the existing actual A/P action on literal Spin/Z(Spin), at the prime field generator, for every quotient element.
