Purpose

This thin neutral adapter combines the independently constructed ordinary and Brauer descents.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

For a prime-to-p-kernel surjection, a kernel-constant ordinary irreducible character and its compatible irreducible Brauer reduction descend to a pair satisfying the same reduction equality downstairs.

Argument or construction

Descend the ordinary character using kernel constancy and descend its irreducible Brauer partner on a kernel-trivial realization. Prime regular surjectivity and compatible roots reflect the original reduction equality downstairs.

External assumptions and dependencies

A finite surjection, prime-to-p kernel for the defect/reduction conclusions, ordinary kernel constancy, and compatible finite Brauer roots and prime regular lifting where required. Ordinary descent itself comes from representation averaging.
