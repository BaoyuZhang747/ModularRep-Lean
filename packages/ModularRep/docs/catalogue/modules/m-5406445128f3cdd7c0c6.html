Purpose

This module isolates the block-theoretic input needed to identify the entire weight fibre of a defect-zero block.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

A defect-zero block has one Brauer character and one weight class if its ordinary reduction is unique and all its weight subgroups are trivial.

Argument or construction

The weight-subgroup law puts every weight in the trivial-radical image. Reduction injectivity identifies its ordinary character with the unique defect-zero character, giving a singleton weight fibre and hence the fibre equivalence.

External assumptions and dependencies

The finite actual group, characteristic prime, compatible coefficient/root data, specified sets of primitive blocks and characters, and the imported transport results are fixed as in the declaration context.
