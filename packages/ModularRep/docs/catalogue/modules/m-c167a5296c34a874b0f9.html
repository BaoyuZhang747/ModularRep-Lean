Purpose

Normalizes the cancelled three-block correspondence at every defect-zero datum.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

Any block-preserving global equivalence sends each canonical defect-zero reduction to its canonical trivial-radical weight.

Argument or construction

Restrict Omega and its inverse to the defect-zero block fibre. The independent defect-zero block theorem makes the Brauer fibre a singleton, so the preimage of the canonical weight must be the canonical reduction. The combined endpoint retains the separately supplied character-triple relation and known fibre maps.

External assumptions and dependencies

Defect-zero reduction and block uniqueness, trivial-weight source and its block compatibility. The enriched endpoint additionally requires the independent character-triple retention source.
