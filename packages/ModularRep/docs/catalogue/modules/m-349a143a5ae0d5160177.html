Purpose

Proves existence of an extension in any covering block by index-two sign choice.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

One actual irreducible extension and its sign twist meet every specified covering block in odd characteristic.

Argument or construction

The idempotent plus its sign twist is supported on the base. Its central character evaluation is nonzero, so one of the two representation blocks has value one on the selected idempotent. Catalogue delta laws identify that block.

External assumptions and dependencies

Actual restriction and affording identities, compatible roots, covering and complete block catalogues.
