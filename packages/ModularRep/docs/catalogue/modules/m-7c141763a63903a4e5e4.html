Purpose

Provide the shared certificate types, simple-section definition and classical family deductions.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

MainCertificate and AllPrimeMain preserve the specified group, covering data and full family conclusion. SimpleSection and BlockwiseAWC define the finite group reduction objects.

Argument or construction

Transport certificates along group isomorphisms and provide the Type B and Type C deductions. The complete current manuscript endpoint belongs to the separate ManuscriptIBAW application.

External assumptions and dependencies

The stated classical source interfaces and the published finite group reduction source. No complete hypothesis-bundle inhabitant is asserted.
