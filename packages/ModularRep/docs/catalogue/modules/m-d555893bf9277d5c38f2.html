Purpose

Transports the same ordinary factorization into local Brauer and block images.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

Two canonical reductions and an ordinary qW factorization yield a normalizer Brauer pullback identity and, with primitive-image laws, equality of the actual local block images.

Argument or construction

The commuting normalizer square proves equality pointwise on regular elements. Derived root agreement then permits the literal central-quotient block-image theorem to identify the specified primitive idempotents.

External assumptions and dependencies

Two canonical reductions and the ordinary qW factorization. The specified-block theorem additionally assumes central prime-to-p Z, actual upstairs/downstairs operations and compatibility, and the local guarded primitive-image law; it does not assume the ambient weight-block image.
