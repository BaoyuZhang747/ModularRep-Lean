ModularRep.ModularTraceRegularPart

ModularRep/ModularTraceRegularPart.lean

theorem-developmentcurrent

Purpose

For a finite group representation over a field of characteristic p, the trace of an element is the trace of its canonical p-regular part.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

For a modular representation of a finite group, the trace of any element equals the trace of its prime regular part.

Argument or construction

Separate each finite-order element into commuting primary and prime regular parts. The primary operator is unipotent; the extra nilpotent contribution has trace zero, so trace depends only on the regular part.

External assumptions and dependencies

A finite group, p prime and finite dimensional representation over characteristic-p k; the constructed primary decomposition and nilpotent trace facts are prior results.
