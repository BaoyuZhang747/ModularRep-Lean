ModularRep.BlockFibreRestriction

ModularRep/BlockFibreRestriction.lean

theorem-developmentcurrent

Purpose

Restrict a block diagonal integral basic-set isomorphism to a genuine block fibre and its stabilizer action.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

An integral isomorphism preserving the supported submodules of a block in both directions restricts to an isomorphism on that fibre. Compatible actions and the stated Conlon hypotheses then give an equivariant set equivalence on the fibre.

Argument or construction

Restrict the same integral isomorphism and its inverse to the supported submodules of one block. Restrict equivariance to its stabilizer and apply the basic-set and Conlon bridge on those fibres.

External assumptions and dependencies

The given block maps, integral basic-set isomorphism and support-preservation for it and its inverse. Equivariant conclusions require fibre-action compatibility, K-zero naturality, a hypoelementary actor and the Conlon/Burnside sources.
