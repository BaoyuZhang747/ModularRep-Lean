Purpose

Constructs equivariant fibre maps from involution counts.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

For finite block fibres and actions that are either identity or a common involution, equal totals and fixed counts on invariant blocks give stabilizer-equivariant representative maps.

Argument or construction

On fixed block orbits the involution classification constructs the required bijection. On exchanged blocks any fibre bijection is compatible because a nontrivial stabilizer action would contradict the block exchange.

External assumptions and dependencies

Finite block fibres of two equivariant maps, a common involution, an action dichotomy into identity or that involution, equal block totals and equal fixed counts for invariant blocks. No sporadic correspondence source is used in this generic construction.
