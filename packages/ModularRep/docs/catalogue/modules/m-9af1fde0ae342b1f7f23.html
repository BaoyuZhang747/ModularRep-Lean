Purpose

Computes complete specified quotient fibres for an original block reference.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

Canonical centreless quotient transport and individual primitive dictionaries yield both full fibres, reverse maps and the same matching's quotient graph equivariance.

Argument or construction

The quotient root and idempotents are transported through the actual equivalence. The original chosen quotient pair remains the one retaining localReduction exactly.

External assumptions and dependencies

Actual quotient operations and the original witness, without a reverse-fibre or selected-pair source field.
