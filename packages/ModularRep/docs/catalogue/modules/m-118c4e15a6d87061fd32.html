In odd defining characteristic the actual geometric centre of the simply connected type B group Gbar has order two (Malle--Testerman, Proposition 9.15 and Table 9.2, pp. 71--72).

Argument or construction

Identifying the subgroup commutator with algebraic derived points retains the existing point-carrier source boundary. Lean deduction: extract x = a*z from membership in the actual geometric paired Levi; derive a^-1*F(a) = z*F(z)^-1 in the actual centre of Gbar; use its order to derive rationality of a^2 and z^2. This proves square membership in the literal L C_M(L) and then the actual quotient exponent.
