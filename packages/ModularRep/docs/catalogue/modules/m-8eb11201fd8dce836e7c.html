Combines the original-cover family with a possibly nontrivial centre.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

One normalized block-preserving correspondence, an all-inner cover and unselected quotient data produce every original packet.

Argument or construction

All-inner action proves equivariance of the supplied map directly on Brauer functions and weight conjugacy classes. For each actual match, the cover supplies perfectness and prime-to-p centre order and the own-quotient identity constructor supplies its packet.

External assumptions and dependencies

An ell-prime cover with all-inner automorphisms, the actual root/carrier data, a canonical reduction for every raw weight, defect-zero and trivial-weight sources, and one normalized block-preserving global equivalence. Canonical block compatibility, QuotientDataFamily and SpathCoefficientField remain explicit; equivariance follows from the all-inner action.
