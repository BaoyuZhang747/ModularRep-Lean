Purpose

Lifts an exhaustive radical-conjugacy index through a central quotient.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

Under the prime-to-p central quotient hypotheses, a bijective index of quotient radical classes gives a bijective index of their computed lifts.

Argument or construction

The construction composes the supplied class-index equivalence with the inverse radical-class equivalence. The representative formula records the exact lifted subgroup used in subsequent local fibres.

External assumptions and dependencies

The central prime-to-p quotient and its radical equivalence, plus a supplied complete bijective quotient radical-conjugacy index. The lifted index is constructed, not assumed.
