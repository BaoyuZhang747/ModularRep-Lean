Purpose

Proves the small conductor relations used in faithful evaluation.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

Primitive roots of orders 5, 21, 23, 29 and 33 satisfy the displayed polynomial relations.

Argument or construction

Prime conductors use geometric sums; composite conductors combine lower-order geometric sums by explicit polynomial identities. The result is independent of any table realization.

External assumptions and dependencies

The field and primitive-root hypotheses at the specified orders 5, 21, 23, 29 and 33. No Brauer embedding or table realization occurs.
