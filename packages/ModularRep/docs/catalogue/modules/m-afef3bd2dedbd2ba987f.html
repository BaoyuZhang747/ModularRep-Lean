Purpose

Derives the trivial radical from one actual trivial-defect block.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

A block with bottom maximal Brauer defect forces the ambient p-core to be bottom and hence gives TrivialWeightSource.

Argument or construction

The normal p-core has nonzero central Brauer support on this block. Maximal trivial defect therefore makes the p-core trivial. Apply the characterization of the trivial radical; the resulting source is unique because its fields are propositions.

External assumptions and dependencies

One actual maximal trivial-defect statement and the internal normal-p support theorem.
