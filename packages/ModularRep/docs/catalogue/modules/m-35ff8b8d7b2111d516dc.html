Purpose

Derives principal weight-class field fixation and ambient factorization.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

FM Corollary 4.3(a) fixes actual principal Brauer characters, and the same FM bijection transfers this to weight classes upstairs and downstairs.

Argument or construction

Surjectivity and equivariance give weight fixation. In the actual semidirect product the field factor then drops out of the stabilizer test.

External assumptions and dependencies

FM 4.3(a), FM 6.2 and computed central descent; no raw field-stable representative is selected.
