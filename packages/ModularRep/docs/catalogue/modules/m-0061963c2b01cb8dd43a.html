Purpose

Multiply by the inverse of the element implementing the inner twist.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

When twisting is inner, multiplication by the implementing inverse changes twisted conjugation into ordinary conjugation and commutes with the stated inner powers.

Argument or construction

Multiply by the inverse of the element implementing the inner twist. Group identities turn twisted conjugation into ordinary conjugation and prove compatibility with powers of the implementing element.

External assumptions and dependencies

A group and the element implementing the inner twist, with the stated natural-number powers.
