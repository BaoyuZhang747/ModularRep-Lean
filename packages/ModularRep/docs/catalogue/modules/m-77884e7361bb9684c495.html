Purpose

Transports literal weights between inverse central sectors.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

The actual weight action of a centre-inverting automorphism identifies inverse sectors; a centre of order three has two nontrivial sectors with equal weight cardinality.

Argument or construction

The sector projection is equivariant, so twisting restricts to a sector equivalence. The elementary order-three character calculation identifies the remaining sector with the inverse one.

External assumptions and dependencies

The actual equivariant weight sector map and centre-inverting automorphism. The two-sector count specialization uses centre order three and a count in one nontrivial sector; no canonical local reduction occurs.
