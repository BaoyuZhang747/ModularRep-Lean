Purpose

Obtains individual global and local extensions from a single root seed.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

The original ambient with cyclic quotient of order two admits global and local character-extension witnesses whose root conventions agree on the base.

Argument or construction

Derive global and local base invariance. Apply the cyclic extension principle to the ambient quotient and its local subgroup, restricting the same root seed to the local ambient.

External assumptions and dependencies

The full-cover trivial-sector ambient, same matched equivariant correspondence, root seed and cyclic character-extension principle. Common Q=1 choice and block compatibility are established later.
