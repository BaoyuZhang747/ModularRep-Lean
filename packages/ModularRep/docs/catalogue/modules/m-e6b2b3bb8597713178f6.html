Purpose

The standard projective conformal action on the literal matrix Sp4. The coordinate matrix is fixed before the routine E1 form dictionary is introduced.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

The displayed two-half conformal form and mathlib matrix Sp4 are related by the two clauses of an actual form-preservation dictionary.

Argument or construction

The matrix map is injective and its multiplier-one image is exactly Sp4. Conjugation preserves that kernel, and scalar matrices act trivially, so the action descends through the quotient by all conformal scalars.

External assumptions and dependencies

Its specified interpretation is routine E1/U; it contains no chosen action.
