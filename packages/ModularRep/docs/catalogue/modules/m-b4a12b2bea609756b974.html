Purpose

Places quotient Brauer counts at the same specified block used by representative rows.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

The actual trivial-sector block and its computed quotient block have equal Brauer cardinalities and equal fixed cardinalities for intertwined automorphisms.

Argument or construction

Identify the representative module's quotientBlock with quotientBlockIndex. Specialize the specified Brauer block-fibre and fixed-fibre transport theorems to this exact index.

External assumptions and dependencies

Central p-prime quotient hypotheses, the primitive-image source and the actual automorphism square.
