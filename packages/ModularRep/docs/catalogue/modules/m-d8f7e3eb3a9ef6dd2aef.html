Purpose

Identifies a full-size joint radical fibre with its block.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

If the joint block/radical fibre and full block each have three weights, their literal inclusion is an equivalence and preserves fixed points.

Argument or construction

Injectivity and equal finite cardinality imply surjectivity. Restricting this equivalence to fixed elements transfers a fixed count of one without assuming radical support for the entire block.

External assumptions and dependencies

The literal weight block map, chosen radical and block, independent cardinalities three for the joint and full block fibres, and the chosen automorphism. A fixed count on the joint fibre is needed only for the final numerical specialization.
