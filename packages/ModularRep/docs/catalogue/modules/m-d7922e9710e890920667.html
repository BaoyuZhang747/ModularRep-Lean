Purpose

Chooses the strong FLZ output jointly with its root metadata.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

A family already admissible in one modular system receives a matched-pair output and roots for that same output at every required subgroup.

Argument or construction

The joint source retains the strong certificate rather than upgrading an arbitrary old certificate. Metadata alone supplies no extension, block or normalization laws.

External assumptions and dependencies
