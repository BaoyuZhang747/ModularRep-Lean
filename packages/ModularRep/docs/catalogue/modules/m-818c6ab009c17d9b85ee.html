Purpose

This module checks the finite group and finite action deductions in the proof of lem:type-b-regular-levi-orbits.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

The Geck--Malle quotient maps onto the product of centre coinvariants. Their factor maps give the outer quotients of the diagonal action images, while the direct product of rational derived factors supplies all independent inner corrections.

Argument or construction

The strongest interface has the exact coordinate shape supplied by the cocycle argument in the manuscript. Their factor maps give the outer quotients of the diagonal action images, while the direct product of rational derived factors supplies all independent inner corrections. Lean derives surjectivity onto the product of the factor images, factor-supported lifts, and then the Cartesian description of every orbit.

External assumptions and dependencies

In particular, neither product surjectivity nor the orbit conclusion is assumed. Its strongest interface starts with the actual finite fixed-point inclusions, normaliser relation, and intersection, without assuming that fixed points commute with an algebraic central product. The finite fixed-point inputs are exactly those supplied by the regular-Levi construction: L ≤ M, M ≤ N_Γ(L), the central fixed points lie in M, and M ∩ G ≤ L.
