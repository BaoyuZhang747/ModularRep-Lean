Purpose

Constructs the unique dominating Spin block of a matrix Omega block.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

The projection is the actual Clifford norm-kernel vector action and blocks are specified primitive idempotents.

Argument or construction

The central normal two-subgroup principle gives the block equivalence through the group algebra map. The trivial representation preserves principality, so a nonprincipal lower block has a unique nonprincipal dominating block.

External assumptions and dependencies

Navarro central p-kernel principle and existing projection/centre facts; no ordinary coefficient field or q≠3 restriction.
