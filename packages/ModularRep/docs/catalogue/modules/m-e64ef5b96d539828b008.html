Purpose

Constructs the higher-rank principal GGGR basis on the literal Spin norm kernel.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

For odd fields and rank at least four, the original principal GGGR projections form a rational basis indexed by all rational unipotent classes.

Argument or construction

The proof selects ordinary rows from the abelian and nonabelian component cases, combines permutation diagonal blocks, and uses closure vanishing to obtain independence. Natural PIM expansions and the class count supply rational spanning.

External assumptions and dependencies

Chaneb Proposition 2.8 for abelian diagonal identities; the upper same-family selection and coverage of Geck-Hezard with Taylor's small-characteristic removal; actual restriction and component parametrizations; Taylor Lemma 14.15/Proposition 15.2; Chaneb 2.7/CE 21.14 counts; ordinary orthogonality and specified modular-system/PIM sources.
