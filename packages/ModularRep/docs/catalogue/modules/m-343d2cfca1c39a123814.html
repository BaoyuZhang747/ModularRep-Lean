ModularRep.DecompositionMap

ModularRep/DecompositionMap.lean

source-interfaceretained-alternative

Purpose

ExactGrothendieckGroup constructs the relevant exact K₀ presentations, but the classical decomposition map between them still requires the lattice-independence theorem from modular representation theory.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

A supplied additive decomposition interface realizing every stable-lattice reduction forces the classes of any two such reductions of the same ordinary representation to be equal.

Argument or construction

Store an additive decomposition map and a law realizing every stable lattice reduction. Equality of two reduction classes follows by rewriting both with that same supplied map.

External assumptions and dependencies

A supplied additive or exact decomposition homomorphism together with its uniform realization of stable lattice reduction. These interfaces do not independently establish that realization or lattice independence.
