Formalisation.BlockCancellation

packages/formalisation/Formalisation/BlockCancellation.lean

theorem-developmentcurrent

Purpose

Translate an equivariant equivalence into equality of fixed-point and two-cycle counts.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

An equivariant equivalence of finite involution sets cancels a known equivariantly matched invariant part. For exchanged two-block remainders the resulting equivalence can be chosen to preserve the blocks.

Argument or construction

Translate an equivariant equivalence into equality of fixed-point and two-cycle counts. Cancel the known invariant part, then classify the remaining involution sets; for exchanged blocks choose one block map and extend it by the involutions.

External assumptions and dependencies

Finite types with specified involutions and an equivariant equivalence of their decompositions, plus an equivariant equivalence of the known parts. Exchanged-block specializations include their block-switching bijections.
