This file combines the existing one-field Navarro (4.14) Phase-A source with the kernel block-induction selector and the canonical local expansion of an interval central Brauer image.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

Under the literal catalogues and interval central character equality, the local primitive support of an ambient block's normalizer Brauer image is exactly the fibre of the induced-block map.

Argument or construction

Expand the normalizer Brauer image of an ambient block into primitive local support. The interval central character equation and delta evaluations identify that support with the fibre of the induced-block map.

External assumptions and dependencies

The literal local/ambient primitive decompositions and central character catalogues and the Navarro 4.14 interval identity. No exact-defect or full First Main Theorem source is used for this support-fibre identification.
