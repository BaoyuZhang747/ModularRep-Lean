Purpose

Shows that a character stabilizer fixes its computed ambient packet idempotent.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

The source gives covariance of the same finite block-sum family under actual Spin automorphisms.

Argument or construction

Fixing a character fixes its specified supporting block. That block supports the original and transported packet, forcing equal parameter indices and hence equal block sums.

External assumptions and dependencies

Packet-family covariance and specified support formulas; no full-field actor normalization or outer-image realization.
