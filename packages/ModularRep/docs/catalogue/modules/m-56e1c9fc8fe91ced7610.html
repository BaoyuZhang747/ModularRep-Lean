Purpose

This file specializes the universal property of the exact Grothendieck-group presentation to finite-dimensional representations.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

An exact decomposition homomorphism realizing stable-lattice reduction identifies all reduction classes of the same ordinary representation; its realization is a hypothesis.

Argument or construction

Package an exact Grothendieck-group homomorphism and realization of stable reduction. Both lattice classes equal the image of the original representation class, giving lattice independence relative to the supplied realization.

External assumptions and dependencies

A supplied additive or exact decomposition homomorphism together with its uniform realization of stable lattice reduction. These interfaces do not independently establish that realization or lattice independence.
