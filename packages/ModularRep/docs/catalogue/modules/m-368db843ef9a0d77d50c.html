theorem-developmentcurrent

Purpose

Corrects the FM map within diagonal orbits to satisfy the matched relation.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

For an involutive diagonal commuting with the full action, an orbit witness and simultaneous relation covariance produce an equivariant corrected bijection.

Argument or construction

The correction keeps a character when the original pair is good and swaps its diagonal orbit otherwise. Covariance makes the choice invariant, proving involutivity and the relation for the refined map.

External assumptions and dependencies

The derived diagonal-action laws, Brough orbit witness and authentic relation covariance; the corrected map can differ from FM's original choice.
