Purpose

Forces all three weights fixed using two distinct singleton quotient radical rows.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

An actual three-weight block with two distinct fixed radical classes, each having one appropriate quotient row, has three fixed weights.

Argument or construction

Transport the two quotient singleton fibres upstairs and preserve distinctness and fixedness of their radical classes. Each supplies a fixed weight. The involution parity argument then fixes the third; the final constructor derives singleton row counts from exhaustive ordinary catalogues and their block-allocation evaluations.

External assumptions and dependencies

The independent total-three count, quotient/ordinary catalogue identification, canonical reductions and block transport, and the compatible automorphism square.
