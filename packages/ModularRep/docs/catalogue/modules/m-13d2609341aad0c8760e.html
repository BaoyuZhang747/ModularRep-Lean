Purpose

Constructs the principal family's common reference quotient from its trivial character.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

The actual identity prime-to-two cover forces centrelessness of the original base.

Argument or construction

The supported trivial representation supplies the reference. Its common central kernel, quotient cover and all quotient characters are then computed with the same roots and values.

External assumptions and dependencies

Specified principal idempotent, actual identity cover and the existing centreless quotient construction.
