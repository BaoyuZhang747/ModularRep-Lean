Formalisation.SylowObstruction

packages/formalisation/Formalisation/SylowObstruction.lean

theorem-developmentcurrent

Purpose

The new reflection argument in Proposition 3.3 constructs a 2-subgroup of the relevant centraliser which properly contains the centre of a proposed radical subgroup.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

A Sylow p-subgroup cannot lie properly in a p-subgroup, so a purported p-overgroup containing an element outside it is impossible.

Argument or construction

Every p-subgroup lies in a Sylow subgroup. A p-subgroup containing a nominated Sylow can have no larger order, so a purported new element gives a contradiction.

External assumptions and dependencies

A Sylow p-subgroup, a p-subgroup containing it and, for the contradiction form, a proposed element of the larger subgroup outside the Sylow.
