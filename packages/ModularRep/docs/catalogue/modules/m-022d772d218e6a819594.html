Purpose

Computes the actual groups needed for upward central-two inflation.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

The fixed Sp-to-PSp stabilizer projection gives exact base, local and intersection preimages and their image equivalences.

Argument or construction

Surjectivity and kernel calculations use the same ambient projection. The original upstairs raw weight is retained while the downstairs global character can vary.

External assumptions and dependencies

The actual cover, compatible Brauer inflation and quotient-weight naturality; full raw containment is separate.
