Purpose

Locates every defect-zero reduction in specified role two.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

With three exhaustive block roles, literal four-character role one, the principal role and the ordinary singleton law, every global defect-zero reduction belongs to role two.

Argument or construction

A singleton defect-zero fibre cannot be the four-character block. Divisibility of the group order excludes the trivial Brauer character's block, leaving the third role by exhaustive enumeration.

External assumptions and dependencies

The three specified block roles, complete six-row ordinary allocation/value binding for role one, the principal-block identification, group-order divisibility and the retained defect-zero singleton/reduction sources.
