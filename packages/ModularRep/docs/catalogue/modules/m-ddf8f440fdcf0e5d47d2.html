Purpose

The section dependency modules use different node types and, in a few places, duplicate a preliminary or type C result under a section-specific name.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

Explicit semantic links between section ledger interpretations, residual external leaves and derived rules give conditional headline and reduction claims through the checked case divisions.

Argument or construction

Link section ledger interpretations by explicit semantic maps and combine their conditional results with exhaustive case splits. The supplied headline and reduction realizations yield conditional claims, with no independent proof of literature or carrier assumptions.

External assumptions and dependencies

The finite node/case data and, for propagation, the supplied interpretation, external leaves, derived proof rules and semantic links. Ranked acyclicity does not inspect assumptions closed over by a proof rule.
