Purpose

This file isolates the elementary finite group consequences of the order formula |Sp₆(2)| = 2^9 * 3^4 * 5 * 7.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

The specified fixed-point group order of Sp6(2) has the computed prime support and prime-order Sylow groups at five and seven, so every subgroup at either prime is cyclic.

Argument or construction

Compute prime support and the full five and seven parts of the specified fixed-point group order. Sylow subgroups at those primes have prime order, giving cyclicity of every such prime subgroup.

External assumptions and dependencies

The concrete order, field-size, centre-size or cyclic-torus equalities explicitly displayed in the theorem. Arithmetic deductions are internal; no concrete group identification, defect theorem or iBAW theorem follows from an order alone.
