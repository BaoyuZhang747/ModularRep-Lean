Purpose

Proves rank 41 and symmetrized rank 36 for the complete literal trivial-sector matrix.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

At a primitive root of conductor 10015005, the 108 by 91 rows have rank 41 and their outer-permutation symmetrizations have rank 36, including the same-iota specialization.

Argument or construction

Use all closed arithmetic checks to build the representative inverse and expand it to full columns. The representative permutation has 41 points and 31 fixed points, so its symmetrized image has dimension 36. Transport the statement to the original iota's target root.

External assumptions and dependencies

The closed integer checks, local modulus identities and column routing/permutation certificates.
