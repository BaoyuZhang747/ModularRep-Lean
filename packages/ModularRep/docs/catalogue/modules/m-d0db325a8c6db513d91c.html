ModularRep.DefectZeroRepresentation

ModularRep/DefectZeroRepresentation.lean

carrier-constructioncurrent

Purpose

This neutral predicate compares the prime parts of the representation dimension and the finite monoid order.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

A finite representation is defect zero when the p-part of its dimension equals the p-part of the group order; this defines a numerical representation predicate.

Argument or construction

Define defect zero by equality of the p-parts of representation dimension and finite group order. This numerical predicate alone makes no assertion about a block or ordinary character realization.

External assumptions and dependencies

A finite group, coefficient field and finite dimensional representation, and the numerical p-part operation. No character realization or block assertion is included.
