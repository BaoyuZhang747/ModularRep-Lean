Purpose

Realizes the selected local Brauer character on the named base.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

The actual local quotient character inflates to the literal named normalizer base at a root convention derived from the selected weight.

Argument or construction

The radical is a p-group, so passing to its quotient preserves the prime-to-p root exponent. The normalizer equivalence and pullback equation show that the chosen local model affords exactly the named Brauer character.

External assumptions and dependencies

The selected weight's actual local reduction, named normalizer-base equivalence and local model. The radical p-group quotient gives root-exponent agreement; no independent normalizer-root agreement is supplied.
