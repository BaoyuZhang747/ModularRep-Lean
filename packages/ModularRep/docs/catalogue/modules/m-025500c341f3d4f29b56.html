Computes actual simple-quotient coordinates for the case join.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

Whole-centre transport and the finite coefficient field equivalence identify fixed-point models and two-element-field projective models with the desired PSp.

Argument or construction

Only the simple-target coordinate of an existing cover projection is changed. Its total group and cover remain fixed, including the odd-prime double cover of Sp6(2).

External assumptions and dependencies

Actual group equivalences and the existing centreless source identification; no witness transport is claimed.
