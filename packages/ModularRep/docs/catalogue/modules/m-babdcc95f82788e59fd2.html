ModularRep.GroupAlgebraCentralBrauerMap

ModularRep/GroupAlgebraCentralBrauerMap.lean

theorem-developmentcurrent

Purpose

Coefficient restriction from k[G] to k[C_G(P)] is not multiplicative on arbitrary group algebra elements.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

For a p-subgroup P and characteristic-p coefficients, restriction of central group algebra coefficients to C_G(P) preserves products and one, giving the central Brauer algebra homomorphism.

Argument or construction

Conjugation by the p-subgroup acts on product-coefficient factorizations. All nonfixed orbits have zero total in characteristic p, leaving exactly the fixed factorizations and proving multiplicativity of coefficient restriction.

External assumptions and dependencies

Finite G, characteristic-p coefficients, p prime and the nominated p-subgroup; the invariant p-orbit sum lemma supplies multiplicativity.
