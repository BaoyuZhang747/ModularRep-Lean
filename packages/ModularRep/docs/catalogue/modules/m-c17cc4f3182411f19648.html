theorem-developmentcurrent

Purpose

The support predicate below applies the canonical raw centralizer coefficient restriction to the literal centred block idempotent.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

For a literal block and p-subgroup, nonzero central Brauer restriction is equivalent to nonvanishing of the constructed Brauer-map image of its idempotent. The separate maximal-support predicate has no automatic defect interpretation.

Argument or construction

Apply the constructed central Brauer map to the supplied literal block idempotent. Nonzero restriction is exactly nonvanishing of that image; maximal support is a predicate, whose identification with defect requires a separate source.

External assumptions and dependencies

Finite G, characteristic-p k, a p-subgroup and a supplied literal primitive block decomposition; the nonvanishing/maximality predicates do not themselves include a Navarro defect identification.
