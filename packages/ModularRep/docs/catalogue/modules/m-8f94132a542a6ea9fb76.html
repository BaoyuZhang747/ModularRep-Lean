theorem-developmentcurrent

Purpose

Current Lemma 4.6 assumes that theta extends to its full ambient inertia.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

For N normal in Gamma with Gamma/N abelian, H contains N; the prescribed constituent theta of psi extends to its full Gamma inertia, and E is finite with both subgroups stable.

Argument or construction

The Gallagher correspondent is a quotient-linear character times the restricted ambient extension, and each factor is fixed by Gamma_theta. Induction preserves this fixation; a single H-orbit adjustment of the restriction constituent transfers product stabilizer factorization from theta to psi at every prime.

External assumptions and dependencies

Requires the actual compatible roots on N, H, H_theta and Gamma_theta, the given extension of theta to Gamma_theta, and the Navarro restriction-orbit, Clifford-induction and Gallagher product formulas. SpathCoefficientField requires the algebraically closed residue field to be algebraic over the prime field. The ambient quotient is abelian and the finite E action preserves both subgroups.
