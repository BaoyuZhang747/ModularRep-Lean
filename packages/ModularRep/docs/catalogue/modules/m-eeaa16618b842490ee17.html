Purpose

States the actual cyclic/noncyclic numerical source and its join.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

An actual defect representative for every primitive block and guarded cyclic/noncyclic cardinal equalities imply numerical AWC and, in the centreless all-inner setting, Definition 4.1.

Argument or construction

Cyclicity of the chosen defect representative selects the corresponding numerical source field. The module then invokes complete collapse; the intended published J4 identification is not instantiated by the generic theorem.

External assumptions and dependencies

CyclicNoncyclicNumericalSource on the actual primitive blocks, including existence of a defect representative and cardinal equality guarded by cyclicity or noncyclicity. The Definition 4.1 corollary also needs the centreless all-inner cover, canonical local reduction/compatibility and the stated coefficient and defect-zero/trivial-weight data. The published J4 identification is an intended use of the source, not a constructed source inhabitant.
