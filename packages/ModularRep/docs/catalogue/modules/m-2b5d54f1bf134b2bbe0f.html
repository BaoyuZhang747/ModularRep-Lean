Purpose

Identifies the two intermediate-subgroup intervals in the named Spath construction.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

The pair normalizer's subgroups above its local base correspond order-isomorphically to global stabilizer subgroups above the embedded group.

Argument or construction

Send H to the subgroup generated by the base and its image, and send J to its intersection with the pair normalizer. Normality and the proved product decomposition make these operations inverse. The literal intersection equivalence then aligns local character restrictions.

External assumptions and dependencies

The named pair's product decomposition and the actual subgroup embeddings.
