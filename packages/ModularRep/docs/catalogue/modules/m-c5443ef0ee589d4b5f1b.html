Purpose

Defines actual e-JGC pair classes and their relative Weyl groups.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

Restricted pairs consist of an algebraic torus label and an actual Levi character, with block induction; the relative Weyl group is inertia modulo Levi.

Argument or construction

The dependent action gives the orbit quotient. Choosing a representative yields the actual quotient group and the disjoint union of its defect-zero characters.

External assumptions and dependencies

FMZ interpretation of e-JGC membership and block induction, plus actual ordinary block catalogues.
