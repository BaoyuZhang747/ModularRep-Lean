Purpose

For a raw character weight r = (Q, theta) in a semidirect product H semidirect E, this module identifies its stabiliser with the intersection of the stabiliser of the ambient H-class of r and the normaliser of the embedded subgroup Q.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

The stabilizer of a raw weight class is equivalent to the global weight-orbit stabilizer restricted to the normalizer of its selected radical, with the canonical elementwise ambient map.

Argument or construction

A raw class fixed up to ambient conjugacy and with its radical normalized is fixed as a raw class. This identifies the raw pair stabilizer with the orbit stabilizer intersected with the local normalizer and gives the exact group equivalence.

External assumptions and dependencies

Actual raw and conjugacy class weight actions in the canonical semidirect product. Uniqueness at a fixed radical identifies the stabilizers; no triple source is required.
