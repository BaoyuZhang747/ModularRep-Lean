Purpose

This file proves that the block of an irreducible Brauer character is transported by the canonical right action of group automorphisms on literal primitive block idempotents.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

The primitive block supporting an automorphism-twisted irreducible Brauer character is the transported primitive block of the original character, giving the canonical stabilizer action on a fixed IBr block.

Argument or construction

Transport a simple module and its supporting idempotent along an automorphism. Uniqueness of primitive support proves equivariance of its Brauer block, giving the canonical stabilizer action on a fixed fibre.

External assumptions and dependencies

The actual primitive family and root-based IBr carrier, together with the canonical group algebra automorphism and simple-module support transport.
