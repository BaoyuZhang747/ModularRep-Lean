ModularRep.ReductionModulo

ModularRep/ReductionModulo.lean

theorem-developmentcurrent

Purpose

For a surjective homomorphism f : O →+* k, extension of scalars identifies k ⊗[O] M with M / ker(f)M.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

For a surjective coefficient ring map, scalar extension of a module is canonically the quotient by the kernel ideal times that module, with the expected pure-tensor formula.

Argument or construction

Use the first isomorphism theorem for the residue ring and tensor quotient relations to construct k tensor L isomorphic to L modulo the kernel ideal times L. The formula on pure tensors identifies the comparison explicitly.

External assumptions and dependencies

A surjective ring map and a module; the stable-lattice specialization substitutes the modular-system residue map and its kernel equality.
