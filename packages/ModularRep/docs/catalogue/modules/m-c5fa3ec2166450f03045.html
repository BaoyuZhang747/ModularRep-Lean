ModularRep.IrreducibleBrauerCharacterSurjectiveDescent

ModularRep/IrreducibleBrauerCharacterSurjectiveDescent.lean

transport-adaptercurrent

Purpose

For a finite surjection whose kernel has order prime to p, every p-regular target element has a p-regular lift.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

A kernel-trivial Brauer realization descends along a surjection and pulls back to the original function when the root-lift compatibility holds. Its affording representation remains irreducible.

Argument or construction

Choose a realization whose kernel contains that of the surjection and descend it on the same vector space. Irreducibility and the compatible-root pullback formula prove descent of the actual Brauer function.

External assumptions and dependencies

An actual surjection and a kernel-trivial affording Brauer realization. The displayed compatible-root law for all target representations proves the pullback formula; prime regular surjectivity is used only in the corresponding reflection statements.
