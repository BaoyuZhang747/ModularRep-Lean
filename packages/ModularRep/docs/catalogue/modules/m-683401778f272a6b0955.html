Formalisation.SporadicDependencies

packages/formalisation/Formalisation/SporadicDependencies.lean

dependency-ledgerbookkeeping

Purpose

Records the cited, computed and semantic dependencies of the sporadic argument.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

A finite dependency graph has decreasing ranks, and interpretations satisfying its external leaves and derived rules imply the designated boundary and sporadic nodes.

Argument or construction

Exhaust the finite node type to verify that every dependency has smaller rank, ruling out cycles. Check the boundary arithmetic certificate from the four prime lists. Propagation theorems then evaluate the graph under supplied interpretations; this bookkeeping does not establish the external leaves or itself furnish an iBAW certificate.

External assumptions and dependencies

Interpretations of the published results, Atlas identifications, transcript semantics and derived proof rules. Historical graph nodes have dependency-recording credit only.
