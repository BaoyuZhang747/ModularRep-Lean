Purpose

Restricts the same central quotient fibre correspondence to fixed points.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

Intertwined automorphisms and a stable upstairs radical give equivalent fixed representative-character fibres and fixed weight block/radical fibres.

Argument or construction

The inverse ordinary map is literal inflation along the local quotient. Its surjectivity and the derived local-action square show fixedness is equivalent upstairs and downstairs. Restrict the existing representative equivalence, then transfer through the fixed local-character/weight equivalences.

External assumptions and dependencies

The accepted quotient fibre correspondence, canonical reductions, block images and compatible automorphism square.
