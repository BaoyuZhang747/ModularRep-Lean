Purpose

Constructs independently supported rational conjugations in the regular Levi from geometric component projections and a central Lang equation.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

Rational elements are the literal fixedPoints of its Frobenius, and H is the actual geometric derived subgroup inside B. The final source wrapper must bind Q_i and its injection to the actual rational component-orbit factors.

Argument or construction

Rational elements are the literal fixedPoints of its Frobenius, and H is the actual geometric derived subgroup inside B. Geometric central decomposition is Geck--Malle, Remark 1.7.6(a), p. 85; connectedness of the centre uses Digne--Lehrer--Michel, Lemma 1.4 proof, p. 158, together with connectedness of the regular overgroup's centre. The proof corrects a projected geometric element by a central Lang lift, proves the corrected element is rational, and computes its conjugation.

External assumptions and dependencies

Requires geometric central decomposition on the actual paired Levi, the original Frobenius-compatible component projections and conjugation formulas, and CentralLangSource on its literal centre. Geck--Malle Theorem 1.4.8 supplies the intended Lang consequence only after connected-centre and Steinberg hypotheses are authenticated on that same group. The number of component cycles is finite.
