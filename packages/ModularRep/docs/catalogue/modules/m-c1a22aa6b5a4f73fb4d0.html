Purpose

Fixed ordinary regular restrictions imply pointwise fixedness of the actual Brauer fibre.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

If a spanning family of restricted ordinary characters is fixed by an automorphism, every irreducible Brauer character in the bound block is fixed.

Argument or construction

The induced linear map is the identity on a spanning family and hence on the full Brauer span. Basis independence turns equality of vectors into equality of labels, giving fixed-point count equal to total count.

External assumptions and dependencies

The finite actual group, characteristic prime, compatible coefficient/root data, specified sets of primitive blocks and characters, and the imported transport results are fixed as in the declaration context.
