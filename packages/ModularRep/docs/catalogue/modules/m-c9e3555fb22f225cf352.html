theorem-developmentcurrent

Purpose

The product of the finite and connected centralizer subgroups lies in the full centralizer.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

If the product of the specified finite and connected centralizer subgroups is not contained in any proper Levi, neither is the full centralizer containing that product.

Argument or construction

The product of the finite and connected centralizer subgroups lies in the full centralizer. Noncontainment in every proper Levi is monotone under enlarging a set, so the specified strict condition implies the ordinary noncontainment condition.

External assumptions and dependencies

Actual inclusions of the finite and connected centralizer models in the full centralizer and the proper-Levi predicate. Algebraic/rational group identifications and the substantive quasi-isolation criteria remain external.
