Purpose

This file checks the two deductions made in Lemma 3.12 after the integral basic set has been supplied: * the action of a block stabiliser descends through its subgroup of inner automorphisms, and the resulting equivariant basic-set bijection transfers Li's ordinary-character stabiliser factorisation to actual function-valued irreducible Brauer characters; * the stabiliser in the cyclic field group is cyclic, so Navarro's cyclic extension theorem applies after the concrete copy of the base group and its Brauer characters have been identified.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

Actions on ordinary and Brauer characters in the specified blocks descend through the subgroup of inner automorphisms. The equivariant basic set map transfers the supplied ordinary stabiliser factorisation from Li. A separate argument applies the supplied Navarro extension principle to a cyclic stabiliser in the field automorphism group.

Argument or construction

Descend block-stabilizer actions through inner automorphisms and transfer the supplied Li ordinary factorization along the equivariant integral basic-set bijection. Independently, construct the actual cyclic field stabilizer and apply Navarro's explicit extension principle.

External assumptions and dependencies

Li Lemma 5.4's ordinary stabilizer factorization, the FLZ Theorem 2.14 global integral basic set and its exact K-zero action, Conlon/Burnside detection, and Navarro's cyclic extension principle with the stated finite coefficient and carrier identifications.
