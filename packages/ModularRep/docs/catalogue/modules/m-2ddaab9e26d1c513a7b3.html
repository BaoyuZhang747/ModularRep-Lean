Purpose

Theorem 2.3 of Feng--Li--Zhang, *The inductive blockwise Alperin weight condition for type B and odd primes* (2022), p. 537, supplies an integral basic set for each rational semisimple ell'-series and its Broue--Michel union, subject to its good-prime, nondefining-prime, and centre hypotheses.
