Purpose

Extends the own local ordinary character on each actual inertia quotient.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

The normalizer quotient is identified with the local outer kernel, and the supplied raw pair's own character extends to inertia modulo Q.

Argument or construction

Raw-pair fixation proves character invariance after the computed quotient equivalence. The ordinary cyclic extension principle supplies the character extension.

External assumptions and dependencies

Isaacs cyclic extension and the existing Brough group geometry, with no principal-membership premise.
