Purpose

A subgroup containing the canonical field factor splits into its intersections with the two canonical factors.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

The subgroup lies in an actual semidirect product and contains its canonical field factor; in the action application that factor fixes the specified point.

Argument or construction

Split each subgroup element into its left and right coordinates and use membership of the right coordinate to recover membership of the left. Applied to a stabilizer, this gives its literal product decomposition; separate left and right equivariance then imply full equivariance.

External assumptions and dependencies

Equivariance for the two canonical inclusions proves equivariance for the same function under the existing ambient actions. With pointwise field fixation on both full carriers, only the left-factor square remains.
