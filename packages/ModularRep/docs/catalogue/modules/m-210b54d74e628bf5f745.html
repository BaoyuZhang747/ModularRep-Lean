Purpose

Forces the sector-resulting map's literal Q=1 normalization from defect-zero uniqueness.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

Any equivalence of a defect-zero Brauer block fibre with its weight fibre sends the canonical reduction to the canonical trivial-radical weight; this applies to the sector construction.

Argument or construction

The Brauer fibre is a singleton by the independent defect-zero block theorem. Pull the canonical weight back through the fibre equivalence and use uniqueness. Its radical class is then trivial, and any supplied Spath compatible-extension clause rewrites to the normalized pair.

External assumptions and dependencies

Defect-zero reduction, ordinary block uniqueness, trivial-weight source and its block compatibility, plus the sector input. The extension conclusion additionally uses the independent Spath input.
