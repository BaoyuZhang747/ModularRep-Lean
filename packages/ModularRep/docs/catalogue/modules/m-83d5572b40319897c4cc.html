Purpose

Combines the principal criterion on actual SO/Omega fibres.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

Under the specified covering, independent counts and fixed-block source inputs, constructs SO/Omega matchings, matched inertia and normalized principal criterion packets using derived Spin fixation.

Argument or construction

One/two covering fibres and independent counts construct the upper correspondence and its inertia law. Index two supplies the Hall data, cyclic extension supplies the four selector clauses, and the one-block source returns normalized packets.

External assumptions and dependencies

Actual covering, calibrated roots and specified blocks, FYZ generator weight fixation, numerical sources and Brough–Späth Theorem 4.5.
