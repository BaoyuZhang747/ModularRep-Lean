Purpose

Upgrades the selected local representation extension to a pair-level Brauer-character extension.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

The centerless quotient normalizer identification and three precise root compatibility conditions give an extension witness on the selected pair stabilizer.

Argument or construction

Construct the pair-base/quotient-normalizer equivalence and prove that the two routes to the quotient local group coincide. The inflated quotient character therefore affords the transported pair-base character. Use root compatibility for quotient inflation, local-base pullback and pair restriction to convert the representation extension into the required character witness.

External assumptions and dependencies

Centerlessness, selected raw local reduction, cyclic extension principle and the three pointwise root compatibility laws along the displayed homomorphisms.
