Purpose

Retains elementary transport identities behind even-field fixation.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

The Lang cocycle calculation and kernel difference identity hold in their abstract group setting, and irreducibility on a smaller subgroup implies it on an intermediate subgroup.

Argument or construction

Direct group calculations prove the transport formulas. Invariant-submodule inclusion proves the representation restriction result.

External assumptions and dependencies

No algebraic groups, unipotent theory, Gallagher or Clifford theorem is formalised by these helpers.
