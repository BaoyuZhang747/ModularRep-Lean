Purpose

The full centralizer, its multiplier-one subgroup and their inclusion are actual subgroups of the prescribed conformal symplectic group.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

Over an algebraically closed defining field, extracting a square root of a multiplier expresses every full-centralizer element as an actual scalar times an element of the multiplier-one subgroup. In particular, scalar decomposition does not assert connectedness, reductivity, algebraicity, an algebraic isotypy, an equality of finite commutator subgroups, or any unipotent character theorem.

Argument or construction

These are group/field deductions only. Scalar decomposition on algebraically closed defining-field points.
