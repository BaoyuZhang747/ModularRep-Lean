Purpose

Derives the diagonal involution and the two conformal-action alternatives.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

The actual PCSp action lifts through the full-cover map, and each conformal translate acts as the identity or the specified diagonal on principal characters.

Argument or construction

The two-coset theorem supplies the alternatives. Principal ambient commutation and full automorphism surjectivity prove that the diagonal commutes with the complete action.

External assumptions and dependencies

Existing actual group and field-fixedness sources; no orbit relation or correction witness is assumed.
