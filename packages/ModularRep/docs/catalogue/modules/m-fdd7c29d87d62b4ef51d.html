Purpose

This module replaces the global carriers in the concrete Lemma 2.10 adapter by the literal fibres IBrBlock and CharacterWeight.LocalBlockInductionSource.Fibre.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

Literal Brauer and weight block fibres carry the stated inner/outer actions. Inner fixedness and cyclic outer action give actual cyclic stabilizer quotients, allowing the explicit Brauer extension principle to apply.

Argument or construction

Construct inner and outer actions on actual Brauer and weight block fibres using literal block transport. Inner fixedness and quotient embeddings prove cyclic stabilizer quotients, and the supplied cyclic extension theorem gives an actual global extension.

External assumptions and dependencies

The ordinary Isaacs Theorem 11.22 or modular Navarro Theorem 8.12 cyclic extension principle, as selected by the declaration. Actual character realizations, finite compatible roots, invariance and cyclic quotient are required; local applications additionally require their raw quotient and selected-reduction dictionaries.
