Purpose

Constructs the coefficient ring and its two field maps.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

A fixed rational subring containing the inverse denominator has characteristic-zero and characteristic-three maps, and coefficient transport preserves centres and reflects idempotence along an injection.

Argument or construction

The denominator is prime to three, so its inverse has the required images. Coefficientwise injectivity and conjugacy invariance prove the centre and idempotent transport statements.

External assumptions and dependencies

The fixed integer denominator 7031383654400, its proved nondivisibility by three, and the characteristic-zero or characteristic-three target field. Centre/idempotent transport uses the indicated coefficient-map injectivity, which is proved for the ordinary map.
