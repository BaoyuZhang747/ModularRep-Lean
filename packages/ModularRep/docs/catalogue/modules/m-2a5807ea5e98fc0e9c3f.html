Purpose

Constructs joint own reductions from the fixed Sp root convention.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

The downstairs ambient root and every selected quotient root are bound to restrictions of the same table; all own normalizer roots and reductions are then computed.

Argument or construction

Cardinality divisibility bounds the relevant Sp/PSp subquotient exponents. Actual inflation and the local quotient equivalence prove both own-character equations and root squares.

External assumptions and dependencies

The source table-identification laws, not separate reduction-existence or compatibility assumptions.
