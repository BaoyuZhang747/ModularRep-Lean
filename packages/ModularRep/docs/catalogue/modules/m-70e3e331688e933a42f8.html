Purpose

Expresses multiplicity-free restriction and normalized duality on the actual Spin inclusion.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

Given literal upper-family restriction constituents and compatible multiplicity and GGGR induction laws, the lower scalar-product entries are natural and each column sums to one.

Argument or construction

The supplied restriction sum, Clifford transitivity and duality covariance are combined with the specified natural-multiplicity input. Scalar-product restriction and Frobenius reciprocity then prove lower sum-one and zero-entry consequences, rather than assuming a lower matrix.

External assumptions and dependencies

Ordinary restriction/duality theorems, actual root and group realization, and specified nonnegativity supplied by odd-induction PIM expansion.
