Purpose

Transfers the quotient Klein-four signature to the original block.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

Actual quotient local rows, complementary interval evaluations, a commuting local-action square and an independent full weight total identify the original block's fixed count as one.

Argument or construction

The proof first counts the quotient ordinary representative fibre. Central-quotient transport preserves its fixed points, and equality with the full block cardinality proves that this joint radical fibre exhausts the block.

External assumptions and dependencies

The upstairs/downstairs actual block operations and canonical reductions, central prime-to-p quotient with primitive-image laws, complementary quotient ordinary row evaluations, actual local-action square and independent full original-block cardinality three.
