Mathematical statement and assumptions

The inverse conversion between right and left actions preserves stabilizers. Equivariant injections reflect fixedness and equivariant equivalences preserve subgroup fixed-point counts.

Argument or construction

Translate right actions to left actions by inverses and prove the same stabilizers result. Injectivity of an equivariant map reflects fixedness, and an equivariant equivalence restricts to fixed-point equivalences for each subgroup.
