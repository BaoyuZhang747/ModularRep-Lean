Converts stability of a characteristic algebraic torus to equality of its abstract label.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

An injective realisation of the selected label, natural for the selected automorphism, transfers subgroup equality to label fixedness.

Argument or construction

The characteristic-torus lemma gives equality of realised subgroups. Injectivity supplies the dependent-pair label equality.

External assumptions and dependencies

Characteristic-torus naturality and the selected realisation square.
