Purpose

Applies the exceptional odd-prime Sp6(2) branch on its actual double cover.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

The full universal projection to literal PSp6(ZMod 2), with centre of order two, determines the prime-to-ell cover for odd ell.

Argument or construction

Order arithmetic gives exactly 3,5,7 and subgroup cyclicity at five and seven. Schaeffer Fry handles three and the general cyclic-defect source handles the other two on the same family.

External assumptions and dependencies

Actual full-cover and order facts, Schaeffer Fry 5.5 and the cyclic-defect theorem; the total group is not the centreless matrix group.
