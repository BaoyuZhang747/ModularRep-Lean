Purpose

Constructs the rank-three rational GGGR basis in the literal rational PIM span.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

The rank-three specified projected basis, natural PIM expansion and class count imply that the same K-valued projected GGGRs form a basis of the rational principal PIM span.

Argument or construction

Natural expansions prove projected rational membership and the specified rank theorem proves independence. The PIM dimension and class count then construct the rational basis with unchanged vectors.

External assumptions and dependencies

The retained specified rank-three basis, actual PIM formulas, ordinary splitting and original class count.
