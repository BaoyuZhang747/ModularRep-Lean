ModularRep.OrdinaryCharacterDefectZero

ModularRep/OrdinaryCharacterDefectZero.lean

carrier-constructioncurrent

Purpose

The predicate retains one simple representation witnessing both the character and defect zero.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

An ordinary irreducible character is defect zero when the p-part of its degree equals the p-part of the finite group order.

Argument or construction

Define ordinary defect zero by the p-part of the character's degree at one. The numerical condition matches the representation degree condition when the supplied realization has that trace.

External assumptions and dependencies

An actual ordinary irreducible character, finite group order and the numerical p-part operation applied to its degree.
