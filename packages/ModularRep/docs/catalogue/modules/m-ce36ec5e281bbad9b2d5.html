Purpose

The external inputs are nonzero decomposition columns, the block support law, and preservation of nonzero decomposition numbers under quotient inflation in one compatible modular system.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

Nonempty, sound decomposition columns compatible with Brauer/ordinary inflation give a common inflated ordinary character in the two designated ordinary block fibres.

Argument or construction

Choose an ordinary character supporting the quotient Brauer character using column nonemptiness. Soundness of support and the supplied inflation compatibility place this character and its inflation in the two designated ordinary block fibres.

External assumptions and dependencies

Nonempty decomposition columns, block soundness of both support relations and their compatibility with the actual ordinary/Brauer inflation. These are not inferred merely from block label types.
