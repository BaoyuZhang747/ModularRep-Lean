Purpose

Proves transitivity on the small faithful central sectors.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

Any two faithful characters of a centre of order 1 or 2 coincide; for orders 3, 4 and 6 an automorphism acting by inversion carries any given faithful character to every other faithful character.

Argument or construction

Faithfulness embeds the centre into field units and forces the cyclic primitive-root possibilities. The possible coprime exponents reduce to a character or its inverse, realized by the identity or the specified automorphism. The theorem does not assert existence of faithful characters over an arbitrary field.

External assumptions and dependencies

A finite centre, homomorphisms into the units of a field, their faithfulness, and centre order 1, 2, 3, 4 or 6. Orders 3, 4 and 6 additionally require an actual centre-inverting automorphism. No Brauer root, block operation or reduction premise is used.
