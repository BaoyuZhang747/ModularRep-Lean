Purpose

Constructs the nonprincipal census from separate rank, local coverage and corrected-action bindings.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

The six-row actual span with ranks four and three gives Brauer signature (4,2); four distinct exhaustive table weights with corrected fixed entry two give the same weight signature.

Argument or construction

An operator permuting the Brauer basis has trace equal to its number of fixed basis vectors. Involutivity gives trace = 2 rank(id+T) − dimension, hence two. On weights, injectivity and elementwise coverage construct the four-row equivalence; conjugate the actual outer action through it and transport its fixed count.

External assumptions and dependencies

Actual restriction-span and rank alignment, table-to-character data and target induction, four-row coverage, and corrected Table 8 action binding. The internal plus rank three is separate from the printed transcript coordinates.
