Purpose

Restricts the intrinsic local-character/weight-fibre equivalence to a specified central sector.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

The original trivial-sector weight fibre at a radical is equivalent to downstairs local defect-zero characters, with a compatible fixed-point refinement.

Argument or construction

The literal supporting-idempotent identity restricts the intrinsic local equivalence to the sector. Compose with the representative quotient equivalence; the local automorphism square gives the fixed refinement.

External assumptions and dependencies

Canonical reductions, literal local block compatibility, quotient primitive-image data and the fixed-point action square.
