Purpose

Identifies the specified principal block of the exceptional triple cover with B1.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

The complete downstairs principal fibre has twelve characters in the prescribed root convention.

Argument or construction

Principal deflation transfers this cardinality upstairs. The exhaustive named block dictionary has a unique twelve-entry fibre, which forces the principal block to be B1.

External assumptions and dependencies

Complete principal and named Brauer dictionaries with specified block support and literal central deflation.
