Purpose

Each actual raw weight has nonzero ambient Brauer support at its own radical subgroup.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

Canonical block support for a raw weight gives a nonzero Brauer image at its own radical subgroup; maximal defect zero therefore forces that subgroup to be trivial.

Argument or construction

Identify the raw local character's specified block through canonical reduction and apply the support law. A nonzero Brauer image is incompatible with a nontrivial subgroup in a maximal-defect-zero block.
