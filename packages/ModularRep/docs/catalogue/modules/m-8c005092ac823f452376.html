theorem-developmentcurrent

Purpose

For a composition series, this file forms the sum of the isomorphism classes of its simple factors in the free abelian group on module isomorphism classes.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

Each step of a module composition series supplies the canonical short exact inclusion/quotient sequence whose simple quotient determines the corresponding factor coordinate.

Argument or construction

Associate the isomorphism class of each successive quotient to a composition series. The canonical inclusion and quotient form a short exact sequence, providing each relation needed for the Jordan-Holder coordinate construction.

External assumptions and dependencies

A composition series of a module, its successive submodules and the standard exact inclusion/quotient sequence.
