ModularRep.BrauerLinearCharacterTensorAction

ModularRep/BrauerLinearCharacterTensorAction.lean

theorem-developmentcurrent

Purpose

This file constructs the tensor action on the actual function-valued carrier IBr.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

The supplied Brauer linear tensor formula produces an action of modular linear characters on actual IBr, compatible with automorphism twisting and the corresponding tensor/field semidirect product.

Argument or construction

Tensor a modular representation by a linear character and use the supplied Brauer tensor formula to identify its function. Inverse twists and automorphism compatibility give the genuine tensor/field semidirect action.

External assumptions and dependencies

The chosen finite root correspondence, BrauerLinearTensorProductFormula (Navarro Theorem 2.23), and the stated automorphism or normal-subgroup stability. No induced action on blocks or block stability is automatic.
