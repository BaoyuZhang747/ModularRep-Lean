Purpose

Proves uniqueness and Q=1 preservation for the descended weight.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

The quotient weight is determined by its subgroup and ordinary factorization, and its subgroup is trivial exactly when the original subgroup is trivial.

Argument or construction

Surjectivity of the local quotient reflects equality of ordinary characters. A p-subgroup contained in the prime-to-p central kernel must be trivial, giving the reverse implication at Q=1.

External assumptions and dependencies

The same own-quotient construction inputs. The uniqueness statement compares another weight with the same mapped subgroup and ordinary qW factorization; no new selected quotient character is supplied.
