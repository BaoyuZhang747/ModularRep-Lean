Purpose

Constructs the natural local-to-global quotient isomorphism for a named matched pair.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

The pair normalizer modulo its intersection with the embedded base is isomorphic to the global stabilizer modulo that base, by the map induced by inclusion.

Argument or construction

The established product decomposition makes the inclusion-induced quotient map surjective. Its kernel is exactly the local base. Apply the quotient-kernel isomorphism and transport the raw pair-base presentation along the pair-normalizer equivalence.

External assumptions and dependencies

The named pair normalizer and its proved product decomposition.
