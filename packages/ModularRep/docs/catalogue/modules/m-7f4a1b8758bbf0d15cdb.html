Purpose

Feng--Li--Zhang, Proposition 7.5, pp. 573--574, is an E2 source: for each weight (R, phi) of Spin it gives the product factorization of the normalizer inertia inside the special Clifford group extended by prime Frobenius.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

This file states that one-way source on the actual carriers. The subgroup is the subgroup of a raw CharacterWeight; its local character is an ordinary irreducible character of the actual normalizer quotient.

Argument or construction

It does not prove the published factorization or assume any iBAW target. CharacterWeight.Isomorphic here means equality of that subgroup and equality of the canonically transported local character. Transport of the published complex-character statement to the selected algebraically closed characteristic-zero coefficient field is the usual E1 character-field identification and remains explicit.

External assumptions and dependencies

This file states that one-way source on the actual carriers. It is not an unspecified conjugacy relation or an action on free labels. The group, norm and Frobenius sources inherit their E1/U authentication boundary.
