Purpose

Derives four-row nonprincipal weight coverage from a local census and radical support.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

Four injective local rows, local defect-zero cardinality four and support on their radical class imply that every nonprincipal weight is represented by one row.

Argument or construction

Finite cardinal equality upgrades the local row injection to a surjection. Send each supported global weight through the inverse intrinsic radical-fibre equivalence, choose its local row, and map back.

External assumptions and dependencies

The actual local defect-zero census binding and the radical classification's support statement, besides the already supplied four actual rows.
