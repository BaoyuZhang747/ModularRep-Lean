Purpose

A natural-number rank strictly decreases along every declared edge, ruling out self-dependencies and cycles.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

A dependency graph with rank decreasing along every edge has no cycles, and supplied external leaves plus supplied derived-node rules imply all interpreted node claims.

Argument or construction

A natural-number rank strictly decreases along every declared edge, ruling out self-dependencies and cycles. Well-founded induction propagates supplied external facts through supplied proof rules, without auditing hidden assumptions of those rules.

External assumptions and dependencies

The finite node/case data and, for propagation, the supplied interpretation, external leaves, derived proof rules and semantic links. Ranked acyclicity does not inspect assumptions closed over by a proof rule.
