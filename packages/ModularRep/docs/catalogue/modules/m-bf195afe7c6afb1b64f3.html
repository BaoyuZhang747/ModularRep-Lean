theorem-developmentcurrent

Purpose

A cyclic subgroup whose order contains the full p-part of the ambient finite group contains a Sylow p-subgroup.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

If a cyclic subgroup contains the full p-part of a finite group order, a Sylow p-subgroup is cyclic and consequently every p-subgroup is cyclic.

Argument or construction

The full p-part hypothesis places a Sylow subgroup inside the given cyclic subgroup. Sylow conjugacy makes every Sylow cyclic, and containment in a Sylow makes every p-subgroup cyclic.

External assumptions and dependencies

A finite group, p prime and a cyclic subgroup whose order is divisible by the full p-part of the ambient order.
