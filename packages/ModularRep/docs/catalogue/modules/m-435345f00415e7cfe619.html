Purpose

This file replaces two abstract carriers in NormalCoreIBAWTransport by the actual function-valued irreducible Brauer characters and the actual conjugacy classes of representation weights.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

Brauer inflation and representation-weight transport through the fixed p-core quotient compose with a downstairs equivalence to a block-preserving upstairs equivalence under the stated block transport laws.

Argument or construction

Construct Brauer inflation and representation-weight transports on the fixed p-core quotient. Compose them with the downstairs equivalence and use the supplied block transport laws to prove that the actual composite preserves blocks.

External assumptions and dependencies

Actual Brauer inflation and representation-weight quotient transports, compatible finite roots, literal primitive decompositions and the supplied block correspondence/naturality laws. Triple certification and normalized family construction are separate.
