Purpose

Express a selected cyclic extension as a quotient-character twist of the initial one.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

Two extensions of the same Brauer character over a cyclic quotient differ by an inflated linear quotient character under the cyclic-twist source and root agreement.

Argument or construction

Verify the common restriction and its occurrence in the base character. Apply the twist principle and express the chosen extension as the resulting quotient-character multiple of the initial one.
