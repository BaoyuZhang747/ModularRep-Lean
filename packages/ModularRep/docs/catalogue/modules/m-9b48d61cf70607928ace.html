Purpose

Builds Q=1 local block data from a chosen global catalogue.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

A common extension pair and a global restricted Brauer character with a complete catalogue yield the existential intermediate block witness.

Argument or construction

Transport the catalogue along the canonical group equivalence. Apply qOne_intermediate_block_output to the common-character and restriction identities. The local catalogue and induction statement are constructed rather than separately supplied.

External assumptions and dependencies

One global catalogue and the exact common-pair restriction laws.
