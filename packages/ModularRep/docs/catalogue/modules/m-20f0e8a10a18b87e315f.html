Purpose

Gives explicit inverse coordinates for the split diagonal.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

For the previously constructed split diagonal, explicit inverse coordinate maps give a group equivalence with the base-pair semidirect product, without assuming the actor commutative.

Argument or construction

The coordinate maps are inverse homomorphisms. Their formulas identify the original base and field generators when an honest representation is restricted to the diagonal.

External assumptions and dependencies

The previously constructed split diagonal and its two actions.
