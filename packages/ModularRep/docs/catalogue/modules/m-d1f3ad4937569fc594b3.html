Purpose

This file isolates the numerical clause used when applying Feng--Malle--Zhang, Condition 6.1 in the proof of the even-field unipotent proposition.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

For an odd prime, q a power of two and centre cardinality one, the prime does not divide twice q times the centre cardinality.

Argument or construction

An odd prime cannot divide a power of two. Substitute the even-field and trivial-centre cardinality hypotheses to derive the required numerical coprimality condition.

External assumptions and dependencies

The concrete order, field-size, centre-size or cyclic-torus equalities explicitly displayed in the theorem. Arithmetic deductions are internal; no concrete group identification, defect theorem or iBAW theorem follows from an order alone.
