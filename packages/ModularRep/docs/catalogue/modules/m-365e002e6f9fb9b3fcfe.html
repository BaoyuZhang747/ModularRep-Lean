Purpose

Constructs rational-Levi fixed-point equivalences from one witness.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

The standard Levi, Weyl representative, Lang element and Frobenius maps determine both finite Levis and their intertwined field actions.

Argument or construction

Conjugation by the Lang element sends twisted fixed points to defining fixed points. The same formula proves the automorphism square.

External assumptions and dependencies

One unified rational-Levi source; identification with the selected FMZ Levi is handled separately.
