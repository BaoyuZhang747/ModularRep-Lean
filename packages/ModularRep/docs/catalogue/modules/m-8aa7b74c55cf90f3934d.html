Purpose

Proves descent of the actual matched local character.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

Canonical local compatibility and the common specified block force the global central kernel to act trivially on the local Brauer representation and force ordinary constancy on the qW kernel.

Argument or construction

Block induction equates the relevant central scalars. Since the central kernel has prime-to-p order, its elements are regular and the same reduction equation turns modular triviality into ordinary constancy.

External assumptions and dependencies

The original problem and Brauer character, the matched raw weight, its canonical reduction, local block compatibility, their common actual block equation, and prime-to-p order of the character's central kernel. Normalizer quotient source laws are unnecessary for this kernel step.
