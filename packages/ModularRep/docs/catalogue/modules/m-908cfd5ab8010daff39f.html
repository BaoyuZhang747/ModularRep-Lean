Purpose

Turns exact integer-polynomial reconstruction checks into a row-inverse certificate over any characteristic-zero field.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

Checked pivot values, an integer inverse numerator with nonzero common denominator, and reconstruction identities yield a RowInverseCertificate for rows evaluated at arbitrary coordinate roots.

Argument or construction

Soundness of polynomial equality gives the pivot matrix entries after evaluation. Cast the scaled integer inverse identity into the field and divide by the nonzero denominator. Evaluate the polynomial reconstruction identities to obtain a spanning expression for every row, preserving each column's chosen root.

External assumptions and dependencies

Exact polynomial checks and scaled inverse identities; no identification of these rows with an actual character table is inferred.
