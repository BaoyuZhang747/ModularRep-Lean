Purpose

This module records the two literal group-theoretic outputs of Navarro--Tiep, Lemma 2.3(c), needed at one supplied radical subgroup: the radicality of its image and the exact image of its normaliser.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

The fixed central prime-to-p quotient source states radical image and exact normalizer image as in Navarro-Tiep 2.3(c); the induced normalizer map is then surjective.

Argument or construction

Supply the exact fixed central prime-to-p quotient radical and normalizer image conclusions of Navarro-Tiep Lemma 2.3(c). Restriction of the quotient map is then surjective because every target normalizer element lies in that image.

External assumptions and dependencies

The fixed finite central prime-to-p quotient and radical subgroup, with the two Navarro-Tiep Lemma 2.3(c) source conclusions: radical image and exact normalizer image. Surjectivity of the restricted normalizer map is derived.
