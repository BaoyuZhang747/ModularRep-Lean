Purpose

Constructs cyclic field actions from iterated fixed points.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

An endomorphism restricted to its a-th fixed points is invertible and has order dividing positive a, hence defines an action of Z/aZ.

Argument or construction

The inverse is an appropriate iterate. The power relation permits passage through the cyclic quotient and proves the explicit iterate formula.

External assumptions and dependencies

No faithfulness or exact order of the field action is asserted.
