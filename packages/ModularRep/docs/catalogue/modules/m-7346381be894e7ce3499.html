Purpose

This file contains only the formal transport of a complete primitive central-idempotent decomposition through a ring equivalence and, in particular, through an equivalence of the underlying groups.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

A ring equivalence carries a complete primitive central-idempotent decomposition to one with the same index set. A group equivalence provides the group algebra specialization.

Argument or construction

Map centrality, orthogonality, sum one and primitivity across a ring equivalence. Apply the result to the group algebra equivalence induced by a group isomorphism, retaining the original block index.

External assumptions and dependencies

The supplied complete primitive central family and a ring equivalence; the group specialization uses its induced group algebra equivalence.
