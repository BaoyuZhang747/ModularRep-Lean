Purpose

Proves the Brauer-character formula for the actual quotient-sign twist.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

In characteristic different from two, twisting a representation by the index-two sign multiplies its Brauer character by the characteristic-zero sign.

Argument or construction

The root-group equivalence sends minus one to minus one, since it preserves its nontrivial order two. It therefore commutes with negation of the relevant eigenvalue roots. Apply the eigenvalue formula for Brauer characters, using multiplicativity only on roots of unity.

External assumptions and dependencies

Index two, the original root embedding and nonvanishing of two; no additivity of the total root lift is assumed.
