Mathematical statement and assumptions

For a tau-fixed radical class, an inner correction of tau stabilizes the chosen representative and enables the Klein-four local fixed-point calculation.

Argument or construction

Choose a conjugator witnessing equality of radical classes and compose tau with its inner action. Inner automorphisms fix weight classes, so the corrected local calculation gives the fixed-point count for the original tau action.
