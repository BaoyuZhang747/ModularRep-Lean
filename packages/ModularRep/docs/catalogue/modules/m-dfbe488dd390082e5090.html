Purpose

Constructs the identity ambient group in the centreless all-inner case.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

The group itself realizes the original Brauer stabilizer quotient, with top base and identity raw embedding.

Argument or construction

Conjugation has central kernel and is surjective onto the stabilizer because all automorphisms are inner. Centrelessness makes the map bijective and identifies the central quotient with the group.

External assumptions and dependencies

The actual Definition35Problem, centrelessness, original Brauer character, the specified central-quotient Brauer source and all-inner automorphisms. Conjugation identifies the stabilizer and gives the identity embedding.
