Purpose

Computes tuple coordinates under a corrected automorphism.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

A whole-pair equality under conj(g) times a gives stabilizer, base and local-intersection equivalences and transports full raw containment.

Argument or construction

Conjugation of the actual holomorph yields the maps. Their restrictions are proved equal to the specified base and normalizer coordinates.

External assumptions and dependencies

The raw-pair equality and the two selected reductions, without root or relation premises.
