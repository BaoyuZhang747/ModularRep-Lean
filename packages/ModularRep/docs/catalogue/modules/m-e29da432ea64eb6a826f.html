Purpose

Constructs the full automorphism ambient of the principal Spin return.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

Its projection uses the same Spin quotient and the automorphism equivalence derived from the universal central extension.

Argument or construction

The ambient kernel is the Spin centre in the left coordinate. Character and raw-weight inertia groups are exact comaps along this map, giving the required quotient and inclusion identities.

External assumptions and dependencies

The fixed projection, universal-cover automorphism equivalence and existing character/weight descent laws.
