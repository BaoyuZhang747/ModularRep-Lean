Purpose

Transfers Spin principal field fixation through the centre quotient to actual matrix Omega.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

Only finite Spin and the constructed matrix equivalence are needed; prescribed roots retain their whole-lift identifications.

Argument or construction

Central inflation gives the Brauer equivalence and specified principal uniqueness identifies its block. The actual field square makes this equivalence commute with twisting, so fixedness transfers.

External assumptions and dependencies

Central-two inflation, block transport, root calibration and Clifford-to-matrix field compatibility.
