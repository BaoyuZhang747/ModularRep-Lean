Purpose

Constructs the finite inner twist from a Lang witness.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

The element g sigma(g) inverse lies in the defining fixed-point group, and tau is definitionally its inner conjugation times the field action.

Argument or construction

The Lang equation and fixation of the Weyl representative prove finite membership. Restriction of commuting automorphisms then defines both actions.

External assumptions and dependencies

A Lang witness and the fixed Weyl representative.
