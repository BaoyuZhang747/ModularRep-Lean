Purpose

The actual centre-two ambient has no proper intermediate group above its original-group base.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

In the actual centre-two Brauer ambient, every subgroup above the embedded original group is either that base or the whole ambient when the extension quotient has order two.

Argument or construction

Bound the Brauer quotient cardinality by the original extension quotient and apply the finite index-at-most-two subgroup lemma. Faithfulness of the outer action is unnecessary for this step.

External assumptions and dependencies

The original extension quotient has cardinality two, with its actual Brauer ambient and embedded base. The argument does not require faithful outer action or an extension splitting.
