ModularRep.CentralIdempotentBlockExpansion

ModularRep/CentralIdempotentBlockExpansion.lean

theorem-developmentcurrent

Purpose

Every central idempotent in a ring is the sum of exactly those members of a finite complete primitive central idempotent family that it fixes.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

Relative to a complete primitive central-idempotent family, every central idempotent is the sum of the unique subset of blocks on which its product acts as identity.

Argument or construction

Multiply a central idempotent by each primitive block idempotent. Primitivity makes every product either zero or that block, and summing gives the unique expansion over its supported block subset.

External assumptions and dependencies

A supplied finite complete primitive central-idempotent decomposition of a ring and the specified central idempotent.
