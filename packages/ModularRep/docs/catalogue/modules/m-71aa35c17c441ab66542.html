Purpose

Constructs block compatibility for two fixed extensions over a p-group quotient.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

Only induction between their base characters is an induction premise.

Argument or construction

The p-group covering uniqueness theorem identifies the top blocks. Restricting the two extensions and transporting the catalogue then supplies block equations at every intermediate subgroup.

External assumptions and dependencies

Navarro 9.6 p-group covering uniqueness, specified central character catalogues and the fixed base induction law.
