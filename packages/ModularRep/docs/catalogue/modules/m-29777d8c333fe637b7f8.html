Purpose

Guards the Monster odd-prime numerical source.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

The noncyclic-defect count field is usable only for primes at least three; every actual block also has a defect representative and a cyclic count field.

Argument or construction

The adapter passes the prime bound to the noncyclic field and obtains the ordinary cyclic/noncyclic numerical source. Its weight fibre still includes every radical belonging to the block.

External assumptions and dependencies

The actual prime and specified block/character/sets of weights. MonsterOddNumericalSource supplies an actual defect representative for each block, cyclic-defect cardinal equality and noncyclic-defect cardinal equality guarded by p at least three. Its adapter requires that prime bound; it supplies no group identification or table realization.
