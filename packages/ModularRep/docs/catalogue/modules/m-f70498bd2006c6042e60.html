Purpose

An equivariant equivalence reflects fixedness by injectivity.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

An equivalence equivariant for both compatible factor actions transfers the product/semidirect stabilizer factorization from its image point to the original point.

Argument or construction

An equivariant equivalence reflects fixedness by injectivity. Apply that observation to each factor and their product to transfer the supplied ordinary stabilizer factorization to the corresponding Brauer object.

External assumptions and dependencies

An equivalence equivariant separately for the two compatible factor actions, and the ordinary-side stabilizer factorization. No ordinary factorization or basic set is proved in this transport lemma.
