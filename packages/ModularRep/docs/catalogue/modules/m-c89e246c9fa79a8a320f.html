transport-adaptercurrent

Purpose

The uniform hReductionSupport hypothesis is the standard decomposition-number fact that, when chi^0 = phi is irreducible, d_(chi,phi)=1 and hence is nonzero.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

If coefficient-one Brauer reduction is included in the given genuine decomposition support relation, its ordinary block selector equals the literal block of that Brauer character.

Argument or construction

Apply the supplied ordinary-block support law to the Brauer reduction pair. The separate assertion that coefficient-one reduction belongs to the decomposition support relation identifies its literal Brauer block with the ordinary block.

External assumptions and dependencies

LiteralOrdinaryPBlockSource with actual primitive blocks, a genuine nonempty sound decomposition-support relation and selector surjectivity. The reduction adapter separately assumes every displayed coefficient-one Brauer reduction belongs to that support relation.
