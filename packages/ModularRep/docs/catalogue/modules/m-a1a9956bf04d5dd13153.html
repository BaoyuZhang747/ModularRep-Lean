Purpose

Binds the ambient extension to the literal Levi character.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

A representation restricting irreducibly with character lambda gives an irreducible inertia extension; a conjugating ambient element makes that extension invariant.

Argument or construction

Intermediate restriction preserves irreducibility. Cyclic invariance of trace proves the character equality without a fixation source field.

External assumptions and dependencies

Spath extension data on the selected base and an actual conjugation witness.
