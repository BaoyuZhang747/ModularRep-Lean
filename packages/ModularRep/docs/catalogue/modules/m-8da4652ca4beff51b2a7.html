Purpose

Transports actual character weights along finite group equivalences.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

The subgroup is its image and the own ordinary character is pulled through the inverse canonical normalizer-quotient map.

Argument or construction

Normalizer coordinates prove raw composition and inverses. The maps descend through isomorphism and conjugacy and intertwine conjugated right automorphisms.

External assumptions and dependencies

Only the actual group equivalence; no roots, block assignment or character correspondence source is used.
