ModularRep.CyclicExtension

ModularRep/CyclicExtension.lean

source-interfacecurrent

Purpose

Construct extension witnesses and prove that an extension restricts irreducibly and gives conjugation invariance.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

Extension records contain an actual ambient representation restricting to the prescribed base one. Cyclic quotient and invariance give existence only through the separate ordinary or Brauer cyclic extension principle.

Argument or construction

Construct extension witnesses and prove that an extension restricts irreducibly and gives conjugation invariance. The existence theorems apply a separately supplied ordinary or Brauer cyclic extension principle.

External assumptions and dependencies

The ordinary Isaacs Theorem 11.22 or modular Navarro Theorem 8.12 cyclic extension principle, as selected by the declaration. Actual character realizations, finite compatible roots, invariance and cyclic quotient are required; local applications additionally require their raw quotient and selected-reduction dictionaries.
