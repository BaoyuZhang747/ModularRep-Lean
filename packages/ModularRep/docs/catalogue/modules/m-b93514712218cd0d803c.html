Purpose

Preserves radical conjugacy and fixedness through a central quotient.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

The computed radical image/lift equivalence reflects equality and distinctness of radical conjugacy classes and transports fixed classes across a commuting automorphism square.

Argument or construction

The proof applies the central-quotient radical equivalence on conjugacy classes. Its naturality reflects fixedness and its injectivity preserves noncoincident lifted indices.

External assumptions and dependencies

A finite group with its ambient prime regular embedding, a central normal subgroup of prime-to-p order, and the proved central-quotient radical correspondence. Action statements require the actual commuting automorphism square.
