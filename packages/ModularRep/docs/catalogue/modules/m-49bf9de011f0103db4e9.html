Purpose

Handles the generalized-quaternion symplectic-plane basic factor.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

A plane subgroup of the exact generalized-quaternion Sylow type, with source wreath coordinates, is matched to a principal representative and its own local character.

Argument or construction

Plane Sylow conjugacy and repeated wreath transport supply the subgroup match. The selected-character source constructs the raw pair, whose principal status follows from the existing principal weight.

External assumptions and dependencies

Carter--Fong plane type/order, FYZ case-(6) interpretation and An local character source; arbitrary wreath products are not asserted Sylow or principal.
