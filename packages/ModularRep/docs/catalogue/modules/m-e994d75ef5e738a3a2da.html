Purpose

The covering source supplies a Brauer character over the prescribed base character.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

Given a covering block, a cyclic normal quotient and a fixed Brauer extension, the stated covering and twist principles choose an extension in that covering block with the same restriction.

Argument or construction

The covering source supplies a Brauer character over the prescribed base character. The cyclic quotient twist theorem adjusts a given extension into that covering block while root and tensor compatibility preserve its restriction.

External assumptions and dependencies

Navarro9495BrauerCoveringPrinciple and Navarro820CyclicBrauerTwistPrinciple, an actual cyclic normal quotient, fixed compatible roots and Brauer tensor formula, the chosen existing extension and prescribed covering block.
