Purpose

The group is the norm-one subgroup of the explicit split Clifford algebra.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

Blocks are literal primitive central idempotents of its modular group algebra; the idempotent at index b is exactly b.1. Ordinary and Brauer labels are the repository's function-valued sets of characters.

Argument or construction

This file combines the integral basic set and derives inverse block support from forward generator support. Lean derives reduction naturality from those formulas and the checked naturality of twisting. The endpoint proves the integral block-lattice equivalence, the actual stabilizer's 2-hypoelementarity, and the equivariant block bijection.

External assumptions and dependencies

FLZ Theorem 2.3, p. 537, remains an E2 input separately on each rational semisimple ell'-series and its Broue--Michel union. The maps indexing those partitions and the series predicate must still be source-certified as those literal partitions. Instead, the E1 action source identifies its two factors, on exact K_0 and literal blocks, with actual regular conjugation and the actual Spin field action. SpinBlock, RationalIndex and the structural stabilizer/action definitions remain useful. spinBasicSetFromTheorem23 and lemma_4_3_spin_source_instantiated have a superseded modular-system coefficient composition, replaced by TypeBSpinConlonSplittingSourceInstantiation. The superseded applications combine ordinary algebraic closure with the same nontrivial DVR fraction field; retaining their formal conditional declarations does not supply a usable modular system.
