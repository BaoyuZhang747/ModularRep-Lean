Purpose

Adds literal cover and outer data to the J4 numerical join.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

A kernel-one universal cover of a nonabelian simple group and outer quotient order one supply the cover, centrelessness and all-inner facts required by the numerical endpoint.

Argument or construction

The proof derives these structural facts from the actual map and quotient. It then applies the numerical construction with the same character and block sources, leaving the identification with J4 explicit.

External assumptions and dependencies

An actual kernel-one universal central cover over a nonabelian simple group, actual outer quotient of order one, and CyclicNoncyclicNumericalSource on the retained specified blocks. The root/carrier data, selected local reduction, canonical reduction/inflation compatibility, defect-zero/trivial-weight sources and coefficient field hypotheses of the numerical endpoint are preserved.
