Purpose

Derives the Monster-at-two blockwise counts.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

The stated total counts and actual small-defect inputs determine every block's Brauer/weight equality and the resulting centreless Definition 4.1 witness.

Argument or construction

Small defect handles each nonprincipal block. Finite fibre cancellation derives the principal equality from the whole-group totals, and complete collapse supplies the witness.

External assumptions and dependencies

MonsterTwoSource on the actual characteristic-two block decomposition and SmallDefectNumericalSource for the nonprincipal roles. The Definition 4.1 endpoint additionally retains the centreless all-inner cover, actual root/carrier and selected reduction, canonical block compatibility, defect-zero/trivial-weight data and coefficient field hypotheses.
