Purpose

This module proves the elementary support calculation behind preservation of a central prime-to-p character by block induction.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

An induced-block relation preserves support by a central idempotent when its coefficient restriction is also idempotent: support of the ambient block implies support of the local block by the restricted idempotent.

Argument or construction

Evaluate the supported central idempotent under the induced central character. Its value one reflects local support through the local catalogue, giving the same supported idempotent after coefficient restriction.

External assumptions and dependencies

The local and ambient primitive decompositions and central character catalogues, the specified induced-block relation, and a central idempotent whose coefficient restriction is also idempotent.
