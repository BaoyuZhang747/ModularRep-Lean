Purpose

An internal linear lift of quotient group algebra elements recovers the product with the trivial central idempotent.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

Averaging over a central prime-to-p kernel proves that the quotient image of a trivial-sector primitive block is nonzero.

Argument or construction

Lift the quotient algebra linearly and compute its composite with quotienting as multiplication by the trivial central idempotent. On the chosen sector that multiplication is the identity, ruling out a zero image.
