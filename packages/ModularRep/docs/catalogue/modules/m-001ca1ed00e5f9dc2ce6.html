Purpose

Provides a sound evaluator for finite integer-polynomial certificates.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

A successful zero or equality check implies the corresponding equality after evaluation in a commutative ring.

Argument or construction

Induction proves evaluation compatibility with addition, scalar multiplication and multiplication. A checked residual equal to a modulus multiple plus a constant evaluates to that constant whenever the modulus vanishes.

External assumptions and dependencies

A commutative ring evaluation target and the stated Boolean integer-polynomial check. Evaluation at an annihilated modulus is required only for the residual-to-constant corollary.
