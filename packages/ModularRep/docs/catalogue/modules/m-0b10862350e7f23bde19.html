Purpose

Computes connected components of the proper B3 selected-root diagrams.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

Connectivity is the reflexive transitive closure of adjacency restricted to the chosen roots.

Argument or construction

Explicit index equivalences handle empty, singleton, A2, B2 and disconnected subsets. The disconnected components retain the distinct original long and short roots.

External assumptions and dependencies

Finite root adjacency and length definitions; no supplied component count or factor inventory.
