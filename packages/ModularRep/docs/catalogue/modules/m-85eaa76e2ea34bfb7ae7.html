Purpose

Construct a block-preserving correspondence with prescribed defect-zero values and the associated radical partition on the actual sets of characters.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

For specified finite sets of Brauer characters and weights, blockwise numerical AWC and compatible injective defect-zero reductions yield an automorphism-equivariant bijection when every automorphism is inner, normalized at the trivial radical.

Argument or construction

First identify the prescribed defect-zero images. Equal total fibre cardinalities give equal complement cardinalities, so extend each prescribed map and combine over blocks. Inner automorphisms fix both class carriers; radical fibres then give the required partition and local bijections.

External assumptions and dependencies

The finite actual group, characteristic prime, compatible coefficient/root data, specified sets of primitive blocks and characters, and the imported transport results are fixed as in the declaration context. Numerical AWC, injective defect-zero reduction, trivial-weight and local block compatibility sources remain explicit; full Späth packets also retain the required quotient catalogues and coefficient field source.
