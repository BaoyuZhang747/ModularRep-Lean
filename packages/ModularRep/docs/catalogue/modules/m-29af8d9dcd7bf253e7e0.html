Purpose

Derives the specified Klein-four Brauer signature.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

Complete ordinary block allocation and literal values give three Brauer characters; the actual fusion dictionary yields one outer-fixed Brauer character.

Argument or construction

The raw rank-three certificate identifies the block dimension. The rank-two symmetrization and basis-involution formula yield its fixed count.

External assumptions and dependencies

The complete actual ordinary allocation to the selected specified block, regular representative cover, retained root and literal values. The fixed-count theorem additionally uses the actual outer decomposition and complete fusion binding.
