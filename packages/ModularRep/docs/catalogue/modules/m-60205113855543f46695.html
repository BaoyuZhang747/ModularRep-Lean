Purpose

Combines the remaining Fischer numerical inputs with their guards.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

Actual cyclic defects give the generic cyclic join; seven selected noncyclic rows at five and sector cancellation at seven give specified block and fixed-count equality.

Argument or construction

The five-prime proof handles fixed and exchanged role indices separately and uses cyclic sources on the complement. The seven-prime proof cancels the cyclic complement in each sector, using centre inversion to place fixed blocks in the trivial sector.

External assumptions and dependencies

The generic CyclicNumericalData is guarded by actual cyclic defect. The five-prime join requires seven injectively selected noncyclic specified block roles, coverage by those roles or cyclic blocks, and their actual total/fixed counts and action. The seven-prime join retains sector totals/fixed counts, a distinguished noncyclic role with cyclic complement, centre order three and inversion; none is obtained from literal arithmetic alone.
