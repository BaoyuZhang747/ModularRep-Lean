Purpose

Uses local defect-zero uniqueness to identify an actual selected pair.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

On a specified nonexceptional normalizer quotient, uniqueness plus subgroup conjugacy gives equality of own characters, raw pairs and principal classes.

Argument or construction

Character transport supplies two members of the same unique fibre. Their equality proves pair equality and transfers the existing principal block induction.

External assumptions and dependencies

An's scoped uniqueness statement and ordinary splitting; the a=2, gamma=0 three-character case is expressly not covered by uniqueness.
