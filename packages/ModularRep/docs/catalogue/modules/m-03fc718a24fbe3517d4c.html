Purpose

A local three-point block count from two invariant singleton radical rows.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

A weight fibre of cardinality three with two invariant singleton radical contributions has three fixed points; with Brauer cardinality three this supplies the small-block count correspondence.

Argument or construction

The third point cannot move under an involution fixing the other two. Combine this with the two singleton fixed points to compute the full fixed-point count.

The finite actual group, characteristic prime, compatible coefficient/root data, specified sets of primitive blocks and characters, and the imported transport results are fixed as in the declaration context.
