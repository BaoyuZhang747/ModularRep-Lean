ModularRep.SimpleTraceLinearIndependence

ModularRep/SimpleTraceLinearIndependence.lean

theorem-developmentcurrent

Purpose

Prove linear independence of traces of pairwise nonisomorphic simple modules by density and rank-one operators.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

Trace functions of pairwise nonisomorphic finite simple representations over an algebraically closed field are linearly independent, by algebra elements with prescribed delta traces.

Argument or construction

Use the product of pairwise nonisomorphic simple modules and its double centralizer to realize an algebra element acting by a rank-one trace-one map in one coordinate and zero elsewhere. Delta evaluations prove independence of the algebra trace functionals and then of group traces.

External assumptions and dependencies

Algebraically closed coefficient field and the finite family of pairwise nonisomorphic finite simple group algebra modules. Density and double-centralizer results realize the separating rank-one actions.
