Purpose

Recovers principal Brauer fixation from a fixed rational GGGR basis.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

If the full field group fixes a Q-basis of the specified principal PIM span and PIM twisting is injectively indexed by Brauer characters, every principal Brauer character is fixed.

Argument or construction

Two rational-linear maps agreeing on the basis agree on the whole span. The specified PIM twist formula and column independence then identify each twisted Brauer label with the original label.

External assumptions and dependencies

The internally constructed rational basis, specified PIM covariance and independent decomposition columns.
