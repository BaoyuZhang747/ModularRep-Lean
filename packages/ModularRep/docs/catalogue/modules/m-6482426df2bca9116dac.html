Purpose

The pointwise root lift already constructed in TypeBModularLinearCharacterLift is valid for every finite group, despite its historical namespace.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

The fixed finite root correspondence lifts modular quotient linear characters to ordinary ones agreeing on prime regular elements. With stable-reduction and tensor compatibility, decomposition commutes with this comparison.

Argument or construction

Lift modular quotient linear characters through the chosen finite root correspondence and prove compatibility on regular elements. This gives the ordinary/modular comparison and the same decomposition/tensor commutative square.

External assumptions and dependencies

The fixed finite root embedding and linear characters trivial on the displayed normal subgroup. The decomposition endpoint additionally assumes the complete modular system, stable-reduction compatibility and Brauer tensor formula.
