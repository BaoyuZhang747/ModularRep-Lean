Purpose

Conjugacy coverage by actual regular representatives proves evaluation injective.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

Evaluation on a complete family of actual prime regular representatives identifies the sector Brauer span with its coordinate image.

Argument or construction

Coverage of conjugacy classes makes evaluation injective. Restrict the evaluation map to its image and identify that image with the span of the bound ordinary rows.
