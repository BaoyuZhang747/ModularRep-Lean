Composes the quotient Brauer factory with the independent centerless correspondence input.

The exact six residual transport fields and a five-field CentrelessOneSectorInput give the trivial-sector source consumed by the Fischer glue.

Argument or construction

Construct CarrierTransport using the canonical quotient Brauer factory. Pack the independent quotient correspondence mathematics into its centerless one-sector source and invoke the existing trivial-sector specialization. The factory's character equivalence remains the one used throughout.

External assumptions and dependencies

Central p-prime invertibility, quotient realization and descent square, centerlessness, six residual transport fields and the separate quotient An–Dietrich input. The factory does not prove that correspondence.
