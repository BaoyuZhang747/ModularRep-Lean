Purpose

Restrict canonical central-quotient inflation to the actual Brauer block fibres.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

Canonical inflation restricts to an equivalence between the actual Brauer fibre of the selected quotient block and the corresponding upstairs trivial-sector block.

Argument or construction

Use support to show inflation lands in the correct block. Primitive-image injectivity proves the converse fibre condition, so the ambient inflation equivalence restricts in both directions.
