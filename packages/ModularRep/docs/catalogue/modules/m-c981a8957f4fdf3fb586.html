Purpose

Passes from raw normalizer products to the same weight-class product.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

A class-fixed element can be corrected by an embedded base element to fix the whole raw pair, converting M times GE factorization into M times E.

Argument or construction

Opening the actual conjugacy quotient yields the correction. Conjugation by G preserves both factors, so the standard subgroup formula propagates to every corresponding weight.

External assumptions and dependencies

The standard group-coverage and own-character factorization premises on the actual natural action.
