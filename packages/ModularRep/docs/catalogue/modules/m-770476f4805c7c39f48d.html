theorem-developmentcurrent

Purpose

This file connects the scalar action of a central subgroup on an irreducible representation with the corresponding element of the group algebra.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

For an irreducible representation and finite invertible-order central subgroup, the central character idempotent indexed by its scalar character acts as identity and all other such idempotents act as zero.

Argument or construction

Evaluate the central character weighted sum on a simple module with scalar central action. Character orthogonality gives identity for its own character and zero for every different character.

External assumptions and dependencies

An irreducible representation over algebraically closed k, a finite central subgroup with invertible order, and its linear characters.
