theorem-developmentcurrent

Purpose

This file refines the Jordan--Hölder map to the free abelian group on isomorphism classes of simple group algebra modules.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

Exact K-zero of finite dimensional kG-representations is isomorphic to the free abelian group on simple kG-module classes, with inverse given by taking the class of a representative simple module.

Argument or construction

Every simple group algebra module is finite dimensional, and each finite module class telescopes along a composition series. The simple-factor homomorphism and the class homomorphism are inverse, giving a free abelian basis of exact K-zero.

External assumptions and dependencies

Finite group algebra and the previous finite-length/Jordan-Holder construction. No decomposition map or basic-set input is needed for the simple-class basis of exact K-zero.
