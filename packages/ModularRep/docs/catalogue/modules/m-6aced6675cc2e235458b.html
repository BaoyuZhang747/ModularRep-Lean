Purpose

Derives quotient triviality from the representative difference equation.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

An endomorphism preserving a normal subgroup and satisfying x inverse sigma(x) in that subgroup induces the identity quotient map.

Argument or construction

Quotient equality is reduced to subgroup membership. Induction on quotient representatives proves the map equality.

External assumptions and dependencies

Only the stated group-theoretic difference relation.
