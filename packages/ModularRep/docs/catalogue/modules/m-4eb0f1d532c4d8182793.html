Purpose

Derives actual dihedral and defect-zero Brauer counts and fixedness from full original ordinary values.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

The specified allocation and complete literal ordinary values give signature (3,3) for the dihedral role and (1,1) for each of the two zero roles.

Argument or construction

The printed allocation identifies the complete ordinary row fibres for the relevant actual blocks. Exact rank certificates give cardinalities three and one. Invariance of every row under the full column permutation, together with the actual fusion equation, makes every Brauer character in these blocks fixed.

External assumptions and dependencies

Actual ordinary decomposition, specified block allocation, complete regular-class representatives, retained roots, exact character values and outer fusion.
