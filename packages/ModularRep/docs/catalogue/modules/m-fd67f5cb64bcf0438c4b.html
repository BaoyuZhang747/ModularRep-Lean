source-interfacecurrent

Purpose

States the upward modular block-triple inflation theorem.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

For a normal kernel contained in both base and local groups, a quotient relation lifts to the actual inflated characters upstairs.

Argument or construction

The quotient tuple records the literal groups, maps, roots and characters. The consumer invokes MRR 3.14 only in the upward direction.

External assumptions and dependencies

Authenticated standard block-triple semantics and MRR 3.14; no prime-to-two kernel condition is needed for this direction.
