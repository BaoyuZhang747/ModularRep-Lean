Purpose

Makes local total and fixed counts independent of the chosen radical representative.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

Equal radical classes give equal local defect-zero cardinalities and fixed weight-fibre cardinalities; corrected local fixed characters are equivalent to fixed ambient weights.

Argument or construction

Identify local characters with the weight fibre over their radical class. Equal class indices give identical fibres. Restrict the equivalence to fixed points using classAt fixedness and the fact that an inner correction acts trivially on weight classes.

External assumptions and dependencies

The actual local-character/weight-fibre equivalence and a correction stabilizing the representative.
