Purpose

Applies the rank-two odd-divisor theorem on the literal cover.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

For odd ell dividing q squared minus one, Brough--Schaeffer Fry gives the complete family on actual Sp4(F).

Argument or construction

The actual full cover yields the prime-to-ell cover by perfectness. Arithmetic proves the q=3 odd-divisor subcase empty instead of removing it by a stronger source guard.
