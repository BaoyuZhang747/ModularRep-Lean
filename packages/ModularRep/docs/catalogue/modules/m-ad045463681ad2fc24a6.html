Purpose

Constructs the finite-dimensional version of the literal balanced tensor functor.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

For finite representations M of the base pair and W of the Levi, constructs the finite balanced tensor representation and its functorial map, retaining the original actions.

Argument or construction

Finiteness passes through their tensor product and coinvariant quotient. The same residual action and maps are then packaged as finite representations and restricted to supported objects.

External assumptions and dependencies

Finiteness of the given bimodule and input, with the existing balanced tensor construction.
