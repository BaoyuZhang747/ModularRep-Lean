Purpose

Preserves normalization after taking the own quotient.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

For a bottom-radical raw match of a normalized correspondence, the quotient global Brauer character restricts to the same descended normalizer character.

Argument or construction

The equality first holds upstairs by the normalized original Q=1 equation. Injectivity of prime regular pullback across the prime-to-p normalizer kernel descends it.

External assumptions and dependencies

The own-normalizer construction inputs, one globally normalized correspondence, defect-zero reduction and trivial-weight data, the actual raw-match equation and Q=1. Equality of quotient global/local characters is proved by pullback.
