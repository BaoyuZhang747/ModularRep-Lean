Purpose

Proves outer commutativity from the index-two ambient quotient and field action.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

A surjective natural action of an overgroup-by-abelian-field semidirect product acts on the original normal subgroup.

Argument or construction

Every ambient commutator has trivial quotient and field coordinates, hence lies in the base group. Its image is inner, so the full outer automorphism quotient is abelian.

External assumptions and dependencies

Index-two structural data and surjectivity of the same natural automorphism action.
