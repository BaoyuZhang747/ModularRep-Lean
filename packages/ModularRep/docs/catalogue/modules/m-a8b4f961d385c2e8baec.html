Purpose

Realizes the abstract GGGR rank proof on ordinary-character functions and a literal projective subspace.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

The supplied multiplicities, class alternatives, closure vanishing and self-adjoint specified principal projection instantiate the rank theorem and its original projected-vector conclusion.

Argument or construction

The scalar-product coordinate map is constructed from those functions. Fibre permutations and closure triangularity produce the basis through the previously proved rank chain.

External assumptions and dependencies

Published representation theoretic multiplicities/support, specified projection and dimension count, without determinant or basis fields.
