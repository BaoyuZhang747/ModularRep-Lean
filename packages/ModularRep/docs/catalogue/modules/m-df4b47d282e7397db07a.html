Purpose

The original factors may be different groups at different positions in a cycle.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

Each returned factor is the original coordinate subgroup with the action of the original cyclic return subgroup.

Argument or construction

The finite-product coordinate equivalence reorders the actual factors and their inclusion proofs. The generator equation retains the positive original return even when character pullback uses its inverse.

External assumptions and dependencies

The Brauer root is transported canonically, and the actual orbit stabiliser remains the same subgroup of the original external group E.
