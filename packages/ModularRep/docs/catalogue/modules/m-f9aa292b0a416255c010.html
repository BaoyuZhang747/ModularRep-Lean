ModularRep.BrauerQuotientLinearCharacterAction

ModularRep/BrauerQuotientLinearCharacterAction.lean

transport-adaptercurrent

Purpose

For a subgroup N of G, this file defines the group of modular linear characters of G that are trivial on N.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

Modular linear characters trivial on a normal subgroup identify with characters of its quotient. Automorphisms preserving that subgroup combine with their Brauer tensor action in a semidirect action.

Argument or construction

Identify modular linear characters trivial on a normal subgroup with homomorphisms from its quotient. Automorphisms preserving the subgroup act by pullback and combine with tensor action through the established semidirect law.

External assumptions and dependencies

The chosen finite root correspondence, BrauerLinearTensorProductFormula (Navarro Theorem 2.23), and the stated automorphism or normal-subgroup stability. No induced action on blocks or block stability is automatic.
