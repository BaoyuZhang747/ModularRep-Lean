theorem-developmentcurrent

Purpose

This module formalises the cyclic-group special case of the p-hypoelementary condition used in the even-field unipotent argument.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

For a finite cyclic group, the p-core is its Sylow p-subgroup, so the quotient is cyclic and prime to p. The analogous statements hold for cyclic subgroups.

Argument or construction

Identify the p-core of a finite cyclic group with its normal Sylow p-subgroup. Quotient cardinality removes the full p-part, and cyclicity passes to this quotient and subgroup quotients.

External assumptions and dependencies

A finite cyclic group and p prime for the prime-to-p-order conclusion; the normal Sylow subgroup and the constructed p-core are prior results.
