Purpose

Extracts fixed basis counts from a symmetrization rank.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

For an involution permuting a finite basis in characteristic zero, fixed-count plus dimension equals twice the rank of one plus the operator.

Argument or construction

The trace of the permutation operator counts fixed basis vectors. The projector obtained by dividing one plus the involution by two identifies the trace with its image dimension, yielding 31 fixed points from ranks 41 and 36.

External assumptions and dependencies

A finite basis, a linear operator acting by a specified involutive basis permutation, and characteristic zero. The numerical corollary adds independently proved dimensions 41 and 36.
