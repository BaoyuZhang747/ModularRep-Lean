Purpose

Computes specified block reindexing and both block-fibre equivalences.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

Two complete primitive catalogues and an actual group equivalence determine block labels, stabilizers, Brauer fibres and weight fibres.

Argument or construction

Group algebra primitive equations identify the block map. Actual root transport and whole-pair transport give the two fibre maps and their automorphism squares.

External assumptions and dependencies

Individual ambient and own-normalizer primitive equations for the two independent block sources.
