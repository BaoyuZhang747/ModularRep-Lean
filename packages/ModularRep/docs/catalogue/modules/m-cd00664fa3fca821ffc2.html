Purpose

Shows that the trivial Brauer character's block has support at every p-subgroup.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

The principal block idempotent has augmentation one and nonzero central Brauer restriction at every p-subgroup; if p divides the group order, it is distinct from a block with trivial maximal defect.

Argument or construction

Conjugacy-orbit cancellation preserves the augmentation of a central element under Brauer restriction. The principal idempotent acts as one in the trivial representation, so its augmentation is one. A nontrivial Sylow subgroup then contradicts a hypothetical trivial maximal defect representative.

External assumptions and dependencies

The literal block decomposition and its action on the trivial representation, p-group orbit cancellation and the stated maximal-defect characterization.
