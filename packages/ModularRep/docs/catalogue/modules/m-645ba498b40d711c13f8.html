Purpose

Identifies the actual modular role-two idempotent.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

Selected ordinary values and uniform IntegralScalarDescent give the explicit role-two modular coefficients at all class representatives.

Argument or construction

The selected projector is proved integral, central and idempotent, and its action is one on the selected row and zero on unequal-degree rows covering the other roles. Scalar descent transfers those tests to the modular catalogue, whose separation identifies the specified primitive idempotent.

External assumptions and dependencies

Complete actual ordinary degree/block allocation, class coverage and centralizer/value bindings; IntegralScalarDescent is the explicit uniform ordinary-to-modular U/E realization principle and is not proved by the projector calculation.
