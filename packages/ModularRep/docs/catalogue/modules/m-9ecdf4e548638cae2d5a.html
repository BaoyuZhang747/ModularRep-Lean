Purpose

Proves the interval central character formula.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

For a full central Brauer interval, coefficient restriction and the interval Brauer map have identical values under every local block central character.

Argument or construction

Their difference has zero Brauer restriction at the normal local copy of the p-subgroup. The simple-module catalogue theorem therefore annihilates it, establishing the entire linear-map equality.

External assumptions and dependencies

A full CentralBrauerInterval and the actual local primitive decomposition and central character catalogue, over an algebraically closed field of characteristic p. No independent Navarro414 equality is assumed by this theorem.
