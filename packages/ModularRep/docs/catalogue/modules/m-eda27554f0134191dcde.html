theorem-developmentcurrent

Purpose

Constructs the identity prime-to-two cover after removing a full two-kernel.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

A nonabelian simple quotient of a full universal central cover with two-group kernel is its own universal prime-to-two cover.

Argument or construction

Any map from that kernel into an odd central kernel is trivial. The universal lift descends, and perfectness gives surjectivity of the resulting section.

External assumptions and dependencies

The actual full Sp-to-PSp cover, order-two centre and simple quotient, including the scoped (2,3) case.
