Purpose

The conductor is the least common multiple of the orders of the actual special Clifford and conformal symplectic groups.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

Existing sufficient-root hypotheses construct a primitive root for this conductor and an embedding of its rational cyclotomic field into the prescribed ordinary field. Its map into the algebraic closure is the composite of that same embedding.
