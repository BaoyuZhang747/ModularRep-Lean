Purpose

This file checks the composition law behind the direct automorphism transport on local normaliser quotients.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

The normalizer quotient maps for successive right twists satisfy the composition law up to the specified representation isomorphism, giving genuine actions on representation-weight isomorphism and conjugacy classes.

Argument or construction

Compare the canonical normalizer-quotient isomorphisms under two successive right twists. Their coherence gives representation isomorphisms, then action laws on isomorphism classes and finally on ambient conjugacy classes.

External assumptions and dependencies

The actual normalizer quotient transports and representation-weight isomorphism relation; its opposite-automorphism orientation is the specified right action.
