Purpose

Covers the entire original-group interval for a top base.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

A specified induction relation on the original group gives all intermediate block laws above its top base.

Argument or construction

Every subgroup containing the top base is equal to it. The proof therefore transports the existing global and local characters and catalogues through the top-intersection square.

External assumptions and dependencies

Actual global/local roots, Brauer characters, block catalogues and their existing specified induction relation. Since the base is top, all intermediate groups coincide with it.
