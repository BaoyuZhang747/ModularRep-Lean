Purpose

Reindexes the same associated projective models across equal base subgroups.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

Changing base subgroup presentations by proved equalities preserves model operators, reindexes factor sets and retains the quotient square and factor-set comparison.

Argument or construction

Transport the representations and models through subgroup congruence. Factor-set equality and cohomology pull back along the induced quotient equivalences, with the original coboundary cochain composed with the reindexing. On quotient representatives the new quotient map is still induced by the original inclusion.

External assumptions and dependencies

Equalities of the base and local subgroups and the original quotient square, factor-set equality and cohomology witness.
