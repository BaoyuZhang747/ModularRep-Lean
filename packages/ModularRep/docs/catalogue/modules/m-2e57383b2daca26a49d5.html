Purpose

Proves the finite matrix and rank argument behind the GGGR basis.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

Nonnegative entries with column sums one, one exceptional double fibre, equivariance and closure-order vanishing yield class-preserving permutation diagonal blocks and an invertible triangular scalar-product matrix.

Argument or construction

The two-fibre sum and symmetry force a permutation matrix. Reindexing all geometric fibres gives a triangular matrix with unit diagonal, whose determinant is a unit and whose columns yield a basis after the dimension comparison.

External assumptions and dependencies

Concrete component-fibre models, projective coordinate/projection laws and the standard dimension count; no BAW conclusion.
