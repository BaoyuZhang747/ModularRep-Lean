Formalisation.ComputationArithmetic

packages/formalisation/Formalisation/ComputationArithmetic.lean

computation-bindingauxiliary

Purpose

This file checks the finite arithmetic used after the GAP outputs have been accepted: block totals, independently supplied character and weight sector totals, cancellation for the faithful sectors of 3.Ω₇(3), and the orbit signature comparison for the principal block.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

The explicit accepted finite counts satisfy the stated block totals, central-sector subtraction and involution orbit signatures used by the computational applications.

Argument or construction

Evaluate the accepted finite transcript numerals to prove block totals, sector subtraction and involution orbit signatures. These arithmetic deductions do not identify the numeric labels with the claimed characters or weights.

External assumptions and dependencies

The explicit accepted transcript numerals used for block, central-sector and orbit arithmetic. The file does not supply their group/character/weight interpretation.
