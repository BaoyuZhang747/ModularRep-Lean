ModularRep.OrdinaryCharacterCyclicExtensionBridge

ModularRep/OrdinaryCharacterCyclicExtensionBridge.lean

transport-adaptercurrent

Purpose

This is the character-level K consumer of the existing representation-level Representation.CyclicExtensionPrinciple, licensed by Isaacs, Corollary 11.22.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

An ordinary irreducible character invariant under a cyclic normal quotient has an actual extending ordinary character, conditional on the supplied representation cyclic extension principle.

Argument or construction

Realize the invariant ordinary irreducible character, use characteristic-zero trace separation to obtain conjugation invariance of that representation, and apply the supplied cyclic extension principle. Its trace gives the desired extending character.

External assumptions and dependencies

The ordinary Isaacs Theorem 11.22 or modular Navarro Theorem 8.12 cyclic extension principle, as selected by the declaration. Actual character realizations, finite compatible roots, invariance and cyclic quotient are required; local applications additionally require their raw quotient and selected-reduction dictionaries.
