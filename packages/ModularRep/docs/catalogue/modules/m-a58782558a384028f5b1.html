Purpose

Transport normalizers and p-cores through a group isomorphism.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

Group isomorphisms preserve and reflect radicality by mapping their normalizers and p-cores; this includes automorphism pullback and conjugation.

Argument or construction

Transport normalizers and p-cores through a group isomorphism. The resulting image equation proves radicality of both mapped and pulled-back subgroups, including conjugation.

External assumptions and dependencies

A group isomorphism and the established p-core and normalizer transport formulas, applied to the given radical equality.
