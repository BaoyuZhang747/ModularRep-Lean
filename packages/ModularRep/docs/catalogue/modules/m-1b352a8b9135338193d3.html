Purpose

Constructs ordinary irreducibles and defect-zero characters of finite products.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

For finite factor groups over a sufficiently split ordinary field, the supplied ordinary product theorem yields a character equivalence with prescribed product values and proves defect zero exactly factorwise.

Argument or construction

Existence and unique factorization define a bijective external-product map. Degree multiplication and the supplied defect-zero criterion identify defect zero coordinatewise.

External assumptions and dependencies

Isaacs ordinary external-product/finiteness sources and their exact character realization.
