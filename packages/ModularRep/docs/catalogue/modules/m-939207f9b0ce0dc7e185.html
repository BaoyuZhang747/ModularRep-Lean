ModularRep.PrimeRegularRootEmbeddingSubgroup

ModularRep/PrimeRegularRootEmbeddingSubgroup.lean

transport-adaptercurrent

Purpose

A prime regular root embedding for an ambient finite group can be reselected so that it agrees with a prescribed embedding for a subgroup on every root needed by the subgroup.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

Given a root embedding on a subgroup and existence of an ambient root embedding, some choice of ambient embedding agrees with the prescribed subgroup one on its finite roots.

Argument or construction

Compare the prescribed subgroup correspondence with a restricted ambient one. Extend the resulting automorphism of the smaller cyclic root group and adjust the ambient correspondence, obtaining existence of an agreeing choice.

External assumptions and dependencies

A prescribed root embedding on a subgroup and existence of an ambient finite root embedding. The output is a reselected ambient embedding, not equality with an independently fixed one.
