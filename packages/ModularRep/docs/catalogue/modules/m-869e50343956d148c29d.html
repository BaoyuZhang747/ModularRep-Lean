ModularRep.CharacterWeightBlockAssignment

ModularRep/CharacterWeightBlockAssignment.lean

transport-adaptercurrent

Purpose

An Alperin weight consists of a radical subgroup Q and a defect-zero ordinary character of N_G(Q) / Q.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

Given literal local block operations, central character catalogues and induction definedness at raw weights, each character-weight conjugacy class receives an equivariant ambient induced-block assignment.

Argument or construction

For a raw character weight, select its literal quotient block, inflate to the normalizer, and prove induction defined there. The central character selector gives its ambient block; isomorphism, conjugation and twist laws descend the assignment to weight classes.

External assumptions and dependencies

Literal quotient block selection and normalizer inflation operations, the actual normalizer/ambient primitive decompositions and Navarro central character catalogues, induction definedness at each raw weight and uniform covariance laws.
