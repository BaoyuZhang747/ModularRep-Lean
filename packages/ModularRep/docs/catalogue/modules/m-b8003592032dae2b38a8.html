Purpose

Identifies actual principal weight-cover fibres with involution orbits.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

When actual principal weight-cover fibres have size one or two and a split fibre is moved by the involution, proves two lower weights have the same cover exactly when they lie in the same involution orbit.

Argument or construction

In a split fibre the original and moved points are distinct. Cardinality two forces every other member to be one of those points, giving the exact cover equality criterion.

External assumptions and dependencies

Published calibrated cover cardinality/action laws and the nontrivial movement of split weights.
