Purpose

Map p-cores through surjections with p-group kernel and prove radicals contain the ambient p-core.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

A surjection with p-group kernel maps p-cores onto p-cores; radicals contain the ambient p-core. Kernel containment makes its normalizer quotient map an isomorphism, giving representation-weight transport. A separate generic triple-lifting record merely stores its own implication.

Argument or construction

Map p-cores through surjections with p-group kernel and prove radicals contain the ambient p-core. Restrict the quotient to normalizers and identify its kernel to construct the local quotient isomorphism and representation-weight transport.

External assumptions and dependencies

Surjectivity, p-group kernel or kernel containment as stated, finite groups and the established p-core/normalizer lemmas. The separate ModularCharacterTripleLifting record stores its lifting implication and is not derived by the group transport.
