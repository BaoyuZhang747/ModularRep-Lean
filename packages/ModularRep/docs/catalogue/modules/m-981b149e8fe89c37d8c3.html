Purpose

The central Brauer map to C_G(P) can be followed by the literal group algebra embedding into k[H] whenever C_G(P) ≤ H.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

For C_G(P) ≤ H ≤ N_G(P), the embedded central Brauer image lies in Z(kH), and the resulting interval map is an algebra homomorphism.

Argument or construction

Embed the centralizer algebra in the interval subgroup algebra. Normalizer invariance of the original central coefficients makes the Brauer image central there; the composite retains its algebra homomorphism laws.

External assumptions and dependencies

The constructed central Brauer map and the subgroup inclusions C_G(P) ≤ H ≤ N_G(P), with the stated characteristic-p hypotheses.
