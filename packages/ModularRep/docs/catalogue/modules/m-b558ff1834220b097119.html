Purpose

Places P38 on the classified family's own coefficients and specified block.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

The concrete root and selected idempotent are computed from the family, and the protected concrete bijection becomes a bijection on that same family block.

Argument or construction

The construction composes the computed Brauer and weight fibre equivalences with the concrete P38 map. It asserts neither selected-pair equality nor a Definition 3.5 relation.

External assumptions and dependencies

Literal family coefficients, specified block dictionary and the classified P38 source package.
