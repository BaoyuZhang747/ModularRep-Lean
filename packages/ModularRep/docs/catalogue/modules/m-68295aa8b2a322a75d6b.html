Purpose

Transfers matched block induction to every retained original-group projective model pair.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

A faithful Brauer-to-weight equivalence with MatchedBlockOutput gives the original model block law for every raw representative, canonical reduction and compatible global/local model pair.

Argument or construction

Use the literal inflated-normalizer block catalogue already in the carrier adapter. The matched raw block law feeds every_original_model_pair_blocks. Its universal quantifiers then specialize to the two models used in the AD3 comparison, preserving their representations and factor-set trivializations.

External assumptions and dependencies

MatchedBlockOutput, literal block catalogues, canonical raw reductions and the actual global/local product-model identities.
