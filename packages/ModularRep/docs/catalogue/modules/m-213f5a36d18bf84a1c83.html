Purpose

The image subgroups in T/P and their local intersection are identified by the actual quotient maps.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

The quotient triples are formed from the actual global and local inertia groups and the same central kernel.

Argument or construction

The projection maps induce quotient-to-image equivalences. Their pointwise formulas prove the base and local inclusion squares required by character inflation.

External assumptions and dependencies

The normalizer projection is the same qN used to descend the ordinary weight. These are group-theoretic deductions; no character equation or block-triple witness is an external premise.
