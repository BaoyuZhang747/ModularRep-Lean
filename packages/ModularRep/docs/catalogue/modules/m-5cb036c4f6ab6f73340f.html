ModularRep.RepresentationSurjectiveDescent

ModularRep/RepresentationSurjectiveDescent.lean

transport-adaptercurrent

Purpose

This neutral module constructs a descent of a representation whose kernel contains the kernel of a surjective group homomorphism.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

A representation whose kernel contains that of a surjective group homomorphism descends to the target on the same vector space, and irreducibility is preserved.

Argument or construction

Factor the representation through the quotient by the homomorphism kernel, then use the first group isomorphism theorem to define it on the target. Surjective pullback identifies invariant subspaces and preserves irreducibility.

External assumptions and dependencies

A surjective group homomorphism and containment of its kernel in the representation kernel; irreducibility is required only for its preservation statement.
