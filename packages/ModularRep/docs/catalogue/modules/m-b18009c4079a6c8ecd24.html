Formalisation.C2Cancellation

packages/formalisation/Formalisation/C2Cancellation.lean

theorem-developmentcurrent

Purpose

Decompose an involution into singleton and two-element orbits.

Mathematical statement and assumptions

Finite involution sets with equal cardinality and equal fixed-point count are equivariantly equivalent; the corresponding fixed/two-cycle signatures admit cancellation.

Argument or construction

Decompose an involution into singleton and two-element orbits. Total size and fixed-point count determine both orbit counts, proving classification and cancellation; the two-copy model verifies block-preserving extension of a chosen bijection.

External assumptions and dependencies

Finite types with involutive permutations and the stated total/fixed-point cardinality equalities or orbit-count data.
