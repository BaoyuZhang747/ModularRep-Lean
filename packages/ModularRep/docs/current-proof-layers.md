# Formal arguments and assumptions

This guide explains the current mathematical interfaces and their proof dependencies. All `TypeC*` and `TypeB*` modules below lie under `ModularRep.PaperProofs`; the shared root-namespace `ModularRep.CurrentSpath*` and `ModularRep.LinearBlockStabilizer*` modules use the shorter root namespace. Full declaration locations and current printed numbers are in the [manuscript map](current-manuscript-map.json). The [verification record](verification.md) gives the standalone build, audit and archive scope.

Its full raw-stabilizer containment is part of the triple side of the equivalence. The descent's full cover is distinct from the prime-to-the-coefficient-characteristic cover; no assertion that these are the same cover is required. The finite forward implication in the central return is cover-free, so it can apply to the finite downstairs group used in the exceptional principal passage.

`CurrentSpathCriterion` and `CurrentSpathDescent` retain algebraically closed ordinary value fields in the scope of their published sources, without storing a DVR with that same fraction field. Precise Späth, Martínez–Rizo–Rossi and quotient-block source locators are recorded in the [source ledger](source-assumptions.md).

manuscript.

## Retained general mathematics and further library work

The applications use the Conlon proof, rational-span linear algebra, subgroup/Hall calculations, literal block operations, own-character transport and complete criterion structures. The [library guide](library.md) identifies their general imports and possible future extraction boundaries. Useful further work includes canonical finite-splitting character labels across subgroup diagrams, a reusable specified primitive-block family constructor, systematic transport of complete criterion packets across actual quotient squares, full-field Clifford/Levi orbit APIs, and a source-independent decomposition-map framework. These are library directions, not premises silently discharged by the current applications.
