# Source assumptions and their mathematical scope

The application theorems are conditional on their explicit Lean parameters. K denotes the checked deductions; E1 standard facts, E2 published implications, E3 computational/completeness data, and U source-to-carrier identifications describe the external boundary. Acceptance of a parameter type does not construct an inhabitant. In particular, a printed theorem name, a group order, or a table record name does not by itself establish a group isomorphism or a character correspondence.

The exact contract is the full type of the indicated record and theorem, including nested records and instances. This ledger gives a mathematical index to those contracts. General implications must be supplied with their authentic hypotheses; no source may be the current manuscript conclusion, a caller-selected goal, or a correspondence that depends on the correspondence being constructed.

## Shared coefficients, blocks, and transport

The coefficient boundary retains fields `k` and `K`, the prime characteristic, the prime regular root embedding, splitting requirements for the relevant subgroup representations, and compatibility of reduction, inflation, restriction, induction, and character values. Where integral descent is used, a complete DVR system has fraction field `K`, residue field `k`, and integral primitive block idempotents reducing to the same specified blocks. Root agreement is required on each finite domain actually used. The sources do not assert equality of arbitrary independently prescribed roots.

Literal primitive-block catalogues, ordinary and Brauer fibres, local defect-zero rows, radical and normalizer identifications, and action laws remain attached to the same actual groups. The dictionaries must preserve both directions of every fibre equivalence used for surjectivity or representative selection. Quotient and extension data must refer to the same selected global and local character.
