# Manuscript correspondence

The [application statement map](../../../audit/current/statement-map.json)
records the numbered manuscript statements, associated Lean declarations and
their stated assumptions. The [library projection](current-manuscript-map.json)
distinguishes library providers from application providers.

Read the [formalisation report](../../../output/pdf/formalisation-report.pdf)
for the mathematical explanation. A recorded association is not a proof of the
external assumptions or of equivalence with the entire manuscript argument.
